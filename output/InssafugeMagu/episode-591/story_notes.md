# Inssafuge Magu — episode 591 story notes

## Synopsis
A cold, rain-lashed late-September night in Manhattan. On the 44th floor of Vance & Hart Investments in the Financial
District, billionaire founder Victor Vance (50) is found dead at his desk, apparently by his own hand. FBI special
criminal analyst Alex Brand arrives, followed by NYPD Detective Rogers, who calls it an obvious suicide: the cameras
show Victor entering alone at 8:30, the door was locked from inside, Wall Street is in trouble. Alex silently loosens
his tie and walks the room, mapping its geometry. Kneeling by the desk he explains: a suicide locks the door so nobody
stops him, but would not tidy everything — the shot came from the right, yet phone, wallet and papers are lined up on
the left; the desk was staged. Victor's left hand is rigid, clenched, nails dug into the palm. Alex gently opens the
cold fingers: an ancient heavy bronze-gold Roman coin, a laurel-crowned emperor on one side and "Mors omnia solvit"
("death settles everything") on the other — a coin given to the honoured dead. Skin under the nails: Victor fought;
someone held him from behind and forced his own finger on the trigger. The cameras? Hackers loop cameras like a
children's game. Alex orders the servers seized and forensics in: it is murder. At the window, the city below like a
black sea, he senses a long-planned revenge. His phone vibrates — "Father". Judge Richard Brand, trembling, saw the
breaking news. Alex says it is murder. Silence, fast breathing, then a warning: some things should not be dug into,
Vance dealt with dangerous people, accept suicide, think of your career. Alex, stunned that his hero says stop,
mentions the Roman coin — and on the line a crystal glass shatters on stone, a frightened gasp, the call dies. "Dad?
Dad!" Only the dead tone. Then the antique fax machine in the corner starts by itself (Rogers startles); a white sheet
with no sender: "The first step has been taken. Six remain. The sins of 1998 cannot be hidden by buried bones.
Justice comes riding the rain, Alex." The killer knew he'd come and knows his name. Thunder; the lights flicker. A
dangerous game over a twenty-year-old secret — with Alex and his family in the middle. To be continued.
