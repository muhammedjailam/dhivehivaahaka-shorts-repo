# Handi Kujaa — episode 594 (third of five: 580 → 583 → 594 → 605 → 629)

## Synopsis
Continues straight from 583: Naazim at the tent flap sees the handi standing by the waves, her dark "hair" spread over
the surf, red eyes glowing like lamps. Zaahid comes up behind him — "Ya Allah!". A scream from the dhoni in the lagoon:
skipper Sameer, who stayed aboard to fix the engine. Aadhambe shouts "Don't go out!", but the brothers run through rain
and surf, climb aboard and find Sameer dead at the engine-room door (never shown). A soft laugh from the stern: the
handi sits by the tiller, soaked — "Now it's your turn, you two...". She rises; the planks creak with every step. A huge
wave heels the dhoni; the men fall; she flies onto the cabin roof, red eyes blazing. "Naazim! Jump!" They dive into the
cold, oily, foul-smelling sea and swim ashore; looking back, she stands on the cabin roof stretching her arm toward the
jungle as if signalling. In the tent: women and children terrified, Aslam twitching on a mat. Aadhambe burns leaves in a
clay pot for smoke and moves the family to the middle. A long, bent, inhuman shadow slides over the canvas; long fingers
scrape "khrees... khrees". Aisha screams and drops beside Aslam. Aadhambe recites over a bowl of water, blows three times
and sprinkles it round the tent; a pained cry outside. The lantern dies; in the dark Aslam sits up and seizes Aisha's
throat (never shown). Raaidhu blows the clay pot's embers alight. Shareefa pulls her son's arm — "Let go, my son!". The
handi's voice from Aslam: "He is mine! None of you can save him today!". Aadhambe recites the strongest verses and strikes
with his stick (never shown as contact); Aslam lets go and falls aside. Zaahid and Naazim burst in stinking of the sea:
"Sameer has been killed!". Naazim: "How will we be saved? The engine is broken!". Aadhambe: only sunrise saves us; her
power lives in the dark; we stay shut in this tent. A heavy thud on the roof, the poles shake; the voice outside, now like
a hundred voices: "Aslam... come to me... or your whole family will be buried here tonight." Aslam's unconscious body
slowly rises into the air, limbs hanging, face toward the roof. Aisha weeps and reaches for his foot — Aadhambe: "Don't
touch him! Get back!"
