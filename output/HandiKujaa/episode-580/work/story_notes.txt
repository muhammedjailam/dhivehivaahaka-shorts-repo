# Handi Kujaa — Episode 580 (series opener) — story notes

## Synopsis
On a sunny holiday morning a wooden dhoni carries grandfather Aadhambe's family of ten across a silk-blue sea to
Beenaafushi, a famous uninhabited picnic island. Aslam (22), the family chatterbox, calls from the prow that the
weather is lovely; his father Zaahid smiles at the sky — though this is the season when the weather turns fast. They
land in the hot early afternoon: Raaidhu helps grandfather down, Aslam helps his mother Shareefa. Grandfather and
mother set up cooking and tea, the little children swim and play. By midday the two other groups' dhonis leave and the
family has the island to itself. After 4 pm a black cloud from the north swallows the sky; Aadhambe shouts for Zaahid to
call the skipper, but in the wild waves something tangles in the propeller and the engine dies. Stranded, they watch the
sunset spread "like a dreadful curtain". Shareefa calls to Aslam not to go far into the jungle; the wind swallows it.
Aslam is on the west shore watching the waves when thunder breaks and the rain pours. Running back through the jungle he
sees, in a small clearing, a beautiful woman in white, her long dark hair flowing in the wind, eyes glowing, a
bewitching smile. Human — or a handi? She beckons; her eyes turn red. Frozen, he hears a soft voice whisper "Aslam...";
she steps back into the jungle, her soaked gown glowing like light from the deep sea, and he feels pulled after her.
He tears himself away to run — a terrible growl shakes the whole jungle. In the storm-lashed tent Aadhambe recites
Quran; Shareefa, near tears: "Aslam still hasn't come!" Zaahid shouts into the black rain. Naazim takes a torch to
search, Raaidhu wants to come; Aadhambe forbids anyone going alone — "this is no ordinary storm" — and leads the four
men together into the jungle while the women pray. Tree shadows swirl like giant ghosts. A crash on the right: under a
huge nika tree, caught in its roots, a freshly dead seabird and a stench of rot and burning. Aadhambe prods it with his
stick and whispers: "This was not done by humans. Hurry — Aslam's life is in danger." Deep in the jungle Aslam stands
entranced as the handi glides towards him, her feet making no sound on the leaves.

## Locations
Open sea / dhoni prow (sunny morning) → Beenaafushi lagoon and beach camp (hot afternoon) → wide beach (midday) →
storm beach (after 4 pm, bruised violet) → lagoon edge in waves (dhoni stern) → dreadful sunset beach → west shore
coral rocks (stormy dusk, rain begins) → jungle clearing / jungle paths (storm night, sea-green glow) → canvas tent
(lantern) and its flap → torchlit jungle → giant nika-tree roots.

## Tone
Bright, carefree holiday for the first two minutes (turquoise and white, no night colours), turning through a bruised
violet storm afternoon and an orange-violet sunset into a near-black indigo storm night with silver rain, lightning,
lantern and torch light, and the handi's cold sea-green glow. Horror is atmospheric only.
