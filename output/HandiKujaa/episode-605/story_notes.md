# Handi Kujaa — episode 605 (4th of 5: 580 → 583 → 594 → 605 → 629)

## Synopsis
Continues the storm night straight from 594, in the same lantern-lit tent: Aslam's unconscious, possessed body floats
above the mat; Aisha reaches for his foot and Aadhambe stops her. Aadhambe recites over a bowl of water and throws it at
Aslam: tiny blue sparks fly off him and he drops to the floor. A shriek on the roof, and the canvas rips open down the
middle. Through the tear the handi stands on top of the tent pole in the rain, her beauty gone: "Not one of you will
leave this island alive tonight!" Aadhambe raises his stick to face her and sends the family running to the ancient nika
(banyan) tree on the shore: "Nobody look back!" They run through the rain over sand that looks blood-red. Lightning:
Naazim glances back and sees grandfather fallen, his stick snapped in two, and the handi flying after them. Zaahid
shouts "Don't look back! Run!" The handi's laughter drifts over the women and children like a shadow; black roots burst
from the sand to catch their feet; Shareefa recites Aayathul Kursi with the children. They reach the centuries-old
tree, where travellers once sat reciting Quran and where protective recitations were buried long ago, and hide in its
roots. The handi stops at the edge of its shade, cannot enter, claws the air: "Before sunrise I will tear up that tree
too!" Aslam's eyes darken; he grips Aisha's foot and speaks in another voice: "Uncle... grandpa is coming..." A figure
walks out of the rain: grandfather, smiling, holding the broken stick. Zaahid starts toward him; Naazim holds him back.
The old man leaves no footprints on the wet sand, and his feet are turned backwards. "That's not Bappa!" The handi laughs,
and the two figures circle the tree: "Your grandfather is in our power now!" Aslam goes rigid, and Aisha cries for help.
Naazim remembers grandfather's tale that spirits fear iron. Zaahid pulls the big old iron cooking knife from his wet bag,
and Naazim, praying, drives it into the sand beside Aslam's head. Aslam relaxes, and two shrieks sound outside. Then a
rustle above: hundreds of red-eyed black crows in the canopy pour down like a black cloud. Zaahid covers his head,
Naazim whirls a cloth to drive them off, and the women push the children into the roots and shield them.
