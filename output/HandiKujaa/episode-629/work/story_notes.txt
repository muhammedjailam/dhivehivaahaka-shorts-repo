# Handi Kujaa — episode 629 (story order 5 of 5; ends on a cliffhanger)

## Synopsis
Continuation of 605, same place and light: the family shelters inside the giant roots of the ancient banyan (nika) on
the storm-lashed shore, the old iron kitchen knife stuck upright in the sand beside Aslam. Shareefa and Aisha shield the
three small children while red-eyed crows swirl; the handi cannot enter the tree's shade but has turned the island's
birds on them. Aslam suddenly stands, his eyes normal but full of terror, and screams "Stop!" — every crow falls silent
and returns to the treetop. In tears he tells his mother and uncle that the spirit has loosened his tongue: a hundred
years ago travellers killed her child in this jungle; her vengeful spirit rises once every hundred years and tonight is
that night; she wants the life of the family's youngest male — Raaidhu. Raaidhu backs against a root. Outside the shade
the false grandfather stretches out his arms and calls in grandfather's loving voice; his backward feet are hidden by
the roots. Raaidhu, spellbound, walks forward; Zaahid grips his shoulders ("Bappa, it's grandpa calling!"). Naazim
shouts that it is a trick and, hand on the iron knife, recites Aayathul Kursi. The handi descends and lays her long
nails on the impostor's shoulder: his loving face decays, his eyes turn red; the spell breaks and Raaidhu falls back,
awake. "You won't last till sunrise — the sea is coming." The waves turn red and creep up the shore; where they touch
the outer roots, the roots blacken. Aslam: they must reach the old ziyaarath in the island's centre, the oldest and
safest building, which the wave cannot reach. Zaahid fears the jungle, but there is no other way. Naazim pulls the knife
from the sand; the eight leave the shade. With thunder the handi dives, her smoky "hair" spreading like a net; Naazim
swings the iron and she recoils shrieking into the treetops. Aslam leads through black mud and thorns; the women carry
the children; Zaahid keeps Raaidhu's hand. A crash on the right: the false grandfather bursts out on backward feet and
rakes Naazim's shoulder; Naazim falls, is nearly dragged off, strikes the figure's arm with the iron — an inhuman roar,
it lets go. Zaahid and Aslam lift Naazim. The handi descends again, intent on Raaidhu. They burst into a treeless
clearing: a small pale ancient whitewashed ziyaarath, a strange silence. Raaidhu prays; a flicker of hope — which dies:
in the shrine doorway stands skipper Sameer, whom they saw dead on the dhoni, upright, death-white face, black eyes, arm
outstretched. (cliffhanger)
