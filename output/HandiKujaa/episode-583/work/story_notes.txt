# Handi Kujaa — Episode 583 (part 2 of 5: 580 → 583 → 594 → 605 → 629)

## Synopsis
Continues straight from 580, deep in the storm jungle of Beenaafushi. The white-clad spirit glides silently towards
the spellbound Aslam, raises her long cold hand with long black nails, and her voice sounds inside his head: "Come to
me..." As he reaches for her hand, his father Zaahid's distant shout ("Aslam! My son! Where are you?") breaks the
spell. Her beauty melts into a dreadful, long-dead face; Aslam tries to scream but no sound comes, stumbles back over a
root and falls. In the search party Raaidhu points to a pale blue-green glow in the west; Zaahid runs to it despite
Aadhambe. They find Aslam unconscious and ice-cold, strange black drawings in the sand around him and a wet black
handprint on his shirt; Zaahid lifts him into his arms. With a bird-like shriek a huge shadow flies off over the
screw-pines, the wind redoubles and every torch dies. In pitch darkness and needle-like rain Raaidhu panics; Zaahid,
clutching Aslam, calls "I'm here!" Aadhambe recites Aayathul Kursi and the dreadful sounds quieten. Naazim's charged
phone is dead. "This is the doing of that bird that flew off — take Aslam to the beach, or these things will play with
our minds." Naazim's torch flickers back on; the brothers carry Aslam out to the tent. Shareefa and Aisha run to him
crying: his face white and swollen, lips black, a stench of rotting fish and burning. Aisha takes his hand — it burns
hot, though Zaahid said it was ice-cold. Aslam opens completely white eyes, sits up, stares out into the rain and
laughs; a heavy woman's voice comes from him: "She has come... she has come for me..." Shareefa hides the little
children; Aisha is stunned; Zaahid: "Father! What has befallen us?" Aadhambe kneels before Aslam and reaches for his
forehead; Aslam grips his wrist like iron: "Old man! Do you think you can break my power?" His eyes turn red-veined.
"By Allah's leave your power will leave this body!" Aadhambe recites Surat al-Jinn aloud; Aslam convulses, hands over
his ears, screaming; the canvas whips, the lantern flares and dims. Zaahid and Naazim hold his arms and legs, Raaidhu
his feet — he has the strength of four men. Black liquid from his mouth (not shown); he collapses unconscious.
Silence but the rain. "Is he saved?" "Only for now. The handi is in this jungle; she wants Aslam's soul. We must
survive tonight." "But the dhoni's engine is broken." A sliding "shhh" sound on the sand outside; Naazim lifts the door
flap: by the frothing waves stands the handi in white, facing the tent, the surf roaring louder where she stands;
lightning; her long "hair" spread over the foam, two red eyes glowing like lit lamps.
