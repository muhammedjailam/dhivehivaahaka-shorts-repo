# Gaathil — episode 608 — story notes

## Synopsis
Night, Shahid's room. Under the desk lamp Shahid turns the silicone mask over: its tiny maker's mark is the kind used
only in films or by foreign spy agencies. How did Ibrahim Naseem get it? Gripping the mouse, he goes through the other
night photos and finds, in a corner, a fishing dhoni far out at sea; the man at its helm has the same build as the "old
man" who ran from him today. Ibrahim does not just live as an old man — he lives in disguises: an old man with a long
beard buying goods at a shop, a young fisherman, a foreign labourer on the harbour. That is why no one has recognised
him in twenty years. Why does he meet the people who use the warehouses? A message arrives from an unknown number; with
a trembling hand he opens it: "That mask in your hand could become your death sentence. Don't try to find its secret!
If Amjad sees it, that night will be the last night of your life!" His breath stops; questions without answers.

Flashback into Ibrahim's memories: years ago, with Amjad laying all suspicion for the island murder on him, Ibrahim fled
for his life — Amjad's secret advice: never set foot on this island again. Amjad sent him away for his own safety.
Ibrahim landed on a lonely, sparsely populated island in another atoll with a new face from the silicone mask Amjad
secretly gave him, and became "Adam", a quiet, hard-working fisherman, fighting his dark past every day at sea. The
kind, innocent Mariyam ended his loneliness; she knew nothing of his past. He asked her family for her hand and the
marriage was tied. Every night he had to remove the mask secretly in the pitch dark — the pain of a husband who cannot
show his own face to his wife. About two years later Amjad called the secret phone: come back to the old island at
once, or he would expose the mask in front of Mariyam. One night Adam told Mariyam they had to flee — "a very secret,
dangerous government mission". Loving and trusting, she followed. On a small launch on a pitch-black night they reached
the island of his past; he led her into the most isolated jungle ("Adam… where are we going?" — "Mari… be quiet! Our
safety is here."), to an iron hatch in the ground and an underground bunker: a small room with electricity, secret
papers, sealed food boxes. On the second night Mariyam wakes to see Adam at the table, hand on his face; he pulls
something from near his ear and lays the whole mask on the table — a completely different, ageing face. Who did she
marry? Frozen, she stares at the mask on the table and the real face of the man sitting there. (Cliffhanger.)

the far island, then darkness tightening (night call, storm, black sea, jungle, bunker) to the reveal. No music; hums
only at fear peaks (message, the call/threat, the hatch, the reveal).
