# Gaathil — Episode 607: story notes

Duration 320.7 s (5 min 21 s), 33 segments/shots. Continues directly from 606 (police-station interview room).

## Synopsis
Officer Amjad stands at the interview-room window, a storm in his head: a secret buried for twenty years has come
back to life through Shahid's photo. He turns, puts his hand on Shahid's shoulder and declares the matter one of
national security: from today he handles it personally; no islander and no other officer may know — "a secret
between you and me." Shahid nods, but Amjad's sudden decision and the colour of his face plant a seed of suspicion.
The moment Shahid leaves, Amjad locks the door, takes a secret phone from the bottom of his drawer and calls:
"Where are you? You've been seen! Your face got into a camera lens… leave that place now!" Shahid walks out uneasy:
was the secrecy for the investigation, or to hide something? He decides to dig on his own. At home he reopens the
photo — this time not Ibrahim's face but the broken iron piece at his feet and the ruined wall behind, which he knows
is not the lighthouse: it is the old warehouses by the east harbour. There he finds fresh footprints and a small note
hinting at a meeting. As he leaves, a strange-looking figure appears behind him, warns him and runs into the
warehouses; Shahid chases but loses him and finds a disguise mask. Amjad arrives with a police team, questions him,
orders "Seal the whole area! Nobody leaves!", then steps close and whispers: "Didn't I tell you this is none of your
business? Today you got away. Next time I see you here, I'll have to arrest you." Shahid is now sure Amjad is hiding
Ibrahim — or silencing him, and maybe Shahid too. Walking away afraid, he feels the weight of his bag: the silicone
mask Ibrahim dropped. At home, door locked, under the bright desk lamp he finds a tiny maker's mark inside its edge —
the kind used only in films or by foreign spy agencies. "How did Ibrahim Naseem get such a thing?"

## Tone
Slow-burn paranoia thriller: grey, desaturated, painterly; suspense from locked doors, a hidden phone, empty
warehouse lanes, footprints in sand, narrowed eyes, a whisper at the ear. No music; hums only on fear peaks.

## Image budget

(seg 19–22) changes action every ~10 s (note → stranger → chase → mask + police arrival), so it has four short
beats; the police room dialogue and the whisper scene are held long on two-shots with crops instead.
