# Gaathil — episode 606 (series opener)

## Synopsis
A pitch-dark stormy night. At the island's east tip the crumbling old lighthouse rises from the black sea like a
giant spirit. Photographer Ali Shahid, who loves unusual scenes, aims his camera at the lighthouse door; thunder,
lightning and the shutter click together. A strange chill, goose-bumps; he hurries home through the rain, locks his
room and loads the photos. On the last one his eyes widen and the coffee mug slips onto the desk: among the shadows
of the trees stands a man in a heavy old-fashioned wool coat, unmoved by the downpour. Zooming 50, 100, 300% he
recognises Ibrahim Naseem, who vanished 20 years ago and whom the island buried as dead: pain and fear on his face,
eyes straight into the lens. The bulb flickers, a slow knock, the window bangs in the wind; trembling he unlocks the
door: his worried old mother heard something fall. He forces a smile ("the coffee mug fell"), sends her off, locks up.
Memory: 20 years ago a powerful businessman was found murdered near the island club warehouse; Ibrahim was the last
person seen there, then vanished; police and islanders searched the island, uninhabited islands and reefs; all
concluded he had died, and the suspicion stuck to him. "But he's alive!" Zooming further: an oddly shaped iron piece at
his feet, an old decaying wall behind him, not the lighthouse. He decides to go to the police in the morning (the
narrator warns: if he knew who lies at the bottom of this, he'd delete the photo). He falls asleep. Golden morning,
dark heart; memory card in his pocket, he suspects every face in the lane. In the cold police room chief Officer
Amjad enters, sits: "What big matter?" Shahid silently opens the photo on Amjad's laptop. As the face enlarges Amjad's
colour changes, he grips a pen, breath short; he controls himself, narrows his eyes: "This is Ibrahim Naseem, who went
missing twenty years ago." Shahid: "He's not dead, he lives on the island, a danger to everyone." Amjad rises and stares
out of the window.
