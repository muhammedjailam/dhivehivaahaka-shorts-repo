# Karuna — Episode 335 (series opener) — story notes (v2 re-cut)

## Synopsis
Shima (20) is the only, adored daughter of Qasim, a proud, strict, wealthy Malé businessman obsessed with his name and
honour, and of gentle Naseema. She lacks nothing. The narrator foreshadows a storm that will shake the family. At
college she meets charming Yazan (22); their eyes meet in the library, he starts talking to her, hours of phone calls
and secret meetings follow, and he promises to marry her after their studies. One night, while Qasim is away on
business and Naseema has gone to sleep early with a headache, Shima slips out to an empty apartment of Yazan's friend
and, giving in to his pleading, crosses every limit (never shown). She comes home and sobs on her bed; his messages and
promises of marriage calm her, and she hides the sin, unable to look her father in the face. Months later: nausea,
dizziness, a missed period. Face covered, she buys a pregnancy test at a far-away pharmacy; the test is positive; it
falls from her hand and she muffles her sobs; panic, terror of her father, thoughts of death. She begs Yazan to meet. At
dusk, in a lonely place, he turns cold: "How do I know it's mine?" She pleads that he promised marriage, that her father
will kill her; he shakes her off, tells her to abort or blame someone else, and walks away while she kneels pleading. He
switches off his phone and vanishes. Shima hides in her room, frozen with fear; Qasim notices and keeps asking her to
see a doctor; she makes excuses. On a terrifying storm night she is sobbing over hundreds of unanswered messages when
Qasim opens her door, sees her try to hide the phone, takes it, reads everything, sees the test kit on her dressing
table, shakes with fury and smashes the phone on the floor (end; 336 continues from here).

lightning for the discovery. No music; low hums on the emotional peaks only.
