# Karuna — episode 336 — story notes (v2 re-cut)

## Synopsis
Picks up straight after 335's phone smash on the storm night. Shima kneels before her father Qasim begging forgiveness
("kill me, but don't throw me out"); Qasim, his honour crushed, disowns her with brutal words ("from today I have no
daughter"). Naseema runs in weeping and tries to stop him but he shoves her aside; he hauls Shima up by the hair, drags
her down the stairs while she pleads that it is raining and she carries a child, opens the big front gate and throws her
out; she falls into a muddy puddle; the gate slams and is locked. In a lightning flash she sits soaked in the mud, hand
on her belly. Reflective narration: a beloved girl now alone on the street because of her own sin; a long dark road
ahead. She wanders the narrow lanes, hides in a dark corner from rowdy youths and sleeps under the eave of a derelict
house, sobbing. Next day people stare and jeer ("there goes that shameless woman"), women point and turn away; she walks
head down, wrapping her dress around herself. Days of hunger make her thin, but a fierce maternal love grows. One hot
afternoon under a seaside tree the baby kicks — tears of joy — and she whispers a promise to protect her child. That
night she prays in a mosque, weeps in sujood and repents; peace and hope fill her. Next morning, faint on a park bench,
she is found by Aisamanike, a kind, lonely, childless elderly woman, who calls her "daughter", strokes her head, says
people make mistakes, repentance is what matters, the baby is innocent, and takes her home: warm food, a clean bed, deep
sleep. Weeks pass: Shima helps with housework and recovers; gossips criticise Aisamanike, who ignores them and tells
Shima to focus on her baby. Shima recites Quran each night, makes dua, discusses names, learns to sew tiny baby clothes
and cries with happiness. She is now a strong mother, at peace through repentance.
