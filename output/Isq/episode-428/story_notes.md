# Isq — Episode 428 — story notes

## Synopsis
Picks up at noon in Nafeesa's kitchen, straight after 393. Jaleel, hands in his pockets, asks Maura whether he may tell her
"something very big". Before she can answer, someone comes towards the kitchen. He steps back, gives her one long, deep
look and leaves fast. Maura stays where she is, full of questions about the closeness she felt in his gaze.
Malé, night: Shaaliya sits down beside her mother-in-law Fauziyya, who has a betel tray on her knees. Fauziyya asks
whether Jaleel has called. Shaaliya's face falls, and she lies that he calls and even sends Mamma his salaam. In fact he
has not called once in three days and does not pick up when she calls. The narration covers the loveless arranged
marriage, Shaaliya's growing love, and Jaleel's rigid principles, power and money: the girl who can light love in his
hard heart must be very special.
The island, night, full moon: after Isha, a bored and friendless Maura (Reema has gone to bed with aching legs) stands at
her gate, then walks to the empty beach and sits on a joali under a tree by the tube-lit beach pavilion. Jaleel comes out
of the mosque, recognises her from behind and, against his principles, walks over. He calls "Maura"; she turns, startled.
He sits on another joali a little apart and asks question after question. She admires him (firm, manly, black shirt with
the sleeves rolled up). She says she has no friends yet: she is from the island but studied business in Malé and has been
back for two days. Their eyes meet. In the silence that follows he worries about gossip that could hurt her name. His
phone rings. It is Shaaliya, and he starts to sweat. While she talks, he watches the wind play with Maura's "hair" (shown
as her hijab). He says he is busy and hangs up on her. Shaaliya, alone, is heartbroken. Jaleel stands, says "I'm going",
then turns back: "Aren't you coming? The house is close." Maura walks over to him, smiling. (To be continued.)

the cold loneliness of the city lights. Shaaliya's scenes are melancholic.
