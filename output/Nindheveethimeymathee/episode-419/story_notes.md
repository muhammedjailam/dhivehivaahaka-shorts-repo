# Nindheveethimeymathee - episode 419 - story notes

Timeline: SCHOOL (A-level year, Lail and Saba 17), a few weeks after Sana's death (ep 401). Lail's loss = his mother Sana.
Duration 12:12 (732 s), 82 shots, 25 beats, 24 new images + 1 in-episode reuse.

## Synopsis
- [00:00] Rooftop pool terrace of Haizum's penthouse on a tall Malé high-rise, late afternoon. The friends are visiting the
  grieving family. Saba and Lail sit apart from the others. Lail thanks her for texting every night to check on him. Saba
  (in English) says her own father died six months ago, so she understands; a tear runs down her cheek. Lail asks what a
  tear tastes like ("salt"), muses that a tear might be centuries old - a lover's, a child's, a parent's - and explains the
  water cycle. Saba smiles and wipes her eyes.
- [02:48] Laira brings pizza, Maama Shafeeqa a basket of soda bottles; Asil, Shahid and Sadhee take them. Maama quietly asks
  Asil to make sure Lail and Laira eat. Everyone eats; Saba and Lail steal glances across the table.
- [03:52] After the meal Laira takes a call, Asil plays a phone game, Sadhee and Shahid chat. Saba goes to the railing;
  Lail joins her. Australia or the Maldives? "Maldives." Then she should start speaking Dhivehi - he will correct her.
- [05:18] Sunset, terrace lights come on. Asil teases them; Saba's Dhivehi comes out wrong; Asil laughs, Lail glares;
  Shahid calls Asil away. Lail teases her about the hair tie in her hand; he murmurs a line of romantic verse; she lowers
  her head, heart stirring.
- [07:22] Time to go. Downstairs, Maama sees them off; Saba remembers her bag in Lail's room. Alone there she admires his
  photo on the study desk; Lail appears, speaks another verse, asks if she will message tonight; as she leaves, he
  (narration) catches her hand, then lets go: "I'll be waiting."
- [10:01] Farewell at the lift; inside, Sadhee, Asil and Shahid tease her ("a flower about to bloom"); "We talked about
  water." Street at night: Asil waves up at the tower; Saba looks up and sees Lail tiny on the balcony; she lowers her head.
