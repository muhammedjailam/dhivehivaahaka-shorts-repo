NINDHEVEETHIMEYMATHEE (ނިންދެވޭތީ މޭމަތީ) - EPISODE 419 - SYNOPSIS
(Transcript length ~12:10. Single continuous evening at Lail's family apartment, then the lift/street outside.)

======================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS
======================================================================

[00:00] Lail's apartment, open terrace/balcony area of a tall building, late afternoon. Saba switches off her phone screen and looks at Lail, who is sitting with her slightly apart from their friends. A quiet, meaningful smile passes between them. Lail softly asks how she is. Saba answers in English ("I am doing great, thank you for asking, what about you?"). Lail says he is not fully okay yet, but he is very grateful that she messages every night to check on him; it makes him happy.

[00:30] Saba smiles but her eyes fill with tears. In English she says her own father passed away six months ago, so she understands what Lail must be going through (implying Lail has recently lost a close family member, apparently a parent). A tear rolls down her cheek and falls onto Lail's open palm, which he had held out. She looks at him, then at his hand; the tear trembles in his palm and he slowly closes his hand around it.

[01:05] Lail asks her what the taste of a tear is. "Salt," she says. From his look she thinks she is wrong; he smiles, shakes his head (not wrong), and opens his palm, where the wetness of the tear still remains. Thoughtfully he says this tear might once, centuries ago, have been the tear of a grieving lover, or of a child, mother, father, sibling, or some other living creature. He asks with a small smile whether he is talking nonsense; Saba says no, it could actually be true, and he eagerly asks how. While she pauses to think, he teases her for taking so long, then explains himself: the amount of water in the world never grows or shrinks; seawater, the water living things drink and excrete, water used for washing all rise as vapour and fall again as rain, a natural cycle repeating for centuries. Saba smiles gently and wipes the corners of her eyes.

[02:48] Lairaa (Lail's sister) comes out carrying three stacked pizza boxes; behind her Shafeeqa (Maama, the grandmother) brings a basket of small Coke bottles. Asil and Shahid take the pizza boxes from Lairaa and Sadhee takes the basket from Maama; they lay everything on a table. Maama whispers in Asil's ear to make sure Lail and Lairaa eat properly (they are grieving); Asil nods. Maama smiles, says "Eat happily," and goes back inside. Lairaa sits by Sadhee and chats.

[03:27] Asil waves Lail and Saba over to eat. They get up together and join the friends at the table: Saba sits beside Sadhee, Lail beside Asil. During the meal Saba and Lail keep stealing glances at each other, as if both want to say something important.

[03:52] After eating, everyone lingers. Lairaa gets a phone call and walks toward the sitting room. Sadhee and Shahid chat off to one side; Asil plays a game on his phone. Saba gets up and stands at the balcony railing, leaning on it and looking down - it is a very high building. Lail follows and leans on the railing with folded arms.

[04:23] Lail asks if he may ask her something: between Australia and the Maldives, where would Saba rather live? "Maldives," she says, holding down her wind-blown hair. Then, Lail says, from today she should try speaking Dhivehi. She is embarrassed; he says there is nothing to be shy about, mistakes don't matter, he will correct her - "we must respect our own language." They smile; silence again.

[05:18] Sunset; as darkness falls the terrace lights come on. Asil comes over and asks what cosy talk the two are having. Lail calmly says that since everyone else was busy, they were just chatting to pass the time. Asil teases ("Sure... you can tell when you look closely"). Saba, pushing hair from her face, tells him in Dhivehi to stop the nonsense, but her sentence comes out wrong; Asil bursts out laughing, Saba is embarrassed, Lail glares at Asil, who says "sorry, sorry" and laughs again. Shahid calls Asil away; Asil leaves laughing at Saba.

[06:23] Alone again, Lail - with a mischievous smile - asks why she doesn't tie her hair with the hair tie in her hand. She had completely forgotten it, and quickly ties her hair back. Lail softly murmurs a couple of lines of romantic Dhivehi verse (about being unable to express it, heart/soul/tongue/pen... love). Though whispered, Saba hears; she lowers her head shyly, feeling a new beat in her heart.

[07:22] Asil comes: "Aren't you going? Let's go." Saba says it's already very late. Asil pats Lail's shoulder affectionately and says he will send the lessons (study notes) once he gets home.

[07:40] Everyone goes down the stairs into the (empty) sitting room. Maama comes out of her room hearing them, and affectionately asks if they are leaving. Saba suddenly remembers her bag - she left it "in that room," pointing to Lail's bedroom. Asil tells her to hurry. Lairaa calls and Maama goes into a room. While the others put on their shoes, Lail slips unseen into his bedroom.

[08:17] Lail's bedroom. Saba is putting her bag on her shoulder when she notices a photo frame of Lail on his study desk; she picks it up and murmurs to herself "What a lovely smile." Turning as she puts it back, she is startled to find Lail leaning in the doorway, smiling mischievously. He steps close and whispers in her ear another line of romantic verse (about his heart beating, about wanting to hear her praise). She is more embarrassed but smiles. He playfully asks: "Will you message tonight too, to check on me?" She replies smiling: "You're fine now, aren't you?"

[09:13] Asil calls; Saba hurries out, but Lail involuntarily catches her hand. He is surprised at himself. She stops and looks at his face; a deep silence. She does not ask why; he has no answer. She gives a shy smile, he smiles lovingly into her eyes, lets go of her hand and says softly, "I'll be waiting."

[09:56] Saba hurries out without daring to look back and puts on her shoes; Lail follows to the front door and waves goodbye. "Going," she says, and walks quickly into the lift. Sadhee jokes: "Did you go to weave the bag?" Through the closing lift doors Saba sees Lail wave again; she smiles and waves back.

[10:32] Inside the lift, Sadhee and Asil nudge Saba's shoulders, hiding grins; Shahid raises his eyebrows teasingly. "What?" Saba asks, frowning. Asil: "Looks like a flower is about to bloom." Sadhee: "What were you talking about by the pool? So cosy! Lail who never talks with me or any other girl - today I saw him sitting close to Saba in deep conversation." Shahid eagerly asks too. As the lift door opens, Saba walks out saying "Nothing like what you think. We were talking about water." Everyone laughs, and they tease her further for trying to speak Dhivehi for Lail's sake.

[11:28] Outside at night at the building's base, walking away. Asil looks up toward Lail's apartment and waves: "Now I know it was water you were talking about - is he standing there watching till Saba disappears hoping to get a drop of water?" Sadhee nudges Saba: "We've come here before, but Lail never comes out on the balcony to watch." Saba looks up and sees Lail, tiny, on the balcony of the tall building. Embarrassed by her friends' innocent teasing, all she can do is lower her head. [END ~12:10]
