## Synopsis
- [00:00] Asil's family apartment in Malé, late afternoon. Lail and Saba study at the dining table. Lail's foot has brushed
  Saba's under the table; she says in English "I am still in my conscious", he bites his knuckle, blushing. She asks why
  he was kicking at Asil's foot (earlier she hit Asil, since she can't hit a guest); Lail shrugs innocently.
- [01:06] His mother calls; he asks her to send the driver and tell his sister to get ready to go to Maama's. Saba teases
  him: can't he go alone? He explains his parents' strict rules; his trusted friends are Aseel and Shahid. "The driver"
  — "You're a silver spoon, aren't you?" (Sadhee told her; rich kids are usually arrogant, Lail isn't.)
- [02:42] Lail borrows a notebook, writes something, tears the page out, folds it into his pocket and goes to Asil's room.
- [03:14] Asil and Sadhee (a girl, Saba's friend) are gaming on bean bags. Lail mocks their score and threatens to "report"
  them to Shahid; "Shut up Lail!". Asil admits they left Lail and Saba alone on purpose and outs Lail's crush (he even got
  Saba's number). Sadhee steps between them: liking someone at their age is fine. Lail begs her not to say anything to
  Saba; asked whether he really loves Saba, he only shrugs — he doesn't know his own heart.
- [05:31] The driver calls. Lail hurries out, says bye to Saba, looks back from the door; they smile and wave; the lift.
- [06:27] Alone, Saba grabs the notebook, finds the pen indentations, shades them with a pencil and reads Lail's verse in a
  whisper. Sadhee comes out; Saba snaps the book shut — "Do we need to talk?"
- [07:40] Night, Maama Shafeeqa's house. Shafeeqa watches the door and the clock; Haizum waits on the veranda swing. Lail
  and Laira arrive laughing; they run to Maama; Haizum holds his children close. Mum Sana is ill (fever, vomiting); Haizum
  can't hide his anxiety. Dinner: Haizum silent; his client meeting tonight — the children let him go if he's back before
  twelve (no school tomorrow). He changes into his suit and watches the children at Maama's side on the sofa.
- [12:31] Haizum's reflection: the children are his whole world; one mistake cost him them; he won't use his family's
  power to take them from Sana — a mother's tears weigh more.
