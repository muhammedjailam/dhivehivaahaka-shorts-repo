## Synopsis
Night at Sana's Malé apartment. Sana, separated from her husband Haizum for seven years and suffering from depression, is
sick with a fever and cough; her children Laira and Lail are out at their grandmother's. Haizum, told by Laira that Sana
is ill, comes to the door (a password door — so it isn't the kids). He begs her to see a doctor and to end the seven
years of separation; he holds her hand and she lets him. Narration: he refused divorce in court; they live apart because
her rage at the sight of him might hurt her or things; he keeps coming and leaving with his head bowed. Bitter memories of
"that day" return; he turns off the TV and stays; in the kitchen she slams a glass, bangs a cupboard, things crash; he
takes a glass dish from her hand. Argument: "Even a sentence has a term — Laira may marry tomorrow" / "Nor do I want your
unfaithfulness passed to them!" He apologises, holds her as she breaks down, says he won't leave tonight or he'll send the
children abroad; she fights then gives up, exhausted. Laira and Lail come home; Mum's door is locked; Lail points out
Dad's shoes; Laira fears Mum's anger for telling Dad. In his room Lail smiles, "you stole my sleep", takes a verse slip
torn from Saba's notebook out of his jeans, reads it and tucks it under his pillow. Later Sana cries turned away from
Haizum; he pleads to stay with his family; she cries "Go make that family with her! Will the child we lost because of
your one mistake come back?"; he weeps — the child was the light of his eyes too. She coughs violently, runs to the
washroom and vomits blood; Haizum cries out "Sana!" (cliffhanger).
