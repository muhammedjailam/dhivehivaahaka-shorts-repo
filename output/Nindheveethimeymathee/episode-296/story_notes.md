## Synopsis
One sunny afternoon at Asil's family apartment (9th floor, Malé). Saba lets Lail in, tidies his shoes into the
shoe cabinet and returns to her schoolbooks heaped on the dining table. In Asil's room the boys game on beanbags until
Saba brings a hard maths question; Lail explains it with pen and A4 paper while she kneels nearby — their eyes lock
in silence until Asil quips that Saba should take tuition from Lail. After she leaves, Asil saves Saba's number in
Lail's phone and teases him: since Saba joined their class last week Lail visits far more often and keeps turning
round in class (Shahid noticed too); "my best friend has fallen for my little cousin". In the kitchen Saba and Sadhee
whip coffee; Lail (who just had coffee with his dad) takes water. At the dining table Sadhee is still angry with Shahid
(seen at a café with another girl). Asil tests Saba ("I gave your number to a boy — very handsome"); Lail kicks at
Asil under the table but hits Saba's foot. She blames Asil, scolds him, then sits in real pain, eyes brimming. Lail
brings an ice pack; Asil takes Sadhee away and the two spy from his bedroom door. Lail apologises and confesses it was
his kick; Saba bursts out laughing — "I know." Lail, surprised, smiles: "What did you say?"
