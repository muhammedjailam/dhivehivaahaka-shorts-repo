# Nindheveethimeymathee (ނިންދެވޭތީ މޭމަތީ) — series bible

Dhivehi audio romance drama, told on **three timelines**. 14 episodes are supplied (not consecutive; in-between episodes
are missing). The cover (`work/episode-<N>-cover.png`, the same for every episode) is a couple on a balcony on a rainy
moonlit night, deep indigo/sapphire blue with warm amber shutters and a blue Thaana title. Match its mood (`style.txt`):
moody, romantic, melancholic, moonlit blue nights and warm amber lamps; bright tropical Malé daylight for school days.
Setting: modern **Malé / Hulhumalé, Maldives** (apartments, a Hulhumalé beachfront penthouse with a terrace pool, Haizum's
multi-level penthouse with a rooftop pool terrace, a TV studio, a school, cafés, the seawall/artificial beach, hospital).
One flashback is set on a business trip in **Vietnam** (hotel ballroom, hotel room, lift, city skyline).

## Timelines
- **PRESENT** (eps 269, 271, 276 first half): Lail (~29), award-winning writer, comes back to the Maldives after nine
  years of silence. Saba (~28), TV presenter of the talk show "Tharinnaa Eku", is unhappily married to Zuhuruf (controlling,
  takes her money; implied abuse — a bruise she hides with makeup; never shown). Cards: `lail`, `saba`, `miya`, `zavee`,
  `sadhee`, `asil`, `shahid`.
- **SCHOOL** (A-level years, ~9–12 years earlier; eps 276 flashback, 295, 296, 339, 400, 401, 419, 420, 437, 552 second
  half): Lail (17), Saba (17, raised in Australia, poor Dhivehi, lives with her cousin Asil's family in Malé after her
  father died), Asil, Sadhee (a girl), Shahid; Lail's family: father Haizum (~48, CEO), mother Sana (separated from
  Haizum for 7 years, depressed, gravely ill; dies in 401), elder sister Laira (~19), grandmother Shafeeqa "Maama".
  Cards: `lail_young`, `saba_young`, `asil_young`, `sadhee_young`, `shahid_young`, `haizum`, `sana`, `laira`, `shafeeqa`.
- **PAST** (~7 years before SCHOOL; eps 442, 445, 552 first half): Haizum (~38) and pregnant Sana (~33), children Laira
  (12) and Lail (10); the business trip to Vietnam where Haizum meets his old friend Shifa — his "one mistake"; Shifa's
  message; Sana collapses and loses the baby at seven months; the marriage breaks. Cards: `haizum`, `sana`, `shafeeqa`,
  `lail_child`, `laira_child`, `shifa`, `daniyal`, `shaneez`. For Haizum in PAST scenes write "younger, about 38, beard
  fully black" in `visual`.

## Content rules (BINDING — they override the literal narration)
1. **Hijab always**: every woman and girl (Saba at 17 and 28, Sadhee, Miya, Laira, Sana, Shifa, nurses, guests) wears a
   hijab fully covering hair and neck, in every shot, also at home, in bed, in hospital, in photos and in flashbacks;
   loose long-sleeved ankle-length clothes. The narration's "long loose hair blowing", "short shorts and a loose T-shirt",
   "short white dress", "sleepwear" are IGNORED → default modest outfit. Men never shirtless (Haizum "rushing out holding
   his T-shirt" → fully dressed).
2. **No touching between unmarried men and women** (Lail↔Saba in every timeline; Haizum↔Shifa; Lail↔married Saba in
   hospital; Asil↔Saba is fine only as cousins standing near each other, no contact needed). No handshakes, no holding
   hands, no hugging, no tear in a palm, no whispering at the ear, no foot on a lap. Show glances, smiles, blushing,
   standing/sitting an arm's length apart, phones glowing, notes, a shared coconut on the wall between them. These are
   teenagers in SCHOOL: keep it innocent.
3. **Married couple Haizum + Sana**: may sit/stand side by side, his hand on her shoulder, holding hands, his hand near
   her pregnant belly; NO kissing, NO embracing, NO lying together, never both in a bed. Fights: a tense face-off at a
   distance; nothing thrown at a person, no hitting (her fists clenched at her sides).
4. **Abuse / injuries / suicide attempts are never shown**: no bruise, no bandaged face, no blood, no wound, no falling
   person, no balcony fall, no razor or knife, no vomiting. Saba in hospital = resting propped up on pillows, white
   hospital headscarf, calm face, an IV stand only in the background. News = a phone's glow + a shocked face + a dropped
   cup. 552's night = symbols only (rule in the 552 synopsis above).
5. **Death / burial**: hospital corridor, family grief, a cemetery at dawn from a distance (white-clad men, low coral-
   stone walls, no body, no bier close-up), raised hands in prayer, an empty chair, Sana's ivory scarf folded on a chair.
   The lost baby is never shown.
6. **Affair**: never show Haizum and Shifa alone in a room; no woman in his hotel room; the hint is only his guilty face,
   a closed bathroom door, a phone glowing on a table.
7. **No readable text**: phone screens = soft glow / blurred bubbles / waveform; the TV-show banner is red and gold
   without letters; business cards, notebooks, poems, letters, diaries and sticky notes show only soft illegible lines;
   no logos on cans/clothes (Coke → plain glasses of soda); no clock digits.
8. No alcohol (party drinks = juice), no smoking, no dogs, no swimmers (pools are empty, calm water), no musical
   instruments being played (an old guitar may lean in a corner as an object).
9. In bed people are SITTING UP against the headboard, never lying down; hospital patients propped up.
10. Faces in the upper two-thirds, calm lower third. Avoid the words kiss, hug, embrace, blood, wound, suicide, bruise,
    bed, bathroom, toilet, shirtless, body, dead in a `visual` — describe the safe substitute.
