## Synopsis
Driving at night, Lail coldly rejects Sara's phone confession and her follow-up voice note ("Sorry Sara, no interest, then or now")
and heads to the hospital. Saba (married to Zuhuruf) is in a private room on a drip; she weeps when she sees him after nine
years; Sadhee stays in the room. Through the night Lail keeps watch, unable to believe Saba tried to harm herself. Zuhuruf calls
Sadhee from Bangkok (just landed via Sri Lanka, back tomorrow afternoon) and hangs up; Sadhee blames Lail for abandoning Saba and
describes Zuhuruf as a parasite who takes her money and pushes her to sign over her house. A nurse asks Lail who he is to Saba and
assumes he is the husband. Next morning on his Hulhumalé penthouse terrace, coffee in hand, Lail watches young people race off on
motorbikes and drifts back to his first A-level year: on a video call with Asil, a girl (young Saba, raised abroad, broken
Dhivehi) walks into Asil's room, takes a phone call and says "meet me around 8".
