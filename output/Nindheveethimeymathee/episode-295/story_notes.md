## Synopsis
- [00:00] Evening. Lail (17), on a video call with Asil, watches an unknown girl in Asil's room end a phone call ("Meet me
  around 8 … same place, bye"). He knows Asil has no sister that age. Smiling at the screen, he suddenly widens his eyes,
  slams the laptop shut and covers his face, taking a deep breath.
- [00:55] Next morning, college class: fist bump with Asil; Shahid joins; Sadhee walks in, glares at Shahid and sits
  apart; Asil hints the two went out to meet one night.
- [02:48] The new girl from yesterday walks in — Saba, Asil's cousin from Australia. She teases Asil, then in broken
  Dhivehi/English exposes Shahid: unfaithful to Sadhee, "caught red-handed", with photos and video. Shahid panics.
  Lail stops her ("not the place or time"); she says "Thank you" and sits by Sadhee.
- [05:15] Asil explains Saba to a sceptical Lail: his cousin, moved to Malé after her father died; he even mentioned it
  the day he fetched her from the airport (brief memory image). Lail glances at Saba; Sadhee close to tears.
- [06:49] Walking home: Lail ties his shoelace; Sadhee snaps at him ("don't plead Shaadde's case"); Sadhee whispers to
  Saba to get tuition from Lail; Saba: "I can't, I have never talked to him." Lail asks what they're whispering — a long
  silent look between Lail and Saba, both hearts racing. Asil and Shahid tease him; Sadhee stalks off; Asil explains
  Sadhee is a second cousin and Saba his mother's younger sister's daughter.
- [10:52] Haizum's company: lobby, lift to 3rd floor, CEO door, knock. Haizum (~48) in his office: "Still angry with
  Dad?" — Mum and sister okay; motorbike licence forms; university abroad; secretary Zeyba brings coffee; dinner invite
  with Grandma tonight.
- [13:38] Lail's car with driver; mother Sana calls (voice only), worried, wants him home soon; he texts his sister.
- [14:48] Asil's building: bell, lift to 9th floor — Saba stands at the open door. Lail freezes. END.
