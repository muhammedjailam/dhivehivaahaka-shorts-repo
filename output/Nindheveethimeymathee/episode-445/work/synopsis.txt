NINDHEVEETHIMEYMATHEE - EPISODE 445 - SYNOPSIS
(Source: work/transcript.txt, 168 lines, ~0:00-11:20. Narrated prose with dialogue.)

======================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS (story order)
======================================================================

[00:00] HOTEL ROOM, VIETNAM - MORNING. Haizum is woken by the doorbell. He pushes off
his blanket, yawning, and checks the peephole: it is his close friend Daniyal. When he
opens the door Daniyal holds out his own phone - "Sana is calling. Where is your
phone?" - and walks into the room. Haizum's heart jolts with fear. On the phone, Sana
(his wife, back home) is frantic: she was terribly worried. Haizum lies that he slept
early last night and didn't hear the phone, and apologises. Sana complains he didn't
even send a message. He says he will call her back shortly and hangs up quickly.

[01:05] Daniyal lingers, sits on the sofa, and says Haizum vanished from last night's
party without a word - Daniyal saw him go into the hall but has no idea what happened
after. Haizum is visibly anxious and tries to send him off ("I'll get ready and come
down"), but Daniyal insists they go together and says they need to discuss something.
Daniyal gets a call and steps out onto the balcony. Haizum seizes the moment, hurries to
the bathroom door and opens it, then glances fearfully toward the balcony and shuts his
eyes - he believes his secret has been exposed. (Strong implication: someone - Shifa -
spent the night in his room; the transcript never states it outright.)

[02:02] Daniyal comes back in frowning and staring at him. Trembling, Haizum begins,
"Daniyal, I'll explain what happened." Daniyal sternly asks "explain what?" - then breaks
into a grin: "We got the contract!" He hugs Haizum, credits Haizum's hard work, and leaves
to tell "Jimmy and the others", telling Haizum to get ready fast.

[02:45] Alone, Haizum finds about twenty missed calls and many messages from Sana. He
sits on the sofa and opens a video message: an ultrasound scan of their unborn baby, with
Sana's face appearing in the video saying joyfully, "This is our little baby." Haizum's
face fills with despair and guilt; he closes his eyes, aware he has been unfaithful.

[03:22] BACKSTORY / TIME PASSAGE (narration). After returning from Vietnam Haizum has
no way to reach Shifa; she has changed her number and he called the old one many times.
She left suddenly without a word, leaving him unsettled. Before the trip there had been
an uneasy distance between him and Sana because of her condition (difficult pregnancy),
and since returning he has failed to give her proper attention. He keeps asking himself
why Shifa left. [04:04] Two months pass; the memories slowly fade.

[04:13] FAMILY APARTMENT (MALE) - EVENING, after office. Haizum comes home to find Sana,
seven months pregnant, tutoring the children Lail (boy, 10) and Naira (girl, 12). He
strokes the children's cheeks, bends to kiss Sana's forehead and gently touches her belly.
"How are you today? Much nausea / vomiting?" She shakes her head. "Is the baby okay?" -
and the baby kicks, as if answering. Both smile; Sana: "Look, the baby answered too."
[05:09] Lail comes and touches his mother's belly ("Mamma, is little one kicking?");
Haizum ruffles his hair; Naira strokes the belly: "Little one, this is big sister."
Haizum pulls Sana and the kids into a hug and kisses her forehead again. Sana tells him to
change quickly and come eat; lessons are done. He kisses her forehead once more and goes
to the bedroom - leaving his phone on the table in front of Sana.

[06:06] Sana sends the kids to put away their books and starts tidying the table. A
message lights up Haizum's phone: "Hi Haizum." She opens and reads it: "Hi Haizum. Sorry
for leaving suddenly without saying anything. I thought a lot about what you said. But I
felt it's too late now. I'm not sending this to upset you, but it's something important
you also need to know, so I'm sharing it." [06:55] Sana's head spins, her vision blurs
and the words swim. Straining to see who sent it, she accidentally deletes the
number/message, then searches for it frantically, shaking, the world spinning - and
finally collapses unconscious onto the floor with the phone.

[07:35] A child screams "Mamma!" Haizum rushes out of the bedroom holding the T-shirt he
had taken off/was about to put on, sees Sana on the floor, runs and lifts her in his arms.
She is limp. He tells Naira to open the door; Lail runs ahead to call the lift. Lail
asks, scared, "What happened to Mamma?" Haizum, voice breaking: "Come quickly with Bappa,
kids." Naira grabs his T-shirt off the carpet and follows.

[08:27] HOSPITAL - outside operating theatre. A doctor comes out: "Very sorry, we
couldn't save the baby. But Sana is fine now." Naira and Lail, either side of their
father, burst into tears - their little sibling is gone before seeing the world. Haizum
holds them close, speechless.

[08:52] HOSPITAL PRIVATE ROOM - NIGHT. Sana is moved to a private room; Haizum's mother
Shafeeqa comes and takes the children home. Haizum sits by Sana in deep grief, head bowed
in a chair, still gripping her hand. [09:20] He wakes when Sana wrenches her hand free.
He strokes her head; her face and nose are red and swollen from crying, eyes full of
tears. Her heart breaks more from distress than from the surgery pain. She pushes his hand
off her forehead as if a stranger had touched her, turns away sobbing. Haizum is baffled
- normally she seeks his comfort in any sorrow. "Love, what's wrong?" No answer; only
sobbing. She won't let him touch her.

[10:23] Not eating and crying constantly, Sana declines and is put on an IV; she spends
seven days in hospital. Haizum feeds and cares for her tirelessly, but she won't say a
word or even look at him. He can't figure out what he did wrong. [10:52] Sitting on the
bed: "Please, Sana, tell me what happened. How can we fix this if I don't know?" She turns
her back to him; the more he tries, the more she cries. [11:14] Finally, not wanting to
make her cry more, Haizum leaves the room, helpless. END.
