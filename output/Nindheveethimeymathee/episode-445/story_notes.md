## Synopsis
Morning in a Ho Chi Minh City hotel room the day after the business-trip party. The doorbell wakes Haizum; his partner
Daniyal hands him a phone - Sana has been calling all night. Haizum lies that he slept early. Daniyal lingers on the sofa,
asking why Haizum vanished from the party; when Daniyal steps onto the balcony for a call Haizum hurries to the closed
inner door, sure his secret is out. Daniyal returns frowning... and announces "We got the contract!". Alone, Haizum sees
twenty missed calls from Sana and her video of the baby's scan; guilt overwhelms him.
Back in Male he can no longer reach Shifa, who left without a word, and he neglects Sana. Two months later he comes home
from the office: Sana, seven months pregnant, is tutoring Lail (10) and Naira/Laira (12). Warm family moment, the baby
kicks. He goes to change, leaving his phone on the table. Shifa's message arrives ("sorry I left without a word... it's
too late... something important you need to know"). Sana reads it, gets dizzy, accidentally deletes it, and collapses.
Haizum rushes her to hospital with the children. The doctor: the baby could not be saved; Sana is fine. Shafeeqa takes
the children home. In the private room Sana pulls her hand away from Haizum, turns her back, will not speak or eat; seven
days on an IV. He pleads; she cries more; helpless, he leaves the room.
