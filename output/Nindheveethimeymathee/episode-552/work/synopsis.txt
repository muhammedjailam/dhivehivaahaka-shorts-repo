NINDHEVEETHIMEYMATHEE (ނިންދެވޭތީ މޭމަތީ) - EPISODE 552 - SYNOPSIS
Source: work/transcript.txt (288 lines, ~19 min). Narrated audio drama, Dhivehi.

STRUCTURE NOTE
The first ~12 minutes (0:00-12:05) are a long FLASHBACK: Haizam's memory of how his marriage to Sana broke down years ago.
At 12:04-12:57 we return to the present: middle-aged Haizam, alone at 1 a.m., folds a letter he has been reading (presumably
from Sana) and puts it in a box. From 13:01 the episode switches to the next generation: his teenage son Lail, Lail's crush
Saba, and their school friends during final exams.

=====================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS
=====================================================================

[00:00] FLASHBACK - Haizam's home, sitting room (penthouse). Haizam's mother Shafeeqa gently asks her son, sitting on the sofa,
what is wrong between him and Sana. Haizam sighs: he does not know. Since Sana "woke up" (came round, apparently after losing a
pregnancy) she will not speak to him or even look at him; she acts as if the baby they lost was hers alone. His voice breaks and
he cries; Shafeeqa's eyes fill too.

[00:53] Montage: Sana spends whole days in bed crying, neglects the children, barely eats. Shafeeqa finally takes her to a
psychiatrist / therapist.

[01:11] Shafeeqa's home / sitting room, after the clinic visit. Sana lies with her head in Shafeeqa's lap sobbing and reveals the
truth: "In Vietnam Haizam was with another girl." She doesn't know the girl's name; she read a little of it in a message; Haizam
has even decided to marry her. Shafeeqa frowns in disbelief that her son could do such a low thing, but strokes Sana's head.
Narration: Sana is an orphan with no parents, and Shafeeqa loves her more than her own child.

[01:56] Evening. Haizam comes home from the office and is met by his mother's questions. He is shocked: nobody could have known
that Shifa went to his hotel room that night - so who sent the message? He sits on the sofa searching his phone, apps, e-mails,
using recovery software on call logs and history, until exhausted and the battery dies. He spends the whole night on it. A week
passes; he cannot work, the message consumes him.

[03:07] Office roof terrace, at a coffee table. Haizam and his colleague/business partner Daniyal talk. Daniyal noticed something
big was wrong when their latest bid failed. He asks if Haizam is divorcing. Haizam: never. Daniyal reveals their friend Shaneez,
a lawyer, told him Sana has hired him to act for her in court against Haizam. Haizam angrily phones Shaneez: "How much is Sana
paying you?" Shaneez calmly says he took the case for Haizam's sake - otherwise she would go to another lawyer. Haizam rants: did
he abuse her, lock her up, fail to support the children? Shaneez asks where he is and soon arrives at the terrace.

[05:03] Same terrace, three men. Shaneez teases Daniyal for not keeping a secret. He explains: Sana insists on divorce; she says
that on the recent Vietnam trip Haizam let a woman into his room. Daniyal is not safe either: his wife Sofi has heard the story
too and, even with "six - or sixteen - children", she too will leave him if it is true; Sana and Sofi came to Shaneez together.
Daniyal jumps up angrily ("You want to wreck our lives?"). Shaneez: he will hear their complaints and file Sana's case, but divorce
is Haizam's right; a qazi will not grant it because Haizam houses Sana in his penthouse, pays for the children, has not harmed
her, and as a wealthy man is entitled to a second marriage. The only ground: if Sana proves Haizam is not fulfilling his conjugal
duty to her. Haizam leans back, troubled - they have not shared a bed for almost five months; he now sleeps in an upstairs room of
the penthouse because daughter Laira kept asking why Daddy slept on the sofa.

[08:02] Night, Sana's bedroom in the penthouse. Sana locks her diary in the wardrobe. Haizam walks in; she tries to leave but he
locks the door. She sits on the sofa hugging her knees. He puts wallet and phone on the dressing table, takes off his shirt,
changes, and sits pressed against her and begins touching her. She pushes his hand away; he persists. She stands and shouts: if
he wants this, go to the woman he took to Vietnam. His face changes; he yanks her arm, pushes her down on the sofa and pins both
her arms above her. "Ouch, it hurts, Haizam!" She sobs, struggles, and finally goes limp and still. (Implied marital rape - fade
out; must NOT be depicted.)

[10:23] Later that night. A loud thud wakes Haizam; Sana is not in the bed. Silent dark flat; kitchen light on. He finds Sana
collapsed on the kitchen floor, blood pouring from her left wrist, a trail of blood across the floor (suicide attempt). He screams
her name, holds her, presses the wound, weeping. She tries to push him off with her right hand, then thumps his chest. He begs
forgiveness: she was right, he did spend that night with a girl, but he loves only her - "please don't punish me this cruelly."

[12:04] PRESENT. Haizam wipes his tears, folds "that letter" and puts it into a box. 1 a.m. by his phone. He gets up from the
sofa and stretches. Narration: after that night he left the house and moved to his mother's; he and Sana never dealt with each
other again - he talks about the children directly with the kids. He still doesn't know what became of Shifa and never looked for
her. He lies on a pale-green bedsheet, hand on forehead, eyes closed; a tear escapes. "His beloved wife Sana left him after
enduring so much pain" - the thought stabs him.

[13:01] Night, Saba's bedroom (separate home). Teen girl Saba tosses in bed, unable to sleep; an incident at school today keeps
replaying. Test tomorrow; she sits up with a pillow on her lap and a textbook. Phone lights: message from Lail - "Hi." She smiles.
They chat (she writes carefully in Dhivehi): she is revising; he offers help, he is studying too; "Good night." She sends good
night back. She tries to study but thinks of Lail, heart racing - does she really love him? She remembers the lovely night they
went alone to drink kurumba (young coconut) and the poem he recited. She smiles: maybe she should confess first... "Hmm - wait
until exams are over!"

[16:10] Montage, days pass: final exams. The mock-exam incident still bothers Saba. Lail studies hard. After each exam the friends
gather at Lail's house (spacious, quiet); grandmother Shafeeqa tirelessly feeds them - pizza, burgers, subs.

[17:01] After exams and a week's holiday, evening/night, the friends meet again at Lail's - the penthouse where only Lail and his
grandmother Shafeeqa now live. Narration: sister Laira went to England to study 1 year 2 months ago; Haizam is in Japan.
Top-floor sitting room: Shahid and Aseel playing video games. Open balcony, coffee table: Sadhee and Saba playing chess. Lail
brings ordered food up and calls everyone: "Eat quickly, then a movie." He is gazing at Saba. Shahid teases: "We're 'just
friends', but that look says something else." Aseel laughs; Lail smiles.

[18:15] Downstairs door opens. Shafeeqa greets Saara and Zaara (Daniyal & Sofi's daughters): go up, Lail just took the pizza up.
Sofi (offscreen voice at the door): Saara begged to come; no time to come in; Daniyal will pick them up; she is taking the two
little ones for haircuts. The girls come up the stairs giggling. Lail rolls his eyes; Aseel bursts out laughing; Shahid draws a
hand across his throat - "you're done for" (comic: Saara apparently has a crush on Lail). END.
