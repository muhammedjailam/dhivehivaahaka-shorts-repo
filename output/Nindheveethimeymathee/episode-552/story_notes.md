## Synopsis
**PAST (~7 years before SCHOOL; shots 1–80, 0:00–12:01) — told as a long memory, revealed at shot 81.**
After Sana loses the baby, she shuts Haizum out: she won't speak to him, stays in her room crying, neglects the children and
stops eating. Haizum breaks down in front of his mother Shafeeqa. Shafeeqa takes Sana to a psychiatrist; back home Sana sobs
to her: a message told her Haizum was with another girl in Vietnam and means to marry her. Shafeeqa, who loves the orphaned

searches his phone all night; a week later he still cannot work. On the office roof terrace Daniyal asks if he is divorcing,
and reveals that their lawyer friend Shaneez is taking Sana's case. Haizum phones Shaneez angrily; Shaneez comes up and
explains calmly: Sana (and Sofi, Daniyal's wife) want out; but divorce is Haizum's right, he provides everything, may take a
second wife; the only ground is if Sana proves he does not fulfil a wife's right — they have not shared a room for five
months. That night Haizum locks himself in Sana's room with her and forces himself on her (NEVER shown). Later a thud wakes
him; he finds Sana on the kitchen floor having cut her wrist (NEVER shown); he begs forgiveness and admits the night with a
girl.
**Present-ish frame (SCHOOL era; shots 81–87).** Haizum (~48), years later, at 1 am, folds Sana's letter into a box. After
that night he moved to his mother's; the two never dealt with each other again; he never sought Shifa. He sits with his hand
on his forehead, a tear falls: his beloved Sana "left him" after so much pain (she has since died — ep 401).
**SCHOOL (shots 88–127).** Saba (17) can't sleep after an incident at school; Lail texts "Hi"; a shy chat before her test;
she realises she loves him and remembers their coconut night by the seawall and his poem — "wait until the exams are over!"
Final exams; the friends study at Lail's home, Maama Shafeeqa feeds them. After the exams, a night at the penthouse (only
Lail and Maama live there; Laira studies in England, Haizum is in Japan): Shahid and Asil gaming, Sadhee and Saba playing
chess on the balcony, Lail brings pizza and can't stop looking at Saba; Shahid teases. Daniyal's daughters Saara and Zaara
arrive (Sofi drops them off); Lail rolls his eyes, Asil laughs, Shahid makes a "you're done for" gesture.
