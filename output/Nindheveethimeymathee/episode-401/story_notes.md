## Synopsis
Late at night in Sana's apartment, Haizum helps his exhausted wife back to bed after she has again brought up blood. He
phones his friend Ilyas in New York to get the best specialist, then sits with her; she admits it happened last night
too; both weep, and he takes her to hospital — she senses her time is short. The same night Saba, at her cousin Asil's
home, drifts off smiling at the thought of Lail. At 5:30 am Asil knocks: Lail's mother has died. Hospital ER corridor:
Lail waits outside, Laira sobs on Maama Shafeeqa; Asil and Saba stand silently beside Lail; relatives gather. Burial at
the cemetery. Home: Lail breaks down behind his locked door, Maama comforts Laira, Haizum prays and whispers "Sana...".
That night Saba gets Lail's number from Asil and texts him; his sudden "Hi" makes her drop the phone on her face
(comic beat, ice pack); they exchange good-nights. She remembers her own father's death five months ago, when only her
mother consoled her — "few visit the poor man's hut, many the rich man's mansion". A week later: Haizum stays home for
Laira; Shafeeqa says only justice for the accident will bring peace; a lawyer reports that the motorcyclist has been
arrested. Lail's friends visit; eyes meet in his room; Maama sends them upstairs to the rooftop pool terrace, where
Lail finally slips away from the others and sits on the lounger beside Saba — a silent, meaningful smile.
