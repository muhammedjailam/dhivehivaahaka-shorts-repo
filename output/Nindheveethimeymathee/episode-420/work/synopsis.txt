NINDHEVEETHIMEYMATHEE (ނިންދެވޭތީ މޭމަތީ) - EPISODE 420 - SYNOPSIS
Transcript length: ~16 minutes (0:00-16:00). Narrated audio drama (narrator + dialogue).

=====================================================================
1. SYNOPSIS (story order)
=====================================================================

[00:00] NIGHT - SANA'S (the late mother's) BEDROOM, family home.
Laiel opens the door of his late mother Sana's bedroom. His father Haizam is lying on one side of the bed, forearm over his forehead, staring at the ceiling. Hearing the door, Haizam quickly wipes his tears with both hands; Laiel realises his father has been crying. Laiel lies down on the other side of his father and quietly says he misses Mum terribly. Haizam strokes his head and consoles him: this is the reality of the world - Mum went because her appointed time (ajal) had come; nobody can go before their time nor refuse when it comes. His own eyes fill with tears and Laiel wipes them. Haizam asks whether "Dhontha" (big sister, Laira) is asleep; Laiel doesn't know. Haizam tells him he must go back to school next week - Mum would be very sad if he stopped studying. Laiel lies silent, crying; friends' visits had comforted him a little but the emptiness returns once they leave.

[01:44] Laira (the elder sister) opens the door, comes in and lies down on Haizam's other side. He strokes her head too. Crying, she says: "Bappa, promise you won't bring us another mother." Haizam promises he will never give their mother's place to anyone and hugs both children. Narration: he is now both mother and father to them; he could never love anyone the way he loved Sana. Sana's later moody, stubborn, bad-tempered nature was the bitter result of the one mistake Haizam once made; in the past she had been gentle, kind and loving, and their marriage was very happy until "someone's eye" fell on it. Haizam closes his eyes and tears run down his cheeks; all three cry silently, surrounded by Sana's sweet and bitter memories in the room.

[03:48] NIGHT - LAIEL'S BEDROOM (past midnight). Laiel goes to his own room. He still can't accept his mother is gone - feels as if she'll walk in to tidy up, bring food, tuck the blanket over him. He sits on the edge of the bed, knees up, hand under his chin, tears gathering. His phone on the study desk (plugged in to charge) lights up: six messages from Saba and one from Asil. He sends Asil a short reply, then lies on the bed reading Saba's messages and a faint smile appears.

[05:10] TEXT CHAT (Laiel in bed / Saba in bed at her home). Saba: she thought he was asleep. Laiel: he was sitting with Dad and sister; sleep has abandoned him because of Mum's passing "...and because someone stole my sleep." Laiel asks if he can call; Saba agrees.

[06:01] PHONE CALL, late night. Awkward silence, both smiling. Saba asks about "a pen and a tongue" something he said that night; Laiel recites a verse (heart, soul, tongue and pen become enemies when he can't express himself to her). He teases that her face must now be blushing pink. He asks jokingly "Saba loves me, right?" - she quickly says no. He asks why she commented on his photo; she says she only said it was a really nice smile. He thanks her with another verse. Embarrassed, Saba says she'll hang up; they say goodnight.

[08:02] Laiel immediately texts an apology "if I said anything upsetting - but Saba really is that beautiful." Saba replies Laiel is beautiful too, "especially those two eyes." He sends "Good night" with a red heart; she replies with a heart. He kisses the phone, puts it aside and lies down at peace.

[08:44] TWO DAYS LATER - SCHOOL CLASSROOM (morning). Laiel arrives early; classmates offer condolences; he thanks them and sits at his desk. Asil and Shahid come in, then Saba chatting with Sadhee (Sadhiya). Laiel and Saba exchange small smiles; he pretends to look away. At free period he texts Saba and slips out; Saba secretly shows Sadhee the message and follows.

[09:36] OUTSIDE SCHOOL / STREET (daytime). Laiel waits outside the school; Saba looks both ways, crosses the road to him. He suggests getting something to eat at break (or the canteen if she prefers); she says whatever he wants. They walk silently, exchanging smiles. Both phones ring at once - Asil calling Laiel, Sadhee calling Saba; they show each other the screens and cut the calls. Banter: "Asil teases a lot" / "Me too."

[10:46] CAFE (cold-drinks cafe, daytime). They order, sit at a table chatting about everyday life, eat, then return to class separately to avoid friends' teasing. Friends still notice and question them; neither admits anything.

[11:18] TIME-SKIP MONTAGE (narration). Days pass. Haizam returns to going to the office. Laira is looking at universities for higher studies. Shafeeqa moves into Haizam's house to look after the children, renting out her own house. Laiel and Saba's calls and messages increase; secret meetings hidden from friends become routine.

[11:53] AFTERNOON/EVENING - Laiel goes home, changes clothes and goes out with his driver (car). He is dropped near Asil's family's house (Saba apparently lives in the same building/area), sends the car away, doesn't go up, and texts Saba. She comes down shortly.

[12:10] BEACH / SEAWALL (outdoors, by the sea - daytime/late afternoon; sea described as blue). They walk along the road chatting to the beach; Saba sits on the breakwater/seawall stones. Laiel stops at a coconut-seller's cart (gaadiyaa), pays, sits beside Saba facing the sea, and hands her a fresh green drinking coconut. She asks where his - he brought one for both of them. "Then where's the straw?" - she sips; he takes the straw from her hand and drinks from it too (shared straw). She smiles shyly.
[13:03] Laiel asks: "Are we actually just friends?" She shrugs, teasing that she never heard him ask to be friends. "Then what is this - hiding from friends to meet, messaging and calling half the night?" He recites a romantic verse ("tell me you love me..."); her heart races, she looks out at the blue sea. "Laiel is so good at poetry." He laughs - it's his own poem; a new verse is born in his heart every time he sees her. She calls it a made-up story; he recites another verse without taking his eyes off her. She doesn't fully understand the words but they stir her heart. She says she has something to say too: "Laiel's eyes are very beautiful. Laiel's eyes look very much like my little brother's. His name is Ali Laith Haizum." [ends ~16:00 - cliffhanger: her little brother carries Haizam's name as his father-name, hinting Saba's family is connected to Haizam's past "mistake".]
