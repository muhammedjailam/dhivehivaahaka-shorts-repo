1. SYNOPSIS (story order)
======================================================================

[00:00-00:39] OPENING SONG / POEM. A sung lament (title-song style): a wounded heart, tears, broken vows and promises made under the moon, lying together in bed watching dreams, loneliness, wounds; "what was my fault?". No dialogue - mood piece about betrayed love and loyalty.

[00:40-01:35] PRESENT DAY - HAIZUM READS SANA'S FAREWELL LETTER. The narration quotes the end of a letter: "Don't be lonely in a world without me. Haizum, you spent most of your life in loneliness. My wish is that you find a partner of good character and honesty who will love Haizum and our children without any conditions. I always wish you a prosperous, happy life." Haizum cries uncontrollably and presses the letter to his chest. Narration: it was he himself who broke the heart of his own loving wife; it is a past that will never fade from his memory; he never imagined that the punishment for one single mistake would be a poison he would have to drink for the rest of his life. Sana's "farewell letter" (vadhaaee sitee) reopens the buried bitter pages of his past; with memories of Sana he closes his eyes. (It is implied - not stated - that Sana has died or permanently left him.)

[01:37-03:04] FLASHBACK - VIETNAM BUSINESS TRIP, HOTEL. Haizum went to Vietnam for a business meeting: a four-day trip, first two days of meetings; the third night is a formal reception/party for partners held in the large hall of the hotel where they stay. Haizum, dressed up smartly, comes downstairs while on the phone with Sana (in the Maldives). Sana has a pregnancy scan appointment at 6:30; her mother will come to take her; she is uneasy because it is the first time she has to go for a scan without Haizum. He reassures her: "my heart is always with you", asks her to send a video of the scan; he says if he didn't have to attend this party he'd have joined by video call to see "our baby", but friends insist he come. They exchange "love you, miss you"; Haizum kisses the phone, says "take good care, bye, love you", and hangs up.

[03:04-04:20] MEETING SHIFA. Entering the hall to find his friends, Haizum passes a group of young women and stops: a very familiar face. "Shifa...?" "Hey, Haizum!" Both smile in surprise; she spontaneously offers her hand and they shake hands. "Long time no see, where are you living?" Shifa jokes: she married and moved to Australia, "and now I'm out again looking for a stone to anchor to". Talking, they walk to the tables set beside a big, openly visible swimming pool and sit.

[04:13-05:30] BUFFET. Shifa suggests getting food; Haizum hands her a plate at the buffet. She stares at the food labels. Haizum, as usual, takes pasta with a small salad and waits. Shifa: she can't eat just anything - "that's my problem; it's also the problem between me and Ille - food". Haizum teases "only food?" She laughs; after much looking she takes only a little salad. They sit and eat while talking.

[05:19-07:05] CATCHING UP. Shifa explains that Ille (her husband) would not agree to her working; she is sociable and can't sit locked up at home looking after children (said playfully, with faces and gestures). Haizum laughs and shrugs, sips juice. Narration: Shifa was always talkative, sharp-tongued and stubborn; she and Haizum went to the same tuition teacher and were close friends from then; after she went to Malaysia for her degree they lost touch until now. Shifa asks about his wife - "She's fine; she's unwell so I didn't bring her on this trip." Shifa saw the wedding photos on Facebook; first kid? "No, two children, and the third is on the way." She notes she never saw the children's photos; Haizum explains they don't post the kids on social media because his mother doesn't approve and his wife respects that - "but of course I'll show you". He shows phone photos: "my prince Lail, my princess, my life, Laira, and my beloved wife Sana - my whole world." Shifa: "Mashallah, such a lovely family!" "Thank you."

[07:48-08:39] THE SPILL. A man rising from a nearby table bumps a waiter; the waiter's tray - soup and assorted food - flips straight onto Shifa and Haizum. Both jump up. The man apologizes profusely ("very sorry, sir!") to them and to the waiter. Haizum silently looks at his clothes. Shifa smells the mess on herself and starts retching/gagging at the foul smell; Haizum smells his hand, feels nauseous and steps away quickly. Shifa half-runs to the ladies' toilet, trying to clean her dress while gagging.

[08:39-09:56] THE OFFER. When she comes out, Haizum is waiting outside, worried. Shifa: "how can I go anywhere with this smell?" She is not staying at this hotel - she is on a business trip and was invited here for the dinner. Her short white dress is completely ruined with food and soup stains. Haizum proposes: "Come with me. We'll send the dress to the laundry - this hotel's laundry service is very fast. Wear something else until it comes back, then I'll drop you to your hotel." Shifa agrees (if she can't get rid of the smell she might vomit). She texts someone (her colleagues) that if she's late they should leave without her. Haizum says he still has to go back to the party - Dhaaniyaal and the others haven't even seen him yet.

[09:56-11:55] TO THE LIFT. Walking to the lift, Shifa still nauseous. "Dhaaniyaal is here too?" (she smiles). "Yes, Jimmy and Vildhaan too." Shifa wants to change and meet the whole group - it's been so long. Haizum: "you're the one who ran away." Shifa, feigning indifference, asks if Dhaaniyaal is married. Haizum laughs: of course - married before him, now has six children. "Oh my God! Did he marry a Japanese fish?" "Twins three times, so they multiply fast." Shifa: "lucky that didn't happen to me - got photos?" Haizum teases she should look at them on Dhaaniyaal's own phone - "and your past with him too". Shifa, embarrassed, taps his arm: "that was a childhood thing." Haizum: "still like a little kid - that's why you're always bickering." Shifa sniffs her dress again, gags. "Luckily it wasn't something hot." "Yes, otherwise we'd both be going to hospital tonight." (Episode ends in the lift heading up to Haizum's room - setting up the "one mistake".)
