NINDHEVEETHIMEYMATHEE (ނިންދެވޭތީ މޭމަތީ) - EPISODE 437 - SYNOPSIS
(transcript runs 0:00 - ~19:00; ends on a cliffhanger mid-letter)

1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
[00:00] Beach / seashore, day. Laiyl and Saba sit together. Saba shows him a photo of her younger sibling ("kokko") on her phone. Laiyl jokes "Another child of my father?" because the name sounds like "Laith Haizam" (a play on his own father's name). Saba laughs. Laiyl asks whether the sibling is still in Australia.
[00:29] Saba's smile fades and her eyes fill with tears: in a terrible accident her younger sibling died together with her father. Laiyl wipes her tears tenderly and draws her close; she hides her face against his chest - the first time the two have been that close. He also tears up, remembering his own dead mother. After a quiet moment they separate and wipe their eyes.
[01:32] They get up and walk slowly along the shore towards Maafannu (Malé). Laiyl takes Saba's hand and interlaces his fingers with hers. Nothing is said, but the feeling is deep.
[01:52] Time-skip montage: days pass; they never say "I love you" but spend more and more time together. Year-end exams approach; revision sessions happen in class and in students' homes.
[02:14] School classroom, last day of classes. Many classmates crowd around Laiyl asking questions; he patiently explains, drawing and writing on sticky notes. As each student gets an answer they leave. Saba and their friend Aseel help collect the discarded paper scraps and put them in the dustbin.
[02:45] Next day, exam hall/classroom. Students write their papers. The invigilating teacher (a male "Sir") pacing the room suddenly stops and picks up a small paper from beside Saba's chair, unfolds it and harshly orders "Aishath Saba Ilyas! Go to the office right now!" Everyone stares; Saba starts crying.
[03:20] Laiyl stands up: "Sir, that isn't Saba's, it's mine." Class is stunned. Teacher warns he may be suspended for copying; Laiyl insists, head bowed. Teacher realises Laiyl (straight-A O-level student, maths genius, helps everyone) would never cheat and is lying to protect Saba; Saba is known to be very hardworking. Teacher orders both to finish the paper and then go to the principal.
[04:49] School office / principal's room. After the exam both wait; the teacher takes their papers into the principal's room; ~15 minutes later they are called in. Laiyl repeats it's his note; Saba says it has nothing to do with her and she did not copy.
[05:25] Haizam (Laiyl's father) knocks and enters with the teacher. Laiyl is deeply embarrassed - first time a parent has been summoned for him. Saba is sent to wait outside, eyes swollen with tears. Principal greets Haizam and invites him to sit; Laiyl stands with head bowed.
[06:18] Principal: "Your father is here, tell the truth." Teacher shows Haizam the note; Haizam confirms it is Laiyl's handwriting. Teacher explains the problem is Laiyl taking blame to save another student. Haizam gently asks for the truth. Laiyl explains: he wrote the note yesterday to explain a lesson; it fell unnoticed when they threw away the scraps; Saba should not be punished for something she didn't do - he'd defend any innocent person, friend or not. Haizam smiles faintly, proud - this is Sana's upbringing.
[08:10] Laiyl is sent out; Haizam talks further with the principal. Coming out, he sees Saba crying and Laiyl beside her, hand on her shoulder comforting her. Haizam realises she is not an ordinary friend but someone special to Laiyl. He watches silently and leaves without a word, planning to talk at home.
[08:55] Haizam driving (car, daytime). Flashback thoughts: he and Sana met at this same school, same grade; friendship became love; after degrees they married and had their first child; Haizam got his PhD when Laiyl was 3; Sana stopped after her Master's to raise their two children. He wonders whether Saba is right for his son and decides to look into Saba's background.
[10:09] Home, dinner (night). Dead silence between Haizam and Laiyl; Laiyl thinks Dad is angry and misses his mother. Grandmother (Maama) and Laira talk excitedly about Laira leaving to study abroad next January; Haizam answers only "hmm"/"we'll see". Laira notices but daren't ask.
[10:55] After dinner Haizam calls Laiyl to his bedroom. Haizam sits on a sofa with two photo albums on the table. He asks if Laiyl hides anything; Laiyl shakes his head. Haizam opens an album Laiyl has never seen: his parents' school days - a photo of them at Laiyl's age in the same school uniform. "Mum is so lovely." "And Dad?" "Dad is very smart." Do they look alike? "Yes... the eyebrows." Haizam laughs; Laiyl relaxes, relieved he isn't angry.
[12:45] Photo of Sana's 18th birthday, a small cake in class; Haizam names classmates. Laiyl surprised Mum played guitar; Haizam says she also sang and that Laiyl's writing/poetry talent comes from her. Haizam is trying to become his son's closest friend.
[13:39] Haizam opens the wardrobe, searches for a pen drive, and for the first time in ages opens the combination safe - untouched since Sana "went away". He sees a monitor-book-sized box labelled "To dear Haizam, from Sana", sets it aside, plugs the pen drive into a laptop and plays a song: Sana's beautiful voice fills the room - recorded on Haizam's phone the day he fell for her. Laiyl hugs his father; Haizam pats his back.
[15:04] Haizam: he knows Laiyl may now like a girl; that's fine, but keep within limits - she too is someone's beloved child, and a child's honour is everything to parents. Laiyl nods but keeps his secret for now. He leaves after looking at photos a while.
[15:48] Alone, Haizam leans back, hand on forehead, hoping Laiyl will share later. He puts the albums away, tears up with memories, then sits with Sana's box: inside, a password-locked diary with Laiyl's name, plus two letters - one for Laira, one for Haizam. Diary note from Sana: "When you get this, Mum may not be in this world. The password is hidden in your wardrobe. Read it only after A-levels. It is the diary of your mum's and dad's life." Haizam quickly closes the box - he does not want Laiyl to have it, but as it is Sana's wish, decides to hand it over after A-levels.
[17:07] He opens his own letter (read in Sana's voice): she may be breathing her last when he reads it; she thanks him for his love and for the sorrows; she has forgiven him as he asked, but every time she sees him the painful past returns. "One night's betrayal" drove them apart - would he forgive her if she had done it? She didn't want to talk about the past; their future was decided: he didn't want to let her go, she didn't want to live with him; she stayed for the children. Her time is near, she is very tired, wants to close her eyes without complaint - "But... tell me, what was my fault?" [END ~19:00 - cliffhanger]
