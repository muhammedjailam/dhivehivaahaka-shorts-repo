## Synopsis
On the Malé artificial beach Saba shows Lail a photo of her little brother; when he asks if the boy is still in
Australia she breaks down: her father and brother died in an accident. Lail is moved to tears too, remembering his late
mother; they walk along the shore towards Maafannu. Days pass with revision sessions. On the last class day classmates
crowd Lail, who explains lessons on sticky notes; Saba and Asil bin the scraps. In the next day's exam the teacher finds
one of Lail's notes beside Saba's chair and orders her to the office; Lail stands and claims it as his. Both are sent to
the principal; Haizum is summoned. Lail explains the truth; Haizum is proud (Sana's upbringing). Outside, he sees Lail
comforting the crying Saba and realises she is special to him. Driving home he remembers his own school love with Sana,
marriage and his PhD when Lail was three, and resolves to look into Saba's background. At a silent dinner (Laira's study
trip in January is discussed with Maama) Lail thinks his father is angry. Instead Haizum shows him an old album of his
parents' school days, plays a pen-drive recording of Sana singing, and advises him to keep any affection within limits.
Alone, Haizum opens a box Sana left in his safe: a locked diary for Lail (to be read after A-levels) and letters for
Laira and Haizum. Sana's letter (voice-over): she has forgiven him, but "one night's betrayal" parted them; she stayed for
the children; she is tired and dying — "tell me, what was my fault?"
