======================================================================
1. SYNOPSIS (story order, approximate timestamps)
======================================================================

[00:00] Narrated establishing montage: dawn over Hulhumale. Birdsong (koel birds), people
jogging around parks after Fajr prayer, others running along the beach or swimming in the sea,
others having breakfast at hotel and cafe tables by the shore. Parents walking children to
school; people of all ages hurrying along the streets to work.

[01:01] On the west-side beachfront of Hulhumale, among tall apartment towers, LAIL steps out
onto the balcony of a penthouse holding a steaming cup of coffee. He sits in a chair, sets the
cup on a small coffee table and types quickly on a laptop. After a while he leans back, sips
the coffee, then gets up and walks slowly to the balcony railing, left hand in his pocket,
leaning on the railing and sipping coffee.

[01:40] A message tone sounds from the phone in his right pocket. He switches the cup to his
left hand, takes out the phone, reads and replies to the message, then scrolls through
Facebook notifications. Suddenly his hand freezes: a BREAKING NEWS headline in big letters -
"TV announcer Saba jumps from balcony."

[02:09] The phone and the coffee cup fall from his hands. He staggers back a step as if his
strength has left him; the world seems to stop, muffled sounds ring in his ears. He is jolted
back when the phone starts ringing on the green artificial-grass carpet laid on the balcony
floor. Tears run uncontrollably; he trembles slightly. He picks the phone up - through the
tears he cannot read the caller's name.

[02:53] Phone call with his elder sister (DONTHAA): "Lail, why didn't you tell big sister you
were going to Male?" He wipes his tears, says sorry. There is an exchange about "where are
little sister and dad" / "out of the island" (attribution unclear, see Open Questions). He
asks to call her back - "a bit busy right now". She notices his voice sounds strange; he
insists everything is fine and asks after "Saha kokko" (her small child) - how is Saha's
fever? "The fever is down now." "Thank God - I'll call later." He hangs up without waiting.

[03:43] Lail hurries inside from the balcony through the sitting room into the bedroom,
moving like someone whose own loved one has been hurt. He flings open the wardrobe, changes
into a T-shirt, and - without combing his hair or putting on perfume - pockets his wallet from
the bedside cabinet, leaves the apartment and takes the lift down to drive to the hospital.

[04:16] HOSPITAL. Lail parks and practically runs inside, not looking ahead, and bumps into
someone coming out at the door; he apologises and hurries on. He asks at the reception
counter where Saba is and heads that way, anxious because he doesn't know her condition.
From the lift he rushes toward the ICU; near it, he slows - the ICU corridor is quiet, nobody
there. He looks both ways for someone; sees a young nurse and is about to ask about Saba
when a woman's voice behind him calls "Lail."

[05:20] It is SADHEE - the person he bumped into at the entrance. "Sadhee." "Is that really
you, Lail?" (she is unsure). "Where's Saba?" "In the operation theatre." "What actually
happened?" - Sadhee shrugs, doesn't know. "When?" "Late last night - I only found out when
the police called me." Talking, they walk to the operation theatre.

[06:02] OUTSIDE THE OPERATION THEATRE. A fair number of people have gathered. ASIL (about
29) sees Lail, comes forward crying and hugs him. Everyone's eyes turn to them; MIYA watches
Asil's behaviour in surprise. Asil steps back wiping tears: "Where have you been? Nobody
knew where you went or what happened to you. Even Haizumbe wouldn't give any information
about you." Lail (also tearful): "I didn't go far - I was right among you all." SHAHID comes
over: "When did you come back to the Maldives?" "Yesterday morning." Lail asks how Saba is.
Shahid: "Can't say yet - the doctors are working. Her life was saved because it was the
second floor, but nothing is certain yet." Shahid asks Sadhee whether she has reached
ZUHURUF; Sadhee: "Phone is still switched off."

[07:18] Waiting. Time passes heavily; worry and despair on every face, everyone bracing for
bad news.

[07:35] The doctors come out of the theatre relieved - the operation succeeded. A doctor
tells Shahid and Asil not to lose hope and walks off. Sadhee: "Alhamdulillah." Saba is
wheeled from theatre to the recovery room. No visitors allowed, so some go to the canteen to
eat, others leave to finish unfinished work and come back.

[08:06] WAITING AREA. Sadhee almost forcibly takes Lail to sit in the waiting-area chairs,
wanting to hear the diary of the 9 years of his life. Miya follows and sits next to Sadhee;
Shahid and Asil join. Sadhee (complaining): "Where were you all this time? You never answered
a single message of ours. Why did you push us so far away?" Lail: "Not now, Sadhee - I'm not
ready to share anything yet." He only cares about Saba waking up and about why she did it.
Sadhee falls silent, as though laying down her weapons.

[09:11] When Sadhee and Miya get absorbed in conversation, Lail quietly slips away to the
RECOVERY ROOM door and looks in through the glass panel. Two nurses are at Saba's bedside -
one adjusts her IV, then stops to chat; the other checks her pulse and notes it on a file. He
cranes to see her face; when the nurses step aside he sees her whole face is bandaged. His
heart races.

[09:59] Miya stops beside him: "You knew Saba before, didn't you?" Lail nods: "My classmate -
and my best friend's cousin." "Which classmate?" "Asil's cousin." Miya, suspicious: "Then why
didn't Saba recognise you? I even asked her before." Lail just smiles: "Better if Saba
doesn't recognise me now..." Before Miya can ask more, he walks away - unseen by Asil and
Shahid, who wouldn't have let him leave.

[10:51] NIGHT, LAIL'S PENTHOUSE. Another silent, lonely night. Lail paces the sitting room,
exhausted from wondering why Saba jumped. Backstory (narration): he came to Male yesterday at
the film producer's request to prepare for the film's PREMIERE NIGHT. After reaching the
airport a message to his personal number surprised him - Saba, after 9 years, inviting him
to her show "Tharinnaa Eku" ("With the Stars"). He couldn't refuse: long ago they agreed that
if Saba ever became an announcer hosting a film programme, Lail's very first interview would
be with her - and to keep that promise Lail has never given an interview to any TV channel or
newspaper.

[11:51] He goes out to the balcony and sits in one of the chairs by the pool. His friend
JAHAADH phones: "Watching TV? You've been hiding such a beautiful face all these days!" Lail
doesn't answer, says he'll call back and hangs up, hurries in, sits on the sofa and turns on
the TV. The first thing he sees is Saba's smiling face - painful when he imagines her now
lying between life and death in hospital.

[12:35] A message from Sadhee: "Everyone has left now. I'm staying with Saba tonight - come if
you want. She's in a room now." Lail replies "On my way", turns off the TV and leaves for the
hospital.

[12:59] IN THE CAR (night). Before he can start the car the phone rings; he glances at it,
rolls his eyes, irritation on his face, and answers while driving off. A WOMAN: "You're in
Male, huh? I thought you'd never come to Male." Lail, annoyed: "What is it? Calling for
something?" "Yes - I'm still waiting for you. Since you seem to have forgotten, I called to
remind you." Lail takes a deep breath. [END ~13:37]
