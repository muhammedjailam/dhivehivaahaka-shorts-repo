## Synopsis
One night at the TV studio of the talk show "Tharinnaa Eku" ("With the Stars"), Malé. Co-host Miya arrives on a phone
call; Saba slips in unseen, head down. In the makeup room Saba checks a mark at her temple that she has hidden under
foundation, and her eyes fill with tears. Miya grabs her hand: "Tonight too?" Saba: "This is my fate." Miya: "I wouldn't
stay." Saba: "I'm not Miya." Producer Zavee announces the guest has arrived. The guest is Ahmed Laail Haizum (Lail), 29,
award-winning writer. The moment he sees Saba come out of the makeup room, both freeze; he cannot look away, she blushes.
After the greetings they stand gazing in silence until Miya whispers "let's hurry". On air the hosts introduce him and his
film "Lafuz" (premiering next Friday at Olympus). Saba asks how much weight a "word" carries; inside, his words are years
of tears, lost sleep and a love held back. He blurts out that he became a writer "because of feelings for someone"; Saba
goes pale. He laughs it off, thanks Haseena of the magazine "Addu Vas", says he never gives interviews because he is
camera-shy; his gaze stays on Saba, who blushes. After the show Saba rushes off; Miya tells Lail it was Saba who invited
him; Lail leaves his card with Miya. In the makeup room Saba (changed) refuses the card - "you forget I'm married";
Miya: "that's no marriage, it's a life sentence". Saba leaves because "Mod" will come for her. Miya photographs the
card and slips it into Saba's bag, hoping it leads her out of "that pitch-dark, thorny jungle".
