# Sahar - episode 477 - story notes

## Synopsis
About two days after the Deir Yassin attack (spring 1948, around 11 April). The episode alternates between Yazan and Sahar.
- [1-19] At 11 a.m., after five hours locked in a hot iron box on a pickup, Yazan (20) arrives at Haifa port. A soldier
  takes him out, cuffs him and leads him to a big old steamship. In English he taunts him: they were spared from killing
  him only so he would suffer; they looted Deir Yassin's rich; his whole family is dead; his mother and wife were left
  in the desert to die. He takes him up to the top (third) deck, pushes him into a cabin, warns him never to come back
  and slams the door.
- [19-29] At Hashim's house in Ein Karem, Sahar (18, left forearm in a sling) wakes at the Dhuhr adhan and prays. Her
  father Hamza and the host Hashim sit in the hall; her brother Ameen is drawing water at the well. Hashim says they
  must leave in this lull ("wind between two clouds"). Hamza agrees, since God never abandons one who makes hijra. Hashim:
  "Safoora and I have two children, both studying in Jordan. Let's go in this house's truck."
- [30-41] The ship sails toward an unknown country. The cabin is luxurious (bed, wardrobe, small refrigerator,
  shower, sea-view balcony). Yazan blames himself for his wife and mother, weeps, makes wudu (painful on all four limbs)
  and prays two rak'ahs, telling himself that God is stronger than all.
- [42-54] Safoora, Laila and Fathimaa cook lunch in the big kitchen. Sahar talks with Ameen at the well and goes
  toward the kitchen. Laila grieves over the couple's few loving days. Safoora consoles her: her husband and one child are
  martyred, but Yazan is alive and will come back. Fathimaa cries. Sahar enters; Safoora sends her to call the fathers to
  eat. Sahar pretends she heard and saw nothing, and leaves.
- [54-57] Yazan's DREAM: Sahar graduates first among the doctors of Al-Azhar and runs from the stage toward him.
  He wakes to knocking.
- [58-65] He had fallen asleep in his dua, and he weeps. A tall young crew woman (Claire) is at the door. She knows he is the
  prisoner from Deir Yassin and offers to explain on the balcony what has happened and what will happen (cliffhanger).

## Locations
Dusty road into Haifa port; Haifa quay with an old steamship; the ship's polished staircase; the luxurious top-deck cabin
with a sea balcony; the cabin washroom; Hashim's house in Ein Karem (guest room, living hall, courtyard with well and old
truck, big kitchen); a dream courtyard of arches like Al-Azhar (no text).

## Tone
Grief and humiliation (Yazan) set against the fragile calm and quiet sorrow of the women at Hashim's house. Faith as
the anchor. A short golden dream of hope, then the unease of a stranger at the door.
