SAHAR - EPISODE 367 - READER SYNOPSIS
=====================================

1. SETTING
----------
- Place: A Palestinian village under attack by soldiers ("haguraamaveriň" = fighters/soldiers), never named in this
  episode. It is a short truck drive (about 10 minutes, seg 62) from "Al-Quds" (Jerusalem) (seg 61). This matches the
  series opening at Deir Yassin, west of Jerusalem. The village has streets lined with houses, a tree-covered hill/mountain
  ("farubadha") with a cave on it, and Sahar's parents' house at the foot of that hill (seg 43).
- Second location: the truck ride into/through an area of Al-Quds where residents are terrorized with a loudspeaker
  (seg 61-69), then the soldiers' camp (seg 70-73).
- Year/period: Not stated in this episode. Consistent with the Deir Yassin massacre, April 1948 (British Mandate end /
  1948 war). Period details mentioned: trucks, guns/gunfire, loudspeaker, handcuffs, iron pole.
- Season: not stated. If Deir Yassin, early spring (April). Hill is described as thickly wooded (seg 43, 49).
- Time of day: Continues the morning attack (daylight throughout). The women reach the cave "close to noon" (seg 58).
  Sahar plans to hide in the cave "until night darkness" (seg 56). Yazan's thread: about one hour of driving with him
  displayed (seg 68), then the camp - still daytime.

2. SYNOPSIS (scene by scene)
----------------------------
[1-3] Village street, during the massacre. Sahar is terrified; she does not know the fate of her mother, father and
  younger sibling ("kokko") and doesn't know where to look for them. Wailing from every house. Outside some houses whole
  families lined up and their bodies left hanging/displayed. Many houses set on fire and burning to ash.
  Present: Sahar, Yazan, Laila (fleeing together).
[4] On the road a shot from a distance hits Yazan in the leg; he falls.
[5-6] While Sahar and Laila try to lift him, a soldier from behind grabs both women by the throat and pins them to the
  wall of a nearby house; he lets go only when they are about to suffocate.
[7-8] As they double over gasping, another soldier comes shouting "Arrest the man who attacked us!", hauls Yazan up by the
  hair, strikes him in the face and splits his mouth/lips.
[9-10] Sahar, crying "Let my husband go!", grabs the soldier's arm and pulls; enraged, he grabs her left arm and breaks
  it. Sahar collapses (and is unconscious from seg 17 to 23).
[11-14] Laila's inner anguish: these oppressors have already martyred her beloved husband and her daughter, thrown her
  out of the house she built, and now are arresting her son Yazan. She cries out for help - no one can hear. Soldiers
  loot houses, line up the residents of some homes and shoot them dead.
[15-19] People flee in every direction. Laila clings to Yazan's arm. The soldier holding Yazan's hair drags him along the
  ground toward their truck. Yazan, looking at unconscious Sahar, struggles and calls out: "Sahar... be strong... I'll
  come back... Mother! Be strong for me... look after Sahar... I love you very much, Mother... tell Sahar I love her very
  much." (his farewell / "wasiyyat").
[20-22] Because Laila won't let go, the soldier behind grabs Laila by the head/hair, drags her away from Yazan, spits in
  her face and throws her on top of unconscious Sahar. The two soldiers drag Yazan along the ground, load him onto the
  truck and drive off.
[23-28] Laila sits dazed watching until the truck vanishes at the end of the road. Sahar comes to, looks around hoping to
  see Yazan; she sees people with elderly parents, wives and children running dazed in all directions, and people being
  thrown down and beaten. No trace of Yazan. "Mother! Where is Yazan? Where is my husband?" She doesn't even feel her
  broken arm. Laila: "They took Yazan... Mother tried so hard, my child." Both weep, Laila hugging Sahar.
[29-38] Safiyya (Laila's relative; they address each other "daitha"/elder sister and "kokko"/younger sister) and her
  daughter Sama come running. Safiyya's forehead is split and bleeding heavily. Safiyya urges Laila to get up and leave.
  Laila sobs: "I can't be strong anymore... my Mahmood, my Noor are killed... they took Yazan... this child's arm is
  broken... if they catch us again... isn't death better than living?" Safiyya weeps too: "Lhiyanbe (brother-in-law)
  killed too... we two (she and Sama) barely escaped... be strong for Sahar... be patient with Allah's decree, make dua...
  you two are not alone... Allah let us find each other... let's find a safe place." Sama comforts Sahar. Laila accepts,
  resolves to be patient, trusting Allah's mercy and for Yazan's last request, hoping to see her son again.
[39-41] Laila tears a strip from the lower part of her dress and bandages Sahar's broken arm; then tears a piece of her
  head hijab and ties it over Safiyya's head wound. Safiyya, eager to reach safety, barely feels the pain.
[42-46] The four women set off, alert and looking back, to climb the hill. Sahar's parents' house is in that area; the
  thick trees there should make escape easier. On the way: horrific sights - a mother and father shot dead with their
  small child; elderly people tied up inside their houses and burned with the house. Sahar trembles with fear and pain.
[47-48] As the four women near Sahar's parents' house, a loud blast and the house bursts into flames; soldiers are near
  it. The four run together toward the hill.
[49-55] Hidden among the trees on the hill, Sahar looks back at her childhood home burning - memories of mother, father
  and beloved younger sibling. She sobs, hugging Laila: "If only we had been martyred with them." She assumes her parents
  and sibling are martyred like Yazan's father and Noor, and fears for her husband. Safiyya: "Be strong, kokko! Don't
  think ill of Allah... He will not abandon us. Insha Allah." Sahar finds some calm.
[56-57] Sahar: "As a child I used to play with Dad and the others in a cave on this hill... it's wise to hide there until
  dark." All agree. Gunfire continues without pause.
[58-59] Moving under tree cover, resting at points, they reach the cave close to noon, exhausted and parched. Climbing up
  to the cave mouth, they see "a scene hard even to believe" - CLIFFHANGER, not revealed in this episode.
[60-62] CUT to Yazan in the truck (parallel timeline, same day). Blood gushes from the bullet wound in his leg; the
  soldiers ignore it. The truck heads toward Al-Quds; their aim is to parade Yazan as an example and terrorize the
  residents. They enter the area in about ten minutes.
[63-65] Over a loudspeaker: "Come see what happens to the disobedient!" They lift Yazan by his limbs, remove the handcuffs
  from behind his back, nail his two hands and two feet with four large nails and hang him on an iron pole (on the truck).
  He screams; blood drips into the truck bed.
[66-69] Whenever he nearly faints from the pain they splash water on his face to revive him, denying any relief. They
  drive about an hour with him displayed; it feels like many hours to him.
[70-73] At high speed they reach the soldiers' camp. They pull out the nails and bind the nail wounds and leg bullet
  wound with cloths soaked in a foul-smelling substance that burns intensely. Yazan faints again. END.

3. CHARACTERS
-------------
SAHAR (ސަހަރް) - female, title character. Age not stated; est. ~18-22, young married woman. Wife of Yazan
  ("my husband", seg 9, 26); daughter-in-law of Laila (Laila calls her "my child/dharifulhaa", seg 27). Has a mother,
  father and one younger sibling ("kokko", gender unspecified, seg 1, 50) - fate unknown, she believes them dead.
  Grew up in her parents' house near the hill (seg 49); played in the cave as a child with her father (seg 56).
  Injuries: choked against a wall (5-6); LEFT arm ("vaathu/ވައަތް") broken by a soldier (10); unconscious 10-23;
  arm bandaged with a strip of Laila's dress (39). Arc: loses husband to arrest, sees her family home burn, despairs,
  is comforted, then takes initiative to lead the group to the cave.

YAZAN (ޔަޒަން) - male. Age not stated; est. ~22-28. Sahar's husband; Laila's son ("firihen dharifulhu", seg 12);
  son of Mahmood (dead); brother of Noor (dead). Injuries: shot in the leg (4, 60); hauled by hair, punched, lips split
  (8); dragged along ground (16, 22); handcuffed behind back (64); hands and feet nailed, hung on iron pole on the truck
  (64-65); wounds bound with burning foul-smelling cloths (71-72); faints (73). Gives farewell message to Laila and
  Sahar (17-19). Accused by soldiers of "attacking us" (7).

LAILA (ލައިލާ) - female. Age est. ~45-55. Yazan's mother, Sahar's mother-in-law, widow of Mahmood, mother of Noor.
  Clothing: wears a dress ("hedhun", tears strip from its lower part, seg 39) and a head hijab (tears a piece, seg 40).
  Choked against wall (5-6); dragged by the head/hair, spat on, thrown onto Sahar (20-21). Despairs ("isn't death better",
  33), then resolves to be patient. Addresses Safiyya as "daitha" (elder sister).

SAFIYYA (ޞަފިއްޔާ) - female. Age est. ~50-55 (older than Laila). Laila's relative ("Lailaage thimaage meehek", seg 29);
  likely Laila's elder sister (she calls Laila "kokko", Laila calls her "daitha"). Mother of Sama. Calls Mahmood
  "Lhiyanbe" (brother-in-law, seg 34). Forehead split and bleeding heavily (30); head wound bound with a piece of Laila's
  hijab (40). Role: voice of faith and courage (34-36, 54-55).

SAMA (ސަމާ) - female. Age not stated; est. teen to ~20. Safiyya's daughter (29). Comforts Sahar (37). One of "four
  women" (47). No injuries mentioned.

MAHMOOD (މަޙްމޫދު) - male, mentioned only (dead). Laila's husband, Yazan's father ("Yazange bappa", 52). Killed by the
  soldiers before this episode (11, 32). Possibly the "Lhiyanbe" Safiyya mourns (34).

NOOR (ނޫރު) - female, mentioned only (dead). Laila's daughter ("anhen dharifulhu", 11), Yazan's sister. Killed (32, 52).

SAHAR'S MOTHER, FATHER, YOUNGER SIBLING ("kokko") - mentioned only; whereabouts unknown, Sahar assumes martyred (1, 50, 52).
  Sibling's gender unspecified.

UNNAMED GROUPS:
- Soldiers ("haguraamaveriň") - attackers; one chokes the women; a second (who arrests Yazan, breaks Sahar's arm, drags
  Yazan); "the two soldiers" load him on the truck (22). Truck crew with loudspeaker, iron pole (61-72). Soldiers' camp (70).
- Villagers - wailing families, families lined up and killed, people fleeing with elderly parents, wives and children,
  people thrown down and beaten (2, 14, 24-25).
- Dead seen on the hill path: a mother and father shot with their small child (44); elderly people burned tied in houses (45).
- Residents of the Al-Quds area being terrorized by the display (62-63).

6. OPEN QUESTIONS / AMBIGUITIES
-------------------------------
- Village and year are not named in this episode; Deir Yassin / April 1948 inferred from series context and the
  "10 minutes to Al-Quds" detail.
- Sahar's younger sibling ("kokko"): gender unknown.
- "Lhiyanbe" (ޅިޔަންބެ, seg 34): brother-in-law (sister's husband) - most likely Mahmood, i.e. Safiyya echoing Laila's news;
  could conceivably be another relative. Safiyya's own husband is not mentioned.
- Safiyya-Laila relation: "thimaage meehek" (relative); daitha/kokko address suggests sisters (Safiyya elder), but could
  just be affectionate terms.
- Sama's age unknown.
- The "unbelievable scene" at the cave mouth (59) is never revealed - cliffhanger for next episode.
- seg 2 "hashithah eluvaalaafai" - bodies hung up/left hanging; literal reading is families lined up and bodies hung.
- Sahar's broken arm: "ވައަތް" = left arm (seg 10).
- "ވަޒަން" (seg 4, 71) = bullet/gunshot wound.
- The iron pole: described as on the truck ("blood dripping into the truck", 65) - an upright pole on the truck bed.
- Spelling: Safiyya consistently ޞަފިއްޔާ; Mahmood މަޙްމޫދު; Noor ނޫރު; Yazan ޔަޒަން; Sahar ސަހަރް; Laila ލައިލާ; Sama ސަމާ.
