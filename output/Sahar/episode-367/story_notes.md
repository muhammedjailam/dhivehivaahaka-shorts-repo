# Sahar (ސަހަރް) — Episode 367 — story notes

Narration: 647.0 s (10:47), 73 segments. Setting: Deir Yassin and the road to Al-Quds (Jerusalem), the same spring
morning of April 1948 as the end of 359; women reach the cave close to noon; Yazan reaches the soldiers' camp at dusk

## Synopsis
Sahar, Yazan and Laila flee through the burning village (families lined up and killed, houses burning). Yazan is shot in
the leg; a soldier chokes Sahar and Laila against a wall; another seizes Yazan as "the man who attacked us", beats him,
and breaks Sahar's left arm when she pulls at him. Laila's grief: her husband Mahmood and daughter Noor are already
martyred, her house lost, now her son is taken. Yazan, dragged to the truck, calls out his farewell ("look after Sahar…
tell Sahar I love her"). Laila is dragged away, spat on and thrown onto the unconscious Sahar; the truck leaves. Sahar
wakes — Yazan is gone; the women weep. Safiyya (Laila's relative, forehead wound) arrives with her daughter Sama, tells
that her brother-in-law too was killed and only she and Sama escaped, and talks Laila out of despair through faith.
Laila tears her dress hem for Sahar's sling and part of her hijab for Safiyya's head. The four climb toward the wooded
hill, past horrors; Sahar's parents' house explodes into flames near soldiers; from the trees Sahar watches her
childhood home burn and believes her family martyred. Safiyya comforts her. Sahar proposes hiding until dark in a cave
where she played as a child; near noon they reach it and see "a scene hard to believe" (cliffhanger — revealed in 421).
Cut to Yazan: bleeding, he is driven to Al-Quds, nailed by hands and feet to an iron pole and paraded for an hour over
a loudspeaker ("see what happens to the disobedient"), revived with water; at the soldiers' camp the nails are removed
and his wounds bound with foul burning rags; he faints.
