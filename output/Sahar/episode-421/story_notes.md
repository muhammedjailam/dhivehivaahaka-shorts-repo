# Sahar (ސަހަރް) — Episode 421 — story notes

## Synopsis
Palestine, April 1948, the day of the Deir Yassin attack and the night and dawn after it. Sahar (left forearm in a cloth
sling), Laila, Safiyya and Sama reach the hidden hillside cave. The "unbelievable scene" is Sahar's parents Hamza and
Fathimaa and her brother Ameen, alive and hiding there. They have an overjoyed reunion. Then the bad news: Mahmood and
Noor (Yazan's sister) were martyred, Yazan was taken, and the soldiers deliberately broke Sahar's arm. Hamza and Fathimaa
tend the injured. Safiyya tells how Sama's father was killed and how the two of them escaped over a wall. Ameen comforts
Sahar until she sleeps. When she wakes, Fathimaa feeds her and explains that Hamza saw this coming: he followed the radio
and the papers and stocked the cave with food. On the morning of the attack they fled up the mountain when the gunfire
started. At dusk Hamza says they must leave. At midnight they light hand-lamps and walk over rocks and thorny paths
through the dark pine forest toward Ein Karem. Parallel thread: Yazan, a prisoner in the soldiers' field camp, is
chained, humiliated and starved. He prays Fajr sitting and is taken away on a lorry. The family reach Ein Karem near
dawn, and Hashim and Safoora take them in. In the women's room Sahar cannot sleep for thinking of Yazan. She prays Fajr
and dhikr until sunrise, then sleeps beside Laila.
