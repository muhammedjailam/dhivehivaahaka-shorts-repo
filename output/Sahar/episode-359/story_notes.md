## Synopsis
Deir Yassin, Palestine, 9 March 1948: the lavish wedding of Sahar (18), eldest daughter of Hamza and Fathimaa, and
Yazan (20), eldest child of Hamza's best friend Mahmood and Laila. Yazan has loved her since he was 15. Sahar moves into
Mahmood's two-storey stone house with its flowering garden; the couple plan to study at Al-Azhar in Egypt (Sahar wants
to become a doctor to help Palestinians). The narrator explains the Zionist movement and the plan to establish Israel.
One month later, an evening on the terrace: Yazan naps, Sahar and his sister Noor tease him; Noor throws a rose at him
and runs downstairs. In the yard Mahmood tells them to sleep early — the truck leaves at dawn. Noor is sad; Sahar
comforts her; they go inside. Dawn: the couple pack; Laila and Noor prepare a farewell breakfast. Gunfire: Irgun and
Lehi fighters shoot Mahmood, who dies trying to say the Shahada in Laila's arms. Laila begs Yazan to save Noor. In the
kitchen five men assault Noor and cut her throat while Yazan is held. He breaks free, tells his mother and wife, Laila
bids her husband farewell, and Yazan strikes a pursuer with a stone at the door and runs off with his mother and wife.
