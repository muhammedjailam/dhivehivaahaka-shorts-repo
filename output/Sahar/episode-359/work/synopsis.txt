2. SYNOPSIS (scene by scene)
==================================================================
[1-5] WEDDING, 9 March 1948, Deir Yassin. A joyful wedding: Sahar (18), eldest daughter of Hamza and Fathima, marries Yazan (20), eldest child of Hamza's close friend Mahmood and his wife Laila. Many of the area's ~750 residents attend. Both fathers are well-off, so the celebration is lavish and perfect; bride and groom are very happy with the arrangements. Present: Sahar, Yazan, Hamza, Fathima, Mahmood, Laila, guests/villagers (Noor and Ameen presumably present but not named here).

[6-9] CHARACTER PORTRAITS (narration). Yazan is tall with a handsome build; since his youth he has captured girls' hearts. Sahar is modest, well-mannered; her smile has won many boys' hearts. Because of the closeness of the two families, Yazan never looked at any girl but Sahar; he has loved her since he was 15. Married with both families' blessing, he is content.

[10-13] NEW LIFE. After the wedding Sahar moves into Yazan's family home: a spacious two-storey house with a yard full of flowering plants. Yazan studies religious knowledge and helps in his father's business; Sahar most loves his bravery and generosity. Time passes. Both have decided to go to Al-Azhar University in Egypt for further study; Sahar dreams of becoming a doctor to help the people of Palestine. (Possible montage: couple at the house, studying, Yazan at his father's shop.)

[14-16] HISTORICAL CONTEXT (narration). This was the period of the effort to establish the "terrorist" state called Israel on Palestinian land, pursued since the Zionist movement of the 1800s through deceit and wicked plots. Unaware of any of this, Sahar and Yazan reach one month of marriage. (Possible map/archival-style visuals.)

[17-24] TERRACE, one month later, evening. Yazan has fallen asleep on a chair on the terrace; he wakes to Sahar and his younger sister Noor laughing/teasing. Yazan, rubbing his eyes: "Wow... I got startled." Noor laughs: "Big brother falls asleep anywhere." Sahar sits beside him: "Yazan, Dad is looking for you; I came up to find you and you were sleeping with your mouth open, so I laughed." Yazan (eyes locked with hers): "Smile once more - how lovely." Noor: "Excuse me... I'm here too... you two are flirting..." - she tosses the rose in her hand at Yazan teasingly and runs downstairs. Narration: Yazan and Noor are very close; Noor is also close to Sahar, which eases Sahar's missing her own younger brother Ameen. Yazan and Noor run downstairs.

[25-31] YARD, same evening. Mahmood is sitting on a chair in the yard; Sahar comes down after them. Mahmood rises: "Children, are you ready to leave?" Yazan laughs: "Yes Dad, ready to board the truck right now." Mahmood: "Then go to sleep early - you have to get up very early. Noor, you too." He goes inside. Noor sulks: "It'll be so boring when big brother and sister leave." Sahar takes her hand: "Don't worry, we'll come back in between, or you can come too - but it's a very long journey." Yazan comes from behind and hugs both. (Line in seg 30, apparently spoken by Sahar while looking at Yazan, seg 31:) "Let's go to sleep; we have to get up early and pack a few things." The three go into the house and shut the door.

[32-34] NEXT MORNING, dawn. Praising God, Yazan and Sahar rise early and pack boxes for Egypt. A farewell breakfast with both families is planned; Laila and Noor are busy in the kitchen. Yazan and Sahar (upstairs) are startled by gunfire and the sound of Laila starting to cry. Coming downstairs, they see an unimaginable scene.

[35-44] DEATH OF MAHMOOD (ground floor). Irgun and Lehi fighters have entered the house and shot Mahmood; he lies on the floor drenched in blood, trying to recite the Shahada. Laila clings to him, crying and screaming; Noor's anguished crying comes from the kitchen. Yazan and Sahar freeze - "this is not a film, not a dream". They run to him; Laila sobs: "Son! Save my Noor from those evil men! I beg you!" Wounded Mahmood breathes fast and deep; after a farewell look at Laila's face he breathes his last. Laila screams; she tries to be patient, but Yazan and Sahar cannot - both cry out "Bappaa!" and weep. Present: Yazan, Sahar, Laila, Mahmood (dies); soldiers implied (in kitchen).

[45-55] KITCHEN - ASSAULT AND MURDER OF NOOR. Gunfire breaks out across the whole area. Heartbroken Yazan runs into the kitchen to save his sister and is horrified: three Israeli militia fighters have thrown Noor to the floor and are forcibly sexually assaulting her ("ބަދުއަޚްލާގީ އަމަލު"), while two more watch from a distance and laugh mockingly at Yazan. Screaming, Yazan strikes the three on the head with a heavy kitchen object ("ފިހިގަނޑު") and knocks them down, trying to rescue Noor. The two watchers run and seize Yazan; one of them slits Noor's throat with his dagger in front of Yazan. Narration mourns "lovely lovely Noor", "killed". Yazan weeps and struggles. One of the assaulters shouts that they killed the girl too fast - "she was a pretty girl who should have been taken captive". The five men start arguing; seizing the moment, Yazan wrenches free and runs. Present: Yazan, Noor (killed), 5 militiamen.

[56-63] ESCAPE PREPARATION (ground floor). Sahar and Laila, not knowing what is happening, hold each other and cry. Yazan bursts out of the kitchen: "Mom! Sahar! They killed Noor. They're coming to kill me too." Another shock. He grabs their hands; before running he gives his father's bloodied body a farewell look. Laila touches her martyred husband's face in farewell and kisses his blood-covered face. Trembling, she says: "I can't leave without seeing Noor - she is the child I carried and bore." Yazan, crying: "Mom! They won't even leave Noor's lifeless body alone - they're doing every evil thing. Now we need courage. Let's go, Mom." Laila gathers herself and stands; the three start to run.

[64-66] DOORWAY ESCAPE. One militiaman comes out of the kitchen and runs toward the door. Alert, Yazan picks up a stone lying at the doorstep and hits him on the head in one blow; the man falls. Yazan shuts the door/gate and runs off holding his mother's and his wife's hands. (Episode ends mid-escape, early morning, gunfire across Deir Yassin.)
