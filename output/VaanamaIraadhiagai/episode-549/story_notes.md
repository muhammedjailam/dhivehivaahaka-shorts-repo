# Vaanama Iraadhiagai — episode 549 (series opener) — story notes

## Synopsis
Calm, clear morning on a small Maldivian island (fishermen, women sweeping lanes). Luyoon (~20), the brave eldest of
three, is introduced; her father died when she was 12 and her mother Aishath raised Luyoon, Zaina and Nawfal alone.
Zaina shouts Luyoon awake; the younger two tease her as "queen of the house"; Aishath scolds them. On her day off Luyoon
does housework, then in the afternoon the three siblings walk to the sandbank to watch the sunset (Zaina meets
classmates). At dusk they hurry home; Nawfal goes to the mosque; Zaina complains about Luyoon to their mother; the
sisters pray. Time passes; Luyoon comes home tired from work and prays. One silent night on the joali Aishath misses her
husband; Luyoon: "if God wills, we'll meet Dad too". Luyoon shyly confesses she likes Maaniu (former classmate from
Hawwa-tha's family house, prays, speaks well) and asks her mother's consent. Aishath will think and talk to his family.
They go in to wake for Fajr. (Full beat-by-beat synopsis: work/synopsis.txt. Transcript "Lubuna" in seg 8 = Zaina.)
