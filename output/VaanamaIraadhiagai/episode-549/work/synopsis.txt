VaanamaIraadhiagai (ވާނަމަ އިރާދަކުރެއްވިއްޔާ? / "If God Wills") – Episode 549 – READER SYNOPSIS
Audio length: ~409 s (segments 1–45). Narrated story with short dialogue lines. Quiet, warm family drama on a Maldivian island.

======================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS (in order)
======================================================================

[Seg 1–2 | 0–20 s] Establishing scene. It is a clear, beautiful day with a clean sky and no wind or storm. Fishermen are busy preparing to go out fishing. Women who sweep the neighbourhood lanes (avashu kuni kahan) are at their work. The narrator says this is a common sight on the islands of the Maldives.

[Seg 3–7 | 20–63 s] Introduction of the heroine, Luyoon, a young woman of about 20. The island praises her bravery and courage. She is described as "different from other girls" because of how brave she is, and her mother Aishath is proud of it. Luyoon is the eldest of three children. She has a younger sister (Zaina) and a younger brother (Nawfal). Their father died when Luyoon was only 12. At that time the sister was 5 and the brother was 2. Their mother raised all three alone, with endless love and care.

[Seg 8–13 | 63–116 s] Morning in the family home. The father's absence is never made to feel. Zaina stands at Luyoon's bedroom door shouting "Dhonthaa! (big sister) Get up quickly!" (Seg 8 is garbled in the transcript. It reads "Zaina's younger sister Lubuna", but the context clearly means Luyoon's younger sister Zaina. Treat "Lubuna" as a transcription error.) Luyoon sleepily answers, "Zaina's started shouting again... I'm up." Zaina keeps calling until Luyoon finally gets up, however much it annoys her. The narrator says the three siblings live in a fun, teasing, happy and united atmosphere. Luyoon opens the bedroom door: "What happened? Why are you yelling so much?" Zaina, laughing, says "Mum is looking for you. Mum said get up fast." Luyoon: "You make such a fuss just to say that... I'm coming out."

[Seg 14–17 | 116–155 s] Luyoon hurries into the bathroom. Afterwards she combs her hair. The two younger siblings tease her: "Here comes the queen of this house... and she woke up so late!" Luyoon ignores them, takes a cup from the tray and sits down to drink tea (transcript garbled, probably "at the table"). Mother Aishath scolds them: "You kids always fight. All I ever hear is 'big sister did this, little sister did that'. Learn to listen!" It is Luyoon's day off work. After breakfast she does housework.

[Seg 18–24 | 155–222 s] She also spends the day joking around with her two siblings. In the afternoon Luyoon, Zaina and their younger brother Nawfal go for an evening walk. They avoid staying home all the time and head to the island's sandbank/shoal side (futtaru), a big point of land where many people go. Along the way they chat, laugh and tease each other. The narrator reflects on how precious the bond between siblings is. Today the three look almost the same age, though Luyoon is the eldest. Time flies without them noticing. Zaina meets some of her classmates and laughs and jokes with them. Watching the sunset is something the three often do. The moment when waves wash over their feet is the moment their hearts feel at peace.

[Seg 25–29 | 222–265 s] The sun has set and darkness begins to fall. Their mother always tells them to come home early, so the three walk back toward the house. The time between Maghrib and Isha prayers is not a good time to be out on the road, so they walk quickly. Nawfal goes into the mosque to pray. Luyoon and Zaina walk home. As soon as they enter, Zaina pours out complaints about Luyoon. After washing her face she says, "Mum, she teased/fought with me today." Aishath pays no attention, because Zaina always does this. Luyoon tells Zaina, "Don't act so big. Go walk and pray."

[Seg 30–31 | 265–281 s] The two girls go to the room together, jostling each other, to pray. Time skip: the days pass without the small family noticing. The days feel short. On work days Luyoon gets home only after the day is over (evening/dusk), exhausted.

[Seg 32–37 | 281–335 s] However tired she is, Luyoon never rests until she has prayed. One night everything is perfectly silent. Mother Aishath is lying on the outdoor jōli (traditional rope/net seat) outside the house. Luyoon walks over slowly and sits beside her. "Mum, you're so deep in thought. What's wrong?" she asks gently. Aishath: "Nothing, my dear... I was just thinking about the future. How good it would be if your father were alive. But today there is no father." Luyoon: "Mum, if God wills (iraadha kurevvijjiyaa) we will meet Dad again too. All we can do is pray (du'a)." (This line echoes the series title.) The narrator says Luyoon helps her mother in everything, and the two younger siblings help as much as they can. Mother and daughter sit in the silence, watching the stars twinkle in the sky.

[Seg 38–45 | 335–409 s] Luyoon lowers her head. "Mum, I want to think about something. I need your consent." Aishath: "What is it now, my dear? If it's something I can do, I'll see if I can do it." Luyoon is nervous that her mother might think hard about it again. Luyoon always cares for her mother. "Mum, I've found a boy I like, but I haven't had the courage to tell him. Maybe he..." She can say no more and becomes shy. Aishath: "My dear, that's a very big matter. Both of you must behave properly. Whose boy is he?" Luyoon: "It's Maaniu from Hawwa-tha's family's house (ހައްވާތަ މެންގޭ). He was in the same class as me. He talks properly, he prays. But I didn't want to do anything without your consent, so I'm telling you." She says it honestly and directly, with no deception. Aishath: "I'll think about it, and I'll talk to them (his family)." The two get up from the jōli and walk back into the house, so they can wake early for the Fajr prayer.

2. CHARACTERS
======================================================================

LUYOON (ލުޔޫން). MAIN CHARACTER / heroine.
 - Female, about 20 ("going on 20").
 - Eldest of three children. Daughter of Aishath. Her late father died when she was 12.
 - Brave and courageous, known across the island for it. Responsible, devout (always prays before resting, prays Fajr). Honest with her mother. Gets shy talking about love.
 - Has a job ("vazeefa", type not stated). Comes home at dusk exhausted on work days. Seg 17 is her day off.
 - Lives in the family house on an island with her mother and two siblings.
 - Called "Dhonthaa / Dhonthi" (big sister) by her siblings. Teased as "the queen of this house".
 - Physical/clothing: not described. She combs her hair after waking (seg 14, indoors).
 - Has a crush on Maaniu and asks her mother's consent (seg 38–43).

AISHATH (އައިޝަތު). MAIN CHARACTER. Luyoon's mother ("Mamma").
 - Female. Widow, raising three children alone. Age not stated: probably mid/late 40s (eldest child is 20).
 - Loving, a little strict ("learn to listen"). Ignores Zaina's habitual complaints. Worries about the future. Misses her late husband.
 - Lies on the outdoor jōli at night (seg 33).
 - Agrees to think about Luyoon's request and talk to Maaniu's family.

ZAINA (ޒައިނާ). Recurring/main family member. Luyoon's younger sister (middle child).
 - Female. She was 5 when the father died, 8 years ago, so she is about 13 now. She is a school student (meets her "class kids" at the beach, seg 23).
 - Playful, loud, teases her big sister, complains to her mother about Luyoon (habitually). Wakes Luyoon up by shouting at her door.
 - NOTE: seg 8 garbles her as "Lubuna". Use ZAINA.

NAWFAL (ނައުފަލް). Luyoon's younger brother (youngest child).
 - Male. He was 2 when the father died, so he is about 10 now. Goes to the mosque alone to pray Maghrib/Isha (seg 27).
 - Joins the teasing and the beach walk.

LATE FATHER ("Bappa", ބައްޕަ). Name not given. Male. Died about 8 years ago when Luyoon was 12. Only mentioned, never shown alive in this episode. Do NOT depict him (or only as an abstract memory, no face).

MAANIU (މާނިޔު). Love interest, mentioned only. Does not appear in this episode.
 - Male, about 20 (Luyoon's former classmate). From "Hawwa-tha's family house" (ހައްވާތަ މެންގޭ; the transcript prefix "ނު" before it is probably noise).
 - Described as well-spoken and someone who prays. Luyoon hasn't told him her feelings.

HAWWA-THA (ހައްވާތަ). Mentioned only as the head of Maaniu's household (probably his mother or grandmother; "-tha" is an affectionate elder-woman suffix). Female, older.

Minor/background: fishermen (seg 1), women sweeping the neighbourhood (seg 2), Zaina's classmates at the beach (seg 23, young teenage school kids), people at the sandbank/point (seg 19–20).
