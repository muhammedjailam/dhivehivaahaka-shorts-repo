## Synopsis
The narrator recaps the small family: Luyoon, her two younger siblings and their mother Aishath live happily. Luyoon's
father died when she was 12 and Aishath carried every responsibility alone (seg 1–5). Aishath raises Maaniu — "a boy of
good character" — as a match after visiting his family's house; Luyoon is overjoyed and answers "whatever Mamma wants"
(6–9). Time passes; Luyoon and Maaniu grow close, laughing about their school days; spring seems to bloom in her life
(10–12). Zaina is sad that Donthi will leave; Luyoon promises to always stay near the little ones and Mamma (13–15).
Evening: the whole island is out at the harbour in the cool breeze (16–17). Luyoon and Maaniu walk out; at a take-away
kiosk he gets a coffee, she a juice (18–20). The little brother ("Kyle" in the narration = Nawfal) sneaks up and taps
Luyoon's shoulder to scare her — Aishath brought the kids to the harbour (21–24). Red sunset; the couple walk home;
"see you later" at the gate; Aishath was waiting and asks where she was (25–30). Aishath is content — she has gained
another child who will call her Mamma; her children are devout and kind (31–32). The couple's bond grows; they solve
their small problems themselves (33–34). One school-holiday morning the family prepares for a trip; Luyoon phones
Maaniu to invite his family; his family agrees; destination: the reef-side beach (35–43). Luyoon shops with her
siblings; this is the couple's first trip together and Maaniu is glowing (44–46).
