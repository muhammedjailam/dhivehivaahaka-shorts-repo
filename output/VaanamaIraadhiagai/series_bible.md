# Vaanama Iraadhiagai (ވާނަމަ އިރާދަކުރެއްވިއްޔާ, "If God wills") — series bible

Dhivehi audio drama, a gentle **island romance / family drama**, drawn in **ANIME STYLE** (the user's explicit request:
"this story must have visuals like an anime"). Every image is a modern Japanese anime-film frame: cel-shaded characters,
crisp line art, expressive eyes, painterly luminous backgrounds, glowing skies (see `style.txt`). Never photoreal.

Story order (site episode ids; one continuous arc): **549 → 565 → 576 → 578 → 589**.
Every cover (`work/episode-<N>-cover.png`) is the same: a young woman (cream-white outfit, black scarf, brown shoulder
bag) and a young man (curly black hair, dark suit) walking away from us along a moonlit seaside path with palms and
lamp-lit houses. That night-blue + warm-lamp mood is the series' signature for evening/night scenes.

SETTING: an unnamed small inhabited Maldivian island — white coral-sand lanes swept each morning, coral-stone garden
walls, small single-storey houses with tin roofs and verandas, the family's outdoor **joali** (rope swing-seat) under a
tree, a sandbank (finolhu) at sunset, a harbour with dhonis and a take-away kiosk, a reef-side beach, a mosque with a
white minaret (exterior only), a small island school, an office (health sector), a small island hospital (578), a park
with swings and a small restaurant (578). Weather: mostly clear sunny days; calm seas.

## Episode synopses (details in each `episode-<N>/work/synopsis.txt`)
- **549** (opener): calm island morning; Luyoon introduced (father died when she was 12; mother raised three alone).
  Zaina shouts her awake, siblings tease her as "queen of the house", Aishath scolds; day off, tea and housework.
  Afternoon: the three siblings walk to the sandbank to watch the sunset, Zaina meets classmates. At dusk they hurry
  home (Maghrib); Nawfal goes to the mosque; the girls go to their room to pray. Later Luyoon comes home tired from
  work and prays. One night on the joali under the stars Aishath misses her husband; Luyoon: "if God wills we'll meet
  him again". Luyoon shyly tells her mother she likes Maaniu (former classmate, prays, speaks well). Aishath will
  think and talk to his family. They go in to wake for Fajr.
- **565**: Aishath proposes Maaniu as a match; Luyoon happily agrees. The couple grow close (laughing about school
  days); Zaina is sad Luyoon will leave — Luyoon promises to stay near. Evening walk to the harbour: Maaniu buys a
  coffee, Luyoon a juice; Nawfal ("Kyle") sneaks up and taps her shoulder to scare her (Aishath brought the kids).
  Red sunset walk home; Aishath content. School-holiday morning: Luyoon phones Maaniu to invite his family on a
  reef-side beach trip; she shops with her siblings; their first trip together is ahead.
- **576**: calm morning; school holidays; Luyoon (on leave) plays on the beach with Zaina and Nawfal; Maaniu startles
  her and they tease. Naaifaa watches jealously. Maaniu buys juice for all at a take-away and they walk home. Days
  later Naaifaa tells Maaniu Luyoon is "a very bad girl"; he begins to doubt; rejects her calls; Naaifaa smiles. The
  planned wedding falls through; Luyoon hides it for weeks. Zaina gets her to open her locked bedroom door; the
  sisters hug and Luyoon sobs, wishing their father were alive, and tells her they have split up. Unresolved.
- **578**: Aishath asks why Luyoon cries for days; Zaina covers. A call from an unsaved number: Maaniu, wanting to
  explain; he rides his motorbike over. Zaina opens the door and angrily tries to shut it; Luyoon comes; Maaniu in
  tears confesses he fell into Naaifaa's trap and begs forgiveness. Luyoon faints and wakes in the island hospital
  (doctors: grief; keep her calm). On discharge afternoon the family goes to a park; Maaniu brings drinks and snacks
  (her favourite vanilla milk); laughter. Family will back her planned business. Time skip: schools reopen; fresh
  start; Naaifaa calls "Ey Luyoon" and is ignored. Morning routine (Maaniu clocks in at 7:30, Luyoon at 8:00). At her
  office break Maaniu takes her to a restaurant: first outing since the breakup; they share their dreams.
- **589**: the couple deeply in love; island talk of their marriage. Zaina says a classmate called Luyoon a bad girl
  (from Naaifaa). Luyoon phones Maaniu upset; he comes to the house; they promise to stay together. Naaifaa texts and
  calls Maaniu declaring love; he begs her to stop, hangs up and blocks her. Naaifaa cries to her mother Hawwa
  Manike ("the world turns like a wheel; nobody is won by force"). Zaina's O-level exams; Luyoon helps her study.
  Night on the joali: Aishath happy; Luyoon tells her about Naaifaa. Neighbour Zahuriyya arrives, calls Maaniu
  good-for-nothing; Luyoon (who never argues with elders) tells her to leave. Next morning Luyoon happily leaves for
  work; Zaina's last exam. Naaifaa stops harassing them and sends a message asking forgiveness.

## BINDING content rules (override the literal narration)
1. **Anime style in every image**, matching `style.txt`; no photoreal faces.
2. **Hijab always**: Luyoon, Zaina, Aishath, Naaifaa, Hawwa Manike, Zahuriyya and every woman/girl in the background
   wear a hijab/headscarf fully covering hair and neck in EVERY image, also at home, in her bedroom, in hospital and
   when waking up. Bedroom/waking scenes: show her sitting up on the bed edge or at the window, fully dressed in hijab —
   never lying in bed, never bedtime clothes. Bathroom (549 seg 14): never shown — show the hallway/closed door or skip.
3. **Luyoon and Maaniu are NOT married**: always at least an arm's length apart, no touching, no holding hands, no
   hug, no leaning together, no shared umbrella under one arm. Exchanged glances, smiles, walking side by side with a
   clear gap, sitting on opposite sides of a table, both holding their own drinks. Never alone in a closed room; when
   he visits the house (589, 578) other family members are present or it is on the open veranda / at the gate.
4. **Motorbike (578)**: never show Luyoon riding on the back of Maaniu's motorbike. Show Maaniu alone on the bike, the
   bike parked outside the restaurant/office, or the two of them walking from the parked bike with a clear gap.
5. **Hospital (578)**: Luyoon sitting up in a hospital bed fully dressed in her hijab and dress, a blanket over her
   legs, Aishath/Zaina beside her; a drip line is OK but no needles in skin, no blood, no fainting fall shown — the
   collapse itself is shown as a symbolic moment (her shoulder bag slipping to the floor, a blurred tilted view of the
   ceiling light, a dropped phone) or a reaction shot of Zaina/Maaniu's shocked faces. Maaniu never carries her.
6. **Sisters' hug (576)**: allowed (two sisters, both in hijab, fully clothed) — a tender sideways embrace sitting on
   the bed edge; tears are fine (anime tears), no distress beyond that.
7. **The late father** is never shown as a person (no ghost, no portrait face): empty chair, his old cap on a hook, a
   fishing rod leaning on the wall, stars over the sea.
8. **Prayer / mosque**: the girls praying are shown from behind on prayer mats in full prayer dress (white prayer
   cover), small and respectful; mosque only as an exterior with a white minaret at dusk; no Arabic text, no Quran
   text. No adhan as sound.
9. **Phones/screens/messages**: never show readable text on screens — glowing blank screens, a call icon-free glow.
   No readable signs, shop names, labels, numbers (no "O-level" text, no clock digits).
10. **Naaifaa's scheming** is shown with looks: watching from behind a palm or a wall, a smug smile, her face lit by a
    phone. Her crying to her mother (589): sitting on a bench, mother beside her, comforting — no hysteria.
11. **Zahuriyya argument (589)**: tense faces at a respectful distance on the veranda; nobody points aggressively,
    no raised hands.
12. Food and drink: only juice, coffee, tea, milk, snacks (short eats), rice and curry; no alcohol, no smoking.
