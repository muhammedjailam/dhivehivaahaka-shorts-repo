## Synopsis
Luyoon and Maaniu (not married) are deeply in love and the whole island is watching them. Luyoon comes home from the
office and her younger sister Zaina says a classmate called her a "bad girl". The classmate heard it from Naaifaa.
Luyoon phones Maaniu, upset. He comes to the house (open veranda, Aishath present) and she cries. They promise to stay
together and ignore the gossip. People on the island talk about their coming marriage, and Naaifaa texts and calls
Maaniu declaring love. One noon he begs her to stop, hangs up, blocks her and buries himself in work. Naaifaa cries to
her mother Hawwa Manike: "the world turns like a wheel; nobody is won by force". During Zaina's O-level exams Luyoon
helps her study at night. Later, on the joali, Aishath says how happy she is and Luyoon tells her about Naaifaa.
Neighbour Zahuriyya arrives and calls Maaniu a good-for-nothing. Luyoon, who never argues with elders, tells her that
only she decides whom she marries and asks her to leave. Relieved, she goes to bed. Next morning she leaves happily for
work and phones Maaniu, and Zaina goes to her last exam. Naaifaa stops harassing them and sends an apology message.
