VaanamaIraadhiagai (ވާނަމަ އިރާދަކުރެއްވިއްޔާ?) - Episode 589 - READER SYNOPSIS
Audio length: ~427 s (50 segments). Narrated prose with dialogue. Setting: an island (ރަށް), not Malé.

======================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS (in order)
======================================================================

[Seg 1-5, 0.0-44.9 s] Narrator's opening reflection: whenever a couple lives in love, many eyes and tongues turn on them, people plot and work to cause heartache; "this is the nature of the world". Luyoon and Maaniu have been living happily together as a couple (dating, not married), and many people are watching them; the couple have noticed many things done against them, but they have chosen to dismiss those watchers as pointless. Naaifa, however, cannot let it go (text says "Naaifa and Luyoon don't make it into anything" - ambiguous, but the point is that Naaifa is the jealous party). Luyoon and Maaniu love each other so intensely that no third person can come between them. Luyoon arrives home after finishing work at the office (late afternoon/evening).

[Seg 6-9, 44.9-78.7 s] Little Zaina comes running excitedly to Luyoon: "Donthi (big sister)... a kid in my class said Donthi is a bad girl. I told her Donthi is not that kind of girl at all." Zaina is always very close to Luyoon. Luyoon asks: "Zaina, did you ask that kid who told her that?" Zaina: "She said Naaifa Dhaththa said it." Hatred for Naaifa rises in Luyoon's heart - there is no reason for Naaifa to say such things in front of children that young.

[Seg 10-13, 78.7-109.7 s] Luyoon immediately takes her phone from her handbag and calls Maaniu: "Maaniu... why is Naaifa doing this? She's going around the whole island spreading bad stories about me. What is my crime? Is being friends with Maaniu the problem?" She fires questions all at once - a moment of releasing a lot of pent-up sadness. Maaniu: "I'm coming to your house right now," and goes to Luyoon's house to see what's going on.

[Seg 14-18, 109.7-154.6 s] As soon as Maaniu enters Luyoon's house, she starts complaining, crying: "How big a thing is this, Maaniu... she's picking on us... why must such things happen?" The narrator says every such lesson has become a hardship for the couple. They decide to go forward patiently, promise to love each other in every circumstance, and resolve to ignore what people say. They deliberately start letting Naaifa see them together more often, knowing that this will make Naaifa burst with heartache and uncontrollable anger.

[Seg 19-21, 154.6-182.5 s] Talk of Maaniu and Luyoon getting married becomes common across the island. This hurts and troubles Naaifa. Naaifa starts texting Maaniu's phone, saying she loves him intensely, and calls him many times. Maaniu pays no attention - turns a deaf ear. One day at noon, Naaifa calls Maaniu again to harass him.

[Seg 22-25, 182.5-218.6 s] PHONE CALL. Maaniu (pleading tone): "Naaifa, why are you doing this... Once you already caused a problem between the two of us, didn't you? So can't you just stop calling me? I'm begging you." Maaniu wants to start a life with Luyoon and will not do anything that hurts her. Naaifa: "Maaniu, just listen to what I'm saying..." - her stubbornness shows. Maaniu hangs up, blocks her number, and throws himself into his work.

[Seg 26-29, 218.6-257.3 s] Narrator: this is a model of true love - because he hopes to build a life with the one he loves, Maaniu keeps doing everything right. NAAIFA'S HOUSE: Naaifa, crying, to her mother Hawwa Manike: "Mother... why is Maaniu refusing to keep in touch with me... I want him so much." Hawwa Manike: "Naaifa, the world turns like a spinning wheel. Those who go will go, those who come will come. Nobody can be brought by force." Narrator agrees: the world turns like a wheel - sometimes sorrow, sometimes joy; that is the way of the world.

[Seg 30-32, 257.3-280.4 s] It is Zaina's O-Level exam period. Luyoon helps Zaina a great deal, clearing up questions she doesn't understand; Zaina's ("Zen") studies improve day by day. Their mother Aishath is extremely happy about this. NIGHT: after Zaina goes to sleep, Luyoon goes outside to her mother, who is sitting on the joali (Maldivian rope swing-seat) outside the house.

[Seg 33-37, 280.4-316.8 s] Aishath: "Child, Mother is so happy today to see you two (Luyoon & Maaniu) living happily." But to Luyoon this has turned into a sad topic: "Mother, don't say that... maybe the two of us will break up completely." Aishath, surprised: "Did something go wrong again? Is Maaniu angry again?" Luyoon: "No, Mother..." - a woman called Naaifa is trying to get between Luyoon and Maaniu, and Maaniu is at his wits' end.

[Seg 38-41, 316.8-354.9 s] Luyoon tells her mother everything, word for word. Neighbour Zahuriyya arrives: "Aishath, what a soft chat the two of you (mother and daughter) are having..." Aishath answers cheerfully: "Well, of course mother and daughter have things to talk about..." Zahuriyya: "No, not that... I've heard the story. That rotten, good-for-nothing boy Maaniu - is this house's fine, decent girl Luyoon going with him?" - said in a disapproving way. Luyoon, unsettled, shouts at Zahuriyya: "Only I know who I should marry, not anyone else. Zahuriyya, it would be better if you stopped talking like that and left."

[Seg 42-45, 354.9-391.0 s] Luyoon has never been heard arguing with anyone in front of elders in a rude way; but with things reaching this point, her anger slipped out of control. Aishath is left sitting speechless - it seems Luyoon had held this in for many days. Having said what she wanted to say to Zahuriyya, Luyoon feels a coolness and relief. "Mother, Luyoon is going to sleep." Still upset, she goes inside; Aishath follows her toward the house. (Time jump) The sun rises to do its duty - next morning.

[Seg 46-50, 391.0-426.9 s] MORNING: the island is busy. Zaina gets ready to go to her exam. Luyoon is busy getting ready for the office; happiness shows on her face. Before going to work Luyoon calls Maaniu; he says he's awake. It's Zaina's final exam, so she spent the last three days reading books and clearing doubts. Closing state: Naaifa's harassment of Maaniu has now lessened, and she has even sent Luyoon and Maaniu a message asking for forgiveness. (Episode ends here.)
