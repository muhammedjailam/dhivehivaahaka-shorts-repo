Transcript: 49 segments, 0.00-454.89 s (about 7.6 min). Narrated drama with dialogue.

NOTE ON A NAME INCONSISTENCY: the younger sister is spelled "Zeeniya / Zeeniaa" (ޒީނިޔާ / ޒީނިއާ) in seg 3-6 and "Zaina" (ޒައިނާ) from seg 14 onward. Both sit in the sitting room on the sofa in consecutive scenes and both are Luyoon's younger sister, so treat them as ONE character, best name ZAINA (ޒައިނާ). In seg 33 and 36 the transcript also says "Zaina" where it clearly means Luyoon (the one going to the office at 8:00 and dropping Zaina and Zen at school) - a narrator/transcription slip.

1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS (in order)
======================================================================

[Seg 1-5, 0.00-46.64] Mother confronts the daughters. At home, the mother AISHATH, worried, asks her daughter: "Are you hiding something from me, child? Don't lie, tell me." She cannot bear to see the children she raised with love now full of tears; for many days her daughter's dreams have had no colour, and she wants to know why. LUYOON, the eldest daughter, answers in a hopeless, sad voice: "Mamma, nothing is wrong..." Luyoon had asked her younger sister ZAINA (here spelled Zeeniya) not to let anyone know about these matters. Aishath presses: "Is it true nothing happened to dhonthi (elder sister)? I just overheard you two talking - what was that about?" Zaina pretends to know nothing: "Mamma, it's nothing... dhonthi is just a bit upset/sulking" (exact reason unclear in the transcript, a minor excuse).

[Seg 6-10, 46.64-97.22] Zaina's worry; the phone call. Zaina walks to the sitting room, sits on the sofa, lets out a deep sigh and thinks: how will these things ever be fixed? Dhonthi had so many hopes, but in a short moment her dreams were scattered. "That is the way of the world." Narration: within a short time Luyoon's life was turned upside down because of someone - and Luyoon has learned it was because of NAIFA. Luyoon's phone rings from an unsaved number. She normally never answers unsaved numbers; it calls two or three times; she answers. "Hi Luyoon, this is Maaniu..."

[Seg 11-13, 97.22-125.14] Maaniu wants to explain. MAANIU, in a pained voice, says he has something to tell her. Luyoon, crying: "After hurting my heart all these days, why are you calling today?" Maaniu: "Luyoon, I want to meet... to tell you how things happened. I'm coming to your house." He hangs up, gets on his motorcycle and rides to Luyoon's house. A while later he arrives and calls out "Assalaamu alaikum".

[Seg 14-18, 125.14-172.98] At the door. Zaina, sitting in the sitting room, returns the greeting, gets up from the sofa and opens the door - it is Maaniu. She fires questions at him: "Why have you come to this house? Who are you looking for? For what?" - because after what he did to Luyoon he has no reason to come here. She tries to shut the door. Maaniu: "Zaina, wait. Is dhonthi here? I've come to talk to dhonthi, to explain what happened." Hearing his voice, Luyoon hurries to the front door: "Maaniu, what happened, why have you come to this house today?" Maaniu: "Luyoon, wait, please listen to this." Tears gather in Maaniu's eyes.

[Seg 19-22, 172.98-207.18] The truth. Maaniu, begging for forgiveness for what he did, tells her the whole story word by word: someone (Naifa) set a trap to separate the two lovers, and Maaniu fell into that trap and was drowned in it. Tears roll down Luyoon's cheeks and her vision starts to darken. She tries hard not to show it, but she is overwhelmed. Softly she says: "Zaina... something is happening to me... I need your help."

[Seg 23-25, 207.18-239.25] Fainting and hospital. Everything around Luyoon goes dark. When she regains consciousness she is in hospital, admitted. Weakly: "Zaina, when was I brought here, who brought me?" Zaina: "Maaniu bebe (big brother) brought you... you collapsed." Luyoon: "Where is Maaniu now?" The doctors say she collapsed because of grief/distress, and request that nobody upset her.

[Seg 26-31, 239.25-292.71] Discharge and the park. Afternoon, the day she is discharged. After leaving hospital the family take her somewhere with fresh air - a park, with small children and elderly people around. Aishath: "Luyoon, are you okay now?" Luyoon smiles: "Yes, mamma..." With new hope and determination, the family start working to lift her spirits. Luyoon looks around: "Where is Maaniu now?" Aishath: "He was at the hospital today too." Then Zaina and Maaniu are seen coming from a distance, each carrying cups of drink ("jugo" - vanilla milk drink cups) in both hands; Zaina also holds snacks (hedhikaa). Their younger brother (ZEN) is beside them, smiling. Maaniu: "Here, Luyoon's favourite drink, take it" - he hands her a vanilla-milk drink. Mother, the two younger siblings, Luyoon (and Maaniu) all join in laughing and joking.

[Seg 32-36, 292.71-342.05] A new start. Everyone resolves to help Luyoon with the business (viyafaari) she most wants to start. Time skip: after the long holidays schools reopen. Zaina and Zen get ready for school; Luyoon gets up to go to the office. After making breakfast with her mother, she showers, freshens up and gets ready. A completely new day, new things. Luyoon gets her mother's (Aishath's) full support for what she wants to do. Maaniu and Luyoon reconnect with a fresh start and make many promises to each other. Naifa is unhappy and restless about this. Since Luyoon's office starts at 8:00, she first takes Zaina and Zen to school.

[Seg 37-43, 342.05-400.95] Morning routine; Naifa's call-out. Once Zaina and Zen are inside the school, Luyoon walks home. "Ey Luyoon!" - Naifa calls out in a displeased voice. Luyoon ignores her and keeps walking home, to have breakfast with her mother before work. After mother and daughter eat together: "Mom, I'm going," and she calls Maaniu. Maaniu works at a government-share (state-owned) company and must fingerprint (attendance) before 7:30, so he is already at work. Luyoon checks her phone: 7:15. "Maaniu, where are you? Come pick me up." Maaniu: "It'll be a while, I'm going to fingerprint first, then I'll come." After fingerprinting he goes to pick her up. Luyoon, who works in the health sector, hurries to fingerprint and asks him to visit during the break.

[Seg 44-49, 400.95-454.89] Lunch-break date. A completely new chapter has begun in Luyoon's life. Her body, which had become thin/sickly (anorexic/anaemic), is now healthy - thanks to her younger sister Zaina's help. Office break time: Luyoon takes her phone from her bag and dials Maaniu to ask where he is. Maaniu: "I'm very busy, but I'm coming." He too wants to spend time with her, to share their many dreams. A while later Maaniu arrives on his motorcycle and picks Luyoon up; they go into a restaurant. This is the first time they have gone out together as a couple again since they were separated. When the waiter comes they order, and Luyoon and Maaniu tell each other their dreams and share many things. (Episode ends here.)
