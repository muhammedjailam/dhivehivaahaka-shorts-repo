## Synopsis
A calm, cloudless island morning in the school holidays: fishermen set out in dhonis, women sweep the lanes, the
sea lies flat as oil (seg 1–4). Luyoon, on family-responsibility leave from her office, plays on the beach with her
younger siblings Zaina and Nawfal; she and her fiancé Maaniu are happier than ever (5–8). The narrator warns that the
world also brings sorrow (9–10). Maaniu startles Luyoon with "Hi Luyoon, up so early?" and they tease (10–12).
Naaifaa watches with jealousy and schemes to separate them (13–15). Maaniu treats the three siblings to juice at a
harbour take-away and they walk home (16–19). A few days later Naaifaa tells Maaniu that Luyoon is "a very bad girl";
he doubts it but says he will look into it, and the relationship weakens (20–24). Maaniu rejects Luyoon's calls
three times; Naaifaa smiles in triumph (25–28). Their planned wedding falls apart (29–30). Mother (Aishath) asks
what is wrong; Luyoon smiles and goes to her room (30–31). For weeks Luyoon hides her pain and tries in vain to
explain herself to Maaniu, who believes Naaifaa (32–38). Zaina gets her to open the locked bedroom door (39–41);
Luyoon hugs her and sobs, wishing their late father were alive (42–43), then tells her that she and Maaniu have
split up and that he has rejected her calls for two weeks (44–48). Zaina is shocked; the sisters hope the hardship
will end (47–50). Unresolved.
