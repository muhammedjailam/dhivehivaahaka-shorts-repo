VaanamaIraadhiagai (ވާނަމަ އިރާދަކުރެއްވިއްޔާ? "If God wills") - Episode 576 - SYNOPSIS
Audio length: ~433.5 s (7 min 13 s), 50 segments. Narrated story with short dialogue lines.

=====================================================================
1. DETAILED SYNOPSIS (in order)
=====================================================================

[Seg 1-4, 0.00-35.32] OPENING: A PEACEFUL ISLAND MORNING
A clear, beautiful day with a cloudless sky. Fishermen are busy setting out to sea in
their dhonis (fishing boats). Women who sweep the neighbourhood (avahu kuni kahaa
anhenun) are working tirelessly. The sea is flat "like oil", with no waves at all.
From inside the island come the sounds of crows and koel birds; together with
people's voices it makes a heart-pleasing music. It is the school holidays, so no
schoolchildren are seen on the roads. (Island setting, morning, sunny, calm sea.)

[Seg 5-8, 35.32-69.47] LUYOON'S HAPPY SEASON
Luyoon is playing on the beach (gondu dhoshu) with her two younger siblings. These
days she has taken family-responsibility leave, so she does not have to go to the
office. A happy "spring season" has come into her life; she feels like she is flying
above the clouds - a joy she never had before. Maaniu too has found a happy life. The
two lovers feel a happiness they never had, and both believe nothing sad will ever
come their way.

[Seg 9-12, 69.47-103.56] NARRATOR'S WARNING + FIRST DIALOGUE
Narrator: this is the world - there are many hardships and many joys, and sometimes
one must cry alone. But today Luyoon and Maaniu find that hard to accept. Maaniu
(arriving/greeting her) says: "Hi Luyoon. You're up so early today - what happened
this time?" Luyoon: "You startled me! Don't do things like that..." The two are
always joking and teasing each other (sakaraaiy) - it is their nature.

[Seg 13-15, 103.56-130.26] NAAIFAA'S JEALOUSY INTRODUCED
Naaifaa watches the couple with great jealousy and is working hard to separate the
two lovers. Maaniu and Luyoon do not know this. Maaniu always says no one can ever
come between the two of them - this is true love. (Seg 15 is ambiguous: someone
"couldn't dare to tell Luyoon"; Maaniu does not know what is in Luyoon's heart -
hints at hidden things / secrets.)

[Seg 16-19, 130.26-164.94] JUICE AT THE TAKE-AWAY
Maaniu, Luyoon, Zainaa (Luyoon's younger sister) and Naufal (Luyoon's younger brother)
walk towards home. Maaniu stops near a take-away (cafe/snack shop) and calls: "Luyoon,
Zainaa and Naufal, come - let's have a juice!" They go over. Maaniu loves Luyoon's
small family immensely, which keeps Luyoon always happy; a man with such good manners
is rare. Holding their juices, the four walk home together.

[Seg 20-24, 164.94-211.34] NAAIFAA POISONS MAANIU (a few days later)
Lots more fun and teasing. A few days later, Naaifaa's jealousy bursts out. She has
many conversations with Maaniu: "Do you know, Maaniu, Luyoon is a bad girl... however
good she pretends to be, she is a very bad girl." Maaniu finds it hard to believe but
wants to look into it - he always looks at things from both sides. Maaniu: "No, it
isn't like that. All these days I have been with Luyoon. She is not such a bad person.
But I will look into it." From that day Maaniu's days turn dark, and the relationship
begins to weaken.

[Seg 25-28, 211.34-248.34] THE REJECTED CALLS / NAAIFAA'S EVIL SMILE
Maaniu's phone rings; the display shows Luyoon calling. He rejects it (makes it
busy). She calls about three times; he doesn't answer, for "many reasons". Naaifaa
senses her wicked plan has succeeded; an evil smile spreads on her face. The hope of
many days is achieved - her sweet dreams have taken colour.

[Seg 29-31, 248.34-272.43] THE BREAK-UP / MOTHER NOTICES
This becomes a great obstacle in Maaniu and Luyoon's life. Even though their wedding
was coming up, they had to drift apart and separate. At home, Mother asks: "Luyoon,
my dear... what happened, did something go wrong again?" Luyoon just smiles and walks
to her room without saying anything.

[Seg 32-38, 272.43-327.21] LUYOON'S LONELY WEEKS
Luyoon wants to keep everything secret and tell no one. Days turn into weeks; one day
after another. Luyoon seems unaware of the world, as if fed up with it. She keeps
trying to explain to Maaniu what really happened, but Maaniu, believing Naaifaa's
words, has grown tired of Luyoon. Narrator: that is how the world goes - loved ones
and closest friends will leave. While the two were preparing for their new marriage,
matched in nature, no one knows how it came to this. Luyoon, who always gave and got
love and care, cannot accept that Maaniu believed what someone else said.

[Seg 39-46, 327.21-404.03] ZAINAA COMES TO THE ROOM
Zainaa at the door: "Dhonthaa (big sister)... open the room door." Luyoon slowly opens
the locked door and Zainaa comes into her bedroom. "Dhonthaa... what really happened?
These days your face looks so sad." Zainaa is Luyoon's only sister; Luyoon shares
everything with her. Luyoon hugs Zainaa tightly and starts sobbing: "Your big sister
has no happiness these days... always hardship... If Dad (Bappa) were alive, we
wouldn't have to live like this. Dad always kept us happy." Zainaa: "Stop it, big
sister. Tell me what's going on." Luyoon pulls back, wipes her tears and makes Zainaa
promise not to tell anyone. Zainaa: "Trust me, big sister." Her little heart can't
wait. Luyoon: "Your big sister and Maaniu Bebe (big brother) have separated. Maaniu
Bebe won't come anymore." She cries again. Only little Zainaa is supporting her now.

[Seg 47-50, 404.03-433.53] "ARE YOU SERIOUS?" / ENDING
Zainaa, eyes wide: "What. Are you serious... when did this happen?" No one knows why
two people who loved so much split up. Luyoon: "For two weeks now, whenever I call,
he rejects the call." Narrator: the world is a house of tests, a journey full of joy
and sorrow. Luyoon and Zainaa keep hoping for the day this hardship ends. (Episode
ends unresolved.)
