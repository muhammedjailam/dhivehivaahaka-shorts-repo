# Emme Fahu Message — episode 326 — story notes

## Synopsis
Every morning the first sound Amaan hears is not her alarm but Eethan: his slippers on the wooden floor, kitchen
cupboards, his tuneless humming as he makes tea — after nine years an ordinary, beloved sound. Golden light, dust
motes, a slow ceiling fan, the smell of tea. Their apartment is small: third floor of a year-old building whose lift has
been "temporarily" broken for a year, a dripping tap, a kitchen so tiny they bump into each other. The walls are full of
photos of ten years together (road trips, beach sunsets, the honeymoon in Venice where Eethan pretended to fear the
pigeons, birthday dinners). Amaan reaches for her phone, frowns at another cold (rainy) day, gets up and finds Eethan at
the stove, frying pan in one hand, phone in the other, burning the pancakes again. Banter ("I prioritise crispiness",
the smoke alarm he "disabled"). She comes close to him at the stove; quiet, teasing ("You're a thirty-two-year-old
woman"), "I love you" / "I love you too". The kettle shrieks ("so jealous") and she steps back. At eight the table is
full: tea, a lopsided pancake tower, strawberries, too much syrup ("that's soup" / "that's diabetes"). Outside the city
wakes on rain-wet streets. Her phone vibrates: a reminder "10:30 Dr. Mathews". She turns it over; Eethan notices, asks
nothing, and holds her hand across the table — a silent promise; her answering smile is not complete. That afternoon:
the fertility clinic waiting room that never changes (cream walls, rows of chairs, an unwatched cooking show, coffee and
antiseptic). Amaan turns her wedding ring; opposite, a couple browse a baby-names book, a pregnant woman laughs at her
husband's whisper; Amaan looks away, ashamed of her envy. "Eethan and Amaan?" Eethan stands first and offers his hand.
Dr. Mathews' familiar kind smile looks sadder today. "Please sit." Amaan already knows. "I know how patiently you both
waited for this cycle... We received the test results this morning." Silence. "I'm very sorry."
