# Suratul Faatihaa (ސޫރަތުލް ފާތިޙާ) — series bible

A reflective, NON-FICTION religious book on the hidden meanings of Surah Al-Fatiha (cover: "ސޫރަތުލް ފާތިޙާގެ
ފޮރުވިފައިވާ ސިއްރުތައް", by Ali Fikry Mohamed), read aloud. There is no plot and no fixed cast: the narration speaks to
"you" (ތިބާ) and illustrates its points with metaphors, everyday examples, hadith and short historical stories. The
video is a sequence of contemplative illustrations that follow those images. Episodes made here: **422 → 423 → 424 →
426 → 427** (425 is not in this batch). Every cover is the same: an emerald-green and gold Islamic arch framing a white
domed mosque on a palm-lined Maldivian shore at golden hour, an open Quran on a carved wooden rehal. Match its mood (see
`style.txt`): serene, reverent, warm gold light, emerald and teal shadows.

## Episode synopses (each `episode-<N>/work/synopsis.txt` has the full section-by-section detail)
- **422** — opening: Al-Fatiha as "a key to the secrets of the universe"; where it sits in daily life; Ibn Arabi; the veil
  of habit (sunrise / flower seen for the first time); virtues (Umm al-Qur'an, Hadith Qudsi of the divided prayer, Iblis
  wailed four times, 20-g key / 200-kg iron door, "no prayer without Al-Fatiha", Abu Sa'id ibn al-Mu'alla); Verse 1 in
  depth: hamd vs shukr, praise in every state, painting/painter → the cosmos as a masterpiece, Rabb the nurturer (womb →
  breath), 'alam = sign, seen/unseen worlds, the Prophet's ﷺ night prayer ("Shall I not be a grateful servant?"), counting
  blessings. Ends: why is mercy repeated?
- **423** — verses 2–4: majesty → mercy (mother touching her sick child's forehead; hadith of the woman who finds her lost
  child), mercy twice, rahma ← rahim (womb); Maaliki yawmid-deen: the powerful stand alone on that Day; present-day
  bombing of Muslim lands (symbols only!); small daily wrongs weighed; khawf & rajaa' (fear and hope); the long-time sinner
  choosing the middle path; Maalik/Malik, Pharaoh and Nimrod; ends: the world is an exam hall, papers will be collected.
- **424** — the Day the veil is lifted, some wish they were dust; hope: the door of repentance is open; Zainul Abidin
  turning pale at wudu; life on a narrow bridge between mercy and justice; verse 5 "Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in"
  (He → You, tawhid, servitude to money/approval/desire, "we" not "I" — the whole ummah, asking for help breaks
  arrogance, the verse at the centre); verse 6 "Ihdina": guidance is a lifelong journey; the Sirat.
- **426** — not knowing you are lost; the straight path is the middle way; the path has footprints: those Allah blessed
  (prophets, the truthful, martyrs, the righteous — symbols only); the real blessing is guidance; "maghdub" (knowledge
  without action) and "dallin" (sincerity without knowledge); recap; cliffhanger: can seven verses change your life?
- **427** — seven verses, seven waves that rebuild a life (verse-by-verse inner change); salah and the divided prayer; the
  door is open now. New chapter: the secret of Bismillah — the void before creation, creation / the Day / Paradise's
  gates begin with Bismillah; Ism, Allah (hidden by the intensity of His light, like the sun), ar-Rahman vs ar-Rahim (sun,
  rain, air for all; a near mercy for open hearts); a young believer of the first generation calls "Bismillah" before two
  armies and his fear vanishes (distant dust and banners only); Bismillah as the door to "the constitution of the
  universe".

## BINDING series rules (override the literal narration)
1. NEVER depict Allah in any form (no figure, face, hand, eye, throne-sitter, "face in the light"). Use light from
   above, sky, sun, cosmos, a mihrab, a lamp.
2. NEVER depict any prophet (incl. the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ), any companion (sahabi, e.g. Abu Sa'id ibn al-Mu'alla, the
   young believer before the armies), the Prophet's family (Ahl al-Bayt, e.g. Zainul Abidin), angels or Jibreel — not
   even as silhouettes, hands, feet, shadows or blurred distant figures. Show the place without people (early Madinah
   mosque of palm trunks and a palm-frond roof, an empty prayer mat in moonlight, a brass ewer and stone wudu basin,
   sandals at a threshold, footprints in sand, a doorway of light, the green dome / Madinah palms at a distance, rows of
   soft light pillars for angels).
3. Iblis/Shaytan/jinn: never a creature or figure — dark smoke or a shadow recoiling from light, storm clouds fleeing
   dawn, a closing garden gate, a smokeless-flame shimmer at dusk.
4. Scholars and later historical figures (Ibn Arabi, Pharaoh, Nimrod): no faces — a back view in a lamp-lit study for a
   scholar; empty thrones, pyramids/ziggurat silhouettes at dusk for Pharaoh/Nimrod.
5. The hereafter (Day of Judgement, the plain, the scale, the Sirat, Paradise, Hell): symbolic only — a vast empty plain
   under a blinding white-gold sky, dust blowing, a great empty two-pan balance scale made of light, a thin glowing
   bridge over a dark misty abyss with at most a faint far red glow, gates of light. NO crowds of resurrected people with
   faces, no fire with people, no punishment, no monsters. At most tiny anonymous silhouettes from far behind.
6. Death, graves, illness: a quiet Maldivian cemetery with simple white coral headstones at dusk (no names/text), an
   empty chair, a sick person sitting up resting in bed by a window (no IV/blood/suffering faces). No body, no shroud.
7. War/violence (423's bombing passage; 427's two armies): ruins and dust haze, a mother kneeling at rubble seen from
   behind, a father's silhouette against dusty light, a small sandal on concrete, distant smoke; distant banners and dust
   clouds on a desert plain. NO bodies, injuries, blood, explosions in progress, weapons, flags, real places or faces of
   politicians; "signing an order" = a hand and pen on a polished desk, no face.
8. Women: loose long-sleeved ankle-length clothing and a hijab fully covering hair and neck in every shot, also at home
   at night. Men: long sleeves or modest shirts, long trousers/sarong. No touch between unrelated men and women; mother
   and son may hug.
9. Quran/books: the mushaf may appear (on a rehal, in hands, on a shelf) but its pages show only soft blurred golden
   ornament, never letters; prefer closed or at an angle. No Arabic calligraphy anywhere — not on mosque walls, banners,
   lamps, rugs or the Kaaba's kiswa (show the Kaaba only small and far away, if at all). No text/numbers on phones, clocks,
   papers, signs, banknotes.
10. Mosques and prayer must be shown respectfully and correctly: worshippers in straight rows facing the qibla, men in
    front; a person praying alone on a mat; hands raised in dua at chest height; prostration only from the side or behind,
    modest. No shoes on prayer mats.
11. No music in the sound (ambience + SFX only); the call to prayer is NOT synthesised.
12. No animals as pets (no dogs); birds, fish, camels in the desert, cats in the street are fine.

## Look per mood
Golden hour / dawn for hope, praise and mercy; deep blue night with stars and warm lamps for reflection and prayer;
cool storm-grey for fear, despair, warning; blinding white-gold for the hereafter; hazy desaturated with soft vignette for
historical stories (early Islam, Pharaoh) and memories. Keep faces in the upper two-thirds, a calm lower third.
