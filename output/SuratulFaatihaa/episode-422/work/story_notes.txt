# Suratul Faatihaa - Episode 422 - story notes

## Synopsis
The opening episode of the book (724 s). Al-Fatiha is presented as "a key that opens the secrets of the universe":
it opens the Qur'an and every prayer, closes many du'as, is recited beside the sick and over the dead. Ibn Arabi said he
recited only Al-Fatiha for every need and was answered. Why is it so special? Habit is "the thickest curtain hiding
the truth": a man who saw the sunrise for the first time would fall to the ground, one who smelled a flower for the
first time would weep with joy - yet we recite Al-Fatiha forty times a day without reflecting. Virtues: no surah like
it in the Torah or Injil; Umm al-Qur'an; the Hadith Qudsi of the prayer divided between Allah and His servant (Allah
answers each verse - an exchange between heaven and earth); Iblis wailed four times, the last when Al-Fatiha was
revealed; a 20-gram key opens a 200-kilo iron door; "no prayer without Al-Fatiha" - it is the foundation of the pillar
of religion; the story of Abu Sa'id ibn al-Mu'alla waiting to learn "the greatest surah". Then Verse 1 in depth: the
Qur'an opens with praise, not a warning; hamd vs shukr; praise in wealth and poverty, health and sickness; contentment
with the decree (losing a job, a loved one, health) - the hard test; praise before you ask; the "Al-" of totality;
praising a painting is praising the painter - the universe as a masterpiece (atom, eye, heart, cells, galaxies); Rabb
= the One who creates and nurtures (womb, childhood, every breath); 'alam = a sign (flower, star, face, a child's
smile); seen and unseen worlds (angels, jinn, grave/barzakh, hereafter); the Prophet's night prayer until his feet
swelled ("Shall I not be a grateful servant?"); forgotten miracles of sight, hearing and heartbeat; the veil of
habituation; counting today's blessings; begin with praise, not complaint. Cliffhanger: why is "Ar-Rahman ar-Rahim"
repeated, twice, in the middle?
