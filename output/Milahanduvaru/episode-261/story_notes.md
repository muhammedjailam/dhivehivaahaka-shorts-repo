# Milahanduvaru — Episode 261 (series finale) — story notes

Duration 555.4 s (9 min 15 s). Ends "= ނިމުނީ =" (The End).

## Synopsis (English)
At dusk Shamaan snaps at his mother not to speak about Zumra again, shuts himself in his room and paces, angry and
anxious, while his parents prepare house and yard for the night's kiyevelli. After Isha, Hassanfulhu and his men sit in
rows in the yard and recite; fires burn at every corner of the yard. Sakeena and the father join in; Shamaan stays inside.
Hassanfulhu sends Sakeena to fetch him. She opens his door and is stunned: Zumra is sitting with Shamaan. Zumra begs
"Mother, don't kill me!" and says she came to protect them — it was she who undid the sorcery someone sent to separate
Sakeena and her husband; that sorcery also reached Shamaan and caused his first wife's death. Sakeena calls it a jinn's
lie. Zumra says she carries Shamaan's child; Sakeena wants no jinn children, swears to wipe her from the earth and pulls
Shamaan by the arm while Zumra clings to him. At Sakeena's cry Hassanfulhu runs in: Shamaan lies collapsed on the floor.
The men carry him to the joali in the yard. Hassanfulhu keeps reciting and asks "Where is the jinn Zumra?" — "Her body
has caught fire." Hassanfulhu vows not to stop until she is ash; Shamaan tries to rise and is held back; "She is burning
to ash." Shamaan gets up, drained; Hassanfulhu steadies him: "Now it is over." Shamaan nods but knows it is not over.
He is put to bed and sleeps.
Next morning he aches all over, bathes, sits for tea; Sakeena strokes his head ("you are saved"). He lies down again and
wakes after sunset. Lying on the joali in the yard, his jinn daughter Nazaaha comes and sits beside him, crying for her
mother and angry at "cruel grandma"; Shamaan says grandma will love her. Sakeena calls him in (not outside at sunset!)
and grabs his arm; Nazaaha cries "Grandma, don't take Bappa!" Sakeena turns, frightened and furious ("what jinn are you?
shall I kill you too?"); Nazaaha: "You killed my mother cruelly... Zumra is my mother, Shamaan is my father." Shamaan
confirms and bitterly offers "hurt her too, then". Sakeena's heart melts: "Grandma will never hurt Nazaaha, but tell
no one you are a jinn child", and hugs her, feeling a strange coldness but greater tenderness. Inside, the father learns
the truth and is kind to her. Sakeena introduces her brother Yameen; Nazaaha already knows him, she was his "imaginary
friend". Nazaaha grows afraid, promising to visit Bappa every moonlit night; at the sound of Hassanfulhu coming she hides
behind Shamaan, trembling and gripping his shirt; Sakeena lays a hand on her head: "Hassanfulhu can't hurt you."
Hassanfulhu enters, suspicious, but the father draws him into conversation; Sakeena slips Nazaaha into the inner room
("Mother always said there are good humans too; now I know"). Through the night Nazaaha's stories of her loving jinn
mother fill their hearts. Near dawn she says it is time to go, she'll come on a moonlit night, "look after Bappa". A cold
breeze enters, and she fades into the moonlight. Only the memory of her innocent smile remains. Shamaan looks out of the
window: the moon is fading but new hope fills him. Sakeena weeps. They all wait again for a moonlit night.
