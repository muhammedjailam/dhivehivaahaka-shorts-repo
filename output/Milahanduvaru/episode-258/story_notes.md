# Milahanduvaru — episode 258: story notes

Duration 843.7 s (narration). 94 shots (from work/segments.json), 30 visual beats.

## Synopsis
**Cold open (rain, night).** Sattar — the strong young reciter who named Shamaan's wife in 257 — sees a girl running
alone through the heavy night rain and chases her; she vanishes. Walking home soaked, he finds her standing motionless
against a wall, unaffected by the cold. When she looks at him, her eyes are not human — a fire-red glow. She seizes his
arm with an icy grip; his strength drains and he bows his head. "I am a jinni. What is this work you are doing?" — it is
the jinn the reciters were trying to drive away. He begs to be let go, his breath fails, he falls, and dimly sees the
figure vanish into the rain.

**Funeral.** Some islanders lose faith in Hassanfulhu, others think his work has made the sorcery stronger; he does not
give up. Many attend Sattar's funeral; every heart asks "Who is next?"

**Hassanfulhu's visit.** In the evening Shamaan and Zumra sit on the joali in the yard with Sakeena, uneasy, talking of the
terror on the island. Hassanfulhu enters with a young man who introduces him as the visiting fanditha-man. Sakeena
introduces her son and his wife; Zumra abruptly gets up and storms into the house. Hassanfulhu, staring after her:
"An unusual girl… is she even human? I think she is a jinni, she may have a hand in what is happening." Shamaan
(mocking him as an old man chasing young girls) and Sakeena order him out; he leaves shaking his head. The family fears
the islanders' talk — Shamaan's first marriage ended so painfully; he will not be parted from Zumra.

**Zumra hurt — confrontation.** Another evening near sunset Zumra comes into the yard hurt and weeping: "The islanders
want to destroy me — that Hassanfulhu did this." Shamaan rushes to Hassanfulhu's lodging, his mother running after him,
Zumra watching from afar. At the gate (Hassanfulhu leaving for the mosque) Shamaan seizes him; heated words ("your wife and
children are jinn" / "my wife is human"); Hassanfulhu pushes him off; Sakeena tries to hold her son back. On the road home
Zumra, in tears, runs and clings to Shamaan; both weep. Hassanfulhu approaches — Zumra flees and vanishes. Hassanfulhu
says he only saw the two of them, "that is why I said she is a jinni"; Shamaan strikes him; youths separate them.

**Friday recitation.** Near sunset the main road is laid with white cloth, a big table with chairs around it, only strong
youths present. After maghrib Hassanfulhu's group sits; he burns incense and reassures them; a youth doubts him ("we still
lost someone last night"). Shamaan charges in shouting with unnatural strength; no one can hold him; water from
Hassanfulhu's bowl hits his face and he collapses, only breathing. He is laid on the table and the men recite; his body
changes, his eyes roll up. Sakeena bursts in and rushes at Hassanfulhu; youths stop her and take a knife from her hand. He
refuses to stop. Fire springs up at the four corners and rings the table; men rush to pull Shamaan out — Hassanfulhu
forbids it ("this fire burns the evil powers in him"). Sakeena screams, held by five men. The fire dies; Shamaan gets up,
barely able to stand; he is carried home on shoulders.

**Night.** Sakeena comforts him ("pay no mind"); Zumra has not come home; she closes the door. Shamaan wakes sensing
someone: Zumra is pacing the room, hurt. "What happened?" — "Don't pretend you don't know… it's Hassanfulhu's doing," she
weeps.
