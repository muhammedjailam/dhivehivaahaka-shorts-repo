## Synopsis
Night: Shamaan holds toddler Yameen and whispers that everything he does is for him; lying down he resolves to build a
happy future for his father and his son. Next morning he reaches the office with seconds to spare (fingerprint scanner)
but cannot concentrate: his lonely life, Zihuna's sudden death and the islanders' accusations (some blame him, there is
talk of sorcery) weigh on him — Zihuna was his whole world. Colleague Vaasif sits by his desk, teases him to "find a nice
girl", then tells him to put it behind him and leaves with a hand on his shoulder. After work Shamaan drops in at
Zihuna's parents' house; her mother and younger sister on the joali first ask where Yameen is — they adore him. In the
lane he meets his father carrying Yameen; he hugs the boy and wants to take him home, but Yameen cries and wriggles, so
Shamaan walks home alone. Exhausted, he showers and falls asleep, waking at the Isha call. The house is empty; he sits
on the joali in the yard; someone seems to come out of the house and leave — he calls "Mother?" but the figure does not
hear. Later Sakeena comes in from outside and casually says yes, she had gone to Alifulhube's house. In his room Shamaan
notices an amulet (thaveedh) on Yameen's arm; he never believed in amulets/fanditha and confronts his mother in the
kitchen ("for the evil eye" — "he looks like an old toddy tapper!"), then slams his door. Sakeena knocks, comes in, sits
on the bed and reveals that when he was a baby his father's family worked sorcery against them: at twenty she fell ill
(the doctor said anaemia, then dizziness and collapsing) until a visiting fanditha man cured her — hence the amulet.
Shamaan weeps; he remembers his own childhood illness and that he only spoke properly at twelve, and wonders whether
Zihuna's death hides the same secret. Sakeena admits that before Zihuna died she noticed strange things: Zihuna felt
followed and watched, lost sleep, had frightening dreams; Shamaan recalls her headaches and exhaustion a week before.
"Could they harm Yameen?" — "That is why the amulet; we must turn to religion and seek Allah's mercy," she says,
stroking his head. Sleepless, he resolves to learn the truth. Next morning in the lane he meets Zihuna's best friend
Fathuma, who hesitantly reveals that Zihuna said someone in his family was troubling her — his aunt Naasira (his
father's eldest sister), who never approved of the marriage and vowed to end it. Shamaan is stunned; suspicion becomes
certainty; he vows justice for Zihuna. Furious, he walks straight home and confronts his father on the joali; the
father says those are old stories, his face changes and he bows his head; Shamaan cries out in tears that "those
people" may have caused Zihu's death and ruined his life. Sakeena comes out of the house: "Shamaan, calm down, don't
talk to your father like that." (ends on her worried face)
