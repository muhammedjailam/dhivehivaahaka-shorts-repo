# Milahanduvaru (މިލަހަނދުވަރު, "Moonlight") — series bible

A serialized Dhivehi drama on a small unnamed Maldivian island village (coral-stone houses with sandy yards, joali rope
seats under big trees, narrow sandy lanes, a mosque, a field, a jetty, a beach "thundi" (sandbank tip) with a big kaani
tree). Themes: grief, suspected sorcery (sihuru/fanditha), a beautiful night-visiting jinn wife, a fanditha-man's
recitations (kiyevelli), and in the finale a jinn daughter who returns on moonlit nights. Episodes 252–261 (261 is the
finale, ends "= ނިމުނީ =").

Series look: `style.txt` (moonlit indigo/silver-blue/sea-teal with warm amber lamplight; soft tropical daylight by day) —
matches the cover (full moon over a dark sea). Caption highlight pill = royal blue #2A5FC4 (`caption_style.json`).
Prompt tail = `tail.txt`. Cover = `work/episode-<N>-cover.png` (same image every episode).

  "Shamaan" and of "Sakeena" approaching — Hassanfulhu: "that's the jinn Zumra".

## Content rules specific to this series (on top of `main prompt.txt` section 6 — binding)
- **Jinn depiction**: Zumra and Nazaaha always appear as normal, beautiful, fully modest humans; the only supernatural
  cues are moonlight glow, a silvery sparkle in the eyes, mist, a fading/translucent silhouette when vanishing. NO
  monsters, horns, demonic faces, glowing red eyes in close-up, levitation horror or gore. For the "red-eyed" jinn moments
  (258 cold open, 260 Thursday night) show her from behind or in deep shadow/silhouette in the rain, face not visible.
  Apparitions (259: "Shamaan"/"Sakeena" in the distance) = a blurred distant figure in the rain, no reference image, no
  weapon.
- **No weapons ever**: Sakeena's knife (258) and the apparition's knife (259) are NOT shown — show Sakeena held back by
  men's outstretched arms with empty hands, or her tearful face; the apparition as a distant blurred figure.
- **Violence**: never depict choking, punching, grabbing a throat, bodies, injuries, burns or blood. Show tense
  face-to-face standoffs at arm's length, men stepping in between, a fallen cap on the sand, a slammed door, storm clouds.
  Alibe/Najmee/Sattar deaths: a crowd at a house gate, a covered bier carried by men in white towards the island cemetery
  seen from a distance, women grieving at a doorway, grave markers under palms at dusk. Zumra's wounds: she sits in shadow
  holding her forearm wrapped in a plain cloth, pained face; no blood (260 "bleeding from her whole body" → she slumps
  against a door in the dark, face in her hands).
- **Exorcism / kiyevelli**: men sitting cross-legged in rows reciting from CLOSED books or with open palms, incense smoke,
  oil lamps or small fires at the yard corners, Hassanfulhu leading; respectful, no readable/Arabic script anywhere, no
  amulets in close-up. Shamaan collapsed: lying fully clothed on a joali/table, eyes closed, men kneeling around; the
  "ring of fire" (258) = flames in a circle on the sand at a distance around the table, Shamaan unharmed. Zumra burning
  to ash (261) = never shown; show a thin wisp of smoke rising into the moonlit sky above the yard fires.
- **Romance before marriage (254–256)**: NO touching — no hand-holding, no head on shoulder, no embrace, no eye-covering.
  Show them walking side by side at a clear arm's-length gap, sitting at opposite ends of a joali / bench, standing apart
  under the kaani tree looking at the moon, two silhouettes at a distance. **After the nikah (256 onward)**: married
  couple may sit side by side or hold hands, fully clothed, no embrace, no bedroom intimacy; "bridal room" = a room
  decorated with flowers and lamps, the couple standing apart by the window. Zumra hugging Shamaan → show them standing
  close side by side, or her hand on his arm.
- **Nikah (256)**: qazi with Shamaan and the two fathers at a table under coloured lights; Zumra seated with Sakeena and
  women nearby, head bowed; guests at tables; no women in the men's circle.
- **Bathing/showers**: never shown (mention only); show a towel on a chair or him coming out dressed.
- **Music show (254–255)**: lit stage far away, crowd seated on chairs/sand, bodu beru drummers small in the distance; no
  dancing women. Akram's group of girls: modestly dressed girls with hijab at a distance.
- **Yameen's affliction (254)**: no marks on his body; show a worried grandmother holding him, the child staring/laughing
  at an empty corner beside a toy box, a dark shadow on a tree trunk.
- **Zihuna**: never a corpse; a smiling framed photo, or a soft hazy memory.
- **Prayer**: respectful (prayer mat, standing figure, mosque at dusk); no Quran pages with script.
