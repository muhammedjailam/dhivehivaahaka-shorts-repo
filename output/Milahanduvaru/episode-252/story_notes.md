## Synopsis
Night. Shamaan, a young widower, wakes to his toddler son Yameen crying and picks him up. He raises Yameen alone; his
wife Zihuna died suddenly barely two years into an arranged marriage, leaving Yameen as a "priceless trust". The island
whispers that her death was sorcery or fanditha aimed at Shamaan, though he saw no harm or anything unusual. Lying
awake he wonders who could have wanted to kill such an innocent girl; he remembers that before the family arranged his
marriage to Zihuna he was with another girl (Nashwa), and a frightening thought rises: did she turn to sorcery?
His mother Sakeena opens the door; he pretends to sleep. She tucks Yameen's blanket, sighs and weeps for the motherless
child. After she leaves he looks at Zihuna's photo on the bedside table, holds Yameen's tiny hand and promises never to
leave him alone. A cold wind blows through the window; he recalls Nashwa's warning when the marriage was called off.
In the dark he pictures Zihuna's last smile and resolves to find the truth, "for Yameen's sake". Dark clouds gather;
he goes to the window — palms thrash in the strong wind. Was it a natural death or someone's plot? He remembers Nashwa's
hateful words, "Shamaan, I will never let you be happy with anyone but me", and shivers at the thought of her "curse".
Yameen startles and cries again; Shamaan soothes him, both in tears, and renews his promise. The child falls asleep
gripping his shirt; Shamaan lays him down, kisses his forehead and lies beside him.
Morning: the phone alarm; Shamaan gets ready. Sakeena cooks holding Yameen on her hip; the father (Bappa), who normally
goes fishing at dawn, sits feverish on the sofa. Breakfast of hot roshi and curry; Yameen giggles on his father's lap
stealing roshi. When Shamaan leaves for the office Yameen cries "Bappa... Bappa..." reaching out; Shamaan, torn, wipes a
tear and goes for the child's future. At the office his mind is at home; at the midday break he phones — Sakeena says
Yameen ate and is sleeping by Bappa, whose fever eased. After work he hurries home; Yameen runs to him shouting
"Bappa!", hugs his legs and is scooped up; the day's pain melts away.
