# Bappage Gatulu — Episode 603 (SERIES FINALE): story notes

## Synopsis
Continuous from 586, in Asim's live-televised 8 pm rally at a Malé convention-centre hall. The main doors crash open and
a large special police unit marches in; the live-TV cameramen swing their cameras and flashes onto the stage, where Home
Minister Asim stands sweating and trembling, still believing the police have come for Iyaan. He roars into the
microphone: arrest him, he is a terrorist trying to topple the government, seize his devices. No officer moves. The
Assistant Commissioner walks straight to him with a court order issued that morning and announces that Asim, Speaker
Sameer and bank MD Fareed are under arrest for the politically planned killing of MP Ahmed Zahir in 2011 and for
embezzling state funds. After a moment's silence the thousands in the hall erupt; the whole nation watches live — the
police cyber division verified Iyaan's evidence and the court issued the warrants on the Prosecutor General's
direction. Asim's world spins; his power blows away. Fareed bolts for a secret door behind the stage and is caught;
Sameer stands silent, head bowed, and is led off. Asim, unhinged, snatches a guard's pistol and threatens Iyaan ("stay
back, make way for me!"). Raaya screams "Bappa, don't!" and sinks to her knees sobbing. Iyaan steps forward, fearless:
"Shoot, Asim — my father didn't kneel before you either; your end has come." Asim: "I'll finish you just like your
father!" As he tries to fire, Iyaan forces his arm up — the shot hits the ceiling — disarms him and throws him to the
stage floor: "This is the son of Ahmed Zahir. Today justice is done." The three are led out through the jeering crowd.
Iyaan thanks the weeping Raaya: without her this justice would never have come. Next morning, in Malé's Aasahara
cemetery, Iyaan prays at his father's grave with his mother Aminath behind him; for the first time in fifteen years he
cries: "Bappa, the nation knows the truth; I kept my promise." He rests his hand on his mother's shoulder and they walk
out; a cold drizzle puts out the fifteen-year fire of revenge, and a deep peace fills him. ނިމުނީ (The End).
