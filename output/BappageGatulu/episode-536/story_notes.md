# Bappage Gatulu — Episode 536: story notes

## Synopsis
9 am, present day. Bank MD Fareed opens the anonymous email Iyaan sent at the end of 529: receipts of the money he paid
into the killers' account in 2011 for the murder of MP Ahmed Zahir, and the line "These secrets can't stay hidden
forever. Get ready to pay." His most trusted IT man traces it: it came straight from Home Minister Asim's house. Fareed,
grinding his teeth, decides the break-in at Asim's house was Asim's own staged drama to extort him, and phones Speaker
Sameer: "We must meet now — Asim wants to destroy us."

Two hours later, in a secret private lounge in Hulhumalé: Sameer seated (the narration has him smoking), Fareed standing,
Asim seated and furious. Asim slams the table: someone nearly opened his safe last night; why would he email Fareed?
Fareed throws his phone down: then why does the IP match your router? You want to pin Zahir's murder on me! Sameer shouts
"Stop!" and reasons it out: Asim is no fool; last night's intruder got into the system and used the house IP. Asim, now
afraid: who knows the three of us were involved? Sameer opens a file on his phone: no camera caught the intruder, but
police cyber-crime saw how the virus was built — a top digital-forensics expert's work; and a young journalist, Iyaan,
has been publishing detailed corruption stories — he is Ahmed Zahir's only son. The floor drops from under Asim: he
remembers patting the young man's shoulder at Raaya's gallery and the deep look in his eyes. "He used Raaya to get into
my house!" Sameer stands: Iyaan must be eliminated.

Iyaan has been listening live through Asim's phone. He puts the headphones down with a dangerous smile: "You found out
too late — you'll walk right into my net." Two steps ahead, he calls Raaya and asks her to come secretly to an art studio,
unseen by her father's security and the police. In a quiet Malé lane, on the third floor of an old building, the studio
is silent; a laptop is open on the table; Iyaan stands at the window. Raaya says her father is furious. Iyaan turns,
unsmiling: "It was me who got into your house" — to find the truth of his father's murder; her father wears white but
his hands are stained with an innocent man's blood. "No! That's a lie!" She turns to leave; he taps the laptop: Asim's
own voice from the safe audio ("close his mouth forever… make tonight his last night"). Her hands fly to her ears; she
sinks to the floor. Iyaan kneels before her: the murdered man was his father, who died in his arms when he was eight, on
her father's order. "What should I do?" she sobs. Asim plans to kill him tonight with Sameer and Fareed; Asim can only be
beaten from inside his house; before Asim's next big rally he needs the second backup drive — only she can get it. After
a long silence she decides, wiping her tears: "I'll do it — I'll help expose my father's crimes to the nation." Iyaan,
inwardly: "Asim, you took my father from me; today your own child stands against you." To be continued.
