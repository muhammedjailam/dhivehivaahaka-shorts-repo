# Bappage Gatulu — Episode 529: story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
It is just past midnight (the bible says just past 2 am). A storm is building over Malé, with a black sky, lightning, thunder
and a cold damp wind. Home Minister Asim and his daughter Raaya are at an official banquet for a foreign delegation at Kurumba
Maldives resort, so only the outer-wall guards are at the mansion. Iyaan waits in a narrow lane behind the house, dressed in
black with a mask. He counts "three, two, one, run". His virus gets into the house server and freezes the CCTV feeds, so the
guards see a quiet scene recorded five minutes earlier. The card he copied from Raaya opens the outer gate with a soft beep. He
crosses the dark sitting room like a shadow and climbs to the secret office. The blue fingerprint reader flashes red when he
presses the silicone print on his right thumb. He holds his breath, it beeps green and the heavy lock opens. By flashlight he
finds the big desk, the bookshelves and the steel safe behind the desk. He attaches a code-breaking device to the safe and the
numbers race.
Then a car stops outside. Raaya says "Bappa, I'm so tired today" and Asim answers "go to sleep, dear". Asim's footsteps come up the
stairs, straight to the office. The safe needs another minute and there is no way out. The lock beeps, Iyaan snatches the device
and hides under the desk, and the light comes on. All he can see is Asim's expensive black shoes. Asim searches the files on the
desk. Iyaan's trembling hand touches a small drawer and a glass paperweight rattles. Asim asks "Who's there?" and starts round the
back of the desk. As he bends to look, the power dies, an alarm and a red-alert siren fill the house, and Asim shouts for his
guards: "Where are you? Why did the power go? Turn on the backup!" Under the desk Iyaan's phone lights up. His software has
overloaded the main fuse system, and the generator will start in one minute. Raaya bursts in with a phone flashlight: "Bappa!
Bappa, where are you?" Asim shouts "Raaya! Don't come in!", takes her hand and leads her downstairs. In those few seconds Iyaan
slips behind the long curtain, through the glass door and onto the balcony. The generator roars and the lights return. Asim looks
back into the empty office, decides he imagined it, locks the door and goes down with Raaya. On the balcony Iyaan breathes again,
soaked in sweat. The device had already sent the safe's data to his phone over wifi. He jumps from the balcony wall, gets
through the trees into the lane, pulls off the mask and walks away, telling himself "Raaya... you saved my life."
At home, in his locked room, he is still trembling. He opens the folder "PZ 2011" on a tablet. It holds a 15-year-old secret
police log of Asim's orders to the lead investigator: the motorbike's number plate and the fingerprint reports from the weapon
were destroyed by a letter with Asim's signature. Below it is an Excel sheet called "Partners' Share". Three men stole millions
from the state budget into offshore accounts: Asim, Speaker Sameer and bank MD Fareed. Fareed paid for the murder, Sameer covered
it up politically and Asim sent the gangsters. "This isn't only Asim's doing. This is the whole system." He pins Sameer's and
Fareed's photos on either side of Asim's and links them with red string. His new plan is to destroy their trust in each other.
He will use Fareed's own bank files to threaten Fareed, so that Fareed thinks Asim is blackmailing him.
At 8 am Raaya calls in tears. Someone hacked the house and tried to open Bappa's safe, the police are everywhere, and Bappa is
terrified and blames a political enemy. Iyaan pretends to be shocked and offers to come over. Raaya says no, because Bappa
suspects everyone and the police are checking phones. He feels a twinge of pity, but she is a killer's child. The CCTV logs
show only an empty staircase. Asim's fear is Iyaan's first psychological victory. On a dark hacker network he sends the first
warning to Fareed's private email, with the sender IP pointing to Asim's office. "The storm has begun." To be continued.

escape. After that come a cold revelation and strategic resolve. Noir charcoal/teal with red alarm accents, rain and lightning.
