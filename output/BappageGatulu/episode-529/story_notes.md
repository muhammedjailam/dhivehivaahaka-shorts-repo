# Bappage Gatulu — Episode 529: story notes

## Synopsis
It is just past midnight (the bible says just past 2 am). A storm is building over Malé: black sky, lightning, thunder, cold damp wind.
Home Minister Asim and his daughter Raaya are at an official banquet for a foreign delegation at Kurumba Maldives resort, so
only the outer-wall guards are at the mansion. Iyaan waits in a narrow lane behind the house, dressed in black with a mask.
He counts "3… 2… 1… run". His virus gets into the house server and loops the CCTV, so the guards see a calm scene recorded
five minutes earlier. The card he copied from Raaya opens the outer gate with a soft beep. He crosses the dark sitting room like
a shadow and climbs to the secret office. The blue fingerprint reader shows red when he presses the silicone print on his right
thumb. He holds his breath, it beeps green and the heavy lock opens. By flashlight he finds the big desk, the bookshelves and the
steel safe behind the desk, and clips a code-breaking device onto its keypad while the numbers race.
Then a car stops outside: Raaya says "Bappa, I'm so tired" and Asim answers "go to sleep, dear". Asim's footsteps come up the
stairs, straight to the office. The safe needs one more minute and there is no way out. The lock beeps, Iyaan pulls the device
off and hides under the desk, and the light comes on. All he can see is Asim's expensive black shoes. Asim searches files on the
desk. Iyaan's trembling hand touches a drawer handle and a glass paperweight rattles. Asim says "Who's there?" and starts round
the back of the desk. As he bends to look, the power dies, an alarm and a red-alert siren fill the house, and Asim shouts for
the guards and the backup. Under the desk Iyaan's phone lights up: his software has overloaded the main fuse system, and the
generator will start in one minute. Raaya bursts in with a phone flashlight ("Bappa, where are you?"). Asim shouts "don't come
in!", takes her hand and leads her out. In those seconds Iyaan slips behind the long curtain, through the glass door and onto the
balcony. The generator roars and the lights return. Asim looks back into the empty office, decides he imagined it, locks the
door and goes down with Raaya. On the balcony Iyaan breathes again, soaked in sweat. The device had already sent the safe's
data to his phone over wifi. He jumps from the balcony wall, gets through the trees into the lane, pulls off the mask and walks
away: "Raaya… you saved my life."
At home, in his locked room, he is still trembling. He opens the folder "PZ 2011" on a tablet: a 15-year-old secret police log of
Asim's orders to the lead investigator. The motorbike's number plate and the fingerprint reports from the weapon were destroyed
by a letter with Asim's signature. Below it is an Excel sheet called "Partners' Share". Three men (Asim, Speaker Sameer and bank MD
Fareed) stole millions from the state budget into offshore accounts. Fareed paid for the murder, Sameer covered it up politically
and Asim sent the gangsters. "This is the whole system." He pins Sameer's and Fareed's photos on either side of Asim's and links
them with red string. His new plan is to destroy their trust in each other, using Fareed's own bank files to threaten Fareed so
that Fareed thinks Asim is blackmailing him.
At 8 am Raaya calls in tears: someone hacked the house and tried to open Bappa's safe, the police are everywhere, and Bappa is
terrified and blames a political enemy. Iyaan pretends to be shocked and offers to come. Raaya says no: Bappa suspects everyone
and the police are checking phones. He feels a twinge of pity, but she is a killer's daughter. The CCTV logs show only an empty
staircase. Asim's fear is Iyaan's first psychological victory. On a dark hacker network he sends the first warning to Fareed's
private email, with the sender IP pointing to Asim's office. "The storm has begun." To be continued.
