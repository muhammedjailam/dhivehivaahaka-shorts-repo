# Bappage Gatulu — Episode 586: story notes

## Synopsis
Continues directly from 536. Raaya leaves the art studio, heart wounded, sure that helping Iyaan is now her duty. At the
silent mansion (Asim is out, planning Iyaan's end with Sameer and Fareed) she goes into her father's office, moves the
books on the shelf behind the desk, presses a hidden button and takes a red hard drive from a secret compartment — the
original backup of Asim's political life, his black-money network and every document of Zahir's murder.

At that moment two black motorbikes stop outside Iyaan's apartment: four masked thugs sent by Asim and Sameer, ordered
to make sure he never leaves alive. Iyaan sees them on his hidden street cameras ("Did you lot think you could achieve
anything?"), switches off the lights, puts on night-vision goggles and waits behind his door. In the dark he disarms
and stuns the first two; the other two hurl iron bars that miss; he presses a remote and the wall speakers blast Asim's
recorded order to murder Zahir — "What is this?" — "This is the end of your master" — and fells the last two. Lights on,
he calls Raaya: she has the drive; tomorrow night, at the campaign rally, they will release everything before the nation.

Next morning the thugs sit in a corner; from their phones Iyaan texts Asim that the job is done, so Asim believes him
dead. He slips out the back door; Malé's roads are being hung with banners for Asim's huge live-broadcast rally. In the
studio Raaya hands over the drive (she can hardly believe her father's bitter truth); Iyaan calls her brave. The drive
holds the signed originals of every crime of Sameer, Fareed and Asim since 2011 and the plan to murder Zahir. His plan:
when Asim reaches the podium, a signal-hijack program will seize every TV feed and the hall's giant screens — "then
Bappa will have no way out," Raaya says.

At 8 pm the packed convention hall roars as Asim, nearly sure of the presidential ticket, walks to the podium; in the VIP
row he has whispered to Sameer that Zahir's son's story is over. In a black van nearby Iyaan asks Raaya "Ready?" — "The
truth has to come out." Asim promises an anti-corruption government; Iyaan says "Now" and presses Enter. The mic dies,
the screens turn black and red ("the secret of a fifteen-year-old murder"), the control room is locked out and the whole
nation watches: Fareed's 2011 payments to the killers, Sameer's signed cover-up orders, and then Asim's own voice:
"shut his mouth for good, make tonight his last night." The crowd erupts, Fareed sinks into his chair, Sameer signals
his guards. Iyaan, in a black suit, strides in with Raaya carrying a thick file; she orders the guards back. "Aren't you
dead?" gasps Asim; at the microphones Iyaan tells the nation the killers sent to him are locked in his home: "I am Ahmed
Zahir's son — Iyaan Ahmed Zahir." Asim lunges; Raaya steps in front of him: "Bappa, you are a killer!" His world
shatters. The doors burst open and a large police force runs toward the stage. To be continued.
