# Hayaath — Episode 319: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
It is the night of the ring ceremony. When Hayaathu steps out of the house, a car is waiting at the gate with Yasir — Fazaal's friend — leaning on it, smoking; he throws the cigarette away when he sees her. Zoona and Areesha follow her out, and Waheed drives up just then. In front of a guest Waheed is unusually polite: "Going somewhere?" — "Yes, Fazaal asked," says Yasir. Waheed, who has heard (and seen in the photo) that Fazaal is ugly, is eager to see him in person; Yasir only answers with a knowing smile. Areesha mocks: "How would Fazaal come out with that face?" Yasir says "Let's go", opens the front passenger door, and Hayaathu hesitates — someone she cannot make out sits in the driver's seat, head bowed over a dimmed phone. Yasir nods reassuringly, she gets in, he shuts the door and follows on his motorbike. The family is left staring; Areesha is annoyed she couldn't see the face to humiliate Hayaathu.

In the car Hayaathu realises the driver is Fazaal himself; she thinks his ugliness kept him from facing the family, and turns her tearful face away. Fazaal, unseen by her, smiles. They drive along the Link Road, a road full of memories of Maaroof. He hands her a tissue; she whispers thanks. "Why did you agree to marry me? I won't force anything on you — I don't think you even looked at the photo I sent." She confesses she loves someone else — at that moment the car brakes hard ("Lucky...") — but it was only to spare a cat crossing the road. He asks again whether she is being forced. Inwardly: yes — for her sister, for Maaroof, for her own love. Bitterly she asks how much he paid her uncle for her, and begs him to hold the wedding privately, away from everyone; he smiles, because he wants it public and grand. "Are you ashamed?" She sobs and keeps her head bowed, refusing to look at his face.

He drives to a secluded beach under the full moon and opens her door. She walks silently to the sea; he follows and takes her hand; startled, she pulls it away and walks to the water's edge in tears — no one but Maaroof should touch her. She had wanted to slip out tonight to meet Maaroof; now how can she? Fazaal approaches: the feelings of the night he rescued her from the sea return; no girl in Dubai ever made him feel this; he, who never wanted to be close to anyone, longs to hold her. He stands beside her; she shuts her eyes so as not to see his face. "I hope you'll be faithful to me. I'll give you sincere love and faithfulness." Watching her tears he knows they are for Maaroof, not him — yet she is his future wife, and he cannot let her go.
