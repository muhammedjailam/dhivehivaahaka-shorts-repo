# Hayaath — Episode 415: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Picks up mid-scene on the night of the ring ceremony (engagement). Fazaal has taken Hayaathu to a secluded beach. He thinks: she is now his future wife; how could he let her go? A message on his phone brings him back. Hayaathu stands silently crying, staring at the sea. He walks up and gently asks if she wants to share anything.

The beach is where she spent happy times with Maaroof. Flashback: Maaroof was always her shelter from Waheed's harshness, Zoona's schemes and Areesha's mockery. On the day he asked Waheed for her hand he joked that her uncle must be "very poor", since he demanded three million rufiyaa, "Hayaathu's price". Then he slipped into the sea, she laughed, and he chased her along the sand to soak her too.

Back on the beach Fazaal watches her smile. He takes her hand; she shuts her eyes, pulls it away and says she needs time. Marriage is a sacred covenant, her heart belongs to someone else, she is marrying under compulsion and he will get "only this body, not my heart". Fazaal answers that he too is marrying under compulsion. She wonders what compulsion a free man of 32 could have, whose only flaw is "that burned face" (she still believes the photo of his late brother Fawaz is him). She does not know he is compelled by his own heart. Reflection: he had tested many girls by sending his brother's photo. They stopped answering or changed their numbers, so he had come to see women as vain and false. Now he has finally met the girl he wished for.

He drops her home and drives off. She slips back out and runs through the late-night streets to Maaroof's house, knocking and sobbing with no answer. Dhooma phones ("where are you?") and Hayaathu walks home in despair. Fazaal had expected this and was parked nearby watching. He follows her silently in his car, pretending to go elsewhere. He had read the message she sent Maaroof after the ring ceremony before she deleted it, and dropped his plan to visit with **Yasir** so he could go himself. He wants to give her no chance to meet Maaroof.

At home Areesha blocks her way and mocks "the Prince of Dubai", then stops short at her swollen eyes, but keeps going: "you're a wife now, learn to be happy thinking of the ugly prince." In the bedroom Hayaathu sobs to Dhooma that she went to see Maaroof. Waheed overhears, kicks the door open, drags her out and throws her against the sitting-room coffee table. Zoona and Areesha run out and Dhooma wheels out crying. Waheed smashes a glass vase. A shard pierces Hayaathu's back and another cuts Areesha's foot. Zoona scolds Waheed and blames Hayaathu. Waheed threatens Hayaathu: she must never meet Maaroof again, Fazaal is "no ordinary man". Zoona tends only to Areesha, calls Dhooma's stammering cries for help "drama", and shouts at the sisters to pick up the glass. Alone, Dhooma pulls the shard out and presses on the bleeding with trembling hands. Later aunt **Shafeena** (their father's full younger sister) comes in, horrified. She tries to lift Hayaathu by the shoulders, but Hayaathu's body goes limp and slumps sideways (cliffhanger).

## Tone
Bittersweet and moonlit at first: guarded honesty between two people forced into an engagement, plus a warm, playful memory of Maaroof. Then loneliness and despair at the silent door, and finally the most violent and humiliating domestic scene so far, ending on a fearful cliffhanger.
