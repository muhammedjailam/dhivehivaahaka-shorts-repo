# Hayaath — Episode 273: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Night, continuing from the splash at the end of 272. Hayaathu realises Dhooma has gone into the sea; before Maaroof can stop her she jumps in after her sister, although she cannot swim. Maaroof dives after them. Islanders arrive; Maaroof brings Dhooma ashore unconscious and she is taken to hospital, while Maaroof, the police and many islanders keep diving for Hayaathu with no result.

At the hospital Waheed is furious and shouts at Zoona ("tonight Hayaathu will die by my hand"); Areesha, chatting on her phone, smiles. Backstory: Areesha begged Maaroof but he proposed to Hayaathu; she has always been jealous of Hayaathu's beauty, and she pushed her own proposal (the older man) onto Hayaathu by fake-crying to her parents. Now Dhooma's accident will be blamed on Hayaathu too.

On the shore Maaroof sits exhausted; friend Hassan tells him to be strong. Meanwhile, on the far side of the island, a young man (Fazaal — revealed in 274) brings the unconscious Hayaathu out of the sea, gives first aid; she coughs up water; his heart races. The coast guard and boats search for three hours. From the islanders' talk Maaroof learns why the sisters were crying and runs to the hospital, where he confronts Waheed ("aren't you ashamed?") and has to be held back. Areesha lies that Hayaathu wanted the marriage; Maaroof answers that Hayaathu is indeed "not like you", and Zoona pulls Areesha away. Police then bring in an unconscious girl — Hayaathu has been found.

Dawn, after Fajr: sleepless Maaroof by the mosque wall. Hassan tells him Hayaathu was found not in the sea but laid on the beach in front of the big house far from the village — "I don't think it was a human". Asad, who took the anonymous phone call, laughs at the rumour; people were seen staying at the big house last week. At the hospital Waheed refuses Maaroof entry: "bring 3 million"; he is only Hayaathu's father's half-brother. In her room Hayaathu, pale, is offered water by a nurse; Waheed's shout makes her drop the glass. He tells her she will marry Fazaal, "ugly or hideous, lame or blind" — his final decision.
