# Hayaath — Episode 275: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Recap: after Maaroof, Fazaal too dived in to save Hayaathu, and somehow the man who hated women began to feel tenderness and love for her. At sunset at the big house he gazes at her photo and smiles. A call from his friend Yasir: "Hayaathu has accepted the ring." Fazaal hangs up and breathes with relief: the first step is taken. He reflects: seeing Hayaathu's tenderness to her sister wiped away the hatred he had carried since his brother Fawaz's face was changed in an accident; life is given once, and he won't waste it on hatred. He walks down to the beach where, one night, he laid the half-drowned Hayaathu, and remembers her face in the moonlight. He means to keep his secret: for years his "drama" (introducing himself with the age and face of his brother, seven years older) made every arranged match collapse before a meeting. Now he wants the wedding as soon as possible.

Yasir calls again, stunned: "You and Hayaathu have met before, haven't you?" Fazaal won't say. Yasir reports that Hayaathu is asking for Fazaal's number, that she seemed to have cried a lot, and that something is wrong in that house; the uncle doesn't seem a good man. Fazaal will call her himself, but first Yasir should find out why she wants the number. Yasir calls back to tease him ("are you sitting next to some girl?"); Fazaal laughs.

In her room after the ring ceremony Hayaathu collapses on the bed: until yesterday she was Maaroof's, now she belongs to a stranger. She reads Maaroof's long messages and locks the door. Zoona knocks: Yasir has come, sent by Fazaal. Hayaathu says she is bathing, sits on the floor crying, afraid that any reply would bring punishment on Dhooma and Maaroof, and lies down on the floor. Zoona knocks again. A message from an unknown number: "This is my number — Fazaal." She replies curtly, then absent-mindedly writes and deletes many messages, thinking she is in Maaroof's chat, until Fazaal sends "?????". Relieved that nothing was sent, she reads: "I want to meet you... at nine Yasir will pick you up." Dread: will she dare to look at "that face"? The clock ticks towards nine. Dhooma knocks; Hayaathu hurriedly sends a message and lets her in, then sees it went to Fazaal; she deletes it and sends it to Maaroof instead: while out with Yasir to meet Fazaal, she will secretly meet Maaroof (luckily Fazaal hasn't read it). Dhooma asks why, then, she accepted the ring; Hayaathu has no answer. Zoona calls: they've come for her. Hayaathu hugs Dhooma — "I won't take a wrong step, I just want to see Maaroof once" — and leaves. Alone, Dhooma, who went into the sea for her sister's happiness, raises her hands and prays that the man coming into Hayaathu's life will be kind and love her beyond measure.
