# Hayaath — Episode 272: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Night. Hayaathu, an orphaned young woman, sits on her bed sobbing, asking why life keeps punishing her — is it her fault her parents died? Even crying is treated as a crime in her uncle's house. Her elder sister Dhooma, who has used a wheelchair since the car accident 12 years ago that killed their parents, rolls in. Hayaathu hugs her; the cousin Areesha has humiliated her again. She doesn't want to say more and pushes Dhooma out "for some air".

In the living room the uncle's wife Zoona, playing a phone game, sneers that once Hayaathu "leaves" (is married off) Dhooma will go nowhere. Areesha mocks Hayaathu: forget Maaroof, get ready for "the Beast" — "you're Beauty, after all". Zoona pretends to scold her but smirks too.

On the road, lost in thought, Hayaathu tries to cross a busy road; a car brakes hard just in time. The headlights throw Dhooma back into the memory of the accident; both sisters scream. A handsome young man (~25) gets out and shouts at them, then — hearing their conversation and getting a phone message — leaves. Dhooma, panicking, stammers (she stutters when frightened) that if they go home Hayaathu can't meet Maaroof.

At a quiet, secluded beach on the island (where Hayaathu meets Maaroof, always with Dhooma), backstory: uncle Waheed keeps them only for appearances and for the property their father left — the sisters believe the bank took it for debts. He was forced to educate Hayaathu by the grandfather's will, and cannot touch Dhooma because of it; everything falls on Hayaathu. Waheed has decided to marry Hayaathu to the man who was meant for Areesha — older, half his face burned in an accident — because Hayaathu once said looks don't matter. If she refuses he will stop Dhooma's treatment. Hayaathu weeps with her head on Dhooma's lap; Dhooma silently resolves that tonight she will make a decision so her sister is not sacrificed for her disability.

Maaroof arrives. Dhooma rolls away to give them space. Hayaathu begs him to marry her / run away with Dhooma. He refuses to elope — he will raise the 3 million rufiyaa condition and take both sisters out with dignity. Before he finishes, they hear something fall into the sea. (Cliffhanger — implied Dhooma.)
