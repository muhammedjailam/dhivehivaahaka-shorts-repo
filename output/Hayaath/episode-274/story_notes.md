# Hayaath — Episode 274: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
In the hospital, after Waheed's ultimatum, Hayaathu closes her tear-filled eyes — was she born only to be the prey of hatred? The nurse comforts her; Hayaathu explains she jumped in to save Dhontha (Dhooma) and is told her sister is fine.

At home she locks herself in her room: Waheed has forbidden her to see her sister, to see Maaroof, even to go to college. Areesha bangs on the door and hands back her phone — "Prince Fazaal" will call soon. Areesha mocks her: she has sent the photo of Fazaal to Maaroof and to Hayaathu's classmates; "Beauty and the Beast". Ordered to keep the door unlocked, Hayaathu looks at the photo, is frightened by the face and throws the phone aside, sobbing. The phone rings — an unknown number, silence on the line. Waheed appears: get dressed, the witness for the ring ceremony is coming. She sets the phone aside (the call is still open) and pleads; Waheed blames her "big mouth" for Areesha's broken engagement and says she must look at that face for life. To see Dhooma she agrees to everything. Zoona wheels Dhooma in. Dhooma, stammering, grieves that she couldn't give her sister a happy life; Hayaathu says she will marry Fazaal — he too is a victim of fate; if her eyes "don't see", ugliness won't matter. Waheed calls; she leaves with a bitter smile, the phone left on the table.

Fazaal heard everything. Reveal: Fazaal had been glad the marriage to Areesha fell through, because he never wanted to marry; he deliberately sent his late older brother Fawaz's photo. Fawaz's face was changed in a fire, his fiancée left him, and he died of the heartbreak — so Fazaal believes women love only looks and introduces himself in Fawaz's name. Flashback: he flew straight to Addu for a holiday; his car nearly hit the sisters (ep 272); he got out to shout but Hayaathu only cared about Dhooma; her face stunned him. His father's photo message confirmed she was his new fiancée. He followed the sisters listening, learned her heart was pure, and after Maaroof he too jumped into the sea to save her (ep 273 rescue).
