# Sector 7 — episode 385: story notes

## Synopsis
Night in Sector 7: toxic smoke, rusted scrap alleys, patrol drones sweeping red laser beams. Aira creeps through the
alleys with the black box from Junction-9 pressed to her chest, hides behind a steel pipe as a drone whizzes over her
head (if caught: prison for life, or worse), then runs on to the most isolated, ruined workshop of Sector 7 — part of an
old derelict factory — home of Zail, once the upper level's top scientist, exiled for opposing the government's
corruption. Three secret knocks; Zail peers out, pulls her in and locks the door.

Inside (wires, glowing monitors, strange machines) she sets the box on the table. Zail goes pale at its markings: it may
hold the radiation chart the government hides. The steel coating can't be forced; forcing it would trigger a
self-destruct that burns all the data. Aira paces, warning that soldiers will search every home. Zail silently plugs a
handmade device into the box: a red WARNING, a loud beeping, three attempts only. Zail (who wrote the original algorithm
20 years ago) types at lightning speed; the first attempt fails, screen red; on the second, the power cuts — in the dark
Aira reaches for her weapon; Zail pulls the backup-generator lever; with the final code: ACCESS GRANTED. The box opens:
15 years of live satellite data and footage of the surface — blue sky, green valleys, clean rivers, radiation readings
safe. Aira touches the green trees on the screen, a tear falls. Zail explains: the surface healed itself; every week
Marcus airs old recordings and fake visuals to keep the people in a prison of fear, obedient for scraps of food and
oxygen, slaves for the rich.

Then a red folder: PROJECT CLEANSLATE — a map of Sector 7 with red smoke spreading through the ventilation. On the
bunker's 50th-anniversary jubilee, after the upper level moves to the surface, neurotoxin gas will be released into
Sector 7's vents. The countdown: 7 days. Aira's legs give way; she sees the faces of children, elders and toiling families.
Zail slams the table in fury. Aira's fear turns to resolve: the whole bunker must see this — they will break into the
main broadcasting tower and hijack the Council's TV frequency. Zail's dead hopes revive; they need the secret resistance
in the tunnels. At that moment: heavy boots, metal detectors, red laser lights through the door cracks — "They've found
us!" Aira takes up her weapon. The seven-day countdown is now a war for their own lives.
