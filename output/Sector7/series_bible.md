# Sector 7 (ސެކްޓަރ 7) — series bible

Dhivehi audio drama, dystopian sci-fi. Five episodes in this batch form one continuous arc, in this order:
**365 → 385 → 389 → 406 → 454**. The cover (`work/episode-<N>-cover.png`) is the same series poster for every episode:
a vast underground bunker in cross-section, stone shaft with daylight at the top, a gold-lit luxury level in the middle,
rusted smoky machinery levels below. Match that look (see `style.txt`).

## World
Fifty years ago a nuclear war burned the surface. The rich and powerful built four giant bunkers; this one is
**"The Sanctuary" (އަމާން ބިންގަރާސް)**, two miles under the ground near Bremen, Germany. It is split into seven levels:
- **Level 1 "Udubburi" / the upper level**: rulers live in luxury — artificial golden "sun" light, fresh air, real fruit,
  glass walls, gold and white interiors, gardens. The **Council** rules from here; its leader is **Marcus**.
- **Sector 7**, the lowest level: a living hell of rusted steel, steam, toxic drips, dim flickering bulbs, pipes and
  hydraulic machinery. Poor workers do 16-hour shifts keeping the air/water systems for Level 1 running and eat
  "grey paste" rations that dull their minds. Patrol drones with red scanning lights fly overhead at night.
- Between them: corridors, ventilation shafts, the **Sector 1 main lift gate** (the only way up) inside a large storage
  complex.

The truth (revealed in 385): the surface healed fifteen years ago — blue sky, green valleys, clean rivers, radiation
normal. Marcus shows fake footage of a burning wasteland to keep everyone enslaved, and plans **Project Cleanslate**:
on the bunker's 50th anniversary, after the upper level moves to the surface, neurotoxin gas will be released into
Sector 7's ventilation to wipe everyone out. Countdown: **7 days**.

## Episodes
- **365** — Prologue: the war, the bunkers, the seven levels, life in Sector 7. **Aira** (26) works in the main engine
  room; a harsh Council overseer bangs a pipe and threatens rations; old co-worker **Bashir** tells her to keep her eyes
  down. On a break she holds the steel pendant her father **Malik** (an engineer taken to a "secret project" 13 years
  ago when she was 10) put round her neck, engraved "50.12.01". Sirens: the main oxygen pipe at **Junction-9**
  ("death zone") has burst. Aira puts on her old oxygen mask and toolkit, goes down alone through flooded, sparking,
  gas-filled corridors, welds the pipe, and finds hidden inside it a black military-grade encrypted box with her
  father's handwriting: "The truth is not buried." The pendant code is its key. She hides it in her toolkit.
- **385** — Night in Sector 7, drones with red lights. Aira sneaks with the box to the ruined workshop of **Zail**, an
  old exiled upper-level scientist. Three-knock code; he lets her in. Monitors, wires, machines. He warns of a
  self-destruct; connects a handmade device; red WARNING, 3 attempts; first fails, power cuts, backup generator, final
  code → ACCESS GRANTED. Live satellite footage: the surface is green and safe. Aira cries; Zail explains Marcus's lie.
  A red folder: PROJECT CLEANSLATE, gas into Sector 7 vents, countdown 7 days. Aira resolves to broadcast the truth by
  hijacking the main broadcast tower; Zail says they need the secret resistance. Boots and metal detectors outside, red
  laser lights through the door cracks — "They've found us!"; Aira readies herself.
- **389** — They escape through tunnels to an old steel warehouse: HQ of the resistance **"Revival"**, hundreds of
  young people lit by fire barrels and lamps. Charismatic leader **Brent** on a raised platform. He sees the evidence,
  smashes the table in rage and calls for revenge and killing the upper level. Aira argues for escape via the main lift
  instead. Revival builds crude devices (welding sparks, gas cylinders). Aira notices Brent has upper-level protein
  ration packs and walkie-talkies. Cut to the upper-level command centre: **Commander Kyle** watches the warehouse on
  hidden-camera screens, coffee in hand; secret file: Brent is an asset paid by Marcus to lead a "controlled uprising"
  into an ambush at the lift gate. **Marcus** calls Kyle on a red line: eliminate Aira and the box too. Back below, Aira
  finds a military communicator under Brent's desk: "AMBUSH READY ... NO SURVIVORS". Brent catches her and aims at
  her; Zail strikes him from behind, he falls. The vans roar off with the crowd towards the trap; Aira and Zail race to
  warn them.
- **406** — The vans speed to the Sector 1 lift complex. Aira and Zail take a ventilation shaft up and come out on a
  steel catwalk above the storage hall. The rebels blast the door open; inside is enormous upper-level waste: bottled
  clean water, red apples, basmati rice, real fish in fridges. The starving youths fall on the food weeping with joy.
  Aira spots the glowing blue "Level 3 central terminal" in a glass room (her chance to find her father's records).
  The only people on duty are unarmed lab and medical staff, cowering. Brent (bandage on his head) arrives and orders
  his men to kill them; they do (massacre). Aira grabs Brent; he strikes her and she falls. Then the skylights shatter:
  black-clad commandos rappel in, laser fire everywhere; rebels fall; Brent escapes through an emergency door with food
  and document cases. A wounded boy clings to Aira; she carries him on her shoulder but is surrounded by four
  commandos, struck down and handcuffed. Kyle walks out of the smoke and looks down at her. Zail is missing. She is
  dragged upward as a "valuable prisoner".
- **454** — Aira wakes in a clean, cold, white detention room on the upper level, unchained, at a glass table opposite
  Kyle (black uniform, silver medals). He shows her a tablet: CCTV of Brent sitting with top Council officers, bank
  transfers, the Cleanslate file: Marcus wants Sector 7 framed as terrorists so the upper level accepts the gassing.
  Aira demands who he is. Kyle opens his collar and pulls out a pendant identical to hers: his father was an engineer
  who worked with Malik; both were killed when Marcus learned their secret plan to lead everyone to the surface. Kyle
  wears the uniform to destroy Marcus's system from inside; arresting her was the only way to save her. He doesn't
  know what happened to Zail. Aira's view of good and evil turns grey. Trust is born. They stand on a high balcony of
  the upper level looking over the cold dark city: the final, most dangerous war begins.

## Binding content rules for this series (override the literal narration)
1. **No weapons visible, ever** — no guns, rifles, pistols, laser guns, knives, blades, bombs. Commandos/soldiers are
   shown in black armour with dark visors, hands empty, holding flashlights, riot shields or restraints. "Aira readies
   her weapon" → she grips a heavy wrench / her toolkit. Brent aiming a gun → his menacing face and outstretched empty
   hand / Aira raising the communicator defiantly. Rebels "making bombs" → welding sparks, hammering metal sheets, gas
   cylinders stacked (no wires/fuses/explosives).
2. **No violence, injury, blood, death or bodies.** Massacre of staff (406) → frightened white-coated staff huddled
   against a wall, shadows on the wall, a dropped clipboard, overturned crates and spilled apples; then the reaction
   on Aira's horrified face. Laser fire → abstract streaks of red and blue light and smoke filling the hall, silhouettes
   ducking and running away from camera, nobody hit. Brent striking Aira → Aira on the floor, hand near her mouth,
   furious eyes; no wound, no blood. The wounded boy → a frightened boy in a grey hoodie clinging to her, no wound.
   Arrest → Aira kneeling, hands behind her back in restraints, armoured figures around her (no weapons). Zail hitting
   Brent (389) → a heavy metal pipe clattering on the floor beside a dropped communicator / Zail breathless in the
   doorway. Nuclear war (365 intro) → distant orange glow on the horizon under ash clouds over an empty ruined skyline;
   no mushroom cloud, no people.
3. **Modesty**: Aira always in her ankle-length coat and hijab fully covering hair and neck. Any woman in a crowd
   wears a hijab. Men in long sleeves/jackets and long trousers.
4. **No touching** between Aira and any man (Zail, Brent, Kyle are non-mahram): no handholding, no hand on shoulder,
   no carrying her; keep an arm's length gap. Aira grabbing Brent's arm (389/406) → she steps in front of him, pointing
   / shouting. Kyle's hand on her shoulder (454) → he stands a respectful step away. Malik's farewell embrace (365) is
   father–daughter: allowed as a gentle hug with the 10-year-old girl.
5. **No readable text/numbers/symbols anywhere** (screens show glowing charts, maps, green landscapes, abstract red
   warning colour; never letters, digits, radiation symbols or logos). Countdown → "a red glowing panel".
6. Excluded always: alcohol, smoking, gore, horror creatures, idols/crosses. Coffee cup is fine.
7. Ethnicity: per the master prompt all characters have South Asian / Maldivian features (cards already set), even
   though the bunker is in Germany and some names are Western. Do not change it per episode.
