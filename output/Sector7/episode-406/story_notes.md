# Sector 7 — Episode 406: story notes

## Synopsis
The Revival vans roar through Sector 7 towards the Sector 1 main lift gate, a hundred-plus young men shouting with joy,
unaware that the only way to the lift runs through the heavily secured storage complex where Commander Kyle's soldiers
have laid a death trap. Aira and Zail (who has just knocked Brent down, end of 389) chase after them on foot. Aira reads a
secret chart on the captured military communicator: ventilation ducts lead straight up to the storage hall. They pry open
a duct and climb, Kyle's message ("AMBUSH READY ... NO SURVIVORS") circling in Aira's mind. They reach a grille above the
main door just as the vans stop; the youths fix charges to the thick steel door — 3, 2, 1 — the door is blasted open and
they storm in through the smoke, with Brent (bandaged head) behind them. Aira and Zail kick out the grille and land on a
steel catwalk above the hall. Below: the upper level's wasted abundance — bottled clean water, red apples, basmati rice,
fish in refrigerators. The starving youths fall on the food weeping with joy. Aira spots the glowing blue "Level 3 central
terminal" in a glass room — her only chance at her father's records — but Zail notes the guards are only unarmed lab and
medical staff, cowering against a wall. Brent strides in and orders them all killed; his men massacre the staff while the
youths recoil in horror. Aira jumps down and confronts Brent; he strikes her to the floor ("This is revenge!"). She
realises from his calm, cunning smile that he planned the trap with Kyle. Then the skylights shatter, black-clad commandos
rappel in, laser fire everywhere; the youths become prey. Brent escapes through a secret emergency door with the best
food crates and document cases. A wounded boy clings to Aira; she carries him on her shoulder but four commandos surround
her, knock her down and cuff her. Kyle walks out of the smoke and looks down at her with a mocking smile. Zail is missing.
Aira is taken upward as a valuable prisoner; an eerie silence falls over the sector.

## Tone
Rush and hope -> awe at the abundance -> horror and betrayal -> chaos -> bleak defeat and silence. Lighting moves from amber
work lights and headlights in Sector 7, to clean white/gold light in the hall, to red/blue streaks and smoke, ending in
cold grey-white haze and the white light of the lift gate. Ambience: bunker_machinery -> corridor_drip -> vent_shaft ->
storage_hall -> chaos_hall -> storage_hall.
