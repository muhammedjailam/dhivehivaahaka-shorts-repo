# Sector 7 — Episode 389: story notes

## Synopsis
Fleeing the soldiers through the back door of Zail's workshop and the narrow bunker tunnels, Aira and Zail reach an
abandoned-looking steel warehouse at the bottom of Sector 7 — secretly the headquarters of the resistance "Revival".
Inside, hundreds of starving, bitter young people gather around fire barrels; on a raised platform stands their
charismatic leader Brent. Aira pushes through the crowd; Brent takes her and Zail into a side room and sees the
evidence (Project Cleanslate, the 7-day countdown, the green surface). He slams the table in rage and calls for
storming the upper level and killing the rich. Aira steps in and argues for escaping together through the main lift
instead; Brent brushes her off, goes back out and whips the crowd into a frenzy of revenge.

The warehouse becomes a crude workshop — welding sparks, cut steel, gas cylinders turned into homemade bombs. Aira
watches with growing unease and tells Zail this road leads to a massacre; Zail agrees. She notices Brent resting with
upper-level protein ration packs, walkie-talkies and devices no one in Sector 7 could own, and her suspicion is born.

Cut to the cold, quiet command centre on the upper level: Chief Security Commander Kyle watches the warehouse live
through hidden sensors, sips coffee and opens a secret file: Brent is a paid asset of Council leader Marcus, tasked to
lead a "controlled uprising" so the soldiers can wipe everyone out "to keep the peace" before the gas. Marcus calls on
the red line; Kyle reports that Brent will bring everyone to the lift gate into heavy artillery. Marcus orders that
Aira and the encrypted box be eliminated too. Kyle, studying Aira's face on the monitor: "There is no salvation for
you."

Below, the vans are loaded amid chaos. Aira slips to Brent's desk and finds a military communicator magnetised under
it: "From Commander Kyle — ambush ready at the Sector-1 elevator gate. Bring them all. No survivors." Brent appears in
the doorway, his friendly mask gone, and threatens her; she confronts him with the message; he sneers that Sector 7
has no future and he wants to live above with the rich. As he is about to shoot, Zail strikes him from behind and he
falls. Outside, the vans roar off with hundreds of youths towards the trap. Aira pockets the communicator and she and
Zail race through a secret shortcut to reach the lift gate first.
