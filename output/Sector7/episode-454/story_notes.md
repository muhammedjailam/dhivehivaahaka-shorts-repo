# Sector 7 — episode 454: story notes

Duration: 708.7 s (11 min 49 s). 73 shots, 25 beats, 19 new images, 6 in-episode reuses.

## Synopsis
Aira wakes after her arrest (end of 406) in a clean, cold, white, steel-panelled detention room inside the most secret
building of the upper level. Gunfire and her friends' cries still echo in her ears; the smells of Sector 7 are gone. She
expects chains but her hands are free; she sits in a soft chair at a glass table. Opposite sits Security Chief Commander
Kyle — immaculate black uniform, silver medals — whose cruel look from Sector 7 has given way to a deep calm. Aira
accuses him of bringing her here to gloat after slaughtering Sector 7. Silently he lays a tablet before her: secret CCTV
of Brent sitting with the Council's top officers, and bank transfer records. Brent was not just bought: on Marcus's
direct order he stirred up the uprising so the youths would be led up into a bloodbath. Kyle opens the "Project
Cleanslate" file: Marcus will paint the lower levels as terrorists to the upper-level public, then seal Sector 7's doors
and gas everyone with their consent; Brent was his pawn. Aira's mind freezes. "Then who are you?" After a long sorrowful
silence Kyle stands, opens his collar and pulls out a pendant identical to Aira's — a childhood token of the bond
between their families. His father was an engineer who worked with Malik building the bunker; both were killed when
Marcus discovered their secret plan to free everyone and lead them to the sunlit surface. Aira remembers faintly her
father's stories of another engineer he met in secret, and realises Malik's death was no ordinary raid but Marcus's
crime. Why then the uniform? Looking out of the glass wall at the city, Kyle says the system can only be destroyed from
inside; he was harsh in Sector 7 because Marcus's spies watched him; arresting her as a "valuable prisoner" was the only
way to save her. Aira asks about Zail: Kyle does not know — he could only save her — and Cleanslate starts in a few hours.
Aira's black-and-white world turns grey: Brent, born in the slums, sold hundreds of lives for comfort; Kyle, in the
Council's most feared uniform, risks his life to free everyone. The real war is between humanity and greed. Kyle asks
for trust: they have one chance. Aira's despair becomes resolve; trust is born. Kyle promises no harm will come to her
while he lives, "get ready". On a high balcony the two look out over the cold dark city: the last and most dangerous
war begins.
