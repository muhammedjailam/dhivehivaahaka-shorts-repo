# Marufas — episode 514 — story notes

## Synopsis (English)
Continuing from 510's lunch: when Khalid relays Saeed's wish to sit in on a recitation, Ghassan turns cold. Nobody on
the island may hear of Yamna's "progress" (the sorcerer would strengthen the sihr) — the usual excuse of a fraud — and he
sneers at "Madinah University" people: he wants a real scholar like Saeed kept far from the house. After lunch he stands
and orders: tomorrow night is a big night, everyone in the room, and send another 20,000 rufiyaa to his account. The
narrator explains how the parents keep sacrificing money and comfort for "kamana", while Ghassan schemes to defeat the
sea maarid that controls Yamna on some days and bring her fully under his own ifreet.

The next night, after isha and dinner, the session begins. Yamna convulses, her eyes redden, and the maarid's heavy voice
fills the room — but this time it laughs and exposes Ghassan: he handed her body to his ifreet and every night, alone in
the room, robs her honour through it. Khalid and Faarish freeze; Ghassan recites faster: "A jinn's lie to divide us!" They
believe him; Faarish shouts back. The jinn mocks: they didn't believe it about Aadhanbe either — go now to Rannikamagu
beach, he is there with a bowl of sihr water ready to pour into the sea. Silence; Ghassan looks startled and afraid.
Faarish and Adheel run out into the 3 am dark. Under a huge beach tree an old man in a checked white sarong, white shirt
and cap sits murmuring over a strange bowl: Aadhanbe. "Stop!" He turns; the young men seize him, the bowl drops and the
black water soaks into the sand; Faarish is now 100 % sure. They gag him with his own cap, take him to a long-abandoned
house far from people and tie him to a chair. Faarish interrogates him; Aadhanbe, weeping, swears he never did sihr on
any child — Jaleel asked him to do something to bring bait fish to the island. Adheel, who has seen Yamna's state, thinks
nothing done to such a sorcerer is a sin. Remembering his little sister, Faarish strikes the old man with a stick; Adheel
gags him again; "Bro, let's go — we must check on the others." They leave him there and slip home through the dark lanes.
Ghassan, finishing the recitation, comes out worried: "Any proof?" — "Caught red-handed!" Khalid and Saahidha come out
furious; Saahidha: "Killing such people is no sin!" Khalid: "Where is Aadhanu now?" Faarish lies: "Exposed, he fled
screaming." His heart pounds. (to be continued)
