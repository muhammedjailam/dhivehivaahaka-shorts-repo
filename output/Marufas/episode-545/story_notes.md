# Marufas — episode 545: story notes

## Synopsis (English)
Continues directly from 544. In the abandoned house the starved, beaten old man Aadhanbe begs for "something to eat".
The word makes Faarish see his sister Yamna in the kitchen that morning, eating raw fish guts from the bin. Grinding his
teeth, he promises the old man "something tasty", strides home, scoops some of the raw guts Yamna left on the kitchen
floor into a bag, returns and flings them before Aadhanbe: "Eat! This is what my sister ate because of you!" He tries to
force them into his mouth; Aadhanbe thrashes, retches and vomits. Faarish and Adheel leave him half-dead, slam the door
and go.
At the hospital (they have showered, but their faces betray them) the ICU corridor is heavy with silence: Saahidha sobs
on a chair, head bowed; Khalid paces. Through the glass Yamna fights for her life among machines and a ventilator, the
heart monitor racing; doctors and nurses work round her bed; her organs are near failing and Faarish weeps. By
afternoon the machines quieten, the monitor steadies; the doctors say the poison is fading, though her body was already
weak from weeks without proper food. She wins; her parents weep with joy. But when she opens her eyes she is silent and
empty-eyed; Saahidha holds her hand and calls her; Yamna opens her mouth but no sound comes — her tongue is "locked",
she is mute; a single tear runs down her cheek. Faarish and Adheel enter: she lies like lifeless flesh, wasted,
dark-ringed eyes; she looks at Faarish with tear-filled eyes, lips moving, desperate to say something — no sound.
Faarish, certain Aadhanbe is the cause, red-eyed and trembling, walks out; Adheel follows without a word.
Back at the abandoned house, Aadhanbe lies half-conscious; Faarish lifts his face by the hair: "You made my sister unable
to speak — after today you will never say another word!" Adheel forces his mouth open and Faarish cuts out his tongue
with a blade from his pocket; they throw the blade down and leave him writhing in the chair without looking back.
Two days later Aadhanbe's family and the islanders begin searching; the police join, checking empty plots and abandoned
houses. A police team enters the lonely house with flashlights, opens the room and recoils: Aadhanbe is dead, slumped in
the chair he was tied to, his tongue beside him. The police seal the area; the news spreads. In the hospital room
Faarish's face shows nothing — he simply sits watching his mute sister. "He silenced the man who silenced his sister,
but the dark silence over their lives will never lift." (to be continued)

## Tone
The darkest episode of the arc: cold, silent, sickening dread around the abandoned house; fragile hope then quiet
heartbreak at the hospital; a numb, hollow ending. Charcoal and cold blue in the abandoned house, sterile
green-grey in the ICU, warm afternoon light for the relief, a single dim lamp for the final image.
