# Marufas — Episode 544 — story notes

## Synopsis
Night, Faarish's dim room. Faarish and Adheel lie awake on the big bed, whispering about what to do with Aadhanbe,
whom they left bound in an abandoned house (514). If it comes out, the island will explode. Adheel: they can't keep him
there forever; sending him off the island would bring the police. The plan: go to him at sunrise, claim they hold proof
of his sorcery and scare him into silence. Lights off; they try to sleep.

Next morning: golden light but a heavy silence. Saahidha sits on the living-room sofa, head bowed, eyes swollen, her tea
steaming untouched. Sleepless Khalid sits beside her and lays a hand on her shoulder; their eyes meet. Suddenly Yamna's
door bursts open: wild-eyed, shaking with rage, a non-human voice ("Get away, everyone!"). She smashes the crystal vases
and ornaments from the Italian marble table against the wall, wrecks the big TV, breaks the showcase glass with her
hands (bleeding, feeling nothing), rips the sofa cushions. Saahidha tries to hug her and is shoved to the floor; Khalid
cannot hold her. She runs to the kitchen: on these days she craves raw fish, so the family no longer keeps any. She tears
open the fridge and smashes its contents, throws down the coffee machine and blender, steel and clay bowls clatter,
opens every cabinet — and stops at the bin under the sink, where the morning's fish guts were thrown. With a terrible
smile she eats them while the family watches in horror; Saahidha retches and sinks to the floor; Khalid and Faarish
can't pull her away. Soon the food poisoning sets in, her strength fades, and she is rushed to hospital — critical.
In the corridor Faarish's grief turns to rage: Aadhanbe is the root of it all. Red-eyed, he grips Adheel's arm: "Let's
go — I won't leave that old man alive today." They stride to the abandoned house and fling the door open; the old man
is terrified. Faarish grabs his collar and strikes him, Adheel threatens; Aadhanbe, bleeding and weeping, begs to be let
go. Faarish only sees his sister fighting for her life. Starved and exhausted, the old man pleads: "I'm dying... a bite
of food... even a drop of water..." (to be continued)
