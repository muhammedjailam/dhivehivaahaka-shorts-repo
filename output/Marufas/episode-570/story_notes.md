# Marufas — episode 570: story notes

## Synopsis (English)
Continues after 545. News of Aadhanbe's terrifying death spreads across the island together with the story of Yamna's
rampage in the kitchen and her fight for life after eating raw fish guts; every street corner and teashop talks of the
family's calamity — some say it was Aadhanbe's sorcery, others suspect a hidden link between his death and Yamna's state.
In hospital Yamna lies mute, staring at the ceiling, tears showing a deeper, terrible secret in her heart.
A special police team investigates: forensic officers take fingerprints from the floor and the chair and take away the
blade found there; the body goes to Malé for a post-mortem (death from blood loss after the tongue was cut and from
injuries). The police turn to Yamna's family: witnesses saw Faarish and Adheel near the abandoned house the night
Aadhanbe vanished; phone records and CCTV are gathered. Officers question the two at the hospital ("Where were you?");
Faarish tries to lie calmly but stumbles; Adheel's anxiety shows. They are not arrested but warned not to leave the
island. Ghassan, hearing of the death and sure Faarish did it, fears for his own life and secretly flees the island.
Outside a shop Faarish overhears Jaleel telling people he had asked Aadhanbe to recite so bait fish would come to the
island — Aadhanbe was on the beach for the fishing, not to bewitch Yamna. The narrator explains the old island custom
(Aadhanbe used to pour water on children's heads, tie amulets, recite over new dhonis — now known to be shirk and
dwindling). Faarish's mind goes blank; though washed and clean, his hands are stained with an innocent old man's blood;
he retches and freezes.
At noon Khalid, buying food, phones Ghassan — no answer; at home Ghassan's room is empty. By afternoon he knows Ghassan
fled. He hurries to Saeed, now the island mosque's imam, hoping for help finding another raqi. Saeed asks which Quran
verses Ghassan recited; Khalid: "We never heard Allah's words — only strange, frightening sounds." Saeed is certain it
was sorcery: asking the mother's name and birth date, gibberish words, collecting hair and clothes, being alone with the
patient in a locked room — none of that is shar'i ruqyah. Khalid realises he handed his beloved daughter to a merciless
sorcerer and breaks down. At the hospital, unable to tell Saahidha, he takes Faarish out to the empty veranda and tells
him; every word stabs Faarish — he too trusted Ghassan; guilt makes his breath frantic and his body freeze. (to be continued)
