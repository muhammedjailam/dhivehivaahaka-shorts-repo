# Marufas — Episode 461 — story notes

## Synopsis
Continues straight from 453. Early afternoon: Yamna, not herself, storms into the kitchen, yanks open the freezer and
eats a whole frozen raw fish; the men who try to hold her are flung off with inhuman strength. She collapses on the
floor just as her elder brother **Faarish** arrives from Malé; horrified, he demands they rush her to hospital for food
poisoning. Before the ambulance arrives she throws everything up. After first aid she is sent home, strangely calm,
"like a doll with no life in it". An ominous sunset. Saahidha bathes her, dresses her nicely and feeds her — the only
calm moment of the last 24 hours.

After isha the family prepares for Ghassan's midnight recitation: Khalid, Faarish and **Adheel** will be in the room.
Saahidha hugs Yamna tightly, praying, leaves weeping, shuts the door and slides down to sit on the floor outside, back
against the door, knees drawn up. Inside: lights dimmed, Ghassan sits by Yamna's head murmuring unintelligible
commands. Yamna moans, then the heavy voice screams; Saahidha covers her ears. Sounds of breaking and of the men
holding Yamna; the room turns icy and the cold is felt even outside the door. Saahidha, head back against the door,
chin raised, prays silently through tears. Inside, the possession peaks; Ghassan demands "Who are you? Who sent you?"
The jinn laughs: "I needn't answer a wretch like you!" Ghassan threatens; a scream, her body rises off the bed; the jinn
begs "Don't hurt me!" After a long fight it gives in and names **"this island's Aadhanbe — that old man"**. Khalid,
Faarish and Adheel exchange looks of disgust: the jinn lied the night before. Faarish: "All lies! You're a liar!"
Saahidha outside thinks the same — the jinn wants to sow suspicion and blame an innocent old man. The jinn thrashes,
Ghassan subdues it with a last threat; Yamna faints, the cold fades. Khalid lays a blanket over her and asks Faarish to
open the door; Saahidha rushes in, hugs her and weeps. Yamna opens her eyes, looks from face to face, expressionless —
her gaze stops on Ghassan and a wide smile spreads across her face. (to be continued)
