# Marufas — episode 430: story notes

## Synopsis
Night, in Yamna's spacious marble bedroom. A terrifying deep voice comes out of 15-year-old Yamna ("Do you people think
what you're doing is so clever?"); everyone near her feels their skin crawl. The narration describes her body wrenched
backwards on the bed, blood-red eyes, foam, a deafening scream, fingers tearing the sheet. The room turns ice-cold and the
bulbs go out. Her mother Saahidha, sobbing, cries to Khalid to fetch Saeed; Khalid runs out into the night. Saeed, a
pious young islander, recites Quran and a short ruqyah; Yamna screams louder, convulses, then collapses into deep sleep.
Saeed: "This is not ordinary — even though I can recite a little, she should be shown to a proper raqi." After he leaves,
the parents sit by her bed; Saahidha strokes her forehead — could it be last week's high fever? Khalid stays silent,
remembering two weeks of strange signs: Yamna always ill, picking garden flowers at sunset and bringing them indoors.
The fajr adhan brings some calm; Khalid goes to pray.
Morning: Yamna wakes aching and remembers nothing; Saahidha doesn't tell her and secretly changes the torn bedsheet while
Yamna is in the bathroom. Yamna, in school uniform, has an important test, asks for tea, blames swollen red patches on an
allergy from takeaway food, and leaves laughing with friends while Saahidha watches, relieved. It is the calm before a
storm: at sunset the sky reddens, Yamna's smile vanishes and a worse attack begins, a heavy male voice screaming. Khalid,
sweating ("Saeed was right!"), phones a famous raqi from another island and sends a launch. Near midnight Ghassan arrives
(white shirt, black trousers, big black bag), whispers secretly with Khalid, then lays his palm on Yamna's forehead and
recites. Yamna thrashes with inhuman strength, flings Saahidha away, rakes her own cheeks, laughs in a man's voice:
"Stop reciting!" Ghassan questions the jinn: who sent you? With a mocking smile she turns to Khalid at the door: "One of
you sent me... it was YOU who handed this innocent girl over to us!" Saahidha hugs Yamna and weeps. The jinn threatens
worse; Ghassan, sensing he must prepare more, slows his recitation and Yamna sinks back. Saahidha glares at Khalid with
hatred: "That's not true," he says; "Get out right now!" Khalid walks out like a scolded child, Saeed after him. After
fajr, Saahidha tries to wake Yamna to pray; Yamna jolts upright, glaring: "What prayer? Go do your own thing — what's it
to me?... Get out of here right now!" Saahidha, shattered, slowly leaves the room. (to be continued)
