# Marufas — Episode 510: story notes

## Synopsis
About a month after episode 461. Night after night Ghassan recites, gives Yamna white cloths drawn with strange
letters and "blown-on" water, and nothing improves; yet with lies about an "extremely powerful sorcery" he has won the
family's total trust. He announces the last, most dangerous stage: the jinn draws strength from the family, so tonight
he must go into the room alone. The desperate parents nod. At 3 am, in an eerie cold, Ghassan takes his secret black
bag into Yamna's room and locks the door. The family (Khalid, Saahidha, Faarish, Adheel) waits on the living-room
sofas. After minutes of silence comes Yamna's own soft voice, not the jinn's: "Don't do this! Please... Mother!
Father! Save me!" — they believe it is her battle against the spirit. After an hour it goes quiet; Ghassan comes out
drenched in sweat, panting ("alone it fought hard... it will get lighter each night") and goes to his own room. Khalid
opens Yamna's door: she sleeps peacefully on her back, dried tears on her cheeks, white blanket neatly spread, a calm
innocent face; Saahidha weeps with joy. Khalid sends Faarish to take Adheel to the mosque for fajr, puts his arm round
Saahidha and goes to rest in a peace he has not felt for months.
The narrator reveals the truth: the calm sleep is a darkness from Ghassan's sorcery. On some days Yamna is silent and
mute, a prisoner of Ghassan's ifreet — she longs to call "Mother", to tell her what Ghassan does to her every night,
but her body has become a silent prison; only silent tears. On the other days the sea maarid controls her: foul words,
self-harm, eating rubbish and insects, never washing, a stench like a dried reef — and even Ghassan's ifreet cannot
overpower it; his nightly visits are to strengthen his ifreet so he can take full control of her body.
Noon: after the dhuhr prayer Khalid and Ghassan come home for lunch (fried fish). Khalid met Saeed outside the shop:
Saeed asked about "kamana" and, wanting to learn shar'i ruqyah, asked to sit in on the recitations. Khalid looks at
Ghassan for permission. (to be continued) Saeed is mentioned only — not on screen.
