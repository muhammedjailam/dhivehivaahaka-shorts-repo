## Synopsis
FRAME (night): the whole palace sleeps except King Shahryar, impatient for the rest of last night's tale. Dunyazad
whispers to her sister to go on; Shahrazad asks the king's leave and, smiling, continues.
TALE - the Merchant and the Jinni (continued): the giant jinni is about to strike the merchant who accidentally killed
its son. The merchant begs for one year to repay debts, return trusts, provide for his wives and children and say
farewell; he swears by God to return ("a Muslim's promise is worth more than his life"). The jinni agrees, threatens his
family if he fails, and sinks into the ground as smoke. At home joy turns to mourning; for a year he returns trusts,
pays debts, gives charity, frees his slaves, divides his wealth and writes his will. On the promised day he bathes,
takes his shroud, bids a heartbreaking farewell and rides back to the tree, where he prays and waits. A very old man
with a gazelle arrives, hears the story, praises his faithfulness and stays; a second old man with two black dogs does
the same. A sandstorm rises; the jinni appears, drags the merchant up and brings its blow down...
FRAME (dawn): the first golden light and the call to the dawn prayer; Shahrazad falls silent. The king begs to know
more; she promises a more wondrous tale tomorrow; he spares her again and goes to court. Cliffhanger.
