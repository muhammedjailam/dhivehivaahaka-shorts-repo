# Aliflaila — Episode 585 (Night 8) — story notes

Narration: 756.2 s (12.6 min), 83 shots. Plan: `pipeline/plan_aliflaila_585.py`.
Title card: "Chapter Eight: The Fisherman's Cunning and the Tale of King Yunan (Part Two)".

## Synopsis
Three story levels:
- FRAME (shots 1–6, 81b–83): Shahryar's bedchamber. Before dawn Dunyazad asks Shahrazad to continue; the king permits.
  At dawn Shahrazad stops; the king, eager to learn the secret of the four-coloured fish and the woman from the wall,
  spares her one more day (cliffhanger).
- TALE (Shahrazad → the Fisherman and the Jinni, shots 58–81a).
- TALE-WITHIN-THE-TALE (the fisherman → King Yunan and Sage Duban, shots 7–57), drawn with a soft golden storybook glow.

Yunan tale: the jealous vizier privately convinces King Yunan that Duban, who cured him, is a foreign spy who could kill
him as easily as he cured him. The king first defends Duban, then is gripped by fear and summons him. Duban arrives
hopeful, is accused and sentenced; he pleads ("spare me and God will spare you"), then accepts and asks for a day to
bid his family farewell, offering the king a wondrous book: after his beheading his head, set on a tray, will answer
questions as the pages are turned. The next day he brings the book; he is executed; his head speaks; the king licks his
finger to turn the stuck, blank pages, which Duban had smeared with poison. The king collapses; the head calls him a
tyrant and dies; the king dies seconds later.
Back on the shore the fisherman tells the jinni "your case is the same" — he will never free him. The jinni weeps,
swears by God's name to do no harm and make him rich; the fisherman lifts the lead stopper; the jinni re-forms from black
smoke, kicks the jar into the sea, smiles, and leads him over mountains and deserts to a crystal lake among four
mountains with white, red, blue and gold fish. Cast only once a day; take one of each colour to the king of the land.
The jinni splits the ground and vanishes. The Sultan pays 400 gold dinars. In the palace kitchen, while the fish fry,
the wall splits, a beautiful woman asks "O fish, are you true to your covenant?" — "If you return, we return" — she
vanishes into the wall and the fish burn black.
