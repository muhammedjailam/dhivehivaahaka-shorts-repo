# Aliflaila — Episode 551 (Night 7) — story notes

Duration 819.7 s (13 min 40 s), 88 shots. Plan: `pipeline/plan_aliflaila_551.py`.

## Synopsis
FRAME: The seventh night falls over King Shahryar's palace. In the last quiet hour Dunyazad asks her sister to finish
the tale of the poor fisherman and the jinni of the brass jar; Shahrazad bows and asks leave; the king says "Begin!"

TALE (seashore): The colossal jinni is bowed on the sand begging "Sulaiman" for mercy. The trembling old fisherman tells
him Sulaiman passed away 1,800 years ago and that he is only the poor fisherman who freed him. The jinni rises in rage,
roars like thunder and orders him to choose how he will die. The fisherman kneels and weeps: "is death my reward for a
kindness?" — "Yes."

TALE-WITHIN-THE-TALE (the jinni's own story): He is Sakhr, who grew proud over the other jinn and disobeyed the Prophet
Sulaiman; the vizier Asif ibn Barkhiya bound him and brought him before Sulaiman; he refused to obey and believe, so he
was shut in this brass jar, sealed with lead and the ring-seal bearing God's Greatest Name, and thrown into the deepest
sea. Century 1: he vowed riches to his rescuer; century 2: all hidden treasures; century 3: three wishes; century 4:
hatred — he would kill his rescuer, letting him choose only the manner of death.

TALE (shore again): The fisherman pleads for his wife and hungry little children; the jinni has no mercy. The fisherman
decides to use his God-given wits. He stops crying, accepts death but asks one question under an oath by God's Name on
the seal; the jinni trembles and swears. The trick: how could a giant who touches the clouds fit in this little jar?
"You lie — I only saw smoke." The jinni's pride is stung; he turns into a sky-covering column of black smoke that
swirls back into the jar ("Do you believe now?"). The fisherman leaps forward and slams the lead stopper shut.
Cliffhanger.

FRAME (dawn): Dawn light and the call to prayer; Shahrazad falls silent; the king sits up begging to know more; she
promises tomorrow night's even better tale. He spares her again, dresses and goes to court. The executioner's sword is
postponed one more day. "To be continued."
