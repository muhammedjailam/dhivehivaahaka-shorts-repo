# Aliflaila - episode 599 (Night 9) - story notes

## Synopsis
FRAME (shots 1-5, 0-45 s): in the last hour of night Dunyazad asks Shahrazad to continue the tale of the four-coloured
fish and the woman from the kitchen wall; Shahryar, curious too, lets her go on.
TALE (shots 6-62, 45-558 s): the cook faints when the fish burn to coal and tells the Sultan's Grand Vizier, who
does not believe it until he sees it. The fisherman catches four more fish; the vizier fries them himself; the wall
splits and a towering dark figure (head to the ceiling, a green branch in hand) asks the fish "do you keep your
covenant?"; they answer "if you return, we return; if you depart, we depart"; he overturns the pan, the fish burn, he
vanishes. The vizier reports to the Sultan, who sees the same thing a third time in the locked kitchen and is sure the
fish are bewitched people. The fisherman says the lake is half an hour away between four mountains - nobody knew of it.
The Sultan marches there with his army, camps by the crystal lake, and at night goes alone into the mountains. At sunrise
he finds a vast castle of black stone, its gate open; inside, splendour (silk, gold, jewels, gardens, fountains) but not
a soul. He hears heartbreaking weeping and verses, follows them to the innermost hall: a handsome young prince on a
golden throne, in tears, apologises for not rising - he lifts the hem of his robe: from the waist down he is black
marble. The Sultan asks his story; the prince says the fish were once living people and that this castle and land were
cursed by the wickedness and sorcery of his own wife.
FRAME (shots 63-70, 558-632 s): dawn; Shahrazad stops; Dunyazad asks why the wife did this and who the fish are;
Shahrazad promises the rest tomorrow night if she lives; Shahryar spares her one more day and goes to his court.
Cliffhanger ("to be continued").
