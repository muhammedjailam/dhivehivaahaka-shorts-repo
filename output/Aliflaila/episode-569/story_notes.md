# Aliflaila — Episode 569 (Night 7–8): The fisherman's trick and the tale of King Yunan

## Synopsis
FRAME (0–57 s): just before dawn in Shahryar's bedchamber, Dunyazad begs Shahrazad to continue the fisherman-and-jinni
story; the king consents. TALE (57–282 s): on the beach the jinni trembles at God's Name; the fisherman says he cannot
believe the jinni ever fit in the jar; the proud jinni pours back in as smoke and the fisherman claps on the lead lid
with Sulaiman's seal. Trapped, the jinni weeps; the fisherman carries the jar into the surf to throw it into the deep
and warn all comers. The jinni begs and promises riches; the fisherman laughs, compares them to King Yunan and the Sage
Duban, and at the jinni's request sits at the water's edge to tell that tale. TALE-WITHIN-THE-TALE (282–611 s): rich
King Yunan of Persia has leprosy that no physician can cure; the old polyglot sage Duban promises a cure without drink
or ointment, makes a hollow polo mallet filled with medicine; the king plays polo, sweats, bathes, sleeps and wakes
cured; he weeps for joy and showers Duban with silk robes, gold and a seat beside him. A black-hearted jealous vizier
plots Duban's ruin. FRAME (611–674 s): dawn light; Shahrazad stops, Dunyazad begs to know more, Shahrazad smiles and
promises the vizier's poisoning of the king's mind, Duban's tragic fate and the jinni's fate tomorrow; Shahryar spares
her another day and goes to court.
