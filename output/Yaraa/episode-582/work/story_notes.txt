# Yaraa — Episode 582 — story notes

## Synopsis
Directly continues 573. As Mifzaal and Naveen walk inland, a huge black cloud rolls over the jungle with a thunderclap.
At the jungle pond they shout Yaraa's and Shifza's names: no reply, not even footprints, no phone range. Mifzaal points
to the old taro field (kandoofa) beside the pond — Yaraa loves learning how Maldivians lived in the old days — but they
find only rustling leaves and tall grass clumps full of spider webs; neither dares go alone into the deep jungle.
Despair and questions pile up on a deserted island cut off from all help. The strange voice spins in Mifzaal's head and
his hands shake; Naveen stands frozen, hand on his head, and breaks down: his baby left this world without warning —
has Shifza left him the same way? Mifzaal puts a hand on his shoulder: "I know what a father feels when he loses his
beloved child." Naveen: "But you only just married — how would you know a father's feelings?" Mifzaal is struck as if
by 32 bolts of lightning; he dodges ("I've got signal"), goes to the shoreline and calls the coast guard, who promise to
come. Brief relief. Strong wind and heavy rain: the two huddle under a small tarpaulin shelter. A message tone — a
message from Yaraa's phone: a loving farewell (we were together on the beach a little while ago; I left hoping to come
back; longing for your gentle hands; what was to happen has happened; don't erase my memory or our resolve to make room
for a little child; married only three days; little hope I can come back; leave this island, go to Malé; tell Naveen
Shifu is fine; I will always love you). Mifzaal weeps, yet is relieved they are alive. They cancel the coast guard
request to search themselves, without thinking it through. Across huge waves they reach Malé at about 7 pm. The hoarse
voice again in his ear: "Ey Mifzaal, this is a new beginning; remember you must still come out under Yaraa's
protection." Is the voice behind the disappearance or is he imagining it? Naveen pats his shoulder: let's go home
tonight, check for news tomorrow. Naveen gets on his motorbike; Mifzaal tells him about the voice; Naveen's eyes widen —
and someone behind calls Mifzaal's name. He turns to see who. (Cliffhanger.)
