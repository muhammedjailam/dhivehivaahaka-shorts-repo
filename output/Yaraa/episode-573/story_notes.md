# Yaraa — Episode 573 (series opener) — story notes

## Synopsis
Opening line: "Even if not now, Yaraa, we will meet again — if it is meant to be." On a calm, cloudless morning of the
Eid al-Adha holidays, Mifzaal (a busy Male businessman, married to Yaraa only three days) takes his bride on a picnic to
a deserted island an hour from Male, together with Naveen and Shifza — a couple who have faced many trials and lost
their first baby. In the dinghy Yaraa sits by Mifzaal on the back seat holding his hands; Naveen teases her that she
will pull his fingers off; Shifza asks Mifzaal if he has nothing to say; he answers that he married Yaraa having given
his whole heart to "that queen", and everyone laughs.
They reach a beautiful island: a dazzling white beach, the interior overgrown into jungle. They leave the bags in the
shade on the beach ridge; Naveen and Shifza play in the sea while Mifzaal takes Yaraa to the island's tip. In the soft
sand she nearly falls, he steadies her. At the tip she runs ahead across the sand, spreads her arms in the wind and
beckons him; a joyful moment in the golden sun. Escaping the heat they rest in the shade of a magoo bush, where Mifzaal
voices a hope for the first time: next time they should bring a little child. Yaraa is speechless with joy.
Past noon Yaraa goes to fetch food from Shifza ("I'll be right back"); Mifzaal pretends to sulk. His closest friend
Faisal phones and teases him for forgetting his friends now that he has a wife; Mifzaal laughs, but something stings.
After the call there is still no sign of Yaraa. As he dials her, a woman's voice calls his name from behind — not
Yaraa's, not Shifza's, but a hoarse, scratchy voice. He turns slowly: only a huge black cloud and the empty jungle.
Yaraa's phone is switched off. He hurries back to the landing, feeling followed, glancing back. Naveen, by the dinghy,
is puzzled: the women went to the pond inland; the signal here is very weak. Mifzaal asks if anyone lives on the
island; Naveen laughs — it has been deserted for ages. Mifzaal keeps the voice to himself and both walk towards the
pond. (Author credit: Shail Abdurrazzaq.)

## Tone
First half: bright, warm, romantic and playful (sunny morning, golden noon, crimson against turquoise). From seg 41:
quietly unsettling horror built only from atmosphere — the black cloud swallowing the sun, empty paths into the jungle,
long palm shadows, Mifzaal's frightened face looking back. No music; hums only under the voice/fear passage.
