# Taubaa — Episode 287: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
A young man lives with his old mother in an ordinary little house. He spends most of his time in front of the TV,
staying up night after night watching films and dramas. He never goes to the mosque for the obligatory prayers, and
every time his frail old mother reminds him to pray he scolds and mocks her and ignores her. If guidance could be
bought she would give everything she owns to buy it for her only child — but she has only one thing: dua, made in the
last part of the night, "arrows that never miss their target". While he stays up watching bad scenes, she rises in the
last third of the night and prays for his guidance; a mother's love cannot be compared to anything.

One night, in the deep silence, she sits in her room with both hands raised, tears of grief running down her cheeks.
Suddenly a loud crash breaks the silence. Crying "My beloved son!" she runs towards the sound. In his room she finds him
smashing the TV with a mallet, saying it is the thing that kept him from obeying Allah and his mother and from his
obligatory prayers. Then he goes to her, kisses her head, presses her to his chest and embraces her. She stands
stunned, tears running down her cheeks — but now they are tears of joy. Allah answered her dua and guided her son.
Closing verse: Al-Baqarah 2:186 — "And when My servants ask you concerning Me, indeed I am near; I answer the call of
the caller when he calls upon Me." (The end)
