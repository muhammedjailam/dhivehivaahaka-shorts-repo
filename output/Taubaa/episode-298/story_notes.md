# Taubaa — Episode 298: story notes (Jawad)

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
The narrator introduces Jawad, a Saudi man who had fallen into many haram things (alcohol, zina, dancing) and loved
them; "let us look at his story in his own words". Jawad opens with a reflection: Allah is kind and generous to His
servants, gives them time but does not abandon them, His mercy surpasses His wrath; otherwise what would stop Him from
punishing whenever He wills — "when He wills a thing, He only says to it 'Be', and it is" (Yasin 36:82). Perhaps Allah
willed good for him; he always fears a bad ending and asks himself what stops him from praying.

His nafs and Shaytan were against him. He travelled from country to country — Greece, France, Holland, Canada,
America — about three years in America, where he remembers praying only one Friday prayer in a mosque. His sound
fitrah and upbringing called him to Allah, but Shaytan wiped Allah's remembrance from his heart and he sank into sin
without fear. In America he lived at a crossroads: religious friends kept visiting him, he admired their manners and
obedience and hoped to be like them — yet every weekend he went to discos and nightclubs. At first he didn't drink,
then fell into the trap: drinking, and with it zina.

He came home, married a kind, righteous girl from a religious family, took her back to America and secretly returned
to his old ways. Bad friends — themselves married men cheating on their wives — advised him to rent an apartment for
sin. His soul refused; he went straight to his wife: "At the first chance we must go back to Saudi, I can't bear it
any more." Friends tried hard to change his mind; he said no to every deviation, graduated with good results and came
home — yet kept the same life, travelling abroad as a tourist to drink and go to nightclubs.

About a year later, by Allah's decree, he found in his car an audio tape of Sheikh Ali Jaber: Quran verses and the
qunut dua. On the way to work one morning, as the dua began, he broke down crying like a child, could not drive,
pulled over at the roadside and sobbed. It was as if he heard Allah's verses and such a dua for the first time; his
whole being cried "Kill Shaytan and desire, O Jawad! Repent! Don't miss the chance!" He repented, begged
forgiveness, resolved never to return, asked for steadfastness, and cried that day as never before. Slowly his life,
appearance and character changed; he guards his prayers and is strong in worship. He asks Allah to accept his
repentance and to give him and everyone a good ending and steadfastness. (The end)
