# 16 February (16 ފެބްރުއަރީ) — Episode 285 — story notes

## Synopsis
Ahlam, the new boss, wakes at his desk in his resort office cabin from a nightmare (warm breath on his shoulder, a blinding
white flash) at 1:57 a.m. in a thunderstorm. With a thunderclap he hears a young woman scream "Mamma" — the same scream he
heard a week ago. He hurries into the dark open-plan office and sees, from behind, a young woman with her head on a desk and
her hands over her ears: Malak, shaking with fear, wiping tears, unaware anyone is watching.
Next morning, after the night's rain, the resort sparkles with dew. Malak, laptop in hand, greets Babu the gardener ("Not
going to the island at night either, madam?" — "Busy, very busy"). Ahlam steps out of his room on a phone call and watches
her pass. Ali tells him she is hard-working, works overtime and has not gone home to her island for a week; Ahlam orders a
staff room for her. Ali teases him; Ahlam turns stern, asks about "the watch I gave you" — Ali has searched every shop on the
nearby islands without finding that brand — and gives him seven days, or he will hand the job to someone who talks less.
In the canteen Malak reads a week of emails: her best friend Kiyaara (why did you miss Zain's birthday party?), her teenage
brother Naaif (urgently needs 1000 dollars, afraid to ask Kaif), and Zain ("Mamma has confirmed the wedding date... love
you"). Meanwhile Ahlam and Ali sit down nearby; Ali jokingly says Malak will be the secretary for the new resort project and
praises her beauty and single status; Ahlam glares but cannot stop looking at her. When Malak looks up at the sky to hold back
tears, Ahlam and then Ali look up too ("Is there something up there?"). Malak shuts the laptop and leaves before anyone sees
her cry; Ahlam wonders where he has seen her before. Walking back alone by a quiet path in the midday heat she shivers,
remembering Zain's betrayal and that terrifying night, keeps glancing behind her, stops at the souvenir shop's watch
showcase, looks at her own watch — and its hands seem to turn backwards: the glass shows her the truth of a week ago
(cliffhanger; the flashback itself is not in this episode).
