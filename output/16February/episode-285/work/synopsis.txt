16 FEBRUARY (16 ފެބްރުއަރީ) - EPISODE 285 - READER SYNOPSIS
Transcript length: ~13:50 (last line at 13:34). Story is told from three angles: Ahlam's view, Ali's teasing, and Malak's inner state.

=====================================================================
1. OVERALL SYNOPSIS (in order, with timestamps)
=====================================================================

SCENE A - Ahlam's nightmare in his office cabin, resort, ~2 a.m., thunderstorm (00:00-01:20)
- 00:00 A man (Ahlam, unnamed until 01:05) is in a dream. Just as he sinks into deep thought he startles at the feel of hot breath on his shoulder from behind. He turns, and a light blinds him so he can see nothing.
- 00:15 He wakes suddenly at his desk, panicked, with sweat on his face. He quickly drinks a whole glass of water in one go. Still short of breath, he tells himself it was only a dream.
- 00:35 He runs his right hand up through his thick, very black hair and wipes the sweat off his face with a tissue.
- 00:44 He checks his wristwatch and gets up from the desk. It is 1:57 a.m. Lightning flashes through the office window, and a huge thunderclap shakes the building.
- 01:01 With the thunder he hears a young woman scream "Mamma!" (Ahlam had been putting the room key card from his desk into his pocket.) He checks his watch again, unsure. He heard the same scream a week ago.
- 01:20 He grabs his phone and almost runs out of his cabin into the main office.

SCENE B - Malak alone in the dark open office (01:27-02:00)
- 01:27 He searches the office anxiously for whoever screamed. He stops when he sees, in dim light, a young woman slumped with her head on a desk, hands pressed to her temples/ears and eyes squeezed shut.
- 01:43 (Malak's POV) Malak was startled by the thunder. Even after three deep breaths her heart won't settle, and it races while the rain pours outside. She has no idea anyone is watching her as she wipes away the tears under her lashes. (Ahlam sees her from behind and does not see her face clearly.)

SCENE C - Next morning, resort garden path (02:01-03:04)
- 02:01 Sunrise after a night of non-stop rain. A cool breeze, fresh greenery, and dew on leaves and blooming flowers "like jewels" (narration about nature).
- 02:35 Malak, carrying her laptop, calls out "Babu!" to the gardener watering the plants along both sides of the path. She is walking to the canteen.
- 02:47 Babu, in broken Dhivehi: "Madam, not going to island at night too?" Malak: "Busy. Very busy." She hides her anxiety behind a smile and walks on. Babu smiles and keeps watering.

SCENE D - Ahlam and Ali outside the rooms / walking (03:06-06:15)
- 03:06 Ahlam comes out of his resort room on a phone call and stops to watch Malak pass. Ali stops beside him: "That's Malak. Didn't I mention her? A very hardworking, well-mannered girl."
- 03:27 Ahlam asks how late office work runs. Ali: until 7 at the latest, but Malak works overtime and even takes on other staff's unfinished work. Ahlam asks why the resort doesn't give her a room. Ali says she normally goes home to her island every night after work. For the past week, though, she hasn't gone home and stays late at the office. One day she was very ill and Ali put her in a guest room for the day. "But..." (he trails off).
- 04:12 Ahlam orders a staff-room to be arranged for her: "I don't want to lose hardworking people." He keeps watching Malak walk toward the canteen, greeting and smiling at everyone she passes. He still hasn't seen her face clearly.
- 04:36 Ali clears his throat teasingly. Ahlam snaps: "Did you note down everything I told you?" Ali: "Yes, noted." He wears a cheeky smile.
- 04:57 As they walk, Ahlam asks about "the watch I gave you". Ali hasn't found its source yet. He has checked every shop on most islands in this area and none carries that brand. Ahlam tells him to search all areas, not just this one: "It's very important to find the truth about that watch." It has become a headache for Ahlam.
- 05:34 Ali asks where the boss found the watch. Ahlam tells him not to talk so much and to do his job. He wants every detail of the watch within 7 days, or he'll give the job to someone who talks less. This is a deliberate threat. Ali, panicking: "No no boss, I'm on it." Narration says Ali loves this job, has spent his life at this resort, and is both hardworking and kind-hearted.

SCENE E - Resort restaurant/canteen, daytime, apparently open-air (06:17-11:50)
- 06:17 Malak sips a juice and checks her emails on her laptop. She hasn't opened them in a week.
- 06:30 First, an email from KIYARA. Malak shuts her eyes, reluctant and fed up, but reads it. Kiyara writes: "Hi Malak, still working? No holiday? I called a lot and your phone is off. What's going on with your phone? Please call. Missed you so much. Why didn't you come to Zain's birthday party? Zain is very sad. Are you angry with Zain? Zain says you don't pick up when he calls. At least message Zain, even if you're busy. Love you, miss you lots, bestie."
- 07:15 Malak squeezes her eyes shut harder. A tear falls and she wipes it quickly so nobody sees.
- 07:26 Second, an email from NAIF, her (younger) brother, who calls her "Don": "Where are you? I called your number and the office many times. I urgently need 1000 dollars. Please get it for me, I promise to repay in 2 months. I don't dare tell Dhonbe (elder brother) because of all the questions he asks. Mamma said to ask you. Please, Don." Malak frowns and sips her juice.
- 07:58 Ahlam and Ali walk into the restaurant, sit at a table and look around. Ahlam spots Malak, takes off his glasses and looks at her carefully. Last night in the office, and just now outside, he only saw her from behind.
- 08:17 Ali, jokingly: "That's Malak, secretary to the boss of the resort now under construction." Ahlam: "When was that arranged without my knowledge? You can change my secretary just like that?" Ali says he'd go too, but Malak knows the most about that resort project, and she's hardworking.
- 08:44 Ahlam: "Will a woman suit an active construction site? Only men work there." Ali says women work there too. Malak would go through Admin to the project office as the boss's secretary, would not need to go out on site, and would do whatever the boss assigns. Ali smirks.
- 09:14 Ahlam: "Are you teasing me?" Ali: "Who am I to tease? Chances like this are rare, think of it as luck. Malak is famous for her beauty on her island too. And she's single, she's not that close any more with the boy she was seeing." Ahlam glares: "Did you come here to work, or...?" Ali: "Sorry boss, I won't say it again. You're single, I was just trying to find you a good girl. Sorry."
- 09:58 A waiter comes and Ahlam orders. When the waiter leaves, Ahlam settles back and looks at Malak again. Ali notices and smirks again. Ahlam's attraction to Malak is plain on his face.
- 10:18 Malak, muttering bitterly to herself about Naif: "It's only 'Don' when they need something. Otherwise they forget I exist. Nobody even asked why I don't come home. Need money, and it's 'Don' this, 'Don' that." She closes Naif's email.
- 10:35 Third, an email from ZAIN. Malak hesitates: "Should I open it? Should I see what Zain says?" She looks more desperate than before. She tilts her head up to the sky to stop the tears.
- 10:52 Comic beat: Ahlam also looks up at the sky with her, and Ali follows Ahlam's gaze up: "Is there something up there?" Ahlam glares at Ali silently, then looks back at Malak.
- 11:09 Malak widens her eyes to hold back tears, waits until she is calm, then opens the email. Zain writes: "Malak, where are you? What happened to your phone? Call me. Mamma has now confirmed the wedding date. There's so much to talk about. Love you, miss you."
- 11:30 Tears run. She holds them back only for a few seconds, then bows her head, shuts the laptop and hurries off before anyone sees her cry.
- 11:44 Ahlam, to himself: "She looks so much like a girl I've seen somewhere... where?"

SCENE F - Malak on a quiet path, then at the souvenir shop window, midday heat (11:50-13:50)
- 11:50 Hugging her laptop to her chest and wiping tears, Malak walks back toward the office. She takes a quiet, empty path instead of the one she came by.
- 12:00 Despite the night's rain it is now hot under a strong sun, yet she shivers. Zain's email reminded her of his unfaithfulness, and with it "that terrifying night".
- 12:23 Every step shows fear. She keeps glancing around, checking whether someone is following her, and holds her breath. Even in daylight she feels surrounded by darkness and dread.
- 12:47 She stops at the glass front of a souvenir shop beside the path. Inside is a big glass showcase of modern branded wristwatches. The ticking of their hands echoes in her ears.
- 13:08 She slowly raises her arm and looks at her own wristwatch. It feels as if its hands start turning backward. The scene blurs and time seems to stop.
- 13:26 What she sees in the shop glass is not the present but the truth of one week ago. Her mind is pulled with the ticking second hand back into "that terrifying moment". (CLIFFHANGER: the flashback itself starts next episode. Nothing from it is described here.)
