## Synopsis
The night of 16 February, part 1. At dusk (Maghrib) a ferry dhoni brings Malak home to her island under gathering rain
clouds. Her motorbike has been taken from the jetty — she sees it vanish round a corner — so she walks the long dark
tree-lined main road for over an hour (Isha) to reach Zain: it is his birthday, and she came to answer the question he
asked before she left for the resort. At the abandoned half-built guesthouses she hears a familiar voice; a man falls
from above and dies at her feet. A stranger (the bible's Ahlam; "Azaan" in the narration; here only a faceless
silhouette) pulls her into hiding as police sirens approach, begs her to keep quiet or they'll both be blamed; she
struggles, then nods. "Good girl." Drizzle. Hurt and wet she lets herself into Zain's dark apartment — a cut birthday
cake on the table — and through the closed bedroom door hears Zain with Kiyaara, her best friend since age 8: an affair.
She leaves unseen and runs into the thunderstorm. Next sunny morning at her parents' house: sleepless Malak, Vimla
sweeping and bickering with Aanis about the neighbour Aashiya and about Malak never marrying; Malak comes out in
sunglasses, snaps back; Vimla notices her forehead and swollen lips; police walk into the yard and Malak relives the night.
