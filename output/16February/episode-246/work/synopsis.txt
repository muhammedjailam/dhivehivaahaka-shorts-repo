16 FEBRUARY (16 ފެބްރުއަރީ) — EPISODE 246 — READER SYNOPSIS
Transcript length: ~11:35 (last line at 693s). Narration-heavy, with dialogue in two scenes.

==========================================================================
1. OVERALL SYNOPSIS (in order, with approximate timestamps)
==========================================================================

SCENE A — Ferry arrives at the island at dusk (00:00 – 00:52)
- 00:00 Dusk is falling; a dhoni/ferry approaches the island. Daylight heat gone, cool damp air,
  white clouds giving way to dark rain clouds. Dense trees around, sea waves stirred by the wind,
  engine sound coming closer.
- 00:37 The ferry docks at the jetty (faalan) as the Maghrib call to prayer sounds. Passengers
  slowly disembark.
- 00:44 Malak gets off carrying a paper bag (and a shoulder bag) and walks toward the parked
  motorbikes.

SCENE B — Missing motorbike, long walk on the dark main road (00:52 – 02:09)
- 00:52 Her motorbike is not where she parked it. She spots it turning a corner four lanes away
  (someone is riding it off) and runs to stop it, but it is gone.
- 01:03 She stands at the corner, frowns, adjusts the bag on her shoulder and walks on along the
  road. Narration: this time she will not let "that person" off (implies someone habitually
  takes her bike).
- 01:16 She walks very fast — she is late to get to Zain. She came to the island tonight only for
  Zain: to celebrate his birthday, and to give her answer to the question he asked her before
  she left for the resort (implied marriage proposal).
- 01:33 Heart racing: rain is coming and the road is dark (unlit). Eerie sounds, damp cold wind.
  It is a main road (bodu raasthaa); she keeps thinking how to find the bike. Swaps the paper
  bag between hands.
- 01:59 Isha call to prayer — she checks the time on her phone; she has been on that road for
  more than an hour. Growing anger.

SCENE C — Abandoned guesthouse construction site: the fall and the stranger (02:09 – 04:38)
- 02:09 She reaches some half-built guesthouses. Construction materials piled to one side; work
  stopped long ago. The place is now a hangout for the island's young people ("if a kid goes
  missing you'll find them there"). Recently there was an "incident" there. She is frightened,
  tells herself it's just paranoia; she has been here before but never so late.
- 02:55 She hears people talking — one voice sounds familiar — and walks toward it.
- 03:01 Before she gets there a man falls from above and lands near her feet. She screams.
  Blood comes from his nose and mouth; he closes his eyes and dies (03:15).
- 03:17 Someone clamps a hand over her mouth from behind and pulls her back, hiding her from the
  others' sight. A male voice whispers "Hush." Police sirens can be heard approaching.
- 03:37 Malak is crying in fear and trying to pull the hand away; he keeps dragging her backward.
- 03:48 He whispers: "Please don't shout. The police are coming. I need your help, please. If you
  shout we'll both be arrested — the blame for that man's death will fall on you too."
- 04:05 She hits him with the paper bag and her handbag, struggling to escape. "Please..." he
  pleads, holding her tight and backing away.
- 04:25 Unable to escape, she finally nods "yes". The man — named here as AZAAN — slowly removes
  his hand (04:30). A light drizzle is falling. He whispers "Good girl." (04:34)

SCENE D — Zain's apartment: the betrayal (04:38 – 08:22)
- 04:38 Malak quietly unlocks the apartment door (she has a key) and enters. Sitting room is
  completely dark. She shuts the door and leans on it, puts the key in her bag.
- 04:54 Her condition: blood at her lip/mouth, forehead slightly grazed/bruised, dress dirty, hair
  dishevelled, partly wet. Her bag drops to the floor. Eyes closed, she shivers remembering the
  night, hand on chest, starts crying; resolves never to go that way again.
- 05:27 She hears a woman's voice from inside the bedroom — a girl talking softly. Surprised, she
  walks toward the bedroom door and stops beside it.
- 05:54 She notices a cut birthday cake on the small sitting-room table.
- 05:58 Dialogue overheard through the door (Zain and Kiyaara, not seen):
    Zain: "Hmm... at my birthday last year." (answering a question)
    Kiyaara: "That was me, right..." / Zain: "Hmm."
    Kiyaara: "Then what does Malak do for you?" — Zain doesn't answer.
    Kiyaara (bored): "Tell me. Why didn't you tell? What is Malak for?"
    Zain: "Kiyaara, why bring that up now, don't talk about Malak..."
    Kiyaara: "I'm amazed. How do you manage it? Zain is so patient."
    Zain: "I won't go that far with her without marrying her — Kiyaara knows that well. She's not
           a girl like Kiyaara. That's exactly why I like her."
    Kiyaara: "So boring. Go to that boring woman then."
    Zain laughs: "Jealous?" (07:04)
  (Strong implication that Zain and Kiyaara are in an intimate affair.)
- 07:06 Malak slides down to the floor, drained, biting her lip to stop sobbing aloud.
  Narration: Kiyaara is her closest friend and keeper of her secrets; they studied together
  since age 8; all three (Malak, Kiyaara, Zain) did O-levels and A-levels at the same place and
  went abroad for higher education together.
- 07:32 She covers her ears; still sobbing. 07:39 Gets up, picks up her fallen bag, opens the door,
  takes a last look inside, closes it, leans on it with eyes shut. The two people closest to her
  heart have betrayed and deceived her.
- 08:11 A flash of lightning and loud thunder startle her; rain now heavy. She wipes her tears and
  runs off down the road, vanishing into the dark and pouring rain (08:22).

SCENE E — Next morning at Malak's parents' house (08:26 – 11:33)
- 08:26 The heavy night rain has stopped; morning sunlight bathes the island and streams into
  Malak's bedroom. Sunlight on her face; she lays an arm across her face. She doesn't know when
  she fell asleep; she woke at Fajr, prayed, tried to sleep again but couldn't.
- 08:54 She sits up. Her mother Vimla's voice is heard in the house/compound. Malak rubs her face;
  terrible headache.
- 09:05 Vimla, broom (ilolhi-fathi) in hand, is sweeping. Aanis comes in from outside and hands
  her a bag: "Here, something Aashiya gave." Vimla: "Hmm — so I did tell them about the rat
  burrow in their tree, didn't I." (pulls a face).
- 09:32 Aanis ignores her, lies on a joali (rope swing chair), reads the news on his phone — he
  doesn't want to argue. Vimla sits on the adjacent joali: "Why don't you say anything? Upset
  that I quarrelled with Aashiya? That's fun." Aanis: "Why would I be upset? You two can keep
  bickering — nothing's lost to me."
- 10:03 Vimla: "Of course — when I have to carry everything... Everything is the same to Aanis.
  Even now I'm wondering when that Malak will ever marry someone."
- 10:19 Malak, coming out of the house putting on sunglasses: "Don't think about that. I'll marry
  when I need to."
- 10:28 Both parents look toward the door. Vimla (surprised): "When did you come, dear?"
  Malak (irritated): "What, can't I come to my own home?" Vimla: "I'm not saying anything to make
  you angry — when Mum went to bed you hadn't come, and you never said you'd come at night."
- 10:56 Malak considers her answer — if she says she came for Zain's birthday her mother's
  complaining will never end.
- 11:07 Vimla (worried): "What happened to your forehead? Your lips are swollen too." Aanis gets
  up from the joali and looks at her.
- 11:16 Police officers walk into the house/compound. Malak steps back. Flash of last night: the
  man falling dead from the guesthouse, her panic, racing heart, dry throat — and her run through
  the dark among dense trees in the rain "to save her honour and dignity" (11:33, end;
  cliffhanger).

  forehead, dirty clothes, messy hair, partly wet. Next morning: swollen lips, forehead mark,

VIMLA (ވިމްލާ) — id: vimla
