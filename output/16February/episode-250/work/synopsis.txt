16 FEBRUARY (16 ފެބްރުއަރީ) - EPISODE 250 - READER SYNOPSIS
================================================================
Transcript length: ~12:50 (0s - 765s). Narrated prose with dialogue.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERALL SYNOPSIS (in order, with timestamps)
----------------------------------------------------------------

00:00-00:15  MALAK'S MEMORY (flashback of last night, inside her head).
  Malak remembers the man who fell dead from the top of the guest house;
  fear shows on her face, her heart races. She remembers running through
  the darkness, through pouring rain and dense wild bushes, "to save her
  honour" (implies she escaped an attempted assault).

00:15-00:38  HOME, MORNING - KAIF ARRIVES.
  A young policeman (Kaif) walks into the family house, takes off his
  police cap and fixes his hair - tall, decent height, fair-ish complexion.
  Vimla lights up happily when she sees him; Aanis also smiles warmly.
  Malak takes a deep breath and starts walking out, feeling unwanted.

00:45-01:04  Vimla calls "Malak, dear, wait - Mum still has something to
  say." Malak comes face to face with Kaif. He looks at her with concern
  and notices her SWOLLEN LIP and a BRUISE on her forehead.

01:04-02:15  INTERROGATION-LIKE QUESTIONS.
  Kaif (casually): "What time did you get home last night?"
  Malak: "Kaif doesn't need to check that."
  Kaif (softly): her motorbike was seen last night somewhere it shouldn't
  have been. Malak looks worried. Vimla doesn't understand what they're
  talking about. Aanis sits back on the joali (rope swing-chair) reading
  news on his phone.
  Malak (indifferent): her bike was stolen last night; she reported it to
  the police - doesn't he check reports? Kaif (right hand in pocket, cap
  in left hand): when did she find out, why didn't she call him? Malak:
  after she came back from the resort the bike wasn't by the jetty, so she
  reported it. "Do I have to call Kaif every time something happens?"
  (angry, harsh). Kaif sighs, closes his eyes.

02:25-03:15  Kaif explains gently: last night someone was found murdered;
  her bike is known to have travelled in that area; police will come
  tomorrow to question her - so tell him everything, hide nothing.
  Malak: "If there was anything to tell, I'd tell. Nothing to hide."
  Narration: she is lying and scared about it; she stays silent so as not
  to drag her mother/family into the worry - and above all she does not
  want Kaif to know or to share anything with him.

03:17-04:00  Aanis (lying on the joali, reading phone news): "Son Kaif,
  the one killed on this island last night isn't from this island, right?"
  Malak seizes the moment and walks quickly out; Kaif stops her firmly:
  "Malak, wait. I haven't finished." She can't go; he tells her to come
  back inside. Kaif to Aanis: "Yes Bappa, not from this island - but known
  to be the child of an upper-class/high family. Whose, the name hasn't
  been released yet."

04:01-05:05  NAAIF QUESTION.
  Kaif: "Aunt (Daththa), where's Naaif?" Vimla: "Sleeping." Kaif: "Did
  Naaif go out last night?" Vimla (too quickly): "No, no - Naaif came home
  very early and slept." Aanis glances at Vimla - he seems to know she is
  lying. Aanis: "Son, why are you looking for your little brother?" Kaif:
  just checking, haven't seen him in days. Vimla (warmly): have tea before
  you go, it's been so many days since you came home. Malak looks at her
  mother; her mother's warmth toward Kaif doesn't sit well with her;
  disappointment on her face. Kaif: not now, Daththa, heading to the
  station; will come another time; tell Naaif I came looking, ask him to
  call. "Bappa, I'll go now, I'll come back later."

05:07-05:41  Aanis: "Son, on your way drop Malak at the jetty - I think
  she hasn't even had tea." Kaif doubts she'll go with him; Malak, against
  her will, agrees, to keep peace at home. Kaif notices her face change
  but won't refuse his father. Says "come" and walks out.

05:41-07:03  BROKEN PHONE.
  Malak (leaving): "Bebe, I'm going. Mamma, I'm going. Don't call my
  phone, it's broken." Kaif's mind: another "mystery" - bike seen where
  the man died, phone broken, marks on forehead and lip - all tell a story.
  Vimla, surprised: phone bought only last month, why broken so soon?
  Aanis dismisses it: "Vimla... things break. That girl buys things with
  her own work. Let the two kids go quickly or Malak will miss the resort."
  Vimla shakes her head and folds her arms. She wanted to put medicine on
  Malak's forehead wound but knew the stubborn girl would refuse. Malak
  has changed a lot; the old closeness is gone; mother and daughter have
  drifted apart. Vimla sighs.

07:03-08:31  MOTORBIKE RIDE TO THE JETTY.
  Outside, Kaif sits on his motorbike; Malak won't get on: "Go, Kaif, I'll
  take a taxi." Kaif: "I won't eat you, Malak. Get on. Calling a taxi takes
  time, and the launch leaves at a fixed time." She reluctantly sits behind
  him. He starts the bike, watches her in the mirror - she looks fed up.
  Kaif: "What happened to your lip?" No answer; he repeats. Malak: "Fell."
  "Fell? Where?" Malak: "I don't have to answer every question you ask -
  you're not my brother or my father. Not my boyfriend either." Kaif is
  hurt, sighs: "I know. I ask because I worry about you." Malak: "Better if
  you don't worry."

08:31-09:48  JETTY - KAIF ALONE.
  At the jetty Malak gets off and walks to a launch; Kaif notices she is
  LIMPING slightly - certain something big happened to her last night.
  She boards; at the same moment the ferry she came on last night is also
  leaving the harbour. Kaif sits on the bike until the launch vanishes.
  Narration/backstory: Malak is his stepmother's daughter; they grew up
  together. Close friendship, fun, hours-long phone calls - all stopped.
  Slowly she pulled away; living in the same house they became strangers;
  on the road she bows her head and walks past as if not seeing him; his
  check-in messages get short careless replies; she pushed him out of her
  life without warning. His phone rings; he answers, says "on my way", and
  hangs up.

09:50-10:48  RESORT - SOUVENIR SHOP.
  The launch nears the resort pier; Malak disembarks with other staff and
  heads, without looking at anyone, toward the office where she works.
  Walking slowly, she suddenly stops at the souvenir shop. Through the
  shop's dim light she stares at the big glass showcase. The shop
  attendant ("seyt kujja" - shopkeeper boy, likely South Asian) is wiping
  the showcase with a cloth; seeing Malak staring from outside the glass,
  he freezes.
  10:32 MICRO-FLASHBACK (Malak's memory): a modern branded wristwatch being
  taken out of that showcase and placed into a black box - vivid, as if it
  just happened.

10:48-11:58  MIZOO.
  Someone taps her shoulder from behind - Malak screams "Mamma!" in terror.
  "It's me," says her friend Mizoo. Malak, hand on chest: "Sorry!" (shaky).
  Mizoo, touching her back: what happened to your forehead, your lip is
  swollen too. Malak: "a small accident last night" - and rolls up the
  sleeve of her LONG-SLEEVED KURTA: her arm is badly BRUISED. Mizoo (tsk):
  "Ya Rabbee... let's get it treated - why didn't you go to hospital?"
  Malak: you know Mum, she'd make a huge deal, and Kaif would be there
  backing her up; didn't want to stress Mum.

11:58-12:45  OFFICE - THE RED FILE (cliffhanger).
  After treatment at the resort clinic, the two come to the office area;
  Mizoo goes to her post at the front office. Malak enters her office and
  sits at her desk. On it lies a RED FILE with nothing written on the
  outside. Curious, she opens it: inside is a HANDKERCHIEF soaked in FRESH
  BLOOD, still wet; a foul smell spreads. Eyes wide, she flings the file
  from her hand, gasps, heart pounding, breath short - she tries to scream
  but no sound comes out. END.
