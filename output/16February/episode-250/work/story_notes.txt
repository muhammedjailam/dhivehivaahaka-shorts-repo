## Synopsis
The morning after the night of 16 February. Opening memory (0:00-0:16): Malak remembers the man who fell from the
guesthouse and her flight through the rainy dark "to save her honour". At the family house, Kaif — police officer,
Aanis's son, Malak's stepbrother — walks in, takes off his cap; Vimla and Aanis are delighted, Malak tries to slip out.
Kaif notices her face and asks when she came home: her motorbike was seen last night near where a man was found
murdered (a son of a wealthy family, not from this island); police will question her tomorrow. Malak claims the bike was
stolen, snaps at him and lies that she has nothing to hide. Kaif asks Vimla where his teenage brother Naaif was last night;
Vimla answers too fast and Aanis notices. Aanis asks Kaif to drop Malak at the jetty; Malak says her new phone is
"broken" — another mystery for Kaif; Vimla grieves how distant her daughter has become. Malak first refuses the
motorbike, then rides behind him; she tells him he is not her brother, father or boyfriend; he is hurt. At the jetty he
sees her limp onto the staff launch and remembers how close they used to be. At the resort, Malak freezes at the souvenir
shop's watch showcase (Ubey inside freezes too; memory of the watch put in a black box); Mizoo taps her shoulder and she
screams "Mamma!"; Mizoo sees her injuries and takes her to the clinic. At her desk a blank red file holds a handkerchief
soaked in fresh blood; she flings it away and cannot scream.
