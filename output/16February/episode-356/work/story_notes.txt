## Synopsis
- 0:00 Present: Malak stares through the resort souvenir shop's glass watch showcase; the ticking pulls her into memory.
- 0:19–1:58 Memory (a week before 16 Feb): she buys an expensive men's watch "for someone very special"; Ubey teases her
  about the price, boxes it, says he leaves for Malé tonight and then India for a month (his mother is very ill).
- 1:58–2:31 Present: "16 February — a black date that changed my life."
- 2:31–4:06 Flashback, night of 16 Feb, rain: she runs on the dark road clutching the watch bag, trips, the bag flies away,
  dry leaves crunch — a faceless figure comes at her out of the dark.
- 4:06–4:52 Present: Ali calls her name (she startles) and hands her a key — the boss has arranged a place for her to stay
  on nights she can't go home. A meaningful smile.
- 4:52–8:57 Home-island jetty, morning: Zain chases her from his motorbike; she says she can never be what he wants; in
  her heart she despises him for "what he did that night"; he has arranged their ring ceremony on her birthday; "I did come
  on your birthday — on 16 February." She boards the staff launch; Zain is left bewildered.
- 8:57–10:45 Ahlam (before dawn, his villa) opens a box holding the same watch; memory from his side: he hid the screaming
  girl from the "evil people" after the murder, she bit him, threw a phone, fled, fell; he picked up her bag.
- 10:45–11:25 His alarm rings; "If I hadn't met her that night I wouldn't be alive. If only we could meet once."
- 11:25–12:48 Narration: publicly businessman "Ahlam Umar", only grandson of resort owner Hassan Waleed; secretly a
  high-ranking defence-force officer / undercover agent on an unfinished mission.
