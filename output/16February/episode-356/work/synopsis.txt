1. OVERALL SYNOPSIS (in order, with approximate timestamps)
======================================================================

The episode is built around one night, 16 February, which changed the
lives of two strangers: MALAK (heroine) and AHLAM (hero). It has two
halves: Malak's half (0:00-8:50) and Ahlam's half (8:56-end).

--- PART A: MALAK ---

[00:00-00:19] PRESENT. Malak stands looking through the glass window of
a souvenir shop (ސުވެނިޔާ ފިހާރަ). The ticking of a clock pulls her
mind into a memory from "a week earlier".

[00:19-01:58] FLASHBACK (about a week before 16 Feb; daytime, inside the
souvenir shop). Malak carefully inspects an expensive branded wristwatch
(men's watch, a gift). The shop attendant UBEY teases her: "Are you sure?
It's a very expensive brand." Malak says she would spend all her savings
on it; Ubey knew she would buy it. Ubey jokes "for someone very special,
huh? We don't get that kind of luck." Malak shyly confirms: "a very
special person." Ubey hands her the watch; she strokes it lovingly ("it
will look perfect on his wrist"). She declines gift wrapping. Ubey puts it
in its box, in a nice shopping bag, and runs her card. Ubey mentions he is
leaving for Malé tonight and won't be back for about a month - his mother
is very ill and must go to India for treatment. Another (unnamed) shop
attendant enters. Malak wishes his mother a quick recovery and a safe
journey, and leaves.

[01:59-02:22] PRESENT. Malak's bitter inner monologue: "How lovingly I
bought that watch... my whole life turned upside down. 16 February became
the black, dark date that changed my life." She whispers "16 February".

[02:23-04:00] FLASHBACK - NIGHT OF 16 FEBRUARY (rain, dark wooded road).
Clutching the shopping bag with the watch, Malak runs in terror along dark
tree-lined roads, looking back over her shoulder, "as if fleeing a
bloodthirsty hunter". Her hair is soaked by rain and plastered over her
face. She trips on a stone and falls face-down; the bag flies from her
hand. She cries "Mamma!" in pain, pulls up the sleeve of her kurta (ކުރުތާ)
to look at a wound on her arm, touches her aching mouth/lip and sees blood
on her fingers. Dry leaves crunch among the trees - someone is coming fast
toward her. She scrambles backward. Out of the darkness emerges the young
man who had tried to hold her (i.e. Ahlam - see Part B); in the dark she
cannot tell who he is. He comes straight at her...

[04:01-04:52] PRESENT (cut on the flashback's cliffhanger). "Malak!" - a
hand on her shoulder: it is her colleague ALI. She jolts, gasping for
breath. "Malak, it's me! Calm down - what's wrong, did you see something
scary?" She silently shakes her head. Ali holds out a door key: "From now
on the boss (ވެރިޔާ) has arranged for you to stay here at this end/place
on nights you can't get back to the island." He says it with a meaningful,
knowing smile. Malak silently takes the key.

[04:52-08:56] PRESENT (different scene, day; Malak's home island, jetty
and harbour). With a small travel bag on her shoulder Malak gets out of a
taxi near the jetty. ZAIN arrives on a motorcycle, stops, and strides
toward her. Seeing him, she worriedly grabs her bag and hurries toward the
launch (ލޯންޗު) in the harbour. Zain runs after her: "Malak! Wait!"
 - She stops, sighs, turns; her face clearly shows she does not want to
   talk to him.
 - Zain (hurt, clueless): "Why are you running from me? Did I do something
   wrong?" Painful memories flash in her mind. With a reproachful smile she
   says the fault isn't Zain's - the real culprit is herself.
 - Zain: "Say clearly what you mean!" Malak: "Sorry Zain, I can't talk
   now. I can never be the kind of person you want." Her face shows deep
   disgust - the hatred and anger of having given her heart to a
   faithless man.
 - Zain: "Are you leaving so you won't come back to the island?" She nods,
   and makes an excuse: a big project to finish means she must spend most
   of her time on "that island" (the work island). Narration: only her
   conscience knows she is really fleeing a bitter past.
 - Zain: "Won't you even come for your birthday?" She shrugs. Zain: "Even
   when you didn't come on my birthday I didn't complain." Malak inwardly
   seethes: "Complaining is for the noble! Have you forgotten what
   disgusting act you were doing that night? Not an atom of shame?"
 - Zain: "But you must be on the island on your birthday. I've already
   arranged a ring (engagement) ceremony that night." Her head throbs - she
   does not want to marry anyone yet, least of all Zain. "I think it's too
   soon. I need to think a lot... I'm going."
 - She walks a few steps, stops, turns: "On the night of your birthday I
   DID come... on the sixteenth of February." Then she quickly boards the
   launch. Zain stands stunned: "She came to the island on 16 Feb - and
   went where?" The launch departs with Malak.
   (Implication: Zain's birthday = 16 Feb; the watch was his birthday gift;
   Malak came secretly that night and saw Zain doing something shameful.)

--- PART B: AHLAM ---

[08:56-09:15] PRESENT (Ahlam's room/lounge with sofa and table; time of
day not stated - a phone alarm later suggests a scheduled
time/morning). AHLAM sits staring at a small box in front of him, slowly
opens it: inside is the expensive branded wristwatch (the SAME watch Malak
bought - it was in the bag she dropped). Putting it in his hand stirs deep
feeling. "The night that changed my whole life... sixteenth of
February..."

[09:19-10:45] FLASHBACK - NIGHT OF 16 FEBRUARY, AHLAM'S POV (rain, dark,
among trees). He remembers an unknown girl he met that night. When she
screamed in fear, Ahlam clamped his hand over her mouth - with the noble
intention of saving her life. He didn't know who she was, but was sure she
had no part in the MURDER (ގަތުލު) that had just happened there; if "those
evil people" had known the girl was there, she too would have become their
prey. He managed to lead her only a short way: before he could explain,
she BIT his hand and ran, and also threw a phone at him. Ahlam picked up
the phone and ran after her again. Coming out of the trees, he found the
girl fallen on the ground (= Malak's fall in Part A); in the dark he could
not make out her face. He stepped forward to help, but ignoring
everything he said she got up and ran. He picked up the shopping bag
lying on the ground, looked inside, stared after her, and with the bag ran
in her direction again.

[10:45-11:19] PRESENT. His phone alarm wakes him from the memory. He turns
it off, strokes the watch: "If I hadn't met this girl that night, I surely
wouldn't be alive today. Where will I find her? If we could meet just
once, I'd tell her everything that happened on 16 February and why."
With a deep sigh he puts the watch back in its box, sets it on the table,
and leans back on the sofa.

[11:19-12:50] NARRATED BACKSTORY (exposition, no dialogue). Ahlam came to
"that island" not only as the grandson of the RESORT owner but with
weighty duties on his shoulders. Publicly he is famous businessman "Ahlam
Umar"; but the stars shining on his chest in national service mean more
to him. His grandfather HASSAN WALEED's greatest hope was that his only
grandson would carry on the family business. Ahlam did his higher
education in the field his grandfather wanted, then, in the name of a
holiday, left the family for six months (ހަމަސް = ހަ މަސް, likely military
training); the family allowed it hoping he'd return to the business. He
returned instead as a loyal son serving the nation - his own decision, so
even Waleed couldn't easily oppose it. His link with the national defence
force (ގައުމީ ދިފާއީ ބާރު - MNDF) is kept secret for a special purpose:
outwardly a young man running his grandfather's business, in reality a
secret agent/spy holding a high military rank. No one may know his true
identity. The end of the big mission he has started is still ahead.
(Episode ends.)
