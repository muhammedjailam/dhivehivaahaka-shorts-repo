# 16 February — Episode 284: story notes

## Synopsis
Duration 811.2 s (13:31). Full detail with timestamps in `work/synopsis.txt`.

- 00:00 Resort back-office, day: Malak opens a red file and finds a handkerchief "soaked in fresh blood". She flings the file away, tries to scream with no sound coming out, and faints out of sight.
- 00:25 The file lands at the feet of a young man who has just walked in. It is Ahlam, the resort owner's grandson, arriving unannounced as acting boss. He picks the file up and sees nobody.
- 00:49 Ali, the secretary, comes in with his iPad. Ahlam jokes about files flying instead of rose petals. Ali is flustered: he expected a GM sent by Waleed. Ahlam teases him ("expected a female GM?"), and Ali fumbles an excuse.
- 02:22 Ali looks at the red file and is ashamed he missed Ahlam at the jetty.
- 02:40–03:48 The narrator gives a long portrait of Ahlam: principled, witty, sharp, polite and brave.
- 03:48 Mizoo finds Malak collapsed by her chair and calls Rishwan. Ali scolds the crowd, and Rishwan carries Malak to a staff room.
- 04:46 In the staff room Ali phones Zuhoo, the medic. Mizoo covers Malak. Zuhoo checks her pulse and eyes and asks why Malak wasn't taken to the clinic ("Ali.").
- 05:35 Malak wakes with a headache and an IV. She asks about the bloody handkerchief, but Mizoo knows nothing of it.
- 06:37 Mizoo makes her rest and teases her: Ali sent juice, the new boss is the old man's smart grandson, and Rishwan trails him "like a tail".
- 07:25 Malak ignores her, haunted by the handkerchief. Mizoo leaves.
- 07:53 The death site, more than a week later. Kaif and his team are still looking for answers. A young officer says he blacked out that night and woke in the woods. Someone runs toward the stairs unseen. Walking the way Malak went that night, Kaif finds an earring and recognises it: "one piece of evidence from 16 February".
- 09:49 Kaif's room, rainy night. Malak reads his message but doesn't reply.
- 10:30 Flashback: they were inseparable friends until he confessed his love. She refused him: "I have never seen you that way... our friendship will be lost." She drifted away.
- 12:11 Present: Kaif cries over her photo as thunder cracks.
- 12:31 The resort office after midnight in the storm. Ahlam is working alone. At 12:59 he leaves his cabin and hears an unknown sound. He feels warm breath on his shoulder, spins round and is blinded by a flash of light.
