1. OVERALL SYNOPSIS (in order, approximate mm:ss)
====================================================================

SCENE A — Resort admin office, daytime (00:00–03:50)
- 00:00 Malak, alone in the resort office, opens a (red) file and freezes: inside is a handkerchief smeared with
  FRESH BLOOD, still visibly wet, and a foul smell spreads through the room. Eyes wide, she flings the file away.
  Heart pounding, short of breath, she tries to scream but no sound comes out (she then collapses/faints out of
  view — she is later found lying fallen beside a chair).
- 00:25 At that exact moment the door opens and a young man walks in; the thrown file lands at his feet. He bends
  slowly, picks it up, gathers the papers that flew out, and looks round the office — he sees nobody (Malak is
  down, out of sight).
- 00:45 Someone enters behind him: Ali, the office secretary, holding his iPad against his chest. The young man
  hands him the file.
- 00:57 Young man (joking): he expected a grander welcome — rose petals flying as he walked a red carpet — but in
  this office it is files that fly. Ali is taken aback, not immediately sure who he is, then: "Ahlam. When did you
  arrive?" — uneasy; Ali had expected a manager sent by Waleed.
- 01:30 Ahlam: "Didn't you know I was coming? Didn't Grandpa tell you?" — Ahlam is the grandson of the resort's
  owner. Ali: "He did, but he said a new GM was coming, not that Ahlam would arrive today." Ahlam teases: "The GM
  will come, but right now I'm here. What, Ali doesn't like it that I came? Expected a female GM, didn't you?"
  Ali denies it and fumbles an excuse ("if you'd mentioned you were arriving today, I would have...").
- 02:19 Ahlam laughs and strides further inside. Ali looks thoughtfully at the red file in his hand, then at Ahlam;
  he is embarrassed that he failed to meet Ahlam at the jetty — on Ahlam's very first day Ahlam saw Ali's
  carelessness.
- 02:40–03:45 Narrator's character portrait of Ahlam: responsible young man with firm work principles; serious and
  deep-thinking in formal moments, but lights up with a matchless smile among friends; warm, humorous, a very
  sharp mind behind the jokes; puts justice and respect first; empathetic, gentle-spoken and polite; courage and
  determination visible in his eyes.
- 03:48 Ali takes a deep breath and follows Ahlam.

SCENE B — Office: Malak found collapsed (03:53–05:13)
- 03:53 Mizoo: "Ya Rabbee!" She puts the water bottle in her hand down on the desk and tries to lift Malak, who is
  lying fallen next to a chair. She calls loudly: "Rishwan!" — Rishwan is just coming in, hurries over: "What
  happened?" Mizoo: she came to ask Malak something and found her fallen.
- 04:20 Ali arrives: "What are you all crowding in here for? The boss is around. Everyone back to work." Mizoo:
  "Malak has collapsed." Ali (surprised): "Malak collapsed?" ... "Don't stand staring at my face — pick Malak up."
- 04:39 Rishwan carries Malak in his arms, following Ali, who heads toward the staff quarters/staff rooms.

SCENE C — Staff room (resort staff quarters), daytime (04:46–07:52)
- 04:46 Ali opens a staff room; picks up the room phone and dials: "Zuhoo." Tells Zuhoo to come to the room, hangs up.
- 04:58 Rishwan lays Malak on the bed; Mizoo covers her with a sheet/blanket. Ali: "Mizoo, stay here with Malak.
  Rishwan, go to the boss." Rishwan and Ali leave.
- 05:13 Zuhoo arrives: "What happened?" (Mizoo: "Found her collapsed.") Zuhoo checks Malak's pulse at the wrist and
  checks her eyes. "Why bring her here instead of the clinic?" Mizoo: "Ali." Zuhoo: "Hmm." (knowing Ali's ways).
- 05:35 (later — Malak now on an IV drip) Malak opens her eyes: splitting headache, her hand hurts; she slowly sits
  up holding her head and sees an IV line attached to her. Mizoo comes in: "Alhamdulillah. You OK?" sits on the
  bed edge. Malak (hoarse): "Where am I?" Mizoo: in a room, Ali had her brought here; Zuhoo says she fainted from
  not eating for too long.
- 06:08 Malak: "Where's that file? There was a handkerchief soaked in blood in it. Is that the kind of prank people
  should play?" — still frightened, a chill runs over her; she remembers throwing the file but nothing after.
  Mizoo is confused: "File? Handkerchief?" (Mizoo knows nothing about it.)
- 06:37 Mizoo: don't talk, lie down and rest — Ali has given Malak leave; lucky Ali thought of it. Malak lies back.
  Mizoo (laughing): Ali sent juice and things — told everyone she "fell ill at the office".
- 06:54 Malak (eyes closed): "Ill, my foot. I was thinking the boss would turn up soon and we'd get to see Ali's
  antics." Mizoo: whatever antics he pulls, Ali is very loyal, and he's so good to Malak. "The boss has arrived —
  but not the old man this time, his grandson. A very smart lad. When I went to fetch your bag I saw Rishwan
  trailing at his heels like a tail." (Mizoo laughs.)
- 07:26 Malak isn't listening — she keeps seeing the bloody handkerchief. Whose prank/doing was it? Mizoo: "Rest,
  I'm off. What shall I bring you to eat?" Malak waves no. Mizoo leaves; Malak closes her eyes — her health turned
  suddenly after seeing that handkerchief.

SCENE D — Death scene investigation, daytime (07:53–09:48)
- 07:53 A chalk/drawn outline marks where a man who fell from a height and died had lain. Kaif and his (police)
  team examine the area. He scans the objects with a questioning, worried look. More than a week has passed and
  still nobody knows what happened there that night — what led to the man's death? Whose negligence? Kaif sighs
  and shakes his head.
- 08:28 A young police officer comes and stands before him. Kaif (harsh): "Where were you? What happened that
  night?" Officer: "I don't know what happened either. When I came to, I was lying in the woods/bush." Kaif has no
  time to judge whether it's true; waves him away.
- 08:51 Kaif keeps inspecting; steps out onto the road and looks at the spot where the man fell. Suddenly the sound
  of someone running toward the stairs — Kaif turns: "Who's there?" No answer. His team carries on working.
- 09:13 Kaif looks further off and walks slowly in the direction Malak went that night. He spots something
  glinting on the ground, crouches, picks it up, wipes it with a tissue from his pocket, holds it up: an EARRING
  (a piece of one). Shock on his face — he clearly recognises it (implied: Malak's). He clenches it in his fist:
  "One piece of evidence from the incident on 16 February." (09:43)

SCENE E — Kaif's bedroom, night, storm + FLASHBACK (09:49–12:30)
- 09:49 Kaif lies in bed trying to sleep but sees the earring before his eyes — is it the answer to his question?
  He gets up, leans against the headboard with a pillow on his lap; sleep has deserted him.
- 10:10 He picks up his phone, thinks, types a message, sends it, waits. The message is "seen" but no reply. He
  lets his head fall back against the headboard; it hurts to think how far Malak has drifted from him.
- 10:29–12:10 FLASHBACK / backstory (narrated, with one line of Malak's dialogue): Kaif and Malak had an
  inseparable friendship — couldn't go a day without seeing each other, supported each other through every joy
  and sorrow, shared everything. Over time Kaif's feelings became love. He finally confessed, hoping she felt the
  same. Malak's face showed displeasure and disappointment; she objected at once: "Kaif! I have never seen you
  that way. Because of this proposal our precious friendship will be lost." After that day everything changed —
  Kaif's heart broke, he blamed himself; worse than rejection was watching his dearest friend slip away; the gap
  between them grew daily. He realised too late that chasing what you want most can lose you what you already had.
- 12:11 Present: Kaif opens his phone gallery and looks at Malak's photo; tears well up. Lightning flashes and
  thunder cracks; he presses the phone to his chest and closes his eyes. A cold, very dark night, sky covered in
  rain clouds.

SCENE F — Resort office, same stormy night, after midnight (12:31–13:30, cliffhanger)
- 12:31 All other office staff have gone home. Ahlam comes into the office and settles at his desk. A frightening
  silence fills the office; only heavy thunder outside. (He had gone to his room and showered, then remembered
  something and came back.) Absorbed in work, he doesn't sense that someone else is in the office.
- 12:59 After 12 (midnight) Ahlam leaves his cabin, hand going to his pocket for his phone to read messages, and
  walks toward the exit. He stops — listens to a sound he cannot identify; frowns.
- 13:16 Before he can think, he is startled by WARM BREATH on his shoulder from behind. He spins round —
  at that instant his eyes are flooded with (blinding) light / everything goes blank so he can't make out anything.
  END (cliffhanger).
