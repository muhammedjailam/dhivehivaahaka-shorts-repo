# 16 February (16 ފެބްރުއަރީ) — series bible

Dhivehi audio drama, island mystery thriller with a slow-burn romance. Episodes made here: **246 → 250 → 284 → 285 → 356**
(not consecutive; episodes in between exist but are not part of this batch). Every cover (`work/episode-<N>-cover.png`) is
the same poster: a woman in profile (uncovered hair — do NOT copy that), a bearded man, a younger man, a teenage boy, a
fiery orange crack splitting the image, a stormy sky, palms and a dhoni at night. Match its mood (see `style.txt`): dark,
tense, stormy, ember-orange accents; daytime resort/island scenes are bright tropical but slightly desaturated.

SETTING: a Maldivian home island (family house with a sandy yard and a joali swing chair, a long dark tree-lined main
road, half-built abandoned guesthouses where work stopped long ago, a jetty with ferries and the resort's staff launch)
and a nearby resort island (an open-plan back-office with desks and glass cabins, the boss's private office cabin, a
staff canteen with sea view, a staff room, a small resort clinic, garden paths with watered plants, a souvenir shop with a
glass watch showcase). The key night is **16 February**: Zain's birthday, heavy rain, thunder.

THE NIGHT OF 16 FEBRUARY (told in pieces across episodes — NEVER show the violent parts):
Malak comes home by ferry at Maghrib for Zain's birthday; her motorbike has been stolen; she walks the dark road; at the
abandoned guesthouses a man falls from the top and dies at her feet; a stranger (Ahlam, an undercover officer who knew
"evil people" were near) pulls her into hiding and keeps her quiet as police sirens approach; she bites him, throws her
phone at him and flees; she falls in the dark, loses the bag with the watch she bought for Zain; Ahlam picks the bag up.
Hurt and soaked she reaches Zain's apartment and overhears Zain with her best friend Kiyaara in the bedroom (an affair);
she leaves without being seen and runs home through the storm.

## Binding content rules (override the literal narration)
1. Hijab fully covering hair and neck on EVERY woman in EVERY image (home, night, rain, office at 2 a.m., flashbacks).
2. The death on 16 Feb is NEVER shown: no falling man, no body, no blood, no body outline. Show instead: Malak's horrified
   face lit by lightning, rain pouring off the dark unfinished concrete building, a dark empty upper floor, a dropped
   paper bag in a puddle, blue-and-red police lights flickering through the trees, police tape around the building base.
3. No physical touch between Malak and any man. The stranger covering her mouth / dragging her / her biting him = show
   Malak crouched hidden behind a wall in the dark with wide frightened eyes and her own hand over her mouth, and a tall
   dark male silhouette crouched an arm's length away with a finger to his lips; police lights through the bushes. The
   phone she throws = a phone lying in the wet leaves. On Kaif's motorbike (250): show Kaif riding and Malak seated
   sideways behind with a clear gap, holding the seat rail, or simply the motorbike on the road from behind/at a
   distance. Rishwan carrying her (284) = NOT shown; show the staff room with Malak sitting up on a couch.
4. Malak's injuries (swollen lip, grazed forehead, bruised arm, limp) are NEVER shown: show her in sunglasses, a hand at
   her hijab edge, a tired pale face, the long sleeve pulled down over her wrist, a slight careful step.
5. The bloody handkerchief = a red folder lying open on the desk with a crumpled DARK cloth inside, seen from a distance;
   Malak recoiling, hand over her own mouth. No red stains, no blood.
6. Zain + Kiyaara (246) are never shown together: a closed bedroom door with a strip of light under it, the cut birthday
   cake in the dark living room, Malak frozen in the hallway.
7. Fainting / IV: no person lying down. Show the fallen red folder and a chair pushed back; later Malak SITTING UP on a
   staff-room couch with a cup of water while Zuhoo checks her (no needles; at most a small IV stand in the background).
8. No weapons anywhere (the murder weapon, if any, is never shown). No alcohol, no smoking.
9. No readable text: emails on screens are blurred glowing rectangles; files and folders blank; no date "16 February"
   rendered (dates/numbers never appear in images); the watch has a plain dial with no brand.
10. Kaif and Malak (stepsiblings, he loves her): always at a respectful distance; his confession flashback = two young
    people standing apart on a beach at dusk, her turned away.
11. Ahlam and Malak: never touching; glances across the canteen/office are fine.
12. The breath on Ahlam's shoulder / blinding flash (284/285): Ahlam alone at his desk at night, lit by a sudden white
    flash from behind, startled; no figure visible.
