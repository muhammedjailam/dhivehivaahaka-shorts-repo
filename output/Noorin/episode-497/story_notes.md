# Noorin — Episode 497: story notes

## Synopsis
PRESENT. Exhausted after duty, Noorin comes home, changes out of her uniform, showers and sits at her computer. An
e-mail invites her to the SAARC film festival; she phones Zee, who meekly admits she gave the organisers Noorin's ID
because they kept pestering. Noorin hangs up. The festival is held in the Maldives; Noorin goes to the award night with
Zee. Trailers, awards — best screenplay goes to "Rihun", written by "WhiteLily". All eyes wait to see who WhiteLily is;
when Noorin walks on stage and takes the award, everyone is astonished — most of all Uvaish, the famous businessman, who
feels he has seen a ghost: he has searched for her for twelve years. After the ceremony, as Noorin, Zee and producer
Sen Dil talk, Uvaish's voice says "Noorin." She turns and freezes: he is the man who turned her heart to stone, and the
one who once filled it with tenderness. He marvels that she is WhiteLily and that her stories play on the heart's
deepest strings. She, with a forced smile: "This is not Noorin. WhiteLily. Only those who have felt the pain know its
truth — I don't think Uvaish could." She walks away; he catches her hand; she turns and slaps him. Zee and the producer
are stunned. "Let go of my hand." He lets go, hurt more in the heart than on the cheek. At home she flings her things on
the bed and cries out her pain; she always knew this day would come. Memories rise like a diary she is forced to read.
FLASHBACK — TWELVE YEARS EARLIER. Young Noorin, secretary for two months at a resort under construction, hurries towards
the manager's office past busy workers. "Madam, careful!" — a young man pulls her aside; their heads bump; a stone falls
where she was walking and a tarpaulin falls over them; workers pull them out. Dusty, she notices a scrape on her arm and
thanks her rescuer, who teases her: is "thank you" all? "Sorry, thank you — don't you know other words?" As payment he
asks her to join him for breakfast. She walks away without answering; "I'll wait." She stops, closes her eyes, breathes
and goes into her room; he smiles. In her room she cleans the scrape, scolding herself for never looking up when she
walks. Doorbell: co-worker Naahidh with an ointment bottle — "the boss sent it" — and notes her swollen forehead. Later
a knock: the rescuer himself walks in with an ice pack. "The boss." — "Not the boss. Uvaish." He seats her on the sofa,
sits on the coffee table and holds the ice to the bump. He gazes at her: two months earlier, on a rainy night, she had
fainted at his feet on Majeedhee Magu; he saved her, she left only a "thank you" note; he had searched for her since,
then found her file among his resort's staff files by chance and came that night. She lowers her eyes, tries to rise; he
says he came only to meet her. He takes the old note from his pocket; embarrassed, she stands and apologises. "I came to
find something Noorin stole." Panicked, she says she left his door unlocked — maybe a thief got in. He smiles playfully,
leans close and whispers "Learn to say something other than sorry and thank you," walks to the door and looks back. She
stands frozen.
