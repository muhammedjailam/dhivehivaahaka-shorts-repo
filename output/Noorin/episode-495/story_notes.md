# Noorin — Episode 495: story notes

## Synopsis
**Twelve years ago.** A thunderstorm breaks over a crowded Malé street at night, and people run for cover under the shop
awnings. Among them is Noorin, about 22, soaked, her eyes red from crying. After her father left she escaped her cruel
stepmother on her island and came to her birth mother in Malé full of hope. In her mother's house, though, she had to
fight to protect her own honour from her stepfather. Her mother loved her but could do nothing, and Noorin did not want to
break her mother's trust in her husband, so she hid the truth and left the house in tears. At eleven the rain eases, the
shops close and the street empties. Noorin, exhausted, sits at the roadside shivering, faints, and falls against the feet
of a man standing beside her, who lifts her up (this is young Uvaish, not named here). She wakes in a luxurious, unfamiliar
modern room, wearing clean, changed clothes. Her bag is by the wardrobe and nothing is missing. She writes a short note in
a notebook on the desk and hurries out.

**Present, twelve years later.** Noorin comes home in her police uniform, takes off her cap and touches the two stars on her
shoulder in the mirror: she was promoted to Inspector today. Freshened up, she reads her e-mails, then phones Zee. PictureLand
has mailed again, and she will only write as "WhiteLily", never under her real name. Zee says they want to enter her film
"Rihun" in a film festival. Noorin agrees, but only as WhiteLily. In the business news she sees the name "Uvaish" in big
letters and slams the laptop shut.

A case follows. A girl has harmed herself and is rushed to hospital. Noorin confronts the girl's sobbing mother: why did
she leave her daughter in the hands of a beast of a man (the stepfather) for fear of what people would say? At her office
desk Noorin's old wounds reopen. She is haunted by the voices of children from her cases, among them a child who used
"something friends gave" to escape quarrels, mockery and punishment, and she covers her ears. She works hard to rehabilitate
young offenders.

In a prison cell a smirking middle-aged offender says vile things about women. Noorin loses her temper and strikes him
twice; he hits back and grabs her collar, and officers rush in and pull him away. She calmly tells him the whole scene was
recorded. Leaving the court with other officers, she is seen by Aakif, who is overjoyed as if he had found a lost jewel. She
puts on sunglasses, ignores him and gets into the vehicle. He decides he imagined it ("Noorin is far weaker than that"). As
the vehicle pulls away she looks at him in the side mirror. Twelve years ago she put him out of her mind: her life belongs
to her nation and her religion, and there is no room left for worldly love.
