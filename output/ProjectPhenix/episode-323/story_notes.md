# Project Phenix — episode 323: story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
Morning at Malé police headquarters (Shaheed Hussain Adam Building). Ashham and Habeeb, believed dead, slip in
disguised in police uniforms, caps and dark sunglasses. Over a Bluetooth earpiece Habeeb says he needs two minutes in
the main server room to cut the press conference's live feed and broadcast the bunker video; Ashham tells him to be
careful. Ashham leans on the back wall of the press hall watching ACP Fairooz straighten his shirt buttons at the
podium. At 9 am the red camera lights come on and Fairooz tells the nation that Ashham Mohamed and Ismail Habeeb took
a big bribe, destroyed secret files, tried to flee by night and drowned when their launch sank. Ashham grinds his teeth.

Third floor: Habeeb opens the biometric server-room door with a small device and his hacking program and connects the
drive to the main broadcast server ("bypassing… 40 %… 70 %"). A security guard bursts in: "Hey! Who are you? Hands
up!" They fight (never shown); Habeeb wins and the upload completes. In the hall, as Fairooz begins "So this case
now…", the big screen cuts and on every TV in the country the bunker video plays: Naail tied to a chair, Fairooz
removing his mask and ordering the torture, his voice clear. Fairooz's face drains white: "What is this? Stop it!"
Photos and documents of the bunker's victims follow. "That video is the truth, Fairooz!" Ashham throws off cap and
glasses, standing in full combat uniform. Fairooz grabs a shock gun, Ashham is faster, Fairooz goes down screaming;
officers surround him and he is handcuffed live. Ashham to the camera: "The law is not a toy." Fairooz laughs like a
madman: Project Phenix's real owner is far higher up. Chaos, camera flashes; some peace for Ashham, but the warning
echoes.

Naasir, head of Internal Affairs, calls Ashham a national hero, says Fairooz and Moosa Thaahir are arrested and sends
him to the Home Minister at once; Habeeb is "securing the remaining data". In his office Minister Mohamed Saleem
(smoking in the narration — never shown) tells Ashham to sit and praises him; Ashham says big political figures are
behind Project Phenix. Saleem presses a remote, the doors lock, and he confesses: he granted the Kandu-huttaa lease and
the foreign pharmaceutical company's permit; Fairooz only carried out his orders. Ashham reaches for his gun; three
armed commandos step out of a secret door behind him. "Put your hand down — you've walked into the middle of the trap."

Server room: Naasir and two policemen confront Habeeb ("Naasir sir? What is this?"), demand the original hard drive
(Ashham only has a copy). Habeeb refuses; he is punched and falls (not shown); Naasir seizes the drive, orders every
Kandu-huttaa file deleted and Habeeb taken to Dhoonidhoo; they take his phone and devices and lead him away.

Blindfolded, Ashham is driven around for an hour and finds himself on a steel chair in a big dark warehouse, bound
(not shown), facing Saleem and Fairooz. Fairooz taunts and slaps him (not shown); Ashham, choked, says the whole
country saw the video; Saleem will call it an AI deepfake and brand Ashham a terrorist — his death will be news
tomorrow. A man brings a syringe of the Project Phenix "vaccine" that wipes memory (shown only as a glowing blue vial);
Fairooz: "you'll be a living body without a soul". Ashham strains, closes his eyes. Then the huge steel door blows in;
smoke and fire; an MNDF special unit storms in, retired Commissioner Faahid at its head; the commander shouts "Nobody
move! Hands up!" Saleem and Fairooz raise their hands; Faahid frees Ashham: he tracked him through the Ministry's
secret monitoring system and had already sent all the evidence to the head of the military. Ashham rises: "Now do you
know who fell into the trap?" — but Fairooz picks up the syringe from the floor to inject himself. (to be continued)
