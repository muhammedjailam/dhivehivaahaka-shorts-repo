# Project Phenix — episode 323: story notes

## Synopsis
Morning at Malé police headquarters (Shaheed Hussain Adam Building). Ashham and Habeeb, believed dead, slip in
disguised in police uniforms, caps and dark sunglasses. Over a Bluetooth earpiece Habeeb says he needs two minutes in
the main server room to cut the press conference's live feed and broadcast the bunker video. Ashham leans on the back
wall of the press hall watching ACP Fairooz straighten his uniform at the podium. At 9 am the red camera lights come on
and Fairooz tells the nation that Ashham Mohamed and Ismail Habeeb took bribes, destroyed secret files, tried to flee by
night and drowned when their launch sank. Ashham seethes.

Third floor: Habeeb opens the biometric server-room door with a gadget and his hacking program, plugs the drive into
the main broadcast server ("bypassing… 40%… 70%"). A security guard surprises him; they fight (not shown); Habeeb wins
and the upload completes. In the hall the big screen cuts, and on every TV in the country the bunker video plays:
Naail tied to a chair, Fairooz removing his mask and ordering the torture, his voice clear. Fairooz turns white and
screams to stop it; photos and documents of the bunker's victims follow. "That video is the truth, Fairooz!" Ashham
throws off cap and glasses, standing in full combat uniform. Fairooz grabs a shock gun, Ashham is faster, Fairooz goes
down screaming; officers surround him and he is handcuffed live. Ashham to the camera: "The law is not a toy." Fairooz
laughs like a madman: Project Phenix's real owner is far higher up. Chaos, camera flashes; Ashham feels some peace,
but the warning echoes.

Naasir, head of Internal Affairs, calls Ashham a national hero, says Fairooz and Moosa Thaahir are arrested and sends
him to the Home Minister at once; Habeeb is "securing the data in the server room". In his luxurious office Minister
Mohamed Saleem (smoking in the narration — never shown) praises Ashham; Ashham says big politicians are behind
Project Phenix. Saleem presses a remote, the doors lock, and he confesses: he granted the Kandu-huttaa lease and the
foreign pharmaceutical company's permit; Fairooz only carried out his orders. Ashham reaches for his gun; three armed
commandos step out of a secret door behind him. "You've walked into the middle of the trap."

Server room: Naasir and two policemen confront Habeeb, demand the original hard drive (Ashham only has a copy). Habeeb
refuses; he is punched and falls (not shown); Naasir seizes the drive, orders every Kandu-huttaa file deleted and
Habeeb taken to Dhoonidhoo; they strip his phone and devices and lead him away cuffed (no cuffs shown).

Blindfolded, Ashham is driven around for an hour and wakes on a steel chair in a big dark warehouse, bound (not shown),
facing Saleem and the freed Fairooz. Fairooz slaps him (not shown); Saleem will call the video an AI deepfake and brand
Ashham a terrorist; his death will be news tomorrow. A man brings a syringe of the Project Phenix "vaccine" that wipes
memory (shown only as a glowing blue vial). Ashham strains, closes his eyes — the last seconds. Then the huge steel
door blows in; smoke and fire; an MNDF special unit storms in led by retired Commissioner Faahid. Saleem and Fairooz
raise their hands; Faahid frees Ashham: he tracked him through the Ministry's secret monitoring system and had already
sent all the evidence to the head of the military. Ashham rises: "Now do you know who fell into the trap?" — but
Fairooz picks up the syringe from the floor to inject himself. (to be continued)
