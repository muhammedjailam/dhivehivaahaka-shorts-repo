# Project Phenix — episode 321: story notes

## Synopsis
The bunker's heavy steel door slams shut behind Ashham, Habeeb and Dr. Raniya. Cold silence. Raniya is rigid with fear, Ashham alert; Habeeb scans with his tablet: there is a powerful local server linked to Malé by satellite, but they have no contact with the outside. They walk down a long concrete corridor under fluorescent tubes; the chemical, hospital-like smell grows. Raniya leads: the main lab is at the end, where Naail saw the horror. A distant moan of pain; Raniya covers her mouth: "one of their test subjects."

CRIME AGAINST HUMANITY — a hall ringed by small glass rooms, each with a bed; people hooked to tubes and machines, faces neither dead nor alive. "Ya Rabbi!" Habeeb's eyes widen. Raniya: missing foreigners, smuggled people and foreign workers with no records; the "Project Phenix" drug is first injected into them. Ashham remembers Naail's phoenix tattoo: Naail tried to expose this; he copied files. "But his father Moosa Thaahir is one of the main funders." Ashham's blood boils: did Moosa even order his own son's death?

DATA — "Habeeb, the system! Copy everything — our only chance to expose this to the world." Habeeb plugs in his hard drive, hands trembling, breaks in: video recordings — including the recording of Naail's killing. "Play it." A week ago in this hall: Naail tied to a chair, a man in a black coat removes his mask — not Moosa, but Assistant Commissioner Fairooz, top of the Malé Area Command, Ashham's direct boss. "He gave us this case to send us here, lock us in and kill us." The video shows the torture and killing on Fairooz's orders. Raniya recoils, crying out.

THE TRAP — "Downloading?" "Only 50%." Sirens at the main door, red lights: the system shows the bunker's main door opened from outside — "Fairooz's men!" Ashham takes the unfinished drive and pockets it. Raniya: the ventilation duct, a narrow way (a steel grille above). Ashham climbs a chair and knocks the grille off. Raniya first.

CONFRONTATION — as Raniya and Habeeb squeeze into the duct, five armed men enter, Fairooz at their head: "Ashham! Your journey ends here." Ashham fires from behind a pillar: "You're no policeman — you're a criminal!" Fairooz laughs: "Not even your bodies will leave this island; you'll be hidden here like Naail." His men fire together; Ashham's ammunition runs out; Habeeb calls from the duct; Ashham leaps up and shuts the grille as bullets spark on the steel. Trapped in the dark duct.

THE DUCT — cold, narrow, pitch dark; Ashham's shoulders scrape the walls; Raniya behind, Habeeb last. Habeeb's tablet map: the duct splits — over the engine room, or to a secret research lab. Raniya: sick prisoners are kept in an isolation ward next to the secret lab. Right turn; the chemical smell grows — dangerous gas; Ashham tears part of his shirt for cloths over their mouths.

ISOLATION WARD — after ~20 minutes' crawl, light below: a big white room, medical machines, big glass tanks of green liquid with organs inside. Empty. They drop down. Raniya runs to a bed in the corner: her father, tied down, tubes in his body. "Baba! Wake up!" He opens dull eyes: "Daughter… save yourself… they'll destroy everything tonight… Fairooz wants to blow this place up and bury us all with the evidence. He has boarded a launch for Malé."

COUNTDOWN — Habeeb: the self-destruct system is activated, only 15 minutes. Red lights, a loud siren, a robotic English voice: "14 minutes remaining." Raniya: removing the tubes will drop his heart rate. Ashham: Habeeb, find the main switch; Raniya, be ready; we all leave together. Habeeb cuts the main cables, the machines stop; Raniya undoes the belts; Ashham lifts the father onto his shoulder. The main door is locked, but behind the lab a cargo lift goes straight up to the east beach.

CONFRONTATION — bullets from behind; two guards chase them. Ashham hands the father to Habeeb, fires from behind a pillar: the first man falls, the second hides. "5 minutes remaining." Habeeb holds the lift door; Ashham dives in; the lift rises; small explosions below; the whole building shakes.

ESCAPE — the lift opens into an old storehouse on the east side; fresh wind; light rain. They run onto the beach; a massive underground explosion; the ground splits, smoke and fire rise; the island shakes; the storehouse roof caves in; they are thrown onto the sand. The bunker is a pit of cement and rubble; everything turned to ash. But the hard drive in Ashham's pocket is safe — a secret Fairooz doesn't know. Habeeb, breathing hard on the sand: "We survived." Raniya hugs her father and smiles. Ashham stands and looks at the sea: "Not the end. Fairooz is on his way to Malé thinking we died here. We must reach Malé first and expose this." (to be continued)
