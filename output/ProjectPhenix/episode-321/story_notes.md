# Project Phenix — episode 321: story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
The bunker's heavy steel door slams shut behind Ashham, Habeeb and Dr. Raniya. Cold silence. Raniya is rigid with fear, Ashham alert; Habeeb scans with his tablet: a powerful local server linked to Malé by satellite, but no contact with the outside world. They walk down a long concrete corridor under fluorescent tubes; the chemical, hospital-like smell grows. Raniya leads: the main lab is where the corridor ends — where Naail saw the horror. A distant moan of pain; Raniya covers her mouth: "one of the people they experiment on."

CRIME AGAINST HUMANITY — a hall ringed by small glass rooms, each with a bed; people hooked to tubes and machines, faces neither dead nor alive. "Ya Rabbi!" Habeeb's eyes widen. Raniya: missing foreigners, smuggled people and foreign workers with no records; the "Project Phenix" drug is first injected into them. Ashham remembers Naail's phoenix tattoo: Naail tried to expose this; he copied files. "But his father Moosa Thaahir is one of the main funders." Ashham's blood boils: did Moosa even order his own son's death?

DATA — "Habeeb, the system! Copy every file — our only chance to expose this to the world." Habeeb plugs in his hard drive, hands trembling, breaks in: video recordings, including the recording of Naail's killing. "Play it." A week ago in this hall: Naail tied to a chair, a man in a black coat removes his mask — not Moosa, but Assistant Commissioner Fairooz, Ashham's direct boss. "He handed us this case to send us here, lock us in and kill us." Raniya recoils, crying out.

THE TRAP — "Downloading?" "Only fifty percent." Sirens, red lights: the bunker's main door has been opened from outside — "Fairooz's men!" Ashham pockets the unfinished drive. Raniya: the ventilation shaft, a narrow way (a steel grille above). Ashham climbs a chair and knocks the grille loose. Raniya first.

CONFRONTATION — as Raniya and Habeeb squeeze into the duct, five armed men enter, Fairooz at their head: "Ashham! Your journey ends here." Ashham, behind a pillar: "You're no policeman — you're a criminal!" Fairooz laughs: "Not even your bodies will leave this island." His men fire together; Ashham's ammunition runs low; Habeeb calls from the duct; Ashham leaps up and shuts the grille as bullets spark on the steel.

THE DUCT — cold, narrow, pitch dark; Raniya behind Ashham, Habeeb last. Habeeb's tablet map: the duct splits — over the engine room, or to a secret research lab. Raniya, voice trembling: the sick are kept in an isolation ward next to the secret lab. Right turn; dangerous gas; Ashham tears part of his shirt: "Tie this over your mouths."

ISOLATION WARD — after ~20 minutes, light below: a big white room, machines, glass tanks of green liquid with organs. Empty. They drop down. Raniya runs to a bed: her father, tied down, tubes in his body. "Baba! Wake up!" He opens dull eyes: "Daughter… save yourself… they'll destroy everything tonight… Fairooz wants to blow this place up and bury us with the evidence. He has boarded a launch for Malé."

COUNTDOWN — self-destruct activated, fifteen minutes. Red lights, siren, robotic English countdown. Ashham: Habeeb, find the main switch; Raniya, be ready; we all leave together. Habeeb cuts the cables; Raniya undoes the belts; Ashham lifts her father onto his shoulder. Habeeb: the main door is locked, but a cargo lift behind the lab goes straight up to the east beach. Two guards chase them; Ashham holds them off from behind a pillar; "Five minutes remaining"; Habeeb holds the lift door; Ashham dives in; small explosions below.

ESCAPE — the lift opens into an old storehouse on the east side; fresh wind, light rain. On the beach a massive underground explosion; the ground splits, smoke and fire rise; the storehouse roof caves in; they are thrown onto the sand. The bunker is a pit of rubble — but the hard drive in Ashham's pocket is safe. "We… we survived." Raniya hugs her father and smiles. Ashham looks at the sea: "Not the end. Fairooz thinks we died here. We must reach Malé first and expose this." (to be continued)
