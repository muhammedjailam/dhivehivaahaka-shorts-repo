# Project Phenix — episode 320: story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
Dawn: Ashham and Habeeb leave Malé's north harbour as secret passengers on a cargo boat heading south (the captain is
Ashham's old friend). Habeeb tracks Dr. Raniya on a satellite modem: she is on duty at the hospital, surrounded by calls
from unknown numbers — her guards. "She's in a locked cage; unless she's rescued, there's no way to learn the truth."
A silent voyage, a storm brewing.

The next night at 9 pm the boat reaches GA. Villingili. In ordinary clothes they walk dark lanes to the quiet two-storey
hospital and slip in through the emergency door; Habeeb keeps watch, Ashham knocks on Raniya's door, enters and locks it.
Raniya is frightened ("Who are you? Get out!"); Ashham shows his police card: Serious Crimes, Naail's death — and to
save her father. She sinks into her chair in tears ("You... you know everything?"). Habeeb: we have Naail's call
recording. "They killed Naail; I told him not to get into this." Calmer, she explains: Kandu-huttaa hides a foreign
pharmaceutical company's secret lab making a new "master drug", tested on foreigners smuggled into the Maldives (human
trafficking); Naail found out because the shipments came through his father's company. "Who's the mastermind in Malé?"
— every light in the hospital goes out, backup power too. Heavy boots and guns being loaded in the corridor. "They're
here!" Ashham holds the door: "Habeeb, open the window!" The door bursts open: three masked armed men. A rifle butt hits
Ashham; in the dark he falls, a masked man grabs his collar ("Forward, without a sound!"); Raniya grips Habeeb's arm.
Ashham spots a heavy metal file holder, strikes, a gun falls ("Habeeb! Out the door!"), he tackles the second man;
Habeeb and Raniya run down the corridor while the third man fires after them. Ashham overpowers them, takes their
ammunition and escapes through the back door; all three vanish into the rainy lanes.

At an abandoned jetty on the west shore, breathless and soaked, Raniya reveals a fibreglass dinghy she hid to escape but
never dared use because her father is a hostage. They launch it; Habeeb saves his laptop: "Very dangerous trip; another
island?" Ashham: "No time — Kandu-huttaa; inside the bunker we'll learn all their secrets." Raniya has the code on her
phone, but there are armed guards inside.

Storm crossing: mountain-like waves, salt spray, Habeeb bailing, laptop strapped to his body. "Sir! A big launch!" Two
fast criminal launches with a searchlight close in and fire; "They've seen us!" "Zayaan, hold on!" — Ashham swings into
the waves; "Sir, we're going to die!" Ashham aims not at the men but at the searchlight — "Bang!" — it bursts; darkness;
a huge wave pushes the launches away. Through a narrow reef channel into Kandu-huttaa's lagoon; the engine smokes and
dies; the dinghy drifts onto the sand. Raniya: "We've only reached the field of death; under this island is hell."
Laptop at 15%: satellite signal, but a jammer from the bunker blocks messages to Malé — they must get inside and cut the
main switch. At the bunker's steel door two armed guards; Ashham ("Habeeb, wait here with Raniya") throws a stone,
takes both down ("Come quickly!"); Raniya types the code on the keypad; "Beep!"; the steel door opens, cold chemical
air ("This is only the beginning, be careful"). They enter and shut the door — and outside a big launch roars into the
lagoon: the criminals' main group has arrived. They are locked inside the bunker. (to be continued)
