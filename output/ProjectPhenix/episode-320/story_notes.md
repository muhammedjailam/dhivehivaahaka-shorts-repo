# Project Phenix — episode 320: story notes

## Synopsis
Dawn: Ashham and Habeeb leave Malé's north harbour as secret passengers on a cargo boat heading south (the captain is
Ashham's old friend). Habeeb tracks Dr. Raniya on a satellite modem: she is on duty at the hospital, surrounded by calls
from unknown numbers — her guards. "She's in a locked cage; only if we save her do we get the truth." A silent voyage,
a storm brewing.

The next night at 9 pm the boat reaches GA. Villingili. In ordinary clothes they walk dark lanes to the quiet two-storey
hospital, slip in through the emergency door; Habeeb keeps watch, Ashham knocks on Raniya's door, enters and locks it.
Raniya is frightened ("Who are you? Get out!"); Ashham shows his police card: Serious Crimes, Naail's death — and to
save her father. She breaks down into her chair. Habeeb: we have Naail's call recording. "They killed Naail; I told him
not to get involved." She explains: Kandu-huttaa hides a foreign pharmaceutical company's secret lab developing a new
"master drug", tested on foreigners smuggled into the Maldives (human trafficking); Naail found out because the
shipments came through his father's company. "Who's the mastermind in Malé?" — every light in the hospital goes out,
backup power too. Heavy boots and guns being loaded in the corridor. Ashham holds the door, Habeeb opens the window;
the door bursts open: three masked armed men. A rifle butt hits Ashham; in the dark he falls; a masked man grabs his
collar; Raniya grips Habeeb's arm. Ashham spots a heavy metal file holder, strikes, a gun falls, he tackles the second
man; Habeeb and Raniya run down the corridor while the third man fires after them (bullets chip the walls). Ashham
overpowers them, takes their ammunition and escapes through the back door; all three vanish into the rainy lanes.

At an abandoned jetty on the west shore, breathless and soaked, Raniya reveals a fibreglass dinghy she hid to escape
but never dared use because her father is a hostage. They launch it; Habeeb saves his laptop. "The sea is too rough —
another island?" "No time — Kandu-huttaa; inside the bunker we'll learn all their secrets." Raniya has the bunker code
on her phone, but there are armed guards inside.

Storm crossing: mountain-like waves, salt spray, Habeeb bailing, laptop in a waterproof bag strapped to his body. Two
fast criminal launches with a searchlight close in and fire; Ashham swings into the waves; "Sir, we'll die!" Ashham aims
not at the men but at the searchlight — "Bang!" — it shatters; darkness; a huge wave pushes the launches away. Through a
narrow reef channel into Kandu-huttaa's lagoon; the engine smokes and dies; the dinghy drifts onto the sand. Raniya:
"We've only reached the field of death; under this island is hell." Laptop at 15%: satellite signal, but a jammer from
the bunker blocks messages to Malé — they must get inside and cut the main switch. At the bunker's steel door two armed
guards; Ashham throws a stone, takes both down; Raniya with trembling hands types the code on the keypad; "Beep!"; the
steel door opens, cold chemical air. They enter and shut the door — and outside a big launch roars into the lagoon: the
criminals' main group has arrived. They are locked inside the bunker. (to be continued)
