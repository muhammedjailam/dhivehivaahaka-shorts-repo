# Project Phenix — episode 324 (FINALE) — story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
Picks up in the warehouse as the MNDF unit storms in: Fairooz grabs the Project Phenix syringe from the floor and injects
himself to wipe his memory and his secrets; Ashham lunges too late; Fairooz convulses and dies (never shown). Minister
Saleem drops to his knees weeping and is led away. Faahid, hand on Ashham's shoulder: Fairooz took many secrets with
him, but Habeeb matters now — Naasir took him to Dhoonidhoo. In a Dhoonidhoo cell Naasir, with a laptop, demands the
master password of the hacked accounts (Saleem's money abroad is frozen since the video leaked) and gives Habeeb ten
minutes before he starts cutting his fingers. Habeeb secretly presses his modified smartwatch, streaming his location and
the cell audio to a secret server. On a military launch from Malé lagoon, Ashham and Faahid pick up the signal and hear
Naasir; eight minutes left; full throttle. At Dhoonidhoo jetty Faahid commands the startled guards; Ashham runs. Naasir
puts a knife to Habeeb's throat; Habeeb: "My death will bring these secrets alive"; Naasir kicks him. Ashham bursts in,
kicks Naasir down, soldiers arrest him. Habeeb didn't give the password — instead he leaked all of Saleem's and Moosa
Thaahir's black-money transactions and secret documents to world media and Interpol through the watch; world news
explodes on Faahid's tablet. But Saleem is not the top: the container with the real Phenix drugs left Malé port an hour
ago for a foreign ship off Malé's east side. On an MNDF coast-guard launch in a rough night sea Habeeb finds the cargo
ship MV Orion on radar; Ashham zips his jacket; armed mercenaries expected. The ship's searchlight and automatic fire hit
the launch; Ashham shoots out the searchlight; Faahid brings the launch to the stern ladder; Habeeb stays to hack the
ship. Deck fight between the containers; a grenade blast; they reach the red container; Habeeb opens its electronic lock
— racks of thousands of glowing blue vials; Faahid sets a five-minute charge. Dr. Vance appears with four armed men,
claims ownership of the project, mocks "little island nations" as test mice, shows a remote to deactivate the bomb.
Ashham signals Habeeb: the hacked crane swings its cable into the container; shootout; Faahid hit in the shoulder;
Vance flees, his remote locked out by Habeeb (1:30 left). Ashham carries Faahid; Vance aims from the upper deck;
Habeeb's flare hits him and he falls into the sea. "Thirty seconds — jump!" Ashham leaps with Faahid; the ship explodes
and sinks; Habeeb pulls them aboard — "We did it", tears of joy. Three weeks later: crowd outside police HQ; Saleem,
Moosa and Naasir jailed for life; Faahid the new Commissioner pins gold medals on Ashham and Habeeb. Evening on Hulhumalé
beach: Raniya says she and her father go back to the island tomorrow, thanks Ashham; he cannot voice his feelings; she
walks to her father on a bench; Ashham stands alone watching until they vanish, then looks at the sky. THE END.

New audio: 1299.8 s (21:40), 131 segments (v1: 1127.5 s, 122 shots). Same story, colloquial wording, slower read.
