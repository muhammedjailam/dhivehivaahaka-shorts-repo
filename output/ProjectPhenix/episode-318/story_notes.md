# Project Phenix — episode 318 story notes

## Synopsis
After the frightening discovery on Kandu-huttaa (311) the team races back to Malé: the launch they heard was someone
watching the police, so the island is now monitored in secret. Forensics confirm the body is Naail, son of tycoon Moosa
Thaahir, and Malé's political and business circles grow uneasy. At noon, in heavy rain, Ashham (black raincoat) and
Habeeb stand in an alley off Majeedhee Magu: Naail's bank accounts paid big sums to the Maafannu "Black Wolf" gang.
At an old building two young gang members try to slip away; Ashham shows his card and asks for Kalhe. In the dark den
Kalhe (~40, scarred) reads papers and greets "the famous investigator". Ashham leans on the table: Naail paid you.
Kalhe: Naail paid them to get a secret shipment out of a container at Malé port — not drugs, medical supplies under a
false label, bound for GA Kandu-huttaa. Naail later got scared, said it was against humanity, wanted to stop it, and
vanished two days later. "We don't kill people like that — it's someone far more powerful."
Back at the office Habeeb hacks port and customs records: three weeks ago a big container of "medical equipment" was
imported in Moosa Thaahir's company name, signed by Naail using his father's name, then moved to Hulhumalé. Moosa is
hiding something. Naail's calls to the atoll hospital lead to Dr. Raniya, its chief surgeon (photo on screen). "Malé
plans it; the atoll runs it."
Driving to the Hulhumalé warehouse in the rain, a black van with its lights off follows; on Sinamalé bridge it rams the
car; Ashham swerves and saves them; Habeeb signals for backup; the van loses control and hits the railing; backup
arrives as Ashham drives on. "They have people inside the police." At a lonely Hulhumalé spot Habeeb admits he is
scared but won't give up. Ashham decides they go to GA tonight to meet Raniya, and texts Moosa: "Naail's secrets start
with your company. Reveal the truth." No reply — this is an international network.
In a secret police safe house in Hulhumalé (storm, only the blue laptop glow) Habeeb has found, in Naail's iCloud
backup, an encrypted satellite-phone recording made 24 hours before his death. Ashham hangs up his wet coat: "Play it."
Waves; Raniya: "Naail, where are you?" Naail, on a dhoni near Kandu-huttaa, went into the bunker tonight — what horror
are they doing? Raniya: get out now; they're using humans; her father is held hostage, she is forced; "Do you know who
among Malé's big men is behind it? It's…" — a door bursts open, a shot, Naail's cry, the call ends. Ashham: Raniya is
forced to help because her father is a hostage. Habeeb: human drug testing is an international crime; they cut out
Naail's tongue to keep the secret (never shown). They must go to the atoll now, secretly, on an ordinary boat.
"Bring your gear. No time." (to be continued)
