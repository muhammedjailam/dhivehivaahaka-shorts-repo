# Project Phenix — episode 322: story notes (v2 re-cut on the new audio)

## Synopsis
A second underground blast shakes Kandu-huttaa and flings Ashham, Habeeb, Raniya and her father onto the beach. An hour
later Ashham comes to, dusty and dazed; black smoke rises where the bunker was. He calls for Habeeb and Raniya and wakes
Habeeb; Raniya sits crying by her unconscious father. The hard drive is safe in Ashham's pocket, but the dinghy is smashed
on the reef, the tablet and satellite modem are dead: they are stranded, and Fairooz will record them as dead. Raniya
fears they will starve and her father needs care; Ashham refuses to give up and sends Habeeb round the island and Raniya
into the shade. Night: Habeeb lights a frond fire under big trees as warmth and a signal; the father asks for water and
Ashham brings young coconuts. By the fire Habeeb fears Moosa's money plus Fairooz's power; Ashham: money and power are
paper before the truth — the drive holds Fairooz's order to kill Naail and the deals with the foreign company. Raniya asks
how they will reach Malé — a searchlight at sea: Ashham smothers the fire. Fairooz's guards' launch returns to check the
blast site; three armed men with flashlights find the smoke. Ashham slips behind them and, in a short fight in the dark,
neutralises all three. The captain fires from the launch, then tries to reverse away; Habeeb dives in and grabs the stern
rail; Ashham leaps aboard, disarms the captain and throws him overboard. The big twin-engine launch "Eagle Speed" is theirs.
They cross the Huvadhu channel toward Malé; Raniya's father breathes steadily; Habeeb avoids official channels because
Fairooz controls them; Ashham decides to land secretly in Hulhumalé. — Malé HQ: ACP Fairooz smiles at satellite photos of
the destroyed bunker; Moosa comes in, exhausted; Fairooz tells him everyone is buried, the drugs in the Malé warehouse are
ready to ship, and shows the official report framing Ashham and Habeeb as bribe-taking fugitives whose launch sank. Moosa
sighs, trapped in the plan. 3 am: they land on a lonely Hulhumalé shore; Habeeb suggests retired Commissioner Faahid.
They walk to his flat; Faahid opens the door stunned ("they say you're dead!"). On his computer the video and the
experiment documents fill him with fury; he strikes the table. Plan: break into the live broadcast of Fairooz's 9 am press
conference; Habeeb needs to get near the server room. Sunrise: Ashham and Habeeb, in police uniforms, caps and dark
sunglasses, drive to Malé in Faahid's car; Raniya and her father stay at the flat. At HQ they slip in among a group of
police and split up — Habeeb to the server room, Ashham to the press hall to face Fairooz. (to be continued)
