# Project Phenix — episode 322: story notes

## Synopsis
A second underground blast shakes Kandu-huttaa and flings Ashham, Habeeb, Raniya and her father onto the beach. An hour
later Ashham comes to, dusty and dazed; black smoke rises where the bunker was. He wakes Habeeb; Raniya sits crying by
her unconscious father. The hard drive is safe in Ashham's pocket, but the dinghy is smashed on the reef, the tablet
and satellite modem are dead: they are stranded, and Fairooz will record them as dead. Raniya fears they will starve and
her father needs care; Ashham refuses to give up and sends Habeeb round the island and Raniya into the shade.
Night: Habeeb lights a frond fire under big trees as warmth and a signal; the father asks for water and Ashham brings
young coconuts. By the fire Habeeb fears Moosa's money plus Fairooz's power; Ashham: money and power are paper before the
truth — the drive holds Fairooz's order to kill Naail and the deals with the foreign company. A searchlight at sea:
Ashham smothers the fire. Fairooz's guards' launch returns to check the blast site; three armed men with flashlights find
the smoke. Ashham slips behind them and, in a short fight in the dark, neutralises all three. The captain fires from the
launch, then tries to reverse away; Habeeb dives in and grabs the stern rail; Ashham leaps aboard, disarms the captain and
throws him overboard. The big twin-engine launch "Eagle Speed" is theirs: fuel, comms, satellite navigation.
They cross the Huvadhu channel toward Malé; Raniya's father breathes steadily; Habeeb avoids official channels because
Fairooz controls them; Ashham decides to land secretly in Hulhumalé, not the main harbour. — Malé HQ: ACP Fairooz smiles
at satellite photos of the destroyed bunker; Moosa comes in, exhausted; Fairooz tells him everyone is buried, the drugs
in the Malé warehouse are ready to ship, and shows the official report framing Ashham and Habeeb as bribe-taking
fugitives whose launch sank, to be released to the media in the morning. Moosa sighs, trapped in the plan.
3 am: they land on a lonely Hulhumalé shore; Habeeb suggests retired Commissioner Faahid, removed for opposing Fairooz.
They walk to his flat; Faahid opens the door stunned ("they say you're dead!"). On his computer the video and the
experiment documents fill him with fury; he strikes the table. Plan: Fairooz speaks at a 9 am press conference at HQ —
break into the live broadcast and push the video to every channel; Habeeb needs to get near the server room. Sunrise:
Ashham and Habeeb, in police uniforms, caps and dark sunglasses, drive to Malé in Faahid's car; Raniya and her father
stay at the flat. At HQ a crowd of journalists waits for news of their "death"; they slip in among a group of police and
split up — Habeeb to the server room, Ashham to the press hall to face Fairooz. (to be continued)
