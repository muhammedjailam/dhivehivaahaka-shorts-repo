# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 455: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
In Rafhaan's glass cabin the pressure closes in: everyone wants to save the business by bringing Ahna into his life, but proud, beautiful Ahna is not his ideal. Ahmed Zaahir's commanding voice rings like a warning bell and his mother Khadeeja's pleading face cracks his defences — this is a trap that could cost him his life's happiness and the pure love his heart wants. Rafhaan calmly says he will not break company law: he is investigating Layaali's case, will report her if the bank's official reports prove her guilty, and will not discuss marriage until then. Khadeeja shouts — the company can't survive without Zaahir, why defend "that ordinary poor girl"? Rafhaan answers that a leader must investigate before acting on an employee's rights. Zaahir laughs and gives a three-day ultimatum: hand the girl to the police and marry Ahna, or he pulls all his money and Rafhaan ends up on the street — his finger almost at Rafhaan's chin. Zaahir leaves with Khadeeja; Rafhaan slams the desk.

He calls IT head and trusted friend Aasim: he wants the hidden camera footage of Ahna's cabin, two days of main-server change logs, and the upload times and earlier contents of the files given to Layaali. Aasim agrees someone is framing Layaali. Meanwhile Ahna messages Zoya and Raaya: the deadline is three days, finish Layaali off. Zoya promises the bank records with Layaali's signature will reach Rafhaan tomorrow morning with an official bank letter. Ahna smiles — her dream of becoming Rafhaan's wife is near.

At home Layaali weeps on her prayer mat, praying for her innocence to be proven and for her father's healing. Aminath brings an envelope a stranger left: fifty thousand rufiyaa and a note — "help for your father's treatment, the reward of your honesty" — no sender. Layaali refuses to touch it; it may be a trap to disgrace her. Aminath weeps: the father's medicines ran out today, Aasandha doesn't cover them all. Layaali is torn between her honour and her father's life — not knowing Ahna and her friends sent it to strengthen the theft accusation.

Next morning the bank's official envelope on Rafhaan's desk shows Layaali's signature on the cheques, confirmed by a graphology report; his head spins — "Layaali would never deceive me." Ahna walks in: send the police; a large sum has been spent on her father's treatment — where did it come from? Rafhaan rises: he will go himself to hear the truth. Ahna phones Zoya, who confirms the money was delivered last night and laughs. Rafhaan is driven through Malé's narrow lanes, the report in his hand, his heart refusing to believe it. At the small house "Baageechaage" frightened Aminath lets him in; Qaasim lies ill on a bed in the tiny sitting room. Layaali comes out, eyes red. Asked whether money came last night, she bows her head and admits fifty thousand was sent. Rafhaan is crushed — "Will medicine bought with stolen money be blessed?" She pleads: she doesn't know who sent it, never touched it — it is there on the table. Rafhaan picks up the envelope and reads the note (cliffhanger).
