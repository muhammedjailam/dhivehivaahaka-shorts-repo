# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 480: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
After Khadeeja turns away (last lines of ep 479), that night Layaali sits beside her father in the ADK Hospital ward. Qaasim, much better, sits up and talks: Rafhaan is a fine young man who respects humble people like them, but wealth and poverty never mix. Layaali says she never imagined a boss like Rafhaan would care for poor people, but his heart is noble — that is why she agreed to a life with him — and lowers her head shyly.

A masked woman in a nurse's uniform enters with medicine vials and a syringe: Raaya's hired woman. She claims the doctor, at Rafhaan's request, wants to check Layaali's blood pressure. Rafhaan's name dispels Layaali's doubt; the "nurse" prepares her arm and lifts a syringe of heart-stopping poison — and at that moment Rafhaan opens the door. Startled, she trembles and the syringe falls to the floor. Rafhaan sees the syringe and her face and knows something is wrong ("Which nurse are you? The ward's head nurse is outside"). She shoves him aside and runs; he shouts "Help!" and chases her down the corridor and stops her at the hospital's main door. Security arrives; without the mask she is clearly not staff. Under police questioning she weeps: "Raaya paid me! She told me to kill Layaali!" Rafhaan returns to the ward, reassures the terrified Layaali ("I will smash every trap of these wicked people"); she clings to him. Police go to arrest Raaya; Rafhaan resolves to hold the wedding as soon as possible. News of Raaya's arrest relieves the family; the traps of Ahna and her "devil" friends are broken.

Next morning Qaasim is discharged and moved home with Rafhaan's help. One afternoon Ibrahim Faahim and Rafhaan visit the small house (Khadeeja stays away but no longer opposes, since the company is thriving with the new investors). Ibrahim asks for Layaali's hand; Qaasim and Aminath, overjoyed, call it an honour. Layaali, behind the curtain at the bedroom door, blushes; her eyes secretly meet Rafhaan's. The wedding is set for next week; all office staff are invited.

Two days before the wedding Ahmed Zaahir comes to Rafhaan's cabin, humbled and aged. Rafhaan is hard ("nothing left to talk about"); Zaahir, head bowed, says he is ashamed of Ahna, has come to ask forgiveness, and that Ahna in jail asks for Layaali's forgiveness. Rafhaan wonders — if Ahna changed, why did Raaya act? — then tells him Layaali already forgave Ahna when she gave blood. Zaahir weeps, blesses the marriage, and leaves; love and forgiveness have beaten hatred.

The wedding: hall in white and gold flowers; Layaali in a white lace gown, Rafhaan in his suit. The khutbah; the two fathers sit at one side of the table; Rafhaan says "I accept her as my wife" and Layaali's eyes fill with tears of joy. Khadeeja strokes Layaali's head and asks her forgiveness — "you are the greatest blessing in Rafhaan's life"; Layaali holds her hand: "Mother, it's all right." As stars come out over Malé, Rafhaan comes close: "a new chapter of our life has begun; no storm will ever part us."
