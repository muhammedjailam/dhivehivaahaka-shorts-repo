# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 456: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
In Layaali's small house Rafhaan opens the anonymous envelope: money and a computer-typed note — but the handwritten address on the envelope is almost identical to the handwriting of Ahna's secretary. Bedridden Qaasim struggles to rise and begs "Sir" not to make his daughter a criminal — she would never steal; even if he dies, don't ruin her honour. He breaks into a violent cough, blood comes from his mouth (never shown), Layaali and Aminath rush to him and his eyes close. Rafhaan's last doubts vanish; he lifts Qaasim in his own arms, puts him in his car and drives to ADK Hospital, Layaali cradling her father's head in the back seat, Rafhaan watching her in the mirror and vowing to himself to prove Ahna is behind it all.

At the hospital the doctors say Qaasim's lung disease is critical and needs an immediate, expensive operation. While Layaali and Aminath stand helpless, Rafhaan pays everything at the counter with his own card. Layaali asks why he helps someone accused of theft; he says he knows she is no thief, he will find who set the money trap, he did it out of humanity "and for something else too…" — leaving it unsaid; her heart races.

High heels in the corridor: Ahna arrives gloating with Zoya — the police are at Layaali's house, the money was found, the evidence is complete. With a cold smile Rafhaan shows his phone: secret office logs and Aasim's message with IP-address proof that the bank letters were made on Ahna's own laptop, her forged signature, and the money sent through her secretary. Ahna goes pale and screams "lies!", voice trembling; Rafhaan says the evidence is with the police and the police team is on its way to arrest her. Zoya shrieks "I told you not to do this!" and flees. Layaali weeps tears of gratitude to Allah.

The senior doctor comes out of theatre: the operation succeeded but Qaasim is critical and needs O-negative blood — the blood bank has none and no one in the family has it. "My blood is O-negative": Ahna, in tears, her pride gone, offers her blood, begs forgiveness and kneels before Layaali. Layaali lifts her up — no grudge, "save my father" — and nurses take Ahna to give blood. Rafhaan's love for Layaali grows: a heart pure as an angel's. An hour later: good news, Qaasim is improving; Aminath and Layaali hold each other and weep. Ahna comes out exhausted but at peace; two police officers arrest her for forgery and trying to frame someone; she does not run, accepts her punishment, wishes Layaali's father a quick recovery and walks away with them.

At sunset outside the hospital Rafhaan gives Layaali her job back and offers her Ahna's post as head of finance; "Not sir — Layaali. Call me by my name. A special place in my heart is yours." She says "Rafhaan" shyly; her smile is his answer. But Ahna's father Ahmed Zaahir, hearing of the arrest, has begun plotting to bankrupt Rafhaan's company — a danger Rafhaan does not yet see.
