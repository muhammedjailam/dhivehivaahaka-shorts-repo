# Tedhuveriloabi (ތެދުވެރި ލޯބި, "True Love") — series bible

Setting: Malé, Maldives, today. A corporate romance-drama. The office HQ of **Rafhaan Group** is a modern glass high-rise
(glass walls, city and sea view, golden late-afternoon light), with an open-plan finance floor and Rafhaan's luxurious
glass-walled cabin. Layaali's family lives in a small, plain, old house ("Baageechaage") in a narrow Malé lane; her
father lies on a simple bed in the tiny sitting room. Rafhaan's family lives in a large modern mansion with a staircase,
a garden and a balcony over the city. Hospital = ADK Hospital, Malé (cool white corridors, ICU, ward).

Series look: `style.txt` (book-cover romantic drama, plum/burgundy/amber/dusk-blue). Caption highlight pill = plum
#9E2F6A (`caption_style.json`). Cover = `work/episode-<N>-cover.png` (same for every episode).

## Episode synopses (each episode opens by repeating the last lines of the previous one)
- **432** Rafhaan Group HQ; kind young boss Rafhaan. Ahna (head of finance) bullies humble Layaali and lies that she fixed
  Layaali's report; Rafhaan sees through it, sends Ahna out, praises Layaali — the first spark. Ahna phones her "devil"
  friends Zoya & Raaya; at a posh café they plot to frame Layaali for theft during a big project transaction. Meanwhile in
  a small Malé house Layaali's parents Aminath and bedridden Qaasim worry about her.
- **434** Layaali comes home tired. At the mansion Khadeeja presses Rafhaan to marry Ahna (her father is the biggest
  shareholder); he refuses. Next day Ahna hands Layaali a budget file from which she secretly removed cheques, then
  accuses her before Rafhaan: two cheques cashed with Layaali's forged signature. Rafhaan suspends Layaali pending inquiry;
  she goes home weeping; Qaasim weeps and coughs. At night Rafhaan alone checks bank reports and suspects Ahna. Next day
  Ahmed Zaahir arrives with Khadeeja demanding police action and the marriage.
- **455** Zaahir gives Rafhaan a 3-day ultimatum. Rafhaan calls IT head Aasim to pull Ahna's cabin camera footage and
  server logs. Ahna messages Zoya. Layaali prays on her prayer mat at home; an anonymous envelope with MVR 50,000 arrives
  (a trap) — she refuses to touch it, but her father's medicine has run out. Next morning the bank letter shows her
  signature; Ahna urges police; Rafhaan drives to Layaali's house himself. At the house Layaali admits the money came,
  begs him to listen and points to the envelope on the table.
- **456** The address on the envelope is in Ahna's secretary's handwriting. Qaasim tries to rise, collapses coughing
  (blood — never shown); Rafhaan carries him to his car and rushes to ADK Hospital; urgent lung operation — Rafhaan pays.
  In the corridor he tells Layaali he knows she is innocent. Ahna and Zoya arrive gloating; Rafhaan shows Aasim's IP-log
  evidence on his phone — Ahna is exposed, Zoya flees. The doctor: operation OK but he needs O-negative blood — Ahna
  weeps, confesses, kneels and gives her blood; Layaali forgives her. Police arrest Ahna. At sunset Rafhaan restores
  Layaali's job, makes her head of finance, asks her to call him by name. Zaahir begins plotting revenge.
- **479** Qaasim moves to the ward. Zaahir pulls his 40% shares and scares the "Seaview Resort" investors; Khadeeja
  phones furious. Rafhaan meets Layaali at the office door; at home Khadeeja cries, father Ibrahim Faahim worries.
  Rafhaan, Layaali and Aasim work late; Layaali's business plan wins a foreign investor who buys the 40% and funds the
  resort. Rafhaan proposes; she nods. Meanwhile Raaya, in her car, phones a hired man to kill Layaali secretly at the
  hospital. Ibrahim supports Rafhaan; Khadeeja grudgingly accepts.
- **480** At night in the ward a masked fake nurse tries to inject Layaali with poison; Rafhaan walks in, she drops the
  syringe and runs; he catches her at the hospital door, security and police come; she names Raaya. Raaya arrested.
  Qaasim discharged. Ibrahim and Rafhaan visit Layaali's house to ask for her hand; Layaali shy behind a curtain. Zaahir,
  humbled, comes to apologise. The wedding: hall in white and gold flowers; Layaali in a white lace modest gown; nikah;
  Khadeeja blesses Layaali; stars over Malé.
- **481** Three happy months of marriage. Investors' payment delayed: data leaks traced to Layaali's user ID and home
  Wi-Fi. At the mansion Khadeeja introduces Shiyaz (Ahna's cousin) for an IT job — Rafhaan spots his sly smile. They set
  a trap: a fake "secret budget" file; at lunch break Shiyaz photographs it and copies the server to a hard drive while
  Rafhaan, Layaali and Aasim watch on the hidden camera; caught red-handed, arrested; investors pay. Moonlit balcony.
  Two months later Layaali feels dizzy at her desk.
- **482** Doctor: Layaali is five weeks pregnant. Joy; family dinner of both families at the mansion. A letter from
  jailed Ahna (3-year sentence) asks forgiveness. Eight months on: opening night of the Seaview Resort — white-sand
  beach marquee, Rafhaan's speech honouring his wife (pregnant, pale onion-skin coloured gown). Bench by the sea at
  night. Three years later: daughter Raina (2). Ahna walks out of Maafushi prison in plain clothes and smiles.
- **483** Ahna's smile hides revenge; Zaahir welcomes her. She rings Rafhaan's doorbell, kneels before Layaali and begs
  forgiveness; Layaali forgives; Rafhaan is wary. Bedridden Qaasim shows Layaali an old photo album: 30 years ago he
  owned "Qaasim Enterprises"; Zaahir forged documents and stole everything. Meanwhile Ahna plays with Raina in the
  garden, a dangerous look. Layaali takes Raina away. Ahna phones Zaahir. Rafhaan learns the past; Aasim starts digging.
  Ahna brings a "partnership" contract to the office.
- **496** The contract would hand the company to Zaahir Investments via Layaali's signature. Rafhaan files with police;
  Aasim gets a court order for Qaasim's original files in the bank safe. Ahna brings a toy box for Raina hiding a
  bugging device; she hears the plan and phones lawyer Naasif; at night a masked intruder burns the archive files. Next
  morning the bank manager reports the fire. Ahna, smug in Rafhaan's cabin, issues a 24-hour ultimatum. Rafhaan finds the
  bug in the toy box. Next day Zaahir, Ahna and Naasif come to make Layaali sign.
- **525** Layaali, in tears, prays and reaches to sign — Aasim bursts in: the bank's records were digitised to the cloud;
  he recovered the originals and the transfer logs paying the bank guard. Police arrest Zaahir, Ahna and Naasif; Zaahir
  collapses into a chair. Court returns everything to Qaasim; long sentences. Layaali gives her father the court papers;
  he weeps with joy. Months later "Layaali Enterprises" merges with Rafhaan Group. Golden-hour resort balcony: Rafhaan,
  Layaali and little Raina running between them; both families' parents nearby. The end.
