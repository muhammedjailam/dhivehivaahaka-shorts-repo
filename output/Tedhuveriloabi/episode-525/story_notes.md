# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 525 (final episode): story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
In Rafhaan's glass cabin, Ahmed Zaahir laughs: time is up. Layaali must sign the "partnership" agreement now, or at 4 pm all of Rafhaan Group's bid prices go to the market and the company goes bankrupt. Ahna presses her: whatever her father's past, Layaali's future is in their hands. Layaali's tears fall despite herself; she silently prays for a miracle, picks up the pen and reaches for the paper. Rafhaan bows his head, like a man dying of thirst who reaches the water but never tastes a drop.

At that moment the cabin door bursts open. IT head Aasim, laptop in hand, shouts "Don't sign!" Zaahir calls for security, but Aasim sets the laptop down. The archive Ahna had burned did not matter: five years ago the bank digitised every record to a cloud server. After 22 sleepless hours, and with the bank's cooperation, Aasim recovered the original files. They hold complete evidence that 30 years ago Zaahir forged signatures and stole Qaasim's company. He also found the transfer logs: Zaahir's account, through Naasif, paid the bank's security man to burn the archive. Faces drain; Naasif steps back. Rafhaan rises: "Your time of triumph is over." The evidence has already gone to the police. A police team enters with a warrant and arrests Zaahir, Ahna and Naasif for fraud, plotting the bank fire and threats. Zaahir's head spins and he drops into a chair. Ahna screams that she won't be beaten. They are led out past the watching staff. Layaali, weeping with relief, says "Father has got his rights back."

The case makes national headlines. The court quickly returns everything taken from Qaasim, plus the whole ownership of Zaahir Investments, to him, and gives Zaahir and Ahna long prison sentences. Peace returns to the mansion. Khadeeja muses that Zaahir led his own daughter down his path. That afternoon Layaali and Rafhaan sit with Qaasim and give him the court papers. He weeps with joy and recalls being humiliated as a "thief" 30 years ago. He calls them Allah's greatest blessing and takes Rafhaan's hand. Rafhaan replies that this is the reward of honesty.

Two or three months later the company, renamed "Layaali Enterprises", merges with Rafhaan Group. Layaali is now one of the most influential businesswomen in the Maldives, yet still humble and treats the staff like family. On the new resort's balcony at golden hour, Rafhaan and Layaali reflect that every storm has passed. They have learned that a place in people's hearts comes through kindness and honesty, not power and wealth. Little Raina runs between them ("Mummy, Daddy, lift baby up!") and Rafhaan lifts her as they laugh. Both families' parents sit nearby in a cool breeze. True love and justice win. (The End)
