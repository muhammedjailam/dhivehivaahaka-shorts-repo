# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 496: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
In Rafhaan's cabin Aasim, grave-faced, explains Ahna's "partnership" contract: if Layaali signs it, all the company's main shares pass to "Zaahir Investments", and it will look as if Layaali took company money herself. Ahna has come out of jail with far more dangerous plans; Rafhaan vows to have them arrested for the crimes of the past. Aasim has found bank-archive records: the original files proving Zaahir forged Qaasim's signatures are still in the bank's secret safe. Rafhaan calls his lawyer and files a police case; the police start on a court order.

That afternoon Ahna visits the house again with a big toy box "for Raina" — hidden inside are a secret camera and a sound-recording microchip. Layaali puts it in the sitting room; Ahna leaves smug. From her car she listens on a laptop as Rafhaan rushes in: Aasim got the court order, tomorrow morning the original files will be theirs. Furious, Ahna phones Zaahir's greedy legal adviser Naasif: the files must be stolen or burned before morning. Late that night a masked man, helped by an insider who switches off security for five minutes, enters the bank archive by the back door and burns Qaasim's thirty-year-old documents with chemicals.

Next morning Rafhaan and Aasim arrive at the bank with the police and the court order; the manager, head bowed, reports a "short-circuit fire" — everything is ash. Aasim: this was deliberate. Back at the office Ahna sits smugly in Rafhaan's cabin and mocks him; he accuses her, she demands proof, lays the contract on the desk and gives a 24-hour ultimatum: if Layaali doesn't sign, Rafhaan Group's secrets will leak to the foreign investors and the company will go bankrupt. She walks out smiling; Rafhaan sits with his face in his hands. At home Layaali sinks into a chair at the news; wondering how Ahna knew their secrets, Rafhaan notices the toy box, empties it, lifts the false bottom and finds a tiny black device with a blinking green light. Layaali covers her mouth in horror, but the files are gone and they have no evidence. Next morning Zaahir, Ahna and Naasif arrive at the head office in triumph; in the cabin Layaali, beside Rafhaan, trembles as Zaahir says "time's up". (Cliffhanger into ep 525.)
