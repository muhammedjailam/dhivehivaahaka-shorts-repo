# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 432: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
The glass head office of Rafhaan Group rises over Malé: a centre of hopes, jealousy and secret plots. Everyone talks about the young, educated, kind-hearted boss Rafhaan, who built the company on his own; many female staff hang on his every glance, but he only wants to be a fair, kind and honest leader who values people above money.

"Layaali! Are today's financial reports ready?" Ahna, the arrogant, rich head of finance (whose only aim is Rafhaan), startles humble Layaali at her corner desk. Layaali, educated but from a poor family, wishes no one ill and works to support her bedridden father and her mother. Ahna takes the files and marches her to Rafhaan's luxury cabin. When Rafhaan looks up and sees Layaali, something new shows in his eyes. Ahna lies that she stayed up all night fixing Layaali's mistakes; Layaali, hurt, bows her head and says nothing. Rafhaan knows Layaali's work, closes the file and asks Ahna to wait outside. Ahna leaves with a forced smile and clenched teeth: "I'll ruin your days, Layaali."

Alone, Rafhaan asks Layaali to sit: he knows Ahna lied; Layaali is one of the most valuable staff, with the most honest heart. She looks up into his eyes — the first rays of love — then lowers her gaze: he is the sky, she is the earth (yet stars shine brightest in the dark). Outside, Ahna phones her "devil" friends Zoya and Raaya: that beggarly little saint is trying to snatch her Rafhaan. Narrator: a storm of jealousy is heading for Layaali — will this love bring her happiness or ruin?

Back in the cabin Layaali explains the report; Rafhaan signs it with his expensive pen and praises her sincerity. "Thank you, sir — my father says honest work is blessed." Being called "sir" bothers him; he wishes she would speak to him more closely, but position forbids it. Layaali leaves with her heart racing; Ahna is red with anger. At a posh café Ahna, Zoya (admiring her long nails) and Raaya plot: ruin Layaali's name with a theft accusation during a big project money transaction, so Rafhaan himself fires her. Ahna smiles dangerously. Meanwhile, in a small Malé house, bedridden Qaasim coughs and worries that Layaali isn't home yet and works so hard for them; Aminath reassures him that their daughter is strong, stroking his head.
