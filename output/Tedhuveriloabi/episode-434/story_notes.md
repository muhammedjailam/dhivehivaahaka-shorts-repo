# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 434: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Recap: Aminath wonders aloud to bedridden Qaasim why their daughter looks uneasy lately — is something wrong at the office? Layaali comes home tired but smiling, apologises for being late (lots of work), nods when her mother offers rice and sits beside her father, Rafhaan's words and Ahna's resentful looks turning in her mind; she senses the coming days won't be ordinary.

At the mansion Khadeeja is waiting for Rafhaan: Ahna's mother called, they want the marriage settled quickly — Ahna's father is the company's biggest shareholder, delay is bad for business. Rafhaan refuses: he won't build a life for profit; Ahna is arrogant and proud, he wants a humble, kind partner (Layaali's simple face is in his mind). Khadeeja hardens: crossing Ahna's family could bankrupt the company. Rafhaan goes to his room without a word, torn between the company he built and the pure love growing in his heart.

Next day, an unusual gloom at the office. Ahna gives Layaali a thick budget file for the new project — "the cheques are in it too, be careful" — after secretly removing documents and cheques: the first step of the frame-up planned with her friends. In the late afternoon, as Rafhaan walks towards Layaali, Ahna rushes in front of him: money is missing from the budget and the file was given to Layaali. The whole floor falls silent. Rafhaan demands proof; Ahna, coldly smiling, says two cheques have been cashed, the money is in no company account — "Layaali, where is the money?" Layaali stands up trembling, stammers that the file had no cheques, only budget sheets, and cries. Ahna shouts that her secretary was a witness and the bank's verification shows a signature nearly identical to Layaali's. Rafhaan takes both into his cabin so as not to shame Layaali publicly: he trusts her, but he must check with the bank; until then she is suspended. Layaali — whose father's treatment needs money — swears her innocence in tears; Rafhaan, bound by company rules and his mother's pressure, gently sends her home and promises to investigate himself. Ahna sneers that poor people forget their morals when they see money; Rafhaan throws her out. In the corridor Ahna phones Zoya and Raaya: part one succeeded; next, alter the bank records with help from inside the bank. Zoya laughs at Ahna's "great act".

Layaali walks home crying and breaks down; her parents rush to her. Qaasim weeps — his daughter would never touch anything forbidden; they are exploiting the family's helplessness — and starts coughing. Layaali holds his hand: Allah will show a way; inside she wonders how she will pay for his medicine. That night Rafhaan, alone in the office with the bank reports, sees the cheques were cashed at a time Layaali never left the office; he suspects Ahna and decides to get the bank's CCTV himself. But the next day Ahmed Zaahir arrives with Khadeeja demanding police action, the marriage and the transfer of his shares to Ahna's name; Khadeeja presses too. Rafhaan lowers his head — trapped between two families; proud, beautiful Ahna is not his ideal.
