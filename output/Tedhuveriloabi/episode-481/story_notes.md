# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 481: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
On the wedding night Rafhaan tells Layaali a new chapter has begun and no storm will ever part them; truth and humility have won over envy, power and wealth. Three happy months pass: Khadeeja's displeasure has vanished and Layaali is a pillar of the household, and as head of finance she has raised the company's profits.

But "like a wave rising on a calm sea", a dark cloud appears. One morning Layaali brings a file into Rafhaan's cabin: a big payment from the new investors is delayed, and their head office suspects the company's secret data (accounts, bid prices) is leaking to a competitor — and the outside auditors say it was taken with Layaali's user ID. She has never shared her password; old fears of disgrace return. Rafhaan stands by her ("I always trust you completely") and calls Aasim. The logs show the data was sent late at night from Layaali's own laptop over their home Wi-Fi — but she was asleep, and Rafhaan was with her. Rafhaan wonders who could reach the home network and remembers recent house guests — some of jailed Ahna's distant relatives still keep in touch with Khadeeja.

That afternoon at the mansion Khadeeja introduces Shiyaz, a friend's son and Ahna's cousin, who studied IT abroad and has no job; she wants him placed in the company's IT section. Shiyaz greets Rafhaan with a sly smile that does not escape Rafhaan, who realises Shiyaz is behind the leaks, taking revenge for Ahna, and invites him to start at the office tomorrow. In private Rafhaan explains to Layaali that Shiyaz stayed at the house last week and used her laptop and the Wi-Fi; to prove it they will trap him with a file of false figures and have Aasim catch him red-handed.

Next day Shiyaz arrives looking innocent; Rafhaan gives him temporary charge of the finance servers and Layaali leaves a file marked "New resort secret budget" on her desk. At lunch break Shiyaz photographs it and copies the server to a hard drive — while Rafhaan, Layaali and Aasim watch on the cabin's hidden camera and Aasim records everything live. As he unplugs the drive, Rafhaan and Layaali walk in with security. Shiyaz stammers that he was "only checking the system" and tries to hide the drive; Rafhaan says all the evidence is recorded, and Layaali asks whether Ahna's life didn't teach him that envy gains nothing. Shiyaz bows his head, the drive drops, police take him away. Rafhaan sends the evidence to the investors, who transfer the delayed money.

That night on the moonlit balcony Rafhaan says every storm only makes their bond stronger; Layaali answers that no evil can stand before true love. Two months later Rafhaan Group is among the Maldives' leading companies — but Layaali often looks tired, feels dizzy and nauseous; one morning at her desk she suddenly has to lay her head down (sets up the pregnancy reveal of ep 482).
