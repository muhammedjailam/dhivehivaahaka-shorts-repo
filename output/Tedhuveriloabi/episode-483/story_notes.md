# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 483: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
Ahna walks out of prison, looks up at the sky and smiles: did Layaali really believe the letter of apology she sent? She endured three years of jail waiting for the day she would tear Layaali's life apart — she has come back not just for Rafhaan but to take everything. In Malé her father Ahmed Zaahir welcomes her, thinking she has reformed, until she says she will never forgive Layaali and Rafhaan. Her first goal is to win their trust; Zaahir laughs with her.

The next afternoon the doorbell of Rafhaan's house rings strangely. Layaali opens the door to Ahna, who weeps, drops at her feet and begs forgiveness — she wants a new life, free of envy. Kind Layaali raises her up and forgives her. Rafhaan comes home, visibly displeased, but Layaali persuades him Ahna deserves a second chance. Step one of Ahna's plan has worked.

Meanwhile Layaali's bedridden father Qaasim keeps looking at an old photo album of grand buildings and cars. While feeding him, Layaali asks about it. Weeping, he tells her that thirty years ago "Qaasim Enterprises" was the biggest company in Malé's market, until his most trusted partner forged the documents, robbed him of everything and put him on the street — that partner was Ahmed Zaahir, Ahna's father, whose "Zaahir Investments" was built on Qaasim's stolen fortune. Layaali's head spins. At the same moment, in Rafhaan's garden, Ahna strokes little Raina's head with a dangerous look.

Layaali weeps over the truth; then she hears Raina scream from the garden and rushes out to find Ahna holding Raina on her lap. Ahna asks sweetly why her eyes are red; Layaali takes Raina from her and asks her to leave for today. Outside, Ahna's smile turns into a sneer; she phones Zaahir: Layaali has learned something, Qaasim is still alive, the plan must move fast. Zaahir: Qaasim is on his deathbed; their aim is a big betrayal inside Rafhaan Group in Layaali's name, so Rafhaan himself throws her out.

That night Rafhaan finds Layaali lost in thought on the sofa; she tells him everything. Stunned, he realises Ahna's visits are a family plot; he will have Aasim dig into Zaahir Investments' records and fight for her father's rights. Next day Ahna brings Rafhaan a "new partnership agreement" file as a "gift for past wrongs"; he smiles, takes it, and once she leaves calls Aasim to check the contracts for fraud. Two hours later Aasim walks into the cabin, his face grave.
