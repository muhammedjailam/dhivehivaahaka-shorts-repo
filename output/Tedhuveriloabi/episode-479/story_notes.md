# Tedhuveriloabi — Episode 479: story notes

## Synopsis (English, internal only)
At sunset in the hospital (end of ep 456) Layaali shyly calls him "Rafhaan" for the first time; her smile is his answer. But danger waits: Ahmed Zaahir, furious at his daughter Ahna's arrest, has begun planning to bankrupt Rafhaan's company. Driving through Malé, Rafhaan is uneasy — relieved that Qaasim has moved from the ICU to the ward and that Layaali's name is cleared, but sure Zaahir will tear "Rafhaan Group" apart in revenge.

Next morning the office is frighteningly silent. IT head Aasim bursts into the cabin: Zaahir's company has written to the bank to pull its 40% share at once, and Zaahir has phoned the main investors of next month's "Seaview Resort" project, who now hesitate. Rafhaan's head spins — this is revenge for the three-day deadline. Khadeeja phones, furious: "my son is on the street — come home now!" Rafhaan tells Aasim to prepare the financial records and book the bank manager, and strides out. At the main door he meets Layaali, arriving with new hope; she sees his worry. He explains; she blames herself ("because of me you're losing everything"). He reassures her (the narration has him hold her shoulder): work done for justice brings no loss; stay and run the finance section — "you are the only one I trust." She nods, her love and respect growing.

At the mansion Khadeeja sits crying and Ibrahim Faahim is worried. Khadeeja: Zaahir will keep his shares and fund the project if the case against Ahna is dropped and Rafhaan marries her. Rafhaan refuses: Ahna framed a poor girl and endangered a sick man's life. "Do you want to end up on the street?" — "God willing the company will be saved; I'll find new investors." Back at the office he, Layaali and Aasim work late into the night; Layaali prepares the proposals. On the third day the Maldives representative of a big foreign company is amazed by her business plan: they will buy the 40% and fully fund the Seaview Resort. The company is saved. Rafhaan brings Layaali the news; tears of joy; "Without you I couldn't have done it... will you marry me?" Narrator: some storms come to strengthen true love and carry life to a safe harbour. Shy, smiling, she nods — the happiest moment of her life, in the golden afternoon light of the office.

But Ahna, in custody, is planning one last attack through her "devil" friend Raaya. In a dark Malé lane, Raaya sits in her expensive car on the phone to a hired man: she wants Layaali wiped from the world; "with money anything can be done." Not a road accident — something the police won't suspect: her father is in the hospital and Layaali is always there; plan it inside the hospital. Meanwhile at home Rafhaan tells his kind father about Layaali; Ibrahim, hand on his son's shoulder, backs him: "Tomorrow we go to their house and formally talk about the marriage." Khadeeja comes down the stairs, still displeased but with nothing left to say: "Do as you like... but I'm not happy joining that poor family," and turns away.
