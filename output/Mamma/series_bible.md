# Mamma (މަންމަ, "Mother") — series bible

Dhivehi audio family drama by the same narrator voice as the other series. This batch has six episodes that form ONE
continuous arc, each picking up exactly where the previous one stopped: **308 → 350 → 457 → 458 → 460 → 463**
(308 is the pilot). Every episode's cover (`work/episode-<N>-cover.png`) is the same series poster: a young woman in a
black abaya and black hijab (white under-scarf) sitting on a wet wooden park bench at night under a glowing street lamp
and palm trees, looking up with tears on her face. That woman is Shahula (present day).

Look: tender, melancholic family drama. Night scenes = deep blue-black darkness with warm amber lamplight (like the
poster), rain and wet reflections; island days = soft hazy tropical light; Malé interiors = warm homely light that grows
colder and dimmer as the marriage turns dark (see `style.txt`).

## Story structure (two timelines)
- **Present** (308 opening only): a stormy night; adult Shahula, wearing a black abaya and black hijab, steps off a
  passenger dhoni onto her childhood island's jetty with her baby son **Zidhaan** asleep on her shoulder and a bag in
  her hand, and shelters from the storm inside the island mosque's covered entrance. From there the narration goes into
  memory and the rest of the batch is one long flashback that moves forward in time until it reaches the night she
  leaves Aamir's house (end of 463).
- **Past**, in order: age 12 on the island (mother's death, 308–350) → Malé, at Azeeza's house, school years (350–457)
  → first job, Aamir's proposal, marriage, Azeeza's death, Aamir's cruelty (458) → baby Zidhaan, the mistress Reysham
  (460) → the night after, flashback of the lost first baby and of Zidhaan's birth, Shahula packing to flee (463).

## Places (keep them consistent)
- **The home island** (unnamed, local island): a small harbour with a stone jetty and passenger dhonis, a wide white
  sandy main road (bodumagu) with palms and coral-stone walls, the island's **old mosque** (white walls, a wide covered
  front porch with low wall-benches — "the mosque wall" where Shahula sits), and close beside it the **island cemetery**:
  a sandy yard of plain white-washed coral headstones under frangipani trees, enclosed by a low white wall.
- **Zubair's house**: an old coral-stone island house with a sandy yard, an outdoor kitchen area with a low wooden table
  and a tray, a wooden joali seat, a corrugated roof; Shahula's small bare bedroom with a narrow bed, a wooden window.
- **Thahmeena's house** (two-three houses down): a neat island house with a lit doorway and a streetlamp on the lane.
- **Azeeza's house in Malé**: a comfortable older two-storey Malé townhouse: a tiled front yard (gate, potted plants,
  a motorbike parked inside) with a **joali seat on the front porch (askani)**, a bright *fendaa* (hall) with a dining
  table, a kitchen with a fridge and a wall telephone, Azeeza's bedroom (wooden bed, dresser, framed calligraphy-free
  pictures of flowers), Shahula's small room (single bed, little cupboard, a prayer mat folded on it), Aamir's room
  (bed, wardrobe, desk). After the marriage (458 →) the same house becomes Shahula and Aamir's home: the main bedroom,
  a sitting room (*bodu fendaa*) with a grey sofa, a coffee table and a TV, a baby cot in the bedroom (460/463).
- **Malé streets**: narrow streets lined with tall pastel buildings, motorbikes parked along the walls, warm
  streetlights at night.
- **Shahula's school** (350/457): a Malé school gate in a white wall, schoolboys and schoolgirls streaming out, a
  street corner with a low wall nearby.
- **Symphony restaurant** (458): a busy modern Malé restaurant at night, warm pendant lights, a table for four.
- **Hospital** (463 flashbacks): IGMH-like Malé hospital — a night corridor, a maternity ward bed by a window, a small
  empty hospital bassinet, a waiting bench.

## Episodes
- **308** (pilot). PRESENT: black storm clouds, no moon; a dhoni struggles through big waves into the island harbour
  and comes alongside the jetty; passengers hurry off before the downpour; Shahula (black abaya, black hijab) with baby
  Zidhaan asleep on her shoulder and a bag, stands lost looking up the main road; rain begins; lightning and thunder
  make her flinch; drenched, she hurries to the mosque and shelters under its porch, sets the bag down, shifts the baby
  to her other arm ("How will I take Zidhaan in this rain?"); she looks at the cemetery near the mosque, now walled.
  "Mamma" — MEMORY: 12-year-old Shahula, raised by her brave single mother Khadheeja who worked in other people's houses
  for her schooling; Khadheeja died on a stormy night like this. Shahula looks at her mother's face one last time
  (NOT shown — see rules), sobs; Faathanikey pulls her aside and tells her to be patient; the bier (sandhoa) is carried
  out of the house; the eldest aunt (Bodudhaitha) stops Shahula following; she begs, is shut in a room, runs out in the
  heavy rain to the cemetery, sees the burial from the wall; after everyone leaves she sits by the grave mound and puts
  her head down on the sand; she hears her mother's voice: "Don't let your education go dark... be good and obedient".
  That night Shahula burns with fever; Faathanikey changes wet cloths on her forehead; Zubair, Khadheeja's brother, a
  fisherman far away at sea when she died (engine trouble delayed his dhoni) — burial already done on the imam's advice.
  NEXT MORNING: Zubair's dhoni returns; he comes home with his share of fish, no grief on his face, puts the fish on a
  tray to cut; Faathanikey brings a water pot and asks him to look at Shahula ("her fever won't come down"); he snaps
  ("Is this the first time someone died? ... we can't manage our own lives and now Shahula... better if she had taken
  her along!"); he rinses his hands and walks inside with a towel; Faathanikey, shocked and in tears, sits to finish the
  fish; she sees Shahula, wrapped in a blanket, shivering in the doorway, tears running — she heard it all.
- **350**. Afternoon (Asr): Shahula (12) sits on the mosque's porch wall staring at the cemetery, wiping tears;
  old Moosafulhu, leaving the mosque, gently leads her home along the main road; he calls for Faathanikey at the door;
  Shahula goes in to her room; Zubair is asleep outside on the porch; Moosafulhu goes home. NIGHT: dinner; Faathanikey
  goes to call Shahula — the room is empty; she rushes out, Zubair follows; at Leeza's house Thahmeena (busy hosting
  guests from Malé) hasn't seen her; Faathanikey cries; Zubair goes toward the main road; Faathanikey grabs his arm
  begging him not to hurt the girl (husband and wife — allowed, see rules); Azeeza, a wealthy Malé lady (Thahmeena's
  guest) asks what happened; her son Aamir (15) arrives; a girl walks past under the streetlight, face full of despair,
  and goes into a house two doors away — Azeeza follows; Faathanikey says she searched near the cemetery; Azeeza
  realises the girl was Shahula. Azeeza can't sleep, thinking of her. A WEEK later Shahula is silent, always at the
  mosque wall; Azeeza asks Zubair to let her take Shahula to Malé; Thahmeena: "he won't — she's his sister's only
  trust; he's grieving, he's kind really"; Azeeza smiles. MALÉ: Azeeza takes Shahula home, a safe happy house, a good
  school; she studies hard and forgets the island. YEARS PASS: Aamir loves her secretly; he's a handsome playboy with
  countless girlfriends, phone calls and letters (some of which Shahula even had to answer for him); between them a
  quiet distance. Aamir finishes studies, gets a job at his first interview; Azeeza is overjoyed. EVENING at home:
  Shahula (now ~17) calls "Aziththa, dinner's ready"; Azeeza goes to pray; Aamir prayed at the mosque on the way;
  kitchen: Shahula pours water, Aamir asks for warm water (stomach upset), calls "Shahula... stay a moment?"; the phone
  rings — a girl (Zuhdha, an old flame) asks for Aamir; Shahula hands the receiver over and leaves; Aamir hangs up;
  Shahula after prayer folds the prayer mat, uneasy; a knock — Azeeza, in great pain in her leg, asks her to fetch
  Fathumaththa; Aamir arrives on his motorbike with Ayya, calls her "Shahoo" for the first time, offers to take her —
  she shyly refuses ("I'll stay with Aunty") and goes inside; Ayya hops on the back; Aamir: "every obstacle has been
  removed... I'll wait — this is her last year — I won't rest until I have her".
- **457**. Continues: Aamir rides off, returns with Fathumaththa; Shahula leaves Azeeza's room; Aamir and Ayya go into
  Aamir's room; Aamir hands Ayya a bundle of love letters to return to a girl ("tell her not to call again"); Ayya
  jokes (a month of letters, a one-week romance); Aamir: "now I want a settled life". MEMORY (school days): after
  school Shahula waits by a wall corner; a group of schoolboys; Raamee, her sweetheart of a year, refuses to look at
  her; his friend Haneef: "his family doesn't approve"; she gives back his bicycle key she had kept to make him talk;
  he rides off coldly; she cries. The link breaks; school gossip; other boys chase her with flowers. NIGHT (present of
  457): she can't sleep, paces, lies down on the porch joali (shown SITTING — see rules); Aamir's motorbike enters the
  gate, the headlight hits her face; he sits on the joali beside — clear gap; they chat, he jokes ("are you mute?"),
  she laughs; "wake me early"; good night. DAWN: she knocks at his door, enters, calls him from beside the bed; he,
  half asleep, murmurs "are you in my dreams too?"; she calls again; he wakes; she hurries out. TIME PASSES: school
  ends, she gets an office job quickly; Azeeza is happy; Shahula's feelings for Aamir grow but she resolves to study
  law first (her mother's dream: become a lawyer and get justice for her father); she knows Aamir is fickle. An
  EVENING: back from work with a set of application forms, she sits by Aamir on the porch joali; he says he needs her
  advice: "I've decided to get married."
- **458**. Joali talk continues: she teases about his long list; he says his heart wants only one person; she rises to
  fill her university form (Shariah and Law — mother's dream, justice for her father); Aamir is uneasy (he wants a wife
  at home). In her room she hugs the form to her chest. Aamir knocks: "Mamma is calling." Azeeza, sitting up in bed,
  opens an old small jewellery box and takes out two shining gold bangles — the family heirloom, "Aamir has found
  their new owner" — and slips them onto Shahula's wrist. Shahula is stunned; sees Aamir's satisfied smile; realises
  she is repaying Azeeza's kindness; fear about her future; Azeeza: "do you agree?" — Aamir changes the subject.
  NIGHT: Aamir waits on his motorbike; they ride to Symphony restaurant; his colleague Tholaal and Naya; "This is my
  Shahoo — we'll marry very soon"; a table for four; the three colleagues chat loudly; Shahula left out; she learns
  Tholaal is married; Aamir signals "what's wrong?". At home she paces; Aamir knocks; "Isn't Tholaal married?" —
  "Don't take me to such places again"; Aamir: "Stay out of it." AFTER THE WEDDING: Shahula becomes Aamir's
  "possession", must quit her job; nearly a year in, Azeeza dies; Aamir's cruel side appears. One evening Aamir comes
  home to a dark silent house; Shahula is not there; he searches every room. She returns after Isha (she went to the
  office for something urgent); she is seven months pregnant; he sits on the sofa staring at the door; "Where were
  you?"; he kicks the sofa; accuses her of meeting Raamee at the office; she protests — he strikes her (NOT shown),
  grips her by the neck (NOT shown); the first time he laid a hand on her; the loving house has become a dark prison.
- **460**. A few months later, baby Zidhaan. Afternoon: someone calls salaam; Shahula lays the sleeping baby on the bed
  and goes to the hall; a young stranger: "Shahula? I came to meet you" — Reysham, holding a paper gift bag. Shahula
  knows the name. Reysham is fresh and young; Shahula, after the birth, feels worn out (her post-birth body is NEVER
  shown — see rules). She seats her, asks the helper Suneetha to bring a drink. Reysham: "I know you through Aamir —
  we've been together a long time... he's a man, men have freedoms women don't"; Shahula: "say that to Aamir — do you
  think such a man can be held by any woman?"; "colleagues three years, together one and a half; he asked first... you
  hold him with a child"; Shahula remembers Aamir murmuring "Reysham" in his sleep; "whenever you fight he comes to
  me... he says you are his mother's burden"; Suneetha brings masala tea and short-eats; Reysham: "next time I'll be
  the hostess here"; the baby cries, Suneetha runs to him; Reysham: "he's lost his sleep to the baby's crying — move to
  another room"; Shahula: "I have as much say in this house as Aamir"; Reysham leaves, the front door slams; Shahula
  holds her head, then hurls the gift bag away. NIGHT: she rocks Zidhaan in her lap, thinks: "I endured his cruelty
  for his poor mother's sake... but enough"; the baby smiles, then cries with her. Aamir comes home: Shahula freshly
  dressed, shaking the baby bottle Reysham brought; "a gift from your office girl for Zidhaan"; she goes to cook.
  11 p.m.: Aamir comes to bed (intimacy NOT shown — see rules); "Who is Reysham?"; she confronts him with tears, then
  without tears; he slaps her, her lip bleeds (NOT shown); "Do you know what happens when women stand up?"; he grips
  both her arms (NOT shown as contact).
- **463**. MORNING after: Shahula wakes in pain in the dim room; alone; thinks of Aamir's cruelty, prays for a loving
  family; the baby cries; she nurses him (shown modestly as cradling the wrapped baby under a large shawl); Aamir comes
  in with a travel bag: "I'm travelling tomorrow — pack clothes for two days, don't forget my shaving kit"; he sits on
  the edge of the bed, tries to touch her cheek — she pushes his hand away; he grips her arm; "please don't make me
  angry"; she speaks to the baby pointedly: "be kind... never hurt a woman... women are mothers, sisters... sow hatred
  and you reap hatred"; his phone rings, he leaves. Her split lip and swollen forehead (NOT shown). FLASHBACK: the night
  of her FIRST labour Aamir didn't answer his phone; she took a taxi alone to hospital in tears; called her office
  friend Fiyaza; eight hours of labour; emergency C-section delayed because no family member was there to consent; she
  signed the form herself; next morning the baby's cot was empty — Fiyaza's tear-filled eyes told her; Aamir blamed
  her. Within five months she was pregnant again; a hard second birth (blood transfusion, C-section); next morning Aamir
  sat by the bed, stroked her head, seemed changed; he sent a canteen boy with king-coconut water; returned an hour
  later smelling of a woman's perfume. PRESENT (the following day, Aamir gone): dark clouds gathering; Shahula hurries:
  takes the baby's clothes from the line, folds them into a bag, bottle, milk tin, the little money from a tin; each
  gust of wind makes her heart jump; she picks up the crying baby, opens the wardrobe, takes off Azeeza's two gold
  bangles and leaves them in the drawer; with the baby's birth certificate and papers she heads out of the room — and
  stops dead, startled (cliffhanger; who/what she sees is not said — show her frozen in the bedroom doorway, eyes wide,
  looking toward the dark hall).

## Binding content rules for this series (override the literal narration)
1. **Domestic violence is NEVER depicted** (458 kick/slap/neck grip; 460 slap, bleeding lip, arm grip; 463 arm grip,
   bruises). No hand raised, no hitting, no grabbing, no hand on a throat or arm, no blood, no red marks, no bruises,
   no swelling, no split lip, no tears of blood. Show instead: Aamir's furious face in harsh low light from her point of
   view; Shahula stepping back with both hands protectively over her rounded belly; her shocked frozen eyes; a long
   shadow of a man falling across the floor and the sofa; a toppled cushion on the floor; a dark bedroom with only a
   dim night-lamp; Shahula alone afterwards sitting on the floor against the bed / with her face turned away in shadow
   (no marks); her trembling hand. SFX at most a muffled `soft_thud` at −22…−24 dB, no scream.
2. **Death**: Khadheeja's face in death and her body are never shown. Show the white-shrouded bier (*sandhoa*) carried
   by men from a distance under rain, mourners' backs, the cemetery wall, a fresh low sand mound with a plain white
   coral headstone (no text), Shahula's small figure kneeling by it in the rain, head bowed. Azeeza's death: an empty
   wooden bed with a folded shawl, a closed Quran-sized green cloth-bound book on a stand (no visible script), women
   seated reciting with heads bowed (no readable text). The first baby's death: an empty small hospital bassinet with
   a folded white blanket; Fiyaza's tearful face; never a baby's body.
3. **Childbirth / hospital / body**: no labour scenes, no operating theatre, no surgery, no medical procedure, no
   needles, no blood bags. Show: a taxi's headlights on a rainy night street; Shahula in the back seat, hand on her
   belly, eyes closed in pain (fully dressed in abaya and hijab); a hospital corridor at night; her hand signing a paper
   on a clipboard (blank paper, no text); Fiyaza at her bedside; Shahula sitting up in a ward bed in a modest
   long-sleeved patient gown with a hijab. Her post-birth body, scar, weight, breasts and milk are NEVER shown or hinted.
4. **Nursing** (460, 463): never shown. Show her holding the baby wrapped in his blanket against her shoulder or in her
   lap, a large shawl over her, or the baby asleep in the cot; the bottle scene (460) shows her holding a baby bottle.
5. **Marital bed** (460 night, 463 morning): no couple in bed together, no embrace, no kiss, no hand on her. 460: Shahula
   alone lying turned to the wall is NOT shown — show her SITTING on the far edge of the bed in the dim light, fully
   dressed with hijab, Aamir standing by the door or sitting on a chair across the room. 463: Aamir sits on the edge of
   the bed at a distance, she sits up against the headboard holding the baby.
6. **Before marriage, Shahula and Aamir never touch** and keep a clear arm's-length gap (joali scenes 457/458, the
   kitchen 350, the bedroom wake-up 457 — she stands just inside the doorway calling him; he is under a blanket in a
   t-shirt, no touching). The motorbike ride (458) = show them arriving: Aamir standing by the parked motorbike outside
   the restaurant, Shahula already standing a step away. 350 offer = she stands at the gate refusing, Aamir on the bike.
   Teen sweetheart Raamee: always a distance apart, never touching; the key is handed over at arm's length.
7. **Shahula as a 12-year-old** (minor): grief shown with dignity — kneeling with head bowed, sitting on the wall,
   wrapped in a blanket in a doorway. Fever: SITTING propped on a pillow in her narrow bed under a blanket, Faathanikey
   pressing a folded wet cloth to her forehead. Never lying flat, never distressed in an undignified way.
8. **Zubair's fish**: whole fish on a wooden tray / in a basin; NO knife visible (his hands rest on the tray or he
   rinses them); no blood, no cut fish.
9. **The affair** (Tholaal/Naya at the restaurant; Reysham): no touching, no flirtatious poses; Tholaal and Naya sit
   side by side with a gap, laughing at the table. Reysham fully modest (lilac abaya, hijab), mocking expression only.
10. **Women's hijab in every shot** (indoors too); men always in long sleeves or t-shirts and long trousers / sarong.
11. **Prayer** is never depicted; mosque exteriors, the porch and men leaving after prayer are fine; a folded prayer mat
    on a cupboard is fine.
12. **No readable text**: letters, forms, the birth certificate, phones, signs, school gate, restaurant, hospital: all
    blank or illegible. No numbers.
13. **No music, no alcohol, no smoking, no dogs, no other faiths' symbols.** Restaurant drinks = juice and water.
14. The baby is always healthy-looking, fully dressed and wrapped; never crying in distress close-up for long — a
    small open mouth and scrunched face is enough.
