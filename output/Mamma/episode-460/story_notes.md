# Mamma — episode 460: story notes

## Synopsis
A few months after the birth of baby Zidhaan. One afternoon someone calls salaam; Shahula lays the sleeping baby on the
bed and goes to the hall. A young stranger in a lilac abaya asks "Shahula?" — she has come to meet her, holds out a paper
gift bag and introduces herself: "I'm Reysham." Shahula knows the name (Aamir has murmured it) but has never seen her.
Reysham's fresh youth makes Shahula, worn out since the birth, painfully aware of herself. Following Azeeza's tradition
of hospitality, Shahula seats her in the sitting room and asks the helper Suneetha for a cold drink. Reysham: she knows
Shahula through Aamir, they have been together a long time; "he's a man — men have freedoms women don't". Shahula,
steadying herself: "Say that to Aamir — do you think a man like that can be held by any woman?" Reysham: colleagues
three years, together a year and a half, Aamir asked first; "you even had a child to hold him". (Shahula remembers her
first pregnancy, when Aamir was most distant, and hearing him murmur "Reysham".) Reysham: whenever they fight Aamir
comes to her; he says Shahula is the burden his dying mother left him; she laughs mockingly. Shahula keeps her
patience — the real culprit is Aamir. Reysham rises, leaves the gift "for the little one"; Suneetha serves masala tea and
short-eats; "next time I'll be the hostess here". The baby cries, Suneetha runs to him; Reysham: "he's lost his sleep
to the crying — move to another room". Shahula: "You needn't tell me where my child and I sleep — in this house my say
runs as far as Aamir's." "Finished? Then go — I need to go to my child." Reysham leaves; the front door slams. Alone,
Shahula holds her head, weeps, then flings the gift bag away.
Night: she rocks Zidhaan in her lap: "I endured his cruelty for his poor mother's sake, on my promise to her — but
enough." The baby smiles, she lifts him and cries, and he cries with her. When Aamir comes home she is bathed and freshly
dressed, shaking the baby bottle Reysham brought: "a gift your office girl brought for Zidhaan — shouldn't waste it";
she goes to cook; Aamir stares at the bottle, puzzled. Near 11 p.m. he comes to bed; she lies with her back to him;
"Who is Reysham?" She confronts him with tears — she has heard that name from his lips — then without tears: "She's
younger and prettier... she'll never have a baby..." Aamir, feeling she has no right, strikes her (her lip bleeds);
"You've shown you're a strong man — but do you know what happens when women stand up?" He steps forward and grips both
her arms (cliffhanger).
