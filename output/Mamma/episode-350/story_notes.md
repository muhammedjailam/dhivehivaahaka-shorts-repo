# Mamma — episode 350: story notes

## Synopsis
Picks up the day after Khadheeja's burial (308). At Asr, 12-year-old Shahula sits alone on the low wall of the island
mosque's porch, wrapped in a thin shawl, staring at the walled cemetery and wiping her tears; her uncle Zubair's cruel
words from the morning still ring in her ears. Old Moosafulhu, leaving the mosque, gently tells her that her mother would
grieve to see her like this and walks her home along the main road. At Zubair's house he calls softly for Faathanikey;
Shahula slips inside to her room; Zubair is asleep on the porch bench, so Moosafulhu leaves quietly.

That night Zubair sits down to dinner; Faathanikey goes to call Shahula, finds the room empty and rushes out; Zubair
follows, puzzled. At Leeza's house, Thahmeena (busy hosting guests from Malé) has not seen her; Faathanikey breaks into
tears ("she has a high fever"). Zubair grumbles ("what misery now?") and strides off towards the main road;
Faathanikey hurries after him, holding his arm and pleading, afraid he will take his anger out on the girl. Azeeza, a
respected wealthy Malé lady and Thahmeena's guest, asks if a child is lost; Thahmeena explains the girl's mother died
last night. Azeeza steps into the lane; her 15-year-old son Aamir comes along, and a girl walks past under the
streetlamp, her face full of utter despair. "Who is that girl?" — Aamir doesn't know; Azeeza sends him in and follows
the girl, who goes into a house two doors away. Faathanikey returns crying — she searched even by the cemetery and
thinks Shahula ran off after hearing Zubair's words. Azeeza realises the girl was Shahula. She cannot sleep that night
and sits up with a start.

A week later Shahula is still silent and withdrawn, always sitting by the mosque looking at the cemetery. Azeeza asks
Zubair to let her take Shahula to Malé; he asks for time. Thahmeena doubts he will agree ("she's his sister's only
trust; he's short-tempered from grief but kind at heart"); Azeeza only smiles. Azeeza takes Shahula to Malé, gives
her a safe, happy home and a good school; Shahula forgets the island and gives herself to her studies.

Years pass. Aamir has secretly loved Shahula since his school days but never shows it; handsome, he is a playboy with
countless girlfriends, calls and letters — Shahula even had to write some replies. Between them, a silent closeness.
Aamir gets a job at his first interview; Azeeza is overjoyed. That evening Shahula (now ~17, final school year) calls
"Aziththa, dinner's ready"; Azeeza goes to pray, Aamir prayed at the mosque on the way. In the kitchen Shahula pours
water; Aamir asks for lukewarm water (upset stomach), calls her name, asks her to stay a while; the wall phone rings —
a girl asking for Aamir; Shahula hands it over and leaves. Annoyed, Aamir hangs up on Zuhdha, a grade-8 flame; only
Shahula lives in his heart. After prayer Shahula folds her prayer mat, uneasy and afraid; a knock — Azeeza, in great
pain in her leg, asks her to fetch Fathuma. As Shahula hurries out, Aamir arrives with Ayya (Ali) on his motorbike,
calls her "Shahoo" for the first time, offers to take her; she shyly refuses ("I'll stay with Aunty"), Ayya laughs,
and she goes inside. Ayya climbs on the bike and teases him; Aamir: "every obstacle has been removed... I'll wait —
this is her last year — I won't rest until I have her."

## Locations
Island: old mosque porch + walled cemetery (blank headstones), white sandy main road, Zubair's coral-stone house
(porch bench, sandy yard, outdoor dinner table), Thahmeena's lit doorway and the lamp-lit lane, a guest room, Thahmeena's
porch by day. Malé: Azeeza's townhouse gate/tiled yard, bright hall, porch with joali, kitchen with fridge and wall
telephone, Shahula's small room, front yard/gate at night.

## Tone
Grief and dignity on the island (hazy afternoon, then blue-black night with amber streetlamps); warmth and safety in
Malé; a faint unease growing around Aamir's intentions at the end.
