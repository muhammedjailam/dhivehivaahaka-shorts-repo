## Synopsis
Continues from 350, the same night at Azeeza's house in Malé. Aamir, sitting on his motorbike with Ayya, says he will
wait and only breathe easy once Shahula is his; he rides off and returns with the old healer Fathumaththa for Azeeza's
leg. Shahula comes out of Azeeza's room as Fathumaththa goes in and sees Aamir and Ayya go into Aamir's room. There
Aamir hands Ayya a bundle of love letters to return to a girl ("tell her not to bother me or keep calling"). Ayya
teases him: a month of letters for a one-week romance; he himself can't make one friend while Aamir collects and drops
girlfriends. Aamir sighs: now he wants to change, to start a settled life; Ayya jokes that he's trying emotional talk
this time.

MEMORY (school days): after school Shahula waits at a wall corner. A group of schoolboys stops on seeing her; Raamee,
her sweetheart, stops apart and looks the other way. She asks his friend Haneef why Raamee is angry: he won't talk or
call, hasn't answered any letter she sent from the island in two weeks. Haneef delivered the letters and her photo in
front of Raamee's parents; he thinks the family disapproves. Haneef leaves; Shahula begs Raamee to talk; he coldly
holds out his hand: "Give me my bicycle key, I have no time for useless talk." She returns the key she'd hidden to
make him talk; he rides off. Tears: their love was a year old today. If his family disapproves, she will step away —
an orphan, she longs for a mother-in-law who would love her like a mother. The bond breaks; gossip at school, girls
going after Raamee, boys chasing her with bouquets, fake sympathy.

NIGHT: sleepless, she paces her room, then goes out to the porch joali. A motorbike horn: Aamir opens the gate and
brings the bike in; its headlight hits her face as she rises. He asks kindly why she's up, sits on the joali and
invites her to sit; she steps back. He says he's lonely too, has no one to joke with — "Shahoo always lives in her
own world." She sits; he asks if all is well and about her exams — she only nods. "Are you mute?" — she laughs. He
yawns (office tomorrow) and asks her to wake him early; she agrees and goes in. Alone, Aamir: "Again I didn't dare say
it... but at least we talked tonight."

DAWN: she knocks, enters, calls him from near the bed; half-asleep he murmurs "Are you in my dreams too? If only it
were real... I'll go crazy." She calls again; he jolts awake; she reminds him and hurries out. He wonders what he said.

TIME PASSES: school ends; she quickly finds an office job; Azeeza is delighted. The friendship with Aamir grows close
and she begins to see him specially, but she resolves to study first: her mother's dream was for her to become a
lawyer and win justice for her father; she must stand on her own feet, and Aamir is fickle — a good friend, not a
life partner. She waits for university applications to open.

ONE AFTERNOON: home from work with a set of application forms, she sits by Aamir on the porch joali. He needs her
advice on an important decision: "I've decided to get married." (cliffhanger, continues in 458)
