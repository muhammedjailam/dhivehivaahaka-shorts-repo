## Synopsis
Picks up on the porch joali the evening Aamir told Shahula (≈19, now an office worker) he has decided to marry. She
teases him about his long list of girls; he says his heart has wanted only one person since the first day he saw her,
but she doesn't feel it. Laughing, she rises: she has to go and fill her university application — Shariah and Law, her
mother's dream, and a way to get justice for her father. Aamir is uneasy: he wants a wife who stays at home, and he is
sure she will obey him after the marriage. In her room she presses the form to her heart. Aamir knocks: "Mamma is
calling." Azeeza, ill and sitting up in bed, opens an old jewellery box and takes out two shining gold bangles — the
family heirloom from the grandmother's side; "Aamir has found their new owner today" — and slips them onto Shahula's
wrist. Shahula is stunned, sees Aamir's satisfied smile, realises this is how she must repay Azeeza's kindness, and is
gripped by fear. Azeeza asks "do you agree?", but Aamir changes the subject and mother and son forget her.
That night Aamir waits on his motorbike and takes her to Symphony restaurant. His colleague Tholaal and Naya join them;
"This is my Shahoo — we're marrying very soon." At a table for four the three colleagues chat; Shahula is left out and
learns Tholaal is married. Naya shows no regret. Aamir notices her unease. At home she paces; Aamir knocks; she asks
"Isn't Tholaal married? Don't take me to such places again." He says to stay out of it. She falls silent.
After the wedding she becomes his possession, made to fit his life and quit her job. Nearly a year in, Azeeza dies, and
Aamir's cruel side appears. One evening he comes home to a dark, silent house and searches every room. Shahula — seven
months pregnant — returns after Isha (she had to go to the office). He sits on the sofa staring at the door, rises
furious, lashes out at the sofa, accuses her of meeting Raamee at the office, and when she says "Stop it!" he strikes
her and grips her (neither shown). It is the first time he lays a hand on her; the loving house has become a dark prison.
